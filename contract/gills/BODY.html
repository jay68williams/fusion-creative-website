What we will deliver

Package: Growth.

Each month, Fusion Creative will:

  • film [TO CONFIRM: filming days a month] a month at the Gills office or at other locations agreed with you in advance, capturing the full month's content;
  • produce 25 short-form videos from that footage, edited with hooks and captions;

  • send you a monthly performance report so you can see how it is all doing.

The fee

The fee is £1,250 per month, paid in one payment at the start of each month.

Length

This agreement runs for an initial three months, then continues month to month. After the initial three months, either side can end it with 30 days notice.

What we need from you

To do this work, Fusion Creative needs access to the main account's Meta Business Suite (which covers Facebook and Instagram), TikTok and Instagram, plus access to whichever location we film at on days agreed in advance.

This agreement does not include access to, or management of, your advertising accounts or your Google Business Profiles.

Your accounts

Your social accounts, audiences, data and login credentials remain your property at all times. Fusion Creative's access is removed when this agreement ends.
