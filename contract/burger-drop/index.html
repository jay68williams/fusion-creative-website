Social Media Agreement

Fusion Creative and Burger Drop

Who this is between

Fusion Creative, a trading name of Fusion Media Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17347195) with its registered office at 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA (jay@fusioncreative.uk, 07437 571250). A short-form video and social media agency based in Newcastle.

Burger Drop, the smash burger restaurant group at 448 Westgate Road, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE4 9BL.

What we will deliver

Each month, Fusion Creative will:

  • film 1 content shoot day a month at the restaurant, capturing the full month's content (with the supplier sourcing series filmed quarterly);
  • produce 12 pieces of short-form content from that footage, edited with hooks and captions;
  • post them across your Instagram, TikTok and Facebook accounts; and
  • send you a monthly performance report so you can see how it is all doing.

The fee is GBP 899 per month, billed monthly in advance. The first month is due on signing, before the first shoot.

Planning and approvals

Each month, Fusion Creative will prepare a content plan for the month ahead and share it with Burger Drop for sign-off before filming.

Burger Drop has final approval of all content. Nothing is published until it has been approved. Content is sent for review before posting; if no changes are requested within 48 hours of a piece being sent, it is treated as approved so the posting schedule stays on track.

Ownership and raw footage

All final content produced and paid for under this agreement is owned by Burger Drop, with unrestricted rights to reuse it across organic social, paid advertising and any other marketing channels. Fusion Creative keeps the right to feature the work in its own portfolio and case studies.

Raw footage from shoots is made available to Burger Drop on request, in its native format, via a shared drive.

Ideas and intellectual property

Everything brand-specific is exclusively Burger Drop's: the concepts, campaign ideas, series, scripts and creative developed for Burger Drop under this agreement, any ideas originated by Burger Drop or anyone acting on its behalf, and Burger Drop's confidential business information. Fusion Creative will not reproduce, adapt, share or use any of it for any other client without Burger Drop's prior written consent.

Fusion Creative retains ownership of its general content formats, production methods and know-how used across its wider work.

Exclusivity

For as long as this agreement runs, Fusion Creative will not provide social media management, content creation, creative strategy or substantially similar services to another burger-focused restaurant brand without Burger Drop's prior written consent.

Your accounts

Burger Drop's social accounts, audiences, data and login credentials remain the property of Burger Drop at all times. Fusion Creative's access is removed on termination of this agreement.

Delivery and rollover

If contracted content is not delivered in a given month for reasons within Fusion Creative's control, the shortfall remains owed and rolls over to be delivered in the following month rather than expiring. This does not apply where the shortfall results from shoot access, approvals or materials not being made available to Fusion Creative.

Length and termination

This agreement runs for an initial three months, then continues month to month. After the initial three months, either side can end it with 30 days notice.

Either party may also terminate for material breach or persistent failure to deliver the agreed services, by giving 14 days written notice describing the issue. Termination takes effect if the issue is not remedied within those 14 days.
