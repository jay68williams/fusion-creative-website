Social Media Agreement

Fusion Creative and Ranches

Who this is between

Fusion Creative, a trading name of Fusion Media Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17347195) with its registered office at 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA (jay@fusioncreative.uk, 07437 571250). A short-form video and social media agency based in Newcastle.

Ranches, the American-style burger restaurants at Caithness Road, Sunderland SR5 3RE and Ponteland Road, Newcastle upon Tyne NE5 3AH.

What we will deliver

Each month, Fusion Creative will:

  • film 1 content shoot day a month at one of your shops, rotating between Ranches Sunderland and Ranches Newcastle, capturing the full month's content;
  • produce 12 short-form videos from that footage, edited with hooks and captions;
  • post them across your Instagram, TikTok and Facebook accounts; and
  • send you a monthly performance report so you can see how it is all doing.

The fee is GBP 899 per month, billed monthly in advance. The first month is due on signing, before the first shoot.

Length

This agreement runs for an initial three months, then continues month to month. After the initial three months, either side can end it with 30 days notice.

Getting started

Alongside this agreement, please complete our short onboarding form at fusioncreative.uk/ranches-onboarding. It covers your story, the burgers and desserts you would like us to film at each shop, and what we need to know for the first shoot.
