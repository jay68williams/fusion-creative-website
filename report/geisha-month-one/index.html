15 September to 15 October. Two shoots, both venues, 31 videos, and an account reaching ten times the people it was a month ago.

1

Where we started

The 28 days before our first post went out.

Geisha already had the hard part. Two genuinely beautiful rooms, a kitchen turning out food worth filming, and more than 21,000 people already following on Instagram. What was missing was a steady stream of content actually reaching them.

Posting ran at roughly one piece every three days. In the 28 days to 14 September the account was viewed 36,797 times and reached 6,015 accounts. TikTok was effectively not running at all.

2

What we have done

  • Two full shoots, both covering Asia and Fusion. The first on 12 September, the second on 29 September built entirely around the new menu and your offers.
  • 31 videos produced from those two days, against the 24 a month in your agreement.
  • Posting went from once every three days to near daily, across Instagram and TikTok.
  • The new menu launch given its own run of content, from the walkthrough and the POV to the paella, the nduja pizza and the build your own burger.
  • TikTok rebuilt from zero and posted to properly for the first time.
  • A live posting schedule in your portal, so you can always see what is going out and when.

The number worth pausing on is reach. A month ago the account was getting in front of 6,015 people in a four week stretch. It is now getting in front of 60,807. That is ten times as many people seeing Geisha, and the large majority of them do not follow you yet.

What the numbers are telling us

The maths game was the strongest video on both platforms, and it is not a coincidence. The videos where you and the team are playing off each other travel considerably further than the food filmed on its own, however good the food looks.

The second thing is that reach has grown far faster than follower count. That is exactly what we want in month one: Instagram is pushing this content to people who have never heard of Geisha, which is where new covers come from.

5

Month two

The plan for the next month is to lean into what has already proved itself. More of the two of you on camera together, more of the rooms, and the new menu kept in front of people while it is still new.

Alongside that we want to get the Sunday roast and the bottomless brunch properly on camera, since both are easy sells that we have not given a run yet, and keep building TikTok while it is young and growing quickly.
