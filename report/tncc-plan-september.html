The gap

Almost nobody is buying twice

2,720 orders from 2,689 customers

In August that is 1.01 orders per customer. Nearly every sale came from someone new, bought with ad spend.

This is the single biggest opportunity in the business right now. Roughly 2,700 new customers a month are arriving and then not hearing from us again. Candles and melts burn out, so this is a product people should be rebuying every few weeks.

Fixing it makes every pound of ad spend worth more, because the same customer buys two or three times instead of once.

The plan

Three moves for the rest of the year

Starting now

Email, so customers come back

The cheapest revenue available. Automated flows that run in the background off customers we have already paid to acquire.

  • Welcome and first purchase flow
  • Post purchase and replenishment, timed to when a candle runs out
  • Abandoned basket and browse recovery
  • Win back for anyone who has not bought in 60 days
September

Google, to catch demand the ads create

The Meta ads reached 389,000 people in August. A share of those people go and search for us afterwards, and right now that search is uncaptured.

  • Branded search first, small budget, protects the name
  • Google Shopping off the existing product feed
  • Built in September so it is fully trained before the Christmas peak
October

Live selling, from the new unit

Live shopping on TikTok, Instagram and Whatnot is where a lot of product brands are finding their next step. It suits this product: visual, demonstrable, and priced for impulse.

  • Set up a permanent live corner in the new unit
  • Built through October, ready for Black Friday and Christmas
  • Event lives first, tied to gifting moments, before any regular schedule
  • Builds the repeat community the numbers above say we do not have yet
Investment

Monthly fee

£1,400 per month, covering content, paid ads, email and website

Ad spend is separate and paid directly by Northumberland Candle Co, as it is now. This rate holds up to £15,000 of monthly ad spend, and we review together above that.

Live selling sits outside the retainer and would be handled on commission of live sales, so it only costs when it sells.
