Store revenue, order volume, paid social and email performance for The Northumberland Candle Company across August, with July alongside for comparison.

The store took £112,230 in August from 3,650 orders, more than double July, on £14,677 of ad spend. Every £1 of ad spend returned £7.65 in total store revenue.

Activity

What ran in August

Halloween range launched
Range live on site from 15 August with its own collection, homepage hero and theme. The full Halloween ad set (12 ads) went live 29 August.
Payday Mega Weekend
28 to 31 August. 1000ml reed diffusers and the gift set of 3 reduced, supported by 4 campaign emails. 250+ orders and £7,600+ over the weekend alone.
New products
360ml room sprays, velvet and speckled pumpkin candles, singing plush range, crystal touch melter and Halloween bundles all launched during the month.
Store conversion work
Bundle-and-save prompts added across snap bars, tins, mini boxes and pumpkin candles, one-tap add buttons in the cart, and a site-wide quantity bug fixed on product pages.
