September
in review

Our first month together. Two shoots, both venues, and the numbers that came out the other side.

1

Where we started

The 28 days to 14 September, before our first post went out.

Geisha already had the hard part: two beautiful rooms, a serious kitchen and an audience of more than 21,000 people on Instagram. What it did not have was a steady stream of content reaching them.

Posting ran at roughly one piece every three days. A typical post was seen around 2,760 times, and the best performing post on the account had reached 13,020. Across the whole 28 day window the account was seen 36,797 times and reached 6,015 accounts. TikTok was effectively starting from nothing.

2

What we have done

15 September to 1 October.

  • Two full shoots, both covering Asia and Fusion: the first on 12 September, the second on 29 September built around the new menu and your offers.
  • Posting went from once every three days to near daily, across Instagram and TikTok.
  • The new menu launch was turned into its own run of content, now going out through October.
  • TikTok restarted from scratch and is being posted to properly for the first time.
  • A posting schedule you can see, so you always know what is going out and when.

5

Into October

The new menu content from the 29 September shoot is running now and is booked through to the middle of the month, with the paella, the nduja pizza, the build your own burger and the full menu walkthrough all going out.

The focus for October is simple: more of what already works. More of the two of you on camera together, more of the rooms, and the new menu in front of as many people in Newcastle as we can reach.
