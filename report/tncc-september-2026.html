Activity

What ran in September

Warehouse move
The warehouse move completed mid-month, with full dispatch resumed from 17 September and customers updated by email. The transition weighed on order flow through the first half of the month.
Highland Cow Christmas range
The Highland Cow Christmas range was photographed and built out with studio and lifestyle shots, SKUs, dimensions and stock, and taken live on site ready for the Christmas push.
Weekend offers
Three weekend pushes ran: 10 snap bars for £10, £5 ghost bundles, and touch burners at £12.49, each supported by campaign emails. High volume at a lower average order value.
Ad account rebuild
Underperforming ads were paused and refreshed in waves, and a separate campaign was created to revive strong ads that the main campaign had starved of budget. A weekend billing outage on 26 to 27 September took ads offline and is now resolved.
Next

Set up for October

Efficiency over spend
Ad spend is now at the agreed ceiling, so the focus shifts to lifting average order value back toward £30 with bundles and add-ons, and refreshing the fatigued top performers rather than adding budget.
Christmas ramp
With the Highland Cow Christmas range live, the full Christmas range and its ad set move to the front of the queue, ready to scale through the peak trading window.
