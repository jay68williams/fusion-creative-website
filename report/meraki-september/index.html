Fusion CreativeSeptember Report
Meraki Greek Street Food · Hanley, Stoke-on-Trent

September in review.

Our first full month together. Two shoots, a month of content across three platforms, and the numbers that came out the other side.

1

Where we started

Late August, before our first post went out.

Meraki already had the hard part: a genuinely authentic Greek kitchen, a distinctive brand, and an Instagram audience of just under 1,000 followers. What it did not have was a steady stream of content reaching new people, and TikTok was effectively dormant at 36 followers.

Posting was sporadic, and the account had never been pushed properly on TikTok or distributed across Facebook. That was the starting line.

2

What we have done

29 August to 30 September.

  • Two full shoots at the shop: the first on 29 August, the second on 26 September, giving us a month of content plus a head start on October.
  • Posting went from sporadic to daily, across Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.
  • TikTok restarted from scratch and is being posted to properly for the first time.
  • Instagram trial reels, which test each video with new people first and carry the best ones across to Facebook as well.
  • A posting schedule you can see in your dashboard, so you always know what is going out and when.

Into October

The content from the 26 September shoot is running now and is booked through the month, posting daily at 1pm across your platforms.

The focus for October is simple: more of what already works. More of the "best in Stoke" hooks, more of the food reveals that carried across to Facebook, and getting Meraki in front of as many people in Stoke-on-Trent as we can reach.
