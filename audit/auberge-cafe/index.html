01 · The short version

A waterfront room people already film, and a feed that has gone quiet.

Auberge has the things that make short video easy: a terrace on the Majaz waterfront, the skyline across the water, a breakfast and dinner board built for two, lattes with art on top, and a steady stream of creators who come in and post about you. What is missing is you. Your own Instagram has not posted since 14 April 2026, and your website is down with a server error, so the audience those creators send your way has nowhere current to land. What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A terrace on the Al Majaz waterfront, with the Sharjah skyline across the water. That view is the set, and it is in almost every post other people make about you.
  • A breakfast and a dinner board built for two, the kind of spread that fills a frame. Creators are already filming it.
  • Lattes with art on top, cakes and Arabic breakfast plates. Small moments that make easy, repeatable video.
  • About 14,000 followers who already chose to follow you, and a 4.8 rating from more than 650 reviews on Restaurant Guru.
  • A location people travel to for the view. Every table by the water is a reason to film and a reason to book.

You do not need a new room or a new menu. You need your own channels switched back on: a site that works, and a steady flow of short video of the terrace, the food and your guests, posted on a plan, so the people those creators reach can find you and book a table this week.

Your own feed stopped in April. The twelve most recent posts we can read that tag you are almost all from other accounts, creators and promotion pages, and they do well. The pattern:

  • Other people are doing your posting, and it works. A creator's breakfast reel from 1 October, a page running your dinner table to 1,544 likes, another running your breakfast table to 4,531. Your own last two posts drew 35 and 46 likes.
  • Your own channel has the audience and none of the content. About 14,000 people already follow you, and the newest thing they have seen from you is six months old.
  • The view does the heavy lifting. Nearly every post that travels shows the same thing: the board, the latte and the skyline across the water. That is yours to film every week.

What this means. The attention is already there. People are filming your room and your food for you. Putting that same video on your own feed, on a plan, is how it turns into covers for you instead of likes for someone else.

07 · The plan

A plan built around the room you already have.

  1. Step 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the twelve videos. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and set up a working page and a booking link so every viewer can act.

  2. Step 2
    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list: a full breakfast and dinner service on the terrace, the board, the bar and your guests, in one day.

  3. Step 3
    Edit and deliver

    We edit, caption and post the videos to Instagram and TikTok. Each one ends with a reason to book and a tap to do it.

  4. Step 4
    Handover

    We report what the videos reached and what they brought in, then hand over every video for you to keep and reuse.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos filmed on your terrace by a licensed UAE crew to our shot list, edited and captioned, posted to launch them. You keep every video.
