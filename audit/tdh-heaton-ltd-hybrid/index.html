01 · Where you are now

What a new customer finds

Micropub on Heaton Road

Why it matters: Heaton Road is already a drinking street, with several bars within a few minutes' walk. People are out on it every night, so the footfall is there.

What we'd do: Film the walk past the front and the welcome at the door, so locals recognise the shopfront before they visit.

From the team behind the Cock and Bull

Why it matters: Jake has run micropubs in Northumberland for years, and the Cock and Bull is the model for this pub. That experience is a story worth telling.

What we'd do: Introduce the people behind the bar on camera, so new customers feel like regulars before they arrive.

Instagram is your only shopfront

Why it matters: 2,008 followers from 10 posts is a strong start, and it all lives on one app. Your profile has to answer what you are, when you're open and why tonight.

What we'd do: Pin three videos: who you are, your named weeknight and a skit that shows the atmosphere. Post short-form video every week.

No website live

Why it matters: curioustaverns.uk currently shows a disabled page. Anyone searching the group finds a dead end.

What we'd do: Point every bio link at Instagram for now and keep opening hours up to date on Google.

Untappd: no check-ins yet

Why it matters: Craft beer fans use Untappd to find where to drink. An empty venue page tells them nothing about your taps.

What we'd do: Encourage check-ins with a small sign at the bar, and feature the rotating taps in your videos.

No Meta ads running

Why it matters: You don't need ads yet. Four weeks in, organic video is the best way to find out what people respond to.

What we'd do: Build a bank of content first. Once a format works, it can be boosted locally later.

The weeknight opportunity

You told us you want to be better known to students and professionals, and to fill your weekdays. On our check of your street, Wednesday has a quiz and Sunday has acoustic music, but Monday, Tuesday and Thursday aren't clearly owned by anyone. Your Sunday Funday already covers the weekend end of the week.

  • Students go out midweek and like a regular thing to do with friends.
  • Young professionals want an easy after-work spot close to home.
  • One named night gives both groups a reason to pick you, and gives every video a call to action.

Why skits work

We measured 859 posts across our food and drink clients. Skits had about four times the median views of food and process films, and games had the highest median of all.

  • The 70M reel was a skit: "There's always the one impatient customer".
  • Real people in the venue, a phone-shot look and a bold line of text across the top.
  • No dialogue needed, so shy staff can simply work in the background.
