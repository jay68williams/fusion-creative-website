What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The food and the reputation are already there

This is the hard part, and you have nailed it. A menu people rave about and a wall of strong reviews is something most restaurants spend years chasing. You do not need to become good, you already are. You need more people to see it.

✓ Whickham has trusted you since 1989

Over three decades in the same spot is a story in itself. That heritage, the loyalty, the families who have been coming for years, is exactly the kind of authenticity that lands on social. Newer places would love to have it and simply cannot fake it.

✓ You already post good food photography

Your Instagram shows the dishes off and the posting habit is there, which puts you ahead of plenty of restaurants. The raw material is right there on the pass every night. It just needs shaping into video that travels rather than photos that sit in the feed.

✓ Plenty of reasons to visit

Lunch, evening service, a Tuesday menu and takeaway means you have several occasions to sell and several angles to film, from a quick weekday lunch to a proper weekend feast. Each one is a different piece of content aimed at a different diner.

✓ You are genuinely local

Your audience is right on the doorstep, and short-form video is unbeatable at reaching people within a few miles who are deciding where to eat this week. Being the neighbourhood favourite is a huge advantage once the content is built to be found.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the food, the reputation and the heritage. The job is to turn that into reach and covers: get Jamdani in front of hungry people across Newcastle and make it the name they think of when someone says "let's get a curry". These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Food the Content

Nothing sells a restaurant on social like the food itself, filmed properly. The sizzle off a hot karahi, the steam lifting off a fresh biryani, the naan puffing in the tandoor, the first spoon into a rich curry. Shot close, slow and hungry, a single dish becomes a scroll-stopper.

This is the highest-return content a restaurant can make, because it works on the one thing everyone watching is already feeling: hunger. Your kitchen produces the perfect material every single service.

2

Tell the Jamdani Story

Since 1989 is a headline most restaurants would love. The people behind the pass, the recipes handed down, the regulars who have been coming for years, the pride in doing it properly. People do not just eat at a restaurant, they buy into it, and a place with real history has a story worth telling.

Founder and team-led content builds the kind of familiarity that turns a first-time viewer into a booking, and it is content only Jamdani can make.

3

Own "Best Curry in Newcastle"

"Where to get the best Indian in Newcastle." "The Whickham curry house locals keep quiet about." Local, area-led content is watched by exactly the people who could be at your table this weekend, and it positions you as the go-to spot in the area rather than one of many.

This is where a genuinely local, long-established restaurant can out-market the chains and the delivery-only kitchens, because you are the real thing and you can prove it.

4

Turn Reviews and Full Tables Into Proof

Your reviews and your busy nights are your best marketing, and right now they are hidden away. A quick clip of a happy table, a five-star review on screen over a shot of the dish it is praising, the buzz of a full room on a Saturday. Proof that people love it is the most persuasive thing you can post.

It answers the only question a new diner really has, is it actually good, with evidence rather than a claim. Social proof travels, and it fills tables.

5

Short-Form Video and Targeted Local Ads

Good food photos in the feed is a start, but short-form video built to travel, with proper hooks, is what earns reach on Reels and TikTok. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at diners within a few miles of Whickham, and you put your best food and story content in front of people deciding where to eat this week.

For a restaurant, a handful of extra covers a night pays for a lot of content and ads, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in a Single Service

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do every night: the food, the kitchen, the story and the room. Each one could be filmed on a phone during one service.

Idea 01
"The dish that keeps Whickham coming back"
SignatureClose-upSizzle

A slow, cinematic hero reel of your signature dish being finished and served: the sizzle, the steam, the sauce, the first pull of a fresh naan. Strong hook on frame one ("this has been on the menu since 1989 for a reason"). Trending audio. Under 20 seconds. Pure hunger.

Why it works: Food filmed this well is the single most shareable thing a restaurant can post, and it makes people want to book a table there and then.
Idea 02
"Serving Whickham since 1989"
StoryHeritageTrust

A short story reel: the years on the same street, the people behind the pass, a nod to the regulars who keep coming back. Warm, proud, human. A few seconds of history over shots of the food and the room, ending on why you still do it the proper way.

Why it works: Heritage builds instant trust and stands you apart from the chains and delivery kitchens. Only Jamdani can tell this story.
Idea 03
"Behind the tandoor"
KitchenPOVSkill

A satisfying behind-the-scenes look at a signature dish being made: the spices going in, the flames off the tandoor, the naan slapped against the wall, the plating. People love watching skill and craft up close. Hook it with "how we make the dish everyone orders".

Why it works: Kitchen and process content is endlessly watchable and shows the care behind every plate, which is exactly what makes people choose you.
Idea 04
"Where to get the best curry in Newcastle"
LocalReachBooking driver

A local-first reel aimed straight at hungry Newcastle: a quick run through the dishes worth the drive to Whickham, tagged to the area, built to be saved and sent to the mate who always picks where you eat. A love letter to the food and the neighbourhood.

Why it works: Local content reaches the exact people who could be at your table this weekend and turns Jamdani into the default answer to "where shall we eat?".
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into booked tables and orders.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone decides on dinner, they search and read reviews before they book. Your Google profile is doing quiet, heavy lifting, and it deserves to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised for "Indian restaurant Whickham" and "curry Newcastle"
  • Funnel every happy diner into a fresh Google review while they are still at the table
  • Add fresh dish photos and menu updates regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Fill the Quieter Nights

Lunch, the Tuesday menu and midweek covers are where content earns its keep fastest. A steady drip of tempting video tied to your quieter slots gives people a reason to come in when they otherwise would not.

  • Content built around your lunch and Tuesday offers
  • Dish-of-the-week reels to drive midweek bookings
  • Takeaway and collection nudges for the nights people stay in

Targeted Local Ads to Nearby Diners

Because a full table is high value and repeats, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around Whickham and Newcastle puts your best food and story content in front of people deciding where to eat this week.

  • Target diners within a few miles of the restaurant
  • Use your strongest food and "best curry" reels as the ads
  • Drive bookings and takeaway orders straight from the ad
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Jamdani: A handful of extra covers a night covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a restaurant can do.
