What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ You are a genuine North East institution

Serving since 1977 is not a tagline, it is decades of families who grew up on your fish suppers. Heritage like that is gold for content. Most takeaways would kill for a story people already feel something about, and yours is barely being told where new customers actually look.

✓ The product is made for social

Fish and chips is some of the most watchable food there is, the batter crunch, the chips going in, the tray coming together on a Friday night. Food content pulls in reach like almost nothing else online, and you serve it fresh every single day.

✓ A shop network most takeaways would envy

Shops across Tyneside, from Heaton to Ponteland to Sunderland, is not just a business advantage, it is content fuel. Every location is a reason to post, a local audience to reach and a "which Gills is your Gills" story waiting to be filmed.

✓ The commitment to showing up is there

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the food, the heritage and the shops. The job is to turn that into reach and orders: get in front of hungry people across Tyneside, remind the regulars and pull in a new generation who scroll before they decide where to order. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Food the Hero

The fastest win is appetite. Close-up, crunch-and-steam video of the batter breaking, the chips being salted, the tray coming together. Filmed properly with the right audio, a fish supper is one of the most scroll-stopping things you can post, and it costs nothing to shoot because you make it every day.

Get people hungry watching the video and the order follows. That is the whole game with food content.

2

Turn the Shop Network Into a Content Advantage

Most chippies have one location. You have shops across Tyneside, and that is a genuine edge. "Which Gills is your Gills", the friendly rivalry between shops, spotlighting each area, reaching each local audience where they live. A network gives you more stories, more faces and more reasons to post than a single shop ever could.

Handled right, every shop becomes its own little pull, all feeding the one brand.

3

Tell the "Since 1977" Story

Nearly fifty years serving the North East is a story people genuinely care about. The history, the same family recipes, the customers who came as kids and now bring their own. Heritage content builds a level of loyalty and warmth that a discount post never will, and it is uniquely yours.

This is content only Gills can make, and it is exactly what makes people choose you over a faceless newcomer.

4

Turn Customers and Treat Night Into Content

Friday night, the match, the big family order, the tea after a long shift. Your food is tied to real moments, and that is prime content. Reactions, regulars, the "go-to order" of the staff, quick customer clips. Real people enjoying real food is the most persuasive thing a takeaway can post.

It turns your everyday customers into the marketing, and it travels far further than a polished advert.

5

Put the Team on Camera

People follow people, not logos. The staff behind the counter, the fryers who have been there years, the banter, the pride in the job. Personality-led food content is some of the best performing there is right now, and it builds a familiarity that keeps people coming back.

The characters in your shops are a huge asset. Get them on camera and Gills stops being a name and becomes a set of faces the region knows.

6

Reels and TikTok for Reach, Plus Targeted Local Ads

Posting deals as photos keeps you visible to the people who already follow you. Short-form video built to travel, with proper hooks, is what earns new reach. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget across your shop areas, and you put your best food content in front of hungry locals right when they are deciding where to order.

For a takeaway, a steady lift in orders and app downloads pays back that content and ad spend fast, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do every day: the food, the heritage, the shops and the people. Each one could be filmed on a phone in one of your shops.

Idea 01
"The perfect Friday fish supper"
FoodASMRAppetite

A fast, close-up food reel: batter hitting the fryer, the crunch, chips salted, the tray coming together, wrapped and ready. Strong hook on frame one ("this is what a proper fish supper looks like"). Crisp audio on the crunch. Under 20 seconds. Pure appetite.

Why it works: Food this good is scroll-stopping, and appetite content drives orders faster than any offer. It gets people hungry, and hungry people order.
Idea 02
"Serving the North East since 1977"
HeritageStoryLocal

A short heritage piece: the years on the wall, the same recipes, a regular who has come since they were a kid. Warm, proud, a love letter to a North East staple. Lead with the number ("frying for the North East since 1977").

Why it works: Heritage builds loyalty and warmth a discount never will, and it is a story only Gills can tell. It makes people choose you on feeling, not price.
Idea 03
"Which Gills is your Gills?"
ShopsLocalCommunity

A quick tour across your shops, Heaton, Wallsend, Ponteland, Sunderland and the rest, each with its own character and regulars. Tag every location so it reaches each local audience. Playful friendly rivalry between the shops.

Why it works: Your shop network is a genuine edge, and local tags reach each neighbourhood directly. It turns "a chippy" into "my chippy" for every area you serve.
Idea 04
"A day behind the counter at Gills"
TeamPOVBehind the scenes

The team in action through a shift: prep, the lunchtime rush, the banter, the staff go-to order. Honest, warm, full of personality. Strong hook ("come behind the counter on a Friday night at Gills").

Why it works: People follow people. Faces and personality build familiarity fast, and familiarity is what keeps customers loyal and coming back.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into orders and app downloads.

Get Every Shop's Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone is deciding where to eat, they search and they read reviews before they order. With shops across Tyneside, each location has its own Google profile doing quiet, heavy lifting, and they deserve to be fed and front and centre.

  • Keep each shop optimised for "fish and chips" plus its town and postcode
  • Funnel happy customers into fresh Google reviews at each location
  • Keep photos of the food current across every profile
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Drive Online Ordering and App Downloads

You already have online ordering and apps, which most chippies do not. Content should be doing the job of pushing people straight into them, so the attention turns into orders you own rather than orders lost to the delivery platforms.

  • Clear calls to order direct on the website and app in every post
  • Content built around "order direct" moments and regulars
  • Turn app users into repeat orders with the right content rhythm

Targeted Local Ads to Hungry Locals

Because a takeaway order is a quick decision, well-targeted local ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around each shop puts your best food content in front of people nearby right when they are thinking about tea.

  • Target the postcodes around each of your shops
  • Use your strongest food and heritage reels as the ads
  • Drive orders and app downloads, not just views
  • Track cost per order so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Gills: With multiple shops and food this shootable, a steady lift in orders covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a takeaway can do.
