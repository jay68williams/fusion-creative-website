What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A dish with a story: the Original DoughBoy Parmesan, crispy chicken, béchamel and melted cheese, with chunky chips and dips for £11.99.
  • A menu built for the camera: the Parmo Toastie on brioche, the Parmo Burger at £8.49 and the Parmo Loaded Fries.
