01 · The short version

A busy venue with stories it is not yet telling.

Deer in the Dene has more going on than most cafés we look at: breakfast to late food three nights a week, wood-fired pizza, £10 pizza Saturdays, a new Sunday lunch, a takeaway next door, movie nights, venue hire and dogs welcome. Your Instagram already posts most weeks. What is missing is video that travels beyond the 2,950 people who follow you, and a reason for a High Heaton or Jesmond local to book this weekend instead of next month.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A full all-day offer: breakfast, brunch, lunch, tapas, wood-fired pizza and cocktails at £7.95 or two for £14.
  • A new Sunday lunch that sold out on its first day, and you have the post to prove it.
  • Events people buy tickets for, like the Grease movie night with cocktails included.
  • Venue hire, a takeaway next door and dog friendly, three reasons to visit that many cafés cannot offer.
  • Professional photography on your website of the room, the kitchen and the food.

You do not need a new website or a new menu. You need short video of the room, the food and the people, posted on a plan, so locals who have never been in have a reason to book.

Deer in the Dene posts most weeks. The pattern in your recent posts:

  • News beats specials. The Sunday lunch countdown (45 likes) and the sell-out (33) beat the specials and opening-hours posts (5 to 12).
  • Offers need a face. "£10 pizza tonight" earned 11 likes as a graphic. The same offer with a person and a pizza coming out of the oven travels much further.
  • Events sell themselves on video. Your Grease night is the kind of thing people share with friends, if they can see what the night looks like.

What this means. Your audience responds to real moments in the venue. Short video of those moments, posted on a plan, is how they reach locals who do not follow you yet.
