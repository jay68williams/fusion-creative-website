What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ Seven years of showing up

Running your own social media in-house for seven years, across two shops, is a discipline most owners never manage. The habit is built, the brand voice exists, and the photos show real pride in the food. The issue is not effort, it is format and reach, and those are exactly the things we fix.

✓ The product was made for the camera

Smash burgers on the grill, cheese pulls, loaded hotdogs, churros, and a dessert Creation Station where the customer builds their own. Most businesses have to manufacture visual moments. Yours happen every evening service, on demand. That is a rare head start for short-form video.

✓ A claim worth fighting for

"#1 best burgers in the North East" is a big, confident position, and local creators are already asking the question in their own videos. A claim like that is a content engine in itself: every video either proves it or dares someone to disagree, and both get watched.

✓ Two catchments, one brand

Five years of loyalty in Sunderland plus a brand-new Newcastle site is the perfect setup: one shop with a heritage story, one with a launch story, and a brand that gets to speak to both cities at once. Very few independents get two bites at the local conversation like this.

✓ The infrastructure already converts

A proper website, online ordering for both shops, reservations, delivery. When the content starts pulling attention, there is already somewhere for it to go. Most places we audit have to build this after the views arrive. You built it first.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the product, the graft and two shops. The job is to turn that into reach and orders: get in front of the people across Sunderland and Newcastle who would love Ranches but have never heard of it, and make it the burger they already crave before they ever see the menu. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Food the Star

The single biggest change: shift from posting about the food to filming the food properly. The smash on the grill, the flip, the cheese pull, the stack, the first bite. Shot close, cut to the beat, under 25 seconds, built to travel rather than just to fill the feed. Food is the most-watched category in short-form, and yours photographs like it knows it.

One evening service a week gives us everything we need for a steady stream of reels that do the selling for you.

2

Launch Newcastle Properly

The Newcastle shop is new, and new is a story. "Sunderland's favourite burger has landed in Newcastle" is a ready-made narrative arc: the story of the first shop, the move, the fit-out, the first service, the first regulars. Launch-moment content earns attention that steady-state posting never gets, and that window is open right now.

Done properly, the Newcastle launch also reintroduces the Sunderland shop to people who forgot it was there.

3

Own the "#1 in the North East" Debate

Local food creators are already making videos asking whether Ranches is the best burger in the North East. That conversation is happening without you steering it. Lean in: prove-it content, side-by-side builds, "come and disagree" energy, replies to the reviews. A confident claim plus an open challenge is one of the most shareable formats in food.

You do not need to win the argument. You need to be the argument, because every take on it sends people to your counter.

4

Build a Creator and UGC Engine

The creators are already coming. Make it a system rather than a happy accident: first-taste invites for local food accounts, a repeatable "rate the burger" moment in-store, customer reactions filmed at the counter, and the Creation Station as the shareable dessert moment nobody else in the region has. Every visitor with a phone is a potential distribution channel.

UGC also solves your time problem: other people make content about you while you run the shops.

5

Put Arif and the Team on Camera

Seven years, two cities, an American-style menu done properly in the North East: that is a founder story people get behind. The team behind the grill, the prep, the standards, the reason it started. People buy from people, and a face they recognise turns a burger joint into their burger joint.

Personality-led content also future-proofs you: trends change, but an audience that knows the owner sticks around.

6

Reach First, Then Targeted Local Ads

Around 223 posts and 1,200 followers tells you the effort is right but the format is not. Short-form built to travel earns the organic reach, then a small, tightly targeted ad budget puts your best food and launch content in front of hungry people within delivery range of each shop, in the evening, when they are deciding what to eat. Every ad points at your online ordering, so every pound is accountable.

We run exactly this for another North East food client and track it to the order. Ads work hardest when the content already proves the product.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in One Evening Service

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what already happens in your kitchens every evening: the grill, the desserts, the claim and the people. All four could be captured in a single service.

Idea 01
"The smash"
Food ASMRThe grillUnder 20s

Pure product: the ball of beef hitting the grill, the smash, the sizzle, the cheese, the stack, the cut-through. Shot tight, natural sound up, no talking. Caption does the location work ("Sunderland and Newcastle"). The single most reliable format in food content.

Why it works: Nobody scrolls past a proper smash and cheese pull. It sells the product in three seconds flat and it is infinitely repeatable across the menu.
Idea 02
"Build your own dessert"
Creation StationPOVOnly at Ranches

A customer POV at the Creation Station: choosing, building, the pour, the finished dessert held up to camera. Nobody else in the region has this moment, which makes it your signature format. End on "only at Ranches" and the two locations.

Why it works: A dessert you build yourself is inherently filmable and inherently shareable, and a format competitors cannot copy becomes the thing you are known for.
Idea 03
"Best burger in the North East? Come prove us wrong."
The claimChallengeShareable

Lean into the debate the creators already started. The team makes the case on camera, the flagship burger built start to finish, and an open invitation: think your local does it better, come and compare. Pin the creator reviews in the comments.

Why it works: Confidence plus an open challenge is catnip for shares and comments, and every person who disagrees still has to visit to argue.
Idea 04
"Seven years. Two cities. One obsession."
FounderStoryNewcastle launch

Arif to camera: how Ranches started in Sunderland, what seven years taught you, and why Newcastle was the next move. Cut with the kitchens at full tilt. Strong hook ("we spent five years perfecting burgers in Sunderland, then Newcastle asked for them").

Why it works: A genuine founder story builds the kind of loyalty a menu photo never will, and it launches the Newcastle site with a narrative instead of an announcement.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your new visibility into orders at both shops.

Get Both Google Profiles Pulling Their Weight

"Burgers near me" at 8pm is won and lost on Google. With two shops you have two profiles to feed, and the new Newcastle one especially needs reviews building fast.

  • Keep both profiles optimised for "burgers Sunderland" and "burgers Newcastle"
  • Funnel happy customers into fresh Google reviews at the counter, especially in Newcastle
  • Add food photos and shop shots regularly so the listings look alive
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Creator Nights and Group Moments

Local food creators are already reviewing you for free. Make the door official: first-taste invites when new menu items drop, a creator night for the Newcastle site, and group moments built to be filmed.

  • A simple invite list of North East food creators, fed with every launch
  • The Creation Station as the signature moment every visitor films
  • Birthday and group visits nudged toward shareable moments

Targeted Local Ads Around Both Shops

Food ads pay back fast when the content is strong. A small, tightly targeted budget around each shop puts your best reels in front of hungry people at decision time, evenings, within delivery range.

  • Separate targeting for the Sunderland and Newcastle catchments
  • Your strongest food and launch reels used as the ads
  • Every ad pointed at your online ordering
  • Tracked to the order so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Ranches: We run this exact engine for another North East food client, and in a recent three-week window it returned over twelve times what it cost, tracked order by order.
