I

What we are going to do

Four things. Measured in enquiries, showarounds booked and dates filled, not likes.

One

Film a wedding in the shadow of the tower

A ceremony under a 600-year-old pele tower, tipis on the lawn, 600 acres behind it. There is nothing like it nearby, and almost none of it is filmed for couples.

Two

Take the story to where couples look

A capture day a month cut into reels, a TikTok started from zero, and film for your own site, so couples find the tower before they find a barn.

Three

Turn the showaround into a system

You already say "book a showaround." We make it the one repeated ask everywhere, with tracking so you know which post or ad brought each couple.

Four

Give couples a content creator

We film weddings ourselves. Offered as a Preston Tower perk, every wedding we film for a couple becomes your marketing too.

II

How we are going to do it

Week one

Count everything

Analytics and a Meta pixel on the site, so every enquiry has a source. Email and phone stay visible, and "book a showaround" becomes the one button on every page.

Month one

First capture day

The tower, the gardens and the tipis in autumn light, and one full wedding when a couple says yes. Cut into reels within the week.

Every month

Capture, post, report

A third of posts as reels, filmed not stock. A one-page report: enquiries, showarounds booked, dates confirmed.

Month two on

The ad

The wedding film, aimed at engaged couples within an hour. Small spend, judged only on showarounds booked.

@preston_tower on 25 September. 767 followers for one of the oldest wedding settings in Northumberland. The reach does not match the venue.

You have entered the wedding market recently and it shows in the numbers, not the venue. The tower, the gardens and the 600 acres are extraordinary. The audience is tiny by comparison.

The gap is direction and reach. No pixel, no TikTok, no ads, and a following a fraction of Charlton or Doxford, none of whom can offer a wedding under a 1392 tower. That is the opening.

The first step is one wedding, under the tower.

A Newcastle restaurant we work with has passed 87 million views, and the owner puts a 40% lift in weekly sales down to it. A Whitley Bay restaurant went from no TikTok in May to 8 million views by August.

If you have a couple booked this autumn or winter who would let us film their day, that is where we start. From then on everything is measured on enquiries, showarounds booked and dates filled, not likes.
