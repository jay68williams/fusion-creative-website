What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Kathi rolls and tele bhaja, Kolkata street food made in Dubai. Each one is a dish moment on camera.
  • A full Kolkata-style lunch and dinner thali. Your own caption calls it proper ghar ka khana, now in Dubai. That sells a video.
  • A festival calendar most restaurants would envy: Poila Boishakh, Eid, Independence Day, Ramadan and Mahalaya. A reason to post, and to bring people in, all year.

03 · Your website

No website, so every order runs through Instagram and the apps.

You have no own site. Ordering is Talabat and Deliveroo, and bookings come through Instagram and the phone. These are the changes we would make so the video turns into orders you keep.

What we sawWhy it mattersFix
There is no website to order from directlyYour whole ordering presence is Talabat and Deliveroo, with bookings over Instagram and the phone.A simple site with your menu, a table-booking and a WhatsApp order button gives people a way to order from you directly.
The apps take a cut of every orderTalabat restaurants pay an average 17% to 18% commission (FAB Securities, 13 May 2025). On delivery that is the cost of their rider and their customer, and hard to avoid.The honest win is not replacing Talabat. It is filling tables and taking collection, events and catering orders direct, where you keep the lot. 85% of UAE residents would rather deal with a business over WhatsApp (YouGov for Zbooni, February 2024).
Everything lives on rented landYour presence is Instagram, Facebook and the delivery apps. If an account goes quiet, so does your pipeline.A site you own, with your reviews and your booking button, is the one channel nobody else can switch off.
No TikTokWe found Instagram and Facebook only.Post each video to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, so each one reaches a second and third audience across Dubai.

07 · The plan

A plan built around your festival calendar.

  1. Week 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the twelve videos. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and plan a simple way for every post to end in a direct order or booking.

  2. Week 2
    The shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list: rolls wrapped, a thali plated, tele bhaja frying, and guests at the table.

  3. After the shoot
    Edit, caption and post

    We edit, caption and post the videos to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Each one ends with the dish and how to order direct.

  4. Handover
    Review together

    We report what the videos reached and what they brought in, then hand over every video for you to keep and reuse.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos filmed in your kitchen by a licensed UAE crew to our shot list, edited and captioned, posted to launch them. You keep every video.
