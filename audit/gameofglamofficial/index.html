What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The work is genuinely content-ready

Makeup transformations are one of the most reliably watchable formats on TikTok and Reels. A bare face turning into a full glam look has a built-in before and after, which is exactly what the algorithm rewards. You already produce that every single booking. Most businesses have to invent something to film. You do it as your day job.

✓ You are already posting

Game of Glam is active on Instagram and Facebook, so the habit of showing up is there. That is the part most people never manage. The issue is not consistency, it is format, and format is the easy thing to change.

✓ You are a local, personal brand

Booking a makeup artist is a trust decision. People want to see the face behind the brush and the results on real faces near them. Being a genuine Newcastle name is an advantage a big faceless page cannot copy, and it is the thing short-form video is best at showing off.

✓ A clear, in-demand niche

Glam, bridal, prom and event makeup are high-intent, high-value bookings, and they happen on a calendar. Wedding season, prom season, party season, the demand is predictable, which means content can be planned around it rather than left to chance.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the skill, the niche and the local presence. The job is turning that into reach and bookings: getting your work in front of the brides, prom clients and event bookings across Newcastle who have not found you yet. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Film the Transformation, Do Not Just Post the Result

The single biggest change. A finished-look photo shows one moment. A transformation reel shows the whole journey, bare face to glam, and it is that reveal that people watch to the end, rewatch and share. Same booking, same chair, filmed instead of photographed.

Every client in your chair is a ready-made video. Capturing the before, a few seconds of the process and the final turn is the closest thing there is to a guaranteed format for a makeup artist.

2

Put Yourself on Camera

People book a person, not a logo. The most effective beauty content right now is artist-led: your hands, your voice, your take on a look, a quick tip while you work. It builds familiarity, and familiarity is what makes someone choose you over the artist they have never seen.

You do not need to be a performer. Talking through what you are doing, or reacting to a finished look, is enough to turn followers into people who feel like they already know you.

3

Own Bridal and Occasion Season

Bridal is where the highest-value bookings live, and it is deeply visual. A calm getting-ready film, the bride's reaction in the mirror, a soft romantic look coming together, this is content brides save and send to their group chat while planning. The same applies to prom and party season on their own calendars.

Planning content around each season, ahead of when clients start searching, is how you get booked out before your competitors have even posted.

4

Tips and Techniques That Get Saved and Shared

Quick, genuinely useful reels, how to stop makeup sliding off by mid-afternoon, how to make eyes look bigger, the one step most people skip, get saved and sent on, which is a strong signal to the algorithm. They also position you as the expert, so when a viewer needs it done properly, you are the name they remember.

Helpful content pulls in people who are not ready to book yet but will be, and it keeps you visible in between the big transformation moments.

5

Scale to TikTok and Back It With Local Ads

The reach ceiling on Instagram alone is real. The same transformation and tip reels can run on TikTok, where beauty content travels fastest, so you get two audiences from one film. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at Newcastle and the surrounding area, and your best work lands in front of people actively looking to book a makeup artist.

One extra bridal or event booking pays for a lot of content, which makes local video and ads one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film Around Your Next Booking

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the transformation, the bride, the technique and you. Each one could be filmed on a phone in your chair around a single client.

Idea 01
"Bare face to full glam"
TransformationRevealTrending audio

A fast, satisfying transformation reel: the bare-faced before, a few quick cuts through the process, then the final turn to camera. Strong hook on frame one ("wait for the turn"). Trending audio, under 20 seconds. The single most reliable format for a makeup artist.

Why it works: The built-in before and after keeps people watching to the end and rewatching, which is exactly what pushes a reel out to new viewers.
Idea 02
"Getting the bride ready"
BridalEmotionReaction

A soft, cinematic morning-of film: the calm, the look coming together, the bride seeing herself for the first time. Gentle music, real reaction at the end. Tag the location and the season.

Why it works: Bridal content gets saved and shared by brides planning their own day, and it puts your highest-value service in front of exactly the right people.
Idea 03
"How to stop your makeup sliding off by 2pm"
TipSave-worthyAuthority

A quick, genuinely useful technique reel: one common problem, one clear fix, shown on a real face. Lead with the pain point ("if your makeup never lasts, it is probably this"). Fifteen seconds, straight to the point.

Why it works: Useful tips get saved and sent on, which the algorithm loves, and they make you the expert people remember when they need it done properly.
Idea 04
"The look everyone is asking me for"
Artist-ledPOVTrend

You to camera, talking through a look that is trending right now, why it works and who it suits, while you create it. Personable, confident, a little bit of your own opinion. Strong hook ("this is the look I am booked out for this month").

Why it works: People book people. An artist-led reel builds trust fast and turns a follower into someone who feels like they already know you.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into booked-out weeks.

Make Booking Effortless

When a reel lands, the person watching wants to book in that moment. Every extra tap loses them. A clear, obvious way to check availability and book, linked straight from your bio and every post, turns attention into actual appointments.

  • A simple booking or enquiry link pinned in your Instagram and TikTok bio
  • Prices and services laid out clearly so people can self-qualify before they message
  • Story highlights for bridal, prom, occasion and reviews so new visitors get it in seconds

Turn Happy Clients Into Proof

A makeup booking is a trust decision, and nothing builds trust like other people's words and results. Your finished looks and happy clients are your best marketing, and right now they disappear down the feed instead of working for you.

  • Collect a quick review or reaction clip from clients while they are still buzzing
  • Build a running highlight of bridal and event results new clients can scroll through
  • Turn your best transformations into reusable content across every platform

Targeted Local Ads to Future Clients

Because a bridal or event booking is high value, a small ad budget pays back quickly. Tightly targeted ads put your best transformation and bridal reels in front of people in Newcastle and the surrounding area who are actively looking.

  • Target Newcastle and the North East, around wedding, prom and party seasons
  • Use your strongest transformation and bridal reels as the ads
  • Drive enquiries straight to your booking link or inbox
  • Track cost per enquiry so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Game of Glam: One extra bridal or event booking covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things you can do.
