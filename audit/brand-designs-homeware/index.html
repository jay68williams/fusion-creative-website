What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ TikTok Live is a real sales engine

You are not guessing at demand, you are proving it live, several times over. People turn up, watch, and buy in the moment. That is one of the hardest things in retail to build, and you already have it. It is the perfect spearhead to build everything else around.

✓ Around 17 years and a loyal customer base

A family-run shop that has kept going and growing for the best part of two decades is trust you cannot fake. Thousands of happy customers and warm five-star reviews mean people already love what you sell. That goodwill is a huge head start for content and ads.

✓ The store converts

The Shopify site is clean, the product photography is strong and the reviews do a lot of quiet selling. When someone lands on it, it does its job. It just needs more of the right people landing on it, and more reasons for them to come back.

✓ You already show up consistently

TikTok, Instagram and Facebook are all live and active. The habit of posting and going live is there, which is the hardest part for most businesses. The gap is not effort, it is the structure around it, and that is the easy thing to add.

✓ The products are genuinely content-ready

Cushions, lighting, mirrors, furniture, seasonal pieces, colour and texture. Homeware is one of the most watchable categories on short-form because people love a reveal, a restock and a styled space. You are sitting on a shelf full of ready-made content.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the shop, the products and the loyal audience. The job is to build the full engine around your TikTok Lives so the selling does not stop when the show ends. Live stays the spearhead. Filmed content, paid ads and email do the work in between, and the store is where it all converts. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Keep Live as the Spearhead, and Feed It

Your Lives are working, so we would protect them and pour fuel on them. Short filmed clips that tease the next Live, show the pieces coming up, and capture the best moments from the last one give people a reason to set a reminder and turn up. The audience grows because the content between shows keeps pulling new viewers in.

A Live with a warm, growing audience waiting for it sells far more than a Live that starts cold every time.

2

Build a Paid Ads Engine Around Your Audience

This is the shift you want to make, and it is the right one. With filmed content to run and a store that already converts, managed Meta ads (Instagram and Facebook) can put your best pieces in front of brand-new buyers who look just like your existing customers, and retarget the people who watched a Live or browsed the site but did not buy yet.

Done properly, ads stop being a gamble and become a tap you can turn up: more reach, more first-time buyers, more people funnelling into your Lives and your store.

3

Make the Products the Content

The new-in drop, the restock everyone was waiting for, one shelf styled three ways, the satisfying pack-and-send of an order. These are the formats homeware brands are winning with on TikTok and Reels right now, and they are built from what you already do every day in the shop.

Content built to travel does two jobs at once: it reaches new people organically, and it becomes the raw material for your ads.

4

Turn Email Into a Second Storefront

You already have a rewards programme and thousands of customers who have bought before. That is an owned audience you can reach any time, with no algorithm in the way. A simple email rhythm, new-in, restocks, upcoming Lives and seasonal edits, sells to the people most likely to buy again.

Email is the channel that quietly compounds: it turns one-time buyers into repeat customers and reminds your best customers to come back before they drift.

5

Point Everything at the Store

Live, organic content, ads and email should all lead to the same place: the website, where the browsing turns into orders. That means the store stays the hub, with clear links from every platform, easy paths from a viral clip to the exact product, and the moments people love on Live captured as shoppable pieces on the site too.

When every channel converges on the store, each one makes the others work harder instead of pulling in different directions.

6

Scale It Across TikTok, Instagram and Facebook

One content engine, three channels. The same filmed pieces, cut and posted properly for each platform, mean your TikTok energy also builds your Instagram and Facebook, where a lot of your customers already are. Right now the effort is real but it lives mostly inside the Lives. Spreading it out multiplies the reach without multiplying the work.

More surface area, more discovery, more people arriving warm at your next Live and your store.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do in the shop: the drop, the restock, the styling and the story. Each one could be filmed on a phone around a single delivery or Live.

Idea 01
"Come see what just landed"
New inRevealRestock

A fast, satisfying reveal of the newest pieces coming into the shop, unboxed and styled as they come out. Strong hook on frame one ("the new drop is here"). Trending audio, quick cuts, under 25 seconds. Ends with "live on the site now" and a nudge to the next Live.

Why it works: New-in and restock content creates urgency and gives your loyal customers a reason to check back, on the site and in your next Live.
Idea 02
"Styling one shelf three ways"
StylingHow-toSave-worthy

Take one corner, shelf or console and style it three different ways using your pieces, cushions, a mirror, a lamp, some florals. Helpful, watchable, and exactly the kind of content people save and send to a friend. Tag every product so it is shoppable.

Why it works: It shows the products in a real home, sells the look rather than a single item, and gets saved and shared, which the algorithm loves.
Idea 03
"The most satisfying pack you'll see today"
PackingASMRBehind the scenes

A calm, satisfying pack-and-send of a real order: the tissue, the care, the secure packaging your reviews keep mentioning. Quiet, tactile, oddly addictive. End on the finished parcel ready to go out.

Why it works: Packing videos are hugely watchable, they show off your care and quality, and they build the trust that turns a browser into a first order.
Idea 04
"17 years, and we just moved"
FounderStoryFamily-run

Jenine to camera, warm and honest, on the family-run story, nearly two decades, and the move to Larton. A quick tour of the new space. People buy from people, and this is the content that makes a loyal audience feel part of the journey.

Why it works: A genuine founder story builds connection fast, and it is the human side that keeps a community coming back to your Lives and your shop.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your audience into orders and repeat customers.

Make Email and the Rewards Programme Pull Their Weight

Your rewards programme and customer list are an owned audience you can reach any time, no algorithm needed. Right now it is likely doing far less than it could. A proper email rhythm turns one-time buyers into regulars.

  • Welcome and rewards emails that bring first-time buyers back
  • New-in, restock and upcoming Live announcements straight to inboxes
  • Seasonal edits and gift guides timed to your busy periods
  • Win-back messages to customers who have not bought in a while

Paid Ads to New and Returning Buyers

With content worth running and a store that converts, ads become one of the highest-return moves you can make. A managed budget puts your best pieces in front of the right people and brings warm browsers back.

  • Reach new buyers who look like your existing loyal customers
  • Retarget Live viewers and site browsers who did not buy yet
  • Use your strongest reveal and styling videos as the ads
  • Track cost per sale so the spend is always accountable

Get the New Larton Shopfront Found Locally

You have just moved, so it is the perfect moment to make sure the new location is easy to find. Local search and your Google profile do quiet, heavy lifting when someone nearby wants to visit or check you are open.

  • Update and optimise the Google profile for the new Larton, Frankby location
  • Fresh photos of the new space and current stock
  • Funnel happy customers into fresh reviews
  • Content that shows off the shop itself and drives real visits

Why it fits Brand Designs: a physical shop plus a strong online presence means the two feed each other, online discovery drives footfall, and footfall becomes content.
