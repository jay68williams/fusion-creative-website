I

What we are going to do

Four things. Measured in enquiries, viewings booked and dates filled, not likes.

One

Film a wedding in the castle

A whole castle, chapel and grounds for one couple at a time. It is one of the most exclusive settings in the county and almost none of it is filmed for couples.

Two

Actually start posting

Your Instagram has 146 followers and, right now, no posts at all. The single biggest quick win is simply putting the castle where couples look, every week.

Three

Modernise the front door

The website is dated, has no pixel and no clear next step for a couple, just a brochure to download. We add tracking and one "book a viewing" ask.

Four

Give couples a content creator

We film weddings ourselves. Offered as a Chipchase perk, every wedding we film for a couple becomes your marketing too.

II

How we are going to do it

Week one

Count everything

Analytics and a Meta pixel on the site, so every enquiry has a source, and one clear "book a viewing" ask where today there is only a brochure download.

Month one

First capture day

The castle, the chapel and the grounds in autumn light, and one full wedding when a couple says yes. Cut into reels within the week and posted to an account that, right now, is empty.

Every month

Capture, post, report

A third of posts as reels, filmed not stock. A one-page report: enquiries, viewings booked, dates confirmed.

Month two on

The ad

The wedding film, aimed at engaged couples within an hour. Small spend, judged only on viewings booked.
