I

What we are going to do

Four things. Measured in enquiries, viewings booked and dates filled, not likes.

One

Fix the front door

Your website did not load for us on any browser or tool we tried. If couples are hitting the same wall, every ad, post and enquiry leaks before it starts. This is the first thing we would check.

Two

Own TikTok

5,704 on Instagram and nothing on TikTok, where couples now search for a city wedding. We start it from zero with the tower, the rooftop and the skyline.

Three

Film the tower properly

A ceremony above the Tyne bridges, the White Room, the vaulted bar at night. It is one of the most filmable rooms in Newcastle. We make it reels and a hero film.

Four

Give couples a content creator

We film weddings ourselves. Offered as a Secret Tower perk, every wedding we film for a couple becomes your marketing too.

II

How we are going to do it

Week one

Count everything

First we confirm what a couple actually sees when they click your link, because it did not load for us. Then analytics and tracking, so every enquiry has a source, and one clear ask.

Month one

First capture day

The rooftop at golden hour, the skyline, the White Room dressed, and one full wedding when a couple says yes. Cut into reels within the week.

Every month

Capture, post, report

A third of posts as reels, filmed not stock. A one-page report: enquiries, viewings booked, dates confirmed.

Month two on

The ad

The wedding film, aimed at engaged couples within an hour. Small spend, judged only on viewings booked.

@secret.tower on 25 September. 5,704 followers and striking content: the rooftop, the skyline, the White Room. The audience is real. The plumbing under it is not.

The content and the audience are a genuine strength. 5,704 followers, a city-centre tower nobody else can offer, and a feed that already looks the part.

The problem is everything a couple hits after the feed. Your website would not load for us on any browser or tool, there is no TikTok, and nothing measurable behind the enquiries. The setting deserves a funnel that works.

The first step is making sure your front door opens.

A Newcastle restaurant we work with has passed 87 million views, and the owner puts a 40% lift in weekly sales down to it. A Whitley Bay restaurant went from no TikTok in May to 8 million views by August.

We would start by checking what a couple actually sees when they click your link, then film the tower and start the TikTok. Everything after that is measured on enquiries, viewings booked and dates filled, not likes.
