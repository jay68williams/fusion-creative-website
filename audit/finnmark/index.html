I

What your ads are doing right now

Meta Ad Library, GB, 23 September

About 79 live ads, and roughly 15% of them are video

Twelve of the seventy-nine are video. Eight of those twelve are a single garden still with slow parallax and a line of text on top ("Over 1,000 barrel saunas sold", "Premium Cube Saunas"), five to seven seconds long. Two are 18-second brand films from 7 September with people in them, the first ads in the set where anyone appears on camera. One is a 27-second bespoke showcase. Everything else is a static or a dynamic multi-version card.

Across the 41 ads we read in full, 23 send people to a collection page, 8 to the bespoke enquiry form, 8 to a product page and 2 to the homepage. The button is "Shop Now" on 31 of them. For a purchase that starts at £3,995 and runs past £20,000, most of the traffic is being asked to shop rather than to enquire, book a consultation or request a brochure.

Seasonality

The only seasonal line live on 23 September is a summer one

"Your summer wellness routine starts with a Barrel Sauna"Finnmark ad, started 9 July, still active

Sauna season, in the buyer's head, starts when the evenings turn. Two of the biggest spenders in the category have already switched: MyoMaster went live with "Sauna season is here. This winter..." on 16 September and Polar Recovery with "Still dreading winter mornings?" on 3 September. There is no autumn or winter creative in the Finnmark set yet.

Small, and fixable this afternoon

A typo is live in the creative

The overlay line reads "EVELATE YOUR OUTDOOR SPACE" on at least three of the running video ads and on the poster frames of two more. We checked it at full resolution rather than trusting a thumbnail. It is the kind of thing that only matters because the rest of the brand is so exact.

The number that has not moved

Since June: 79 ads running, and about 660 new Instagram followers

When I wrote in June, Instagram was around 7,000 and TikTok was at 133. On 23 September they are at 7,662 and 185. The last six Instagram posts drew between 9 and 48 likes. Meanwhile your best YouTube Short, "The UK's Leading Barrel Saunas", has 301,000 views and your catalogue films do 40,000 to 86,000 each. The audience is there for the product. It is not yet there for the brand, and paid is not currently building it.

III

The five angles we would put in front of your paid team first

The Sauna Twins, to camera

Your job ad says the content hire should complement "the existing founder voice". There is no founder in the ads. Jake and Max walking a customer's garden, standing in Arc Canary Wharf, or explaining why the Micro barrel costs what it costs is the single most persuasive creative in the category, and the only brand doing it well is Polar Recovery.

Evidence: Max's one to-camera TikTok (4 Aug, Boscombe) is the only founder post in the last ten. Polar's founder ad is their longest runner.

Sauna season, this week

Cold evenings sell saunas. A short run of autumn and winter creative (first frost on the barrel roof, steam against a dark garden, the walk back to the house in a robe) replaces the summer line while it still matters, and gives the paid team a fresh set to test against the summer control.

Evidence: both winter hooks in the category went live 3 to 16 Sep. Yours is dated 9 July.

"Built by Finnmark": the install film

Two of the 2026 UK Sauna Awards went to saunas you built: Best Design to Saltwater Sauna at Boscombe, Best New Sauna to Sea Scrub in Folkestone. Arc Canary Wharf is a 65-person build. Tottenham's training ground. None of that is in an ad. A 30-second build-to-first-session film per project is a whole quarter of creative on its own, and it is the proof that a £20,000 modular sauna is a safe purchase.

Evidence: Auroom and MyoMaster both run build and transformation films; your bespoke film shows interiors, not the build.

The monthly number as the hook

"£78.71 a month" is already on your Micro barrel product page through Novuna. It is not in any ad. HotSpring built its whole campaign on "£1 a day". Lead with the monthly figure, keep the total in the small print, and send the click to a consultation rather than a collection.

Evidence: 31 of 41 sampled ads use Shop Now; the finance line appears only on product pages.

The first löyly

A customer, their sauna, water on the stones, and what they say in the two minutes after. It is testimony and sensory content in one, it is the thing Reviews.io already gives you in text (123 reviews at 5.0), and it is the format that stops a thumb. Sound up.

Evidence: no customer speaks in any Finnmark ad we read. Nook and MyoMaster both lead with third-party trust.

IV

Three things on the store worth a look

The sale countdown resets itself every seven days

The "Sale Ends In" bar on collection and product pages is configured to auto-reset on a weekly interval. Regular visitors notice a deadline that never arrives, and it is the one thing on a very well-made store that reads as a trick.

On a phone, the price of the Micro barrel is a thousand pixels down

The product page opens with a 21-image gallery. The title arrives at 657px, the price at 1,037px, and Add to Cart at 1,218px with no sticky bar. For the product most of your ads send people to, the buying decision sits below three screens of scrolling.

Small mobile friction

The cookie modal covers the hero on every first visit with no one-tap reject. The newsletter field is 14px, and a handful of filter controls are 12 to 13px, which makes iPhones zoom the page when tapped. Ten minutes in the theme. The site itself is sound: no horizontal scroll, prices on everything, strong reviews, finance wired in.

Working together

You are hiring a content lead, an ecommerce manager and a paid lead at once. We can be the creative engine while you do.

Fusion films and buys media in the same building. For the candle company that meant twenty-plus new pieces a month going straight into the account, tested, and the winners scaled. For Finnmark it would mean your in-house paid team gets founder films, install films, seasonal sets and customer testimony on a monthly cadence, starting this month, and keeps them when you make the hire.

We are confident in this because we have done it for a brand the same shape: North East, Shopify, shipping nationwide, a product that photographs beautifully and a category that had never seen it move. We would really like to talk about what that looks like for Finnmark.
