What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The product is made for social

Fresh donuts, dipped and finished in front of people, are exactly the kind of thing short-form video was built for. Sweet, colourful, satisfying, instantly recognisable. Half the businesses we work with have to manufacture content that looks this good. You make it every single day.

✓ You already show up on the right platforms

You are on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook already, so the habit of posting is there, which is the hardest part for most businesses to start. The raw material and the accounts exist. The job now is turning that into reach rather than posts that quietly disappear.

✓ Events give you a constant stream of content

Festivals, concerts, corporate hire, busy weekends. Every event is a ready-made backdrop full of crowds, queues, reactions and happy customers. That is content most brands would pay to stage, and you get it for free every time you turn up to trade.

✓ A new store is a genuine event to build around

A physical Newcastle store opening is a real moment, and moments are what social rewards. Build-up, countdown, day one, first customers. Handled well, the launch alone can put Dipping Donuts in front of thousands of local people who have never tried you yet.

✓ You are already set up to convert attention

Being listed on Uber Eats means there is already a way for someone who sees you to actually buy. A lot of businesses build an audience with nowhere to send it. You have the demand side covered, which makes the attention worth chasing.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the product, the accounts and a new store coming. The job is to turn that into reach: get Dipping Donuts in front of people across Newcastle and the North East, and make it the name they think of when they want a treat, book an event, or walk past a new store. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Store Opening a Content Event

A new store is the single best content moment you will get all year, so let people watch it happen. The fit-out, the countdown, the first tray of the day, the doors opening, the first customers and their reactions. Build anticipation for a couple of weeks beforehand and you turn a quiet opening into a queue.

Done properly, the launch content does two jobs at once: it fills the store on day one and it hands you a library of clips to keep posting for months.

2

Film the Donuts Being Made

The most reliable food content on TikTok and Reels is the satisfying, close-up, made-in-front-of-you kind. The dip, the drizzle, the toppings, the fresh batch coming out. Slow it down, get the sound in, land a strong first frame, and this is the content that racks up views while barely costing anything to shoot.

People do not scroll past a fresh donut being dipped. It is the most obvious, highest-return content you are sitting on.

3

Turn Every Event Into a Content Engine

You already trade at festivals, concerts and corporate events. Each one is a set full of atmosphere, crowds and reactions. Capture the queue, the busiest moment, the first-timer trying a donut, the setup and pack-down. Tag the event and the location so the content reaches everyone who was there or wished they were.

It doubles as your pitch for more bookings. When an event organiser sees the buzz you bring, hiring you becomes the easy decision.

4

Build One Clear Home Base, Not Scattered Accounts

Right now the presence is split across a few handles, which spreads the effort thin and makes it harder for anyone to find you or follow the story. Deciding on one main account per platform, with a clear name, bio and link, means every event, post and customer points to the same place, and the following actually compounds instead of starting over.

One strong home base is far more powerful than three half-built ones, and it makes the new store launch land much harder.

5

Reels and TikTok for Reach, Plus a Real Website

Short-form video built to travel, with proper hooks rather than one-off photo posts, is what earns reach. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted local ad budget around the store opening and event dates, and you put your best content in front of people in the right postcodes at the right time. Behind it all you need a proper website, not a holding page, so people can find you, see where you will be, and book you for events.

Content brings the attention, the website turns it into orders and event bookings. Both are missing pieces we can put in place quickly.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the make, the store, the events and the people. Each one could be filmed on a phone in an afternoon.

Idea 01
"The most satisfying donut on your feed"
ASMRClose-upThe make

A slow, close-up, sound-on reel of a donut being made and dipped: the fresh batch, the dip, the drizzle, the toppings landing. Strong first frame ("wait for the dip"). Trending audio or crisp real sound. Under 20 seconds. The most repeatable, highest-reach thing you can post.

Why it works: Satisfying close-up food content is one of the most watched formats on short-form, and you can film a new one every single day.
Idea 02
"New store, day one"
LaunchCountdownLocal

A build-up and reveal series for the Newcastle store: the fit-out, the countdown, the doors opening, the first customers and their reactions. Lead with the hook ("Newcastle, we're finally opening"). Tag the location so it reaches locals scrolling nearby.

Why it works: A launch is a real moment, and moments get shared. Handled well it fills the store on day one and reaches thousands of local people at once.
Idea 03
"POV: you're first in the queue"
EventsPOVCrowd

A point-of-view reel from one of your events: walking up to the stall, the queue, ordering, the first bite, the reaction. Capture the atmosphere and the crowd. Quick, warm, real. Tag the festival or event so everyone who was there sees it.

Why it works: It reaches the event's whole audience, and it is the perfect advert for winning your next festival or corporate booking.
Idea 04
"How Dipping Donuts started"
FounderStoryTrust

You to camera, straight and honest: how it began, the events, and now the first store. A little bit of the person behind the brand. Strong hook ("two years ago this was a stall, next month it's a shop"). Warm, real, no script needed.

Why it works: People back people. A founder story builds a loyal local following that keeps coming back, not just a one-off viral hit.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into orders, footfall and event bookings.

Get the New Store Found on Google and Maps

When someone in Newcastle searches for donuts, or walks near the new store, you want to be the result they see. A proper Google Business Profile does quiet, heavy lifting, and for a new store it is one of the first things to get right.

  • Set up and optimise the profile for "donuts Newcastle" and the local area
  • Add photos, opening hours and the store location from day one
  • Funnel happy customers into fresh Google reviews
  • Keep it fed with new products and event dates

A Real Website, Not a Holding Page

Right now dippingdonuts.co.uk is a placeholder, which means anyone who searches for you lands on nothing. A proper site gives you a home for orders, event enquiries, the store details and your best content, and it makes the whole brand look the part.

  • Menu, store location and opening hours in one place
  • An events and corporate hire enquiry form so bookings come straight to you
  • Links out to Uber Eats and your socials, all joined up
  • Built to be found on Google, not just to sit there

Targeted Local Ads Around Launches and Events

Because your product is an easy yes, a small, tightly targeted ad budget pays back fast. Put your best make and launch content in front of people in the right Newcastle postcodes, timed around the store opening and your event dates.

  • Target people close to the new store and your event locations
  • Use your strongest satisfying and launch reels as the ads
  • Drive footfall, orders and event enquiries
  • Track results so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Dipping Donuts: A treat is a low-friction purchase, so putting great content in front of the right local people turns views into walk-ins quickly.
