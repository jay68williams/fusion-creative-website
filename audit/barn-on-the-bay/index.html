I

What we are going to do

Four things. Measured in enquiries, tours booked and dates filled, not likes.

One

Turn the audience into enquiries

You have 7,000 people watching. Right now nothing asks them to book a tour on a set date, so the attention leaks away. We give it one job.

Two

Film for couples, not just for the feed

Your real weddings are gorgeous. We add the pieces that convert: the beach ceremony walkthrough, the Cowshed turning from empty to dressed, a tour of the day.

Three

Put the pixel to work

You already have a Meta pixel. It is collecting an audience nobody is advertising to. We build ads to your visitors, your enquirers and lookalikes of your past couples.

Four

Give couples a content creator

We film weddings ourselves. Offered as a Barn on the Bay perk, every wedding we film for a couple becomes your marketing too.

II

How we are going to do it

Week one

Count everything

You already have a Meta pixel, so week one is the enquiry side: every tour booking tracked to a source, and one button, "book a tour", kept as the single ask across the site and both Instagram accounts.

Month one

First capture day

A capture day on the bay: the dunes, the Cowshed, the horse-box bar, a beach ceremony if one lands. Cut into reels within the week.

Every month

Capture, post, report

A third of posts as reels, filmed not stock. A one-page report: enquiries, tours booked, dates confirmed.

Month two on

The ad

The wedding film, aimed at engaged couples within an hour. Small spend, judged only on tours booked.
