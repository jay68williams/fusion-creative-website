What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The product is made for the camera

Pizza pulling out the oven, doner shaved off the spit, a loaded wrap being folded. This is the single most watchable, most craved category of content on TikTok and Reels right now. Most businesses have to work to find something visual to film. You have it on the pass every single night.

✓ Over 30 years of reputation

Three decades on Ocean Road is a story most takeaways would kill for. It says trusted, it says consistent, it says the locals have voted with their orders for years. That heritage is proof you can lean on in content, and right now it is barely being told.

✓ A brilliant location

Ocean Road is one of the best known food streets in the North East, busy with locals, students and people heading out or heading home. Being on that street is an asset in itself, and it gives you a ready-made local audience to make content for and about.

✓ You already show up online

An active Instagram, a Facebook page and online ordering through your own site and Uber Eats. The habit of posting and the order routes are already there, which is the hard part for most independents. The issue is not effort, it is format, and format is the easy thing to fix.

✓ A genuine point of difference

Being the first independent in South Shields to run a proper vegan menu, plus opening late when a lot of kitchens have shut, gives you angles most rivals do not have. Those are exactly the hooks that make people stop scrolling and remember you.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the food, the location and the reputation. The job is to turn that into reach: get in front of hungry people across South Shields and beyond, and make Milan Takeaway the name they think of first when they want pizza or a kebab. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Let the Food Do the Talking on Video

The cheese pull, the doner coming off the spit, the pizza sliding out the oven, the wrap being loaded and folded. Filmed close up, in proper light, with sound on, this is the most craved content on the platforms right now. It stops the scroll because people cannot help wanting it.

Right now your food lives mostly as photos. Turning it into short-form video is the single biggest lever you have, because your product is already the star.

2

Own the Late-Night and Ocean Road Angle

You are open when a lot of kitchens have shut, on one of the busiest food streets in the North East. That is a story nobody else can tell in quite the same way. "The best thing you can order at 2am in South Shields" is a hook that writes itself, and it speaks straight to the night-out and after-work crowd.

Lean into the time, the street and the buzz. It gives your content a personality that a generic menu photo never will.

3

Tell the 30-Year and Vegan-First Story

Three decades on Ocean Road, and the first independent in the town to run a proper vegan menu. Those are trust and difference in one, and they are barely being shown. Heritage content ("30 years of this recipe") and a vegan spotlight open you up to whole audiences your rivals are ignoring.

People root for a place with a story. Yours is stronger than most, it just needs telling in a way that travels.

4

Turn Reviews and Regulars Into Proof

Happy customers, five-star reviews and the regulars who have ordered for years are your best marketing, and right now they are hidden. Quick testimonial clips, a customer's first bite, a review on screen over a shot of the food. Social proof travels because it answers the only question a hungry scroller has: is it actually any good.

It is also the easiest content to gather. It is happening in your shop every night.

5

Scale It Across Reels and TikTok, Plus Local Ads

Instagram and Facebook are there, but the reach engine you are not yet running at scale is short-form video across Reels and TikTok. Consistent, hooky food clips are what earn reach and new customers. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted local ad budget around South Shields, and you put your best food content in front of exactly the people who order takeaway.

For a takeaway, a steady flow of new and repeat orders quickly pays for the content and the ads, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do every night: the food, the late-night buzz, the heritage and your regulars. Each one could be filmed on a phone in the shop.

Idea 01
"The cheese pull you didn't know you needed"
FoodClose-upASMR

A slow, close-up reel of a fresh pizza coming out the oven, the first slice lifted, the cheese stretching. Sound on, proper light, trending audio. Strong hook on frame one ("this just came out the oven in South Shields"). Under 20 seconds.

Why it works: Food this good stops the scroll on its own. Cheese pulls and fresh-out-the-oven shots are among the most reliably shared clips on the platforms.
Idea 02
"The best thing to order at 2am"
Late-nightOcean RoadPOV

The doner coming off the spit, the wrap being loaded and folded, the bag handed over, all with the late-night Ocean Road energy. Lead with the hook ("if you know South Shields, you know where this is going at 2am").

Why it works: It owns a moment nobody else can, taps the night-out crowd, and turns your late hours into a personality rather than just a detail.
Idea 03
"30 years of this on Ocean Road"
HeritageStoryTrust

A short piece to camera or voiceover on the story: three decades on the same street, the recipe that has not changed, why the regulars keep coming back. A few seconds of the making, ending on the finished dish and a proud shot of the shop.

Why it works: People root for a place with history. Heritage content builds instant trust and gives your food a story a chain can never match.
Idea 04
"South Shields' first vegan takeaway menu"
VeganSpotlightAudience

A spotlight on the vegan options, filmed just as appetising as the rest: the loaded vegan pizza or wrap, the cheese pull, the reveal. Hook it on the difference ("the first independent in South Shields to do this properly").

Why it works: It reaches a whole audience your rivals ignore, and vegan food content is highly shareable within tight, active local communities.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into orders.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone in South Shields decides on a takeaway, they search and they read reviews before they order. Your Google profile and reviews are doing quiet, heavy lifting, and they deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised for "pizza South Shields", "kebab Ocean Road" and the terms nearby customers actually search
  • Funnel every happy customer into a fresh Google review
  • Add fresh food photos and offers regularly so the profile looks alive
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Make Online Ordering Effortless

You take orders through your own site and Uber Eats. Every video and post should make the next step obvious, so a hungry viewer becomes an order without hunting for the link or the number.

  • Clear order links in bio, captions and profile across every channel
  • Offers and deals tied to the content so a reel turns into a basket
  • A tidy, fast website that makes ordering direct the easy option

Targeted Local Ads to Hungry Customers

Because a takeaway order is a quick, frequent purchase, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around South Shields puts your best food content in front of people who order takeaway near you.

  • Target South Shields and the surrounding postcodes
  • Use your strongest cheese-pull and late-night reels as the ads
  • Push offers and drive orders to your site and Uber Eats
  • Track cost per order so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Milan Takeaway: A steady flow of new and repeat orders quickly covers the content and the ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a takeaway can do.
