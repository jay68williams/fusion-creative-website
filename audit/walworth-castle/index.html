I

What we are going to do

Four things. Measured in enquiries, viewings booked and dates filled, not likes.

One

Film a wedding in the castle

A ceremony in a genuine 12th-century castle, the turrets, the grounds, the great rooms. It sells itself on film and almost none of it is filmed for couples.

Two

Own the channels you are missing

No TikTok, no ads, and a following well behind the barns nearby, none of which is an actual castle. A capture day a month cut into reels, and a TikTok from zero.

Three

Get your wedding proof visible

You have a strong hotel review base, but zero reviews on Guides for Brides, where couples check. We film testimonials and get the wedding proof flowing.

Four

Give couples a content creator

We film weddings ourselves. Offered as a Walworth perk, every wedding we film for a couple becomes your marketing too.

II

How we are going to do it

Week one

Count everything

Analytics and a Meta pixel on the site, so every enquiry has a source. One clear "book a viewing" ask kept across the site and socials, and a review request after every wedding.

Month one

First capture day

The castle and grounds in autumn light, and one full wedding when a couple says yes. Cut into reels within the week.

Every month

Capture, post, report

A third of posts as reels, filmed not stock. A one-page report: enquiries, viewings booked, dates confirmed.

Month two on

The ad

The wedding film, aimed at engaged couples within an hour. Small spend, judged only on viewings booked.
