What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The offer is genuinely rare

Hot yoga, reformer pilates, a boxing circuit, a Finnish sauna, ice baths and a float tank coming, all in one place in the centre of Durham. Most studios do one or two of these. Having the full strength-and-recovery experience under one roof is a real point of difference, and it is gold for content.

✓ The space is made for video

A heated yoga room, steam rising, an ice bath plunge, the glow of the sauna, a reformer class in full flow. These are some of the most watchable, most shareable moments on social right now. You already own the raw material that other businesses have to fake. It just needs filming and shaping into content built to travel.

✓ The website looks the part

somaclubdurham.co.uk is clean, premium and clearly communicates the brand and the offer, with the app and class booking in place. The foundation is solid, so the job is driving attention to it, not fixing it.

✓ You are already posting

The Instagram and Facebook are live and you are showing up. That habit is the hardest part for most businesses to build. The issue is not effort, it is format and reach, and both are the easy things to fix.

✓ The location and audience are perfect

Durham is a compact, young, wellness-minded city full of students and professionals, exactly the people who fill class timetables and live on Reels and TikTok. A premium studio in Prince Bishops Place is sitting right on top of its ideal audience.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the space, the brand and the offer. The job is to turn that into reach and members: get Soma in front of the Durham students and professionals who would love it, and make it the studio they already know before they ever walk in. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Space the Star

Your biggest asset is the studio itself. The heated yoga room, the reformer beds lined up, the boxing circuit, the sauna, the ice bath. Most people in Durham have never seen inside. Slow, cinematic, sensory footage of the space in use is the single most powerful thing you can put out, because it makes someone feel what a session there is like before they book.

Every corner of Soma is a ready-made piece of content, and it is the best advert there is for a first visit.

2

Lean Into Ice Baths, Sauna and Recovery

Cold plunges, contrast therapy and sauna content are having a huge moment on TikTok and Reels right now. The gasp of a first ice bath, the timer counting down, the calm afterwards, these are some of the most watched wellness formats going. You already have the full setup, which most studios do not.

This is exactly the kind of content that travels well beyond your existing followers and pulls in a brand new audience who then want to try it themselves.

3

Own TikTok Before Anyone Else Does

Soma is on Instagram but there is no TikTok yet, and that is the single biggest open channel for you. Durham is a young, student-heavy city and TikTok is where that audience discovers what to do and where to go. A wellness studio with this much visual, sensory content is perfectly suited to it, and getting there early means you can own the space locally.

The same films that build your Instagram Reels can carry your TikTok too, so it is reach for very little extra effort.

4

Show Real Classes and Real Members

"Come to a 6:30am hot yoga class with me." First reformer session. First ice bath reaction. POV and member-led content is the most relatable, most persuasive thing a studio can post, because it answers the only question a nervous first-timer has: what is it actually like. Show the sweat, the effort, the calm afterwards, the community.

People join a place they can already picture themselves in, and this is how you let them picture it.

5

Put the Founders and Instructors on Camera

People do not join a logo, they join a place and the people who run it. The most effective studio content is human led: the founders explaining why they built Soma, an instructor talking through a class, the personality behind the brand. It builds trust and it makes a brand new studio feel familiar and welcoming.

Familiarity is what turns a scroll into a first booking, especially for someone who has never tried hot yoga or an ice bath before.

6

Reels and TikTok for Reach, Plus Targeted Local Ads

Photos and stills fill a feed, but short-form video built to travel, with proper hooks, is what earns reach. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at Durham students, young professionals and the surrounding postcodes, and you put your best studio and recovery content in front of exactly the people who would sign up.

For a membership business, one new member who stays pays back a lot of content and ad spend, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into class bookings and memberships.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone decides to try hot yoga or find a gym in Durham, they search and they read reviews before they book. Your Google profile is doing quiet, heavy lifting, and for a new studio it deserves to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Optimise the profile for searches like "hot yoga Durham", "reformer pilates Durham" and "sauna and ice bath Durham"
  • Funnel every happy member into a fresh Google review
  • Keep it fed with fresh photos of classes, the space and recovery
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Ride the Student Calendar

Durham runs on the university calendar. Freshers, exam season, the January reset and returning terms are all natural moments to fill classes. Content and offers timed around them reach a huge, wellness-minded audience right when they are looking.

  • Freshers and term-start pushes aimed at new and returning students
  • "New year, new routine" and exam-stress recovery angles
  • Student-friendly content that speaks to the exact audience on your doorstep

Targeted Local Ads to Fill Classes

Because a member who stays is worth a lot over time, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around Durham puts your best studio and recovery content in front of the people most likely to sign up.

  • Target students, young professionals and the core Durham postcodes
  • Use your strongest class, ice bath and studio reels as the ads
  • Drive a steady flow of intro-offer and class bookings to your website
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Soma: One new member who stays covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a studio can do.
