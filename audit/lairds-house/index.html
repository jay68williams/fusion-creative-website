What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The venue is genuinely beautiful

A restored Georgian building, boutique rooms with roll-top baths and long loft views, a snug, a bar and a private garden with wooden pods. This is a place people already want to photograph. Most hospitality businesses have to work hard to look this good on camera. You start with it.

✓ Family-run, with real character

Independent and family-run is exactly the story that wins on social right now. People are tired of chains and want somewhere with a heart and a face behind it. That warmth is your biggest advantage over the big hotel brands, and it barely costs a thing to show.

✓ More than one reason to visit

Hotel stays, gastro pub food in the week, fine dining on a Friday and Saturday, Sunday lunch, the bar, weddings and events. That is a lot of different reasons for people to come, which means a lot of different stories to tell. Right now most of them are hidden.

✓ The location does half the work

Northumberland, minutes from Newcastle, near the beach and the woodland walks, dog friendly. The staycation and short-break market is huge and it is exactly the audience that plans trips off Instagram and TikTok. You are sitting in the middle of content people actively search for.

✓ You are already showing up

You post on Instagram and Facebook and keep the venue looking its best. The habit and the discipline are already there, which is the hardest part for most businesses. The issue is not effort, it is format, and format is the easy thing to fix.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the venue, the food and the setting. The job is to turn that into reach and bookings: get in front of the people planning a stay, a meal out or a wedding, and make Lairds House the name they think of first. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Show the Rooms as Content, Not Just Listings

A slow reveal of a boutique room, the roll-top bath, the loft view, the bi-fold doors opening onto Northumberland. "Wake up here" content is one of the most reliable formats in hospitality because it sells the feeling of the stay, not just the price of it.

People book a room they can already picture themselves in. Short video does that in a way a gallery of stills never will.

2

Make the Food Travel

Food is the single most shared thing on social. The fine dining plate landing on a Friday night, the Sunday roast, a cocktail being poured at the bar, the gin selection lined up. Filmed properly and cut to trending audio, this is the content that fills tables.

You already plate it beautifully. The only missing step is capturing it as short video instead of a static photo after the fact.

3

Sell the Setting and the Staycation

"A dog-friendly weekend in Northumberland." "Twenty minutes from Newcastle, a world away from it." The garden pods, the walks, the nearby beach, the coast and castles up the road. Lifestyle and area content pulls in exactly the people planning a short break.

This is content only a genuinely local, independent venue can make, and it is where you can out-market the chain hotels every time.

4

Turn Weddings and Events Into a Showreel

Weddings and private events are high value, and they are booked heavily off Instagram and TikTok. A short, cinematic film of a wedding day at Lairds House, the garden, the room dressed, the couple, is the most persuasive advert you can put in front of the next couple looking for a venue.

One enquiry from this content can pay for a lot of filming, which makes it one of the highest-return things to show off.

5

Put the Family and Team on Camera

People do not book a logo, they book somewhere run by people they warm to. The most effective hospitality content right now is face-led: the family behind Lairds House, the chef, the team, the little moments behind the pass and behind reception. Your independent, family-run story is a genuine advantage here.

Personality builds familiarity, and familiarity is what makes someone choose you over the anonymous place down the road.

6

Reels and TikTok for Reach, Plus Targeted Local Ads

A steady, photo-led feed is a good habit, but reach now lives in short-form video with a proper hook, and TikTok is wide open for you. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at people near Newcastle and across the North East, and you put your best room, food and wedding content in front of people actually planning to book.

For a venue like yours, one booked stay, table or event pays for a lot of content and ads, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already have: the rooms, the food, the setting and the people. Each one could be filmed on a phone in a single afternoon at the venue.

Idea 01
"Wake up here this weekend"
RoomsRevealStaycation

A slow, cinematic reveal of a boutique room: the roll-top bath, the loft view, the bi-fold doors opening onto Northumberland, coffee in bed. Strong hook on frame one ("this is twenty minutes from Newcastle"). Calm, trending audio. Under 20 seconds.

Why it works: "Wake up here" content sells the feeling of the stay, and it is one of the most saved and shared formats in hospitality.
Idea 02
"Fine dining, Friday night at Lairds House"
FoodRestaurantASMR

Close-up, satisfying shots of the fine dining menu coming together and landing on the table, a cocktail being poured at the bar, the candlelit room. Lead with the plate. A few seconds per dish, cut tight to audio.

Why it works: Food is the most shared content on social, and it turns viewers straight into table bookings for the weekend.
Idea 03
"A dog-friendly weekend in Northumberland"
LocalLifestyleStaycation

A mini guide to a weekend based at Lairds House: the walk, the beach, the garden pods, dinner, the room. Helpful, watchable and built to be saved by anyone planning a trip. Tag the location and the nearby spots.

Why it works: It reaches people actively planning a short break and positions you as the place to base it, something only a local independent can own.
Idea 04
"Meet the family behind Lairds House"
FounderStoryBehind the scenes

You and the team to camera, straight talking, telling the story of the Georgian house you restored and why you do it your way. Honest, warm, a little behind the scenes of a service. Strong hook ("we turned a Georgian house into this").

Why it works: People book people. A family explaining why they built something builds trust fast and makes the place impossible to forget.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into stays, covers and event enquiries.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone decides on a hotel or a place for dinner, they search and read reviews before they book. Your Google profile and reviews are doing quiet, heavy lifting, and they deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised for "hotel near Newcastle" and "restaurant Bedlington" and the surrounding towns
  • Funnel every happy guest into a fresh Google review at checkout or after dinner
  • Add fresh room, food and event photos regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Make Weddings and Events a Proper Funnel

Weddings and private events are your highest-value bookings and the ones most influenced by what people see online. A little content and structure around them goes a long way.

  • A dedicated wedding and events showreel and highlight on your channels
  • Real ceremonies and celebrations filmed at the venue, used as proof
  • A simple enquiry path so interest turns into a booked viewing

Targeted Local Ads to Future Guests

Because a stay, a table for a group or an event is high value, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around Newcastle and the North East puts your best content in front of people planning to book.

  • Target people near Newcastle and across the North East planning breaks and celebrations
  • Use your strongest room, food and wedding reels as the ads
  • Drive a steady flow of bookings and enquiries to your website
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Lairds House: One event booking or a run of weekend stays covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a venue can do.
