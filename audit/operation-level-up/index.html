Operation Level Up opened inside Sports Society in Mirdif with paintball, laser tag, archery and target shooting, Gelball, dodgeball and themed escape rooms, and 2,079 people already follow you. The twelve most recent posts run from 3 September to 5 October 2026, and drew two to nine likes each. The pattern:

  • Every caption ends the same way. The same location line and the same "Call or WhatsApp +971 4 315 1777" close on almost every post. The message is "come in", never "watch this".
  • Reels are underused. Only four of the twelve recent posts are reels, and your most recent, a dodgeball clip, drew two likes. Reels are how a page reaches people who do not follow it yet.
  • The best moments are not being filmed. A squad escaping a room with seconds left, a first-timer landing a bullseye, a paintball hit in slow motion. None of that is in your feed, and it is the content that travels.

What this means. People respond when a real person has a real moment in the arena. Posting those moments on a plan is how they reach the people who already follow you and the people who have never heard of you.

06 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your arenas, your games and your players.

Every concept uses something you already run. Six are built to bring bookings in this month. Six are built to keep your Instagram growing.

Videos that bring bookings

6 concepts
Reaction

Last one standing

"Two squads. One arena. Watch the last hit."

A paintball or laser round cut to the final elimination, the winning squad's reaction, then the booking line.

Why: a squad tags the friends they want to beat next.

Challenge

Can you escape in time

"Sixty minutes. Zombies. Go."

A group racing the clock in the zombie or mafia room, the countdown, the door opening with seconds left.

Why: escape rooms sell on "could we do it", so show a real group trying.

First timer

First dart, first bullseye

"She has never held a bow. Watch the third shot."

A first-timer at the archery or target lane, the misses, then the hit and the face.

Why: the first-timer is the guest most likely to ask what to expect.

Groups

Pick your game

"Paintball, laser tag or an escape room. Which one is your squad?"

A fast run through the six games in thirty seconds, one line each, ending on how to book.

Why: helps a group organiser choose before they message you.

Birthday

A birthday in the arena

"This is what a birthday squad looks like here."

A birthday group arriving, playing and celebrating, with permission, built for parents planning parties.

Why: birthdays are the booking that fills a weekend and books again.

This week

This weekend's slots

"Open this weekend. Here is the arena, and the slots still open."

A quick walk through the lit arena, the games running, and the slots still open.

Why: gives the people who follow you a reason to book this week, not someday.

Organic that builds the audience

6 concepts
Series

Beat the staff

"Can a first-timer out-shoot the team?"

A guest takes on a staff member at the target lane each week, honest faces only.

Why: a weekly head to head people come back for.

Behind the scenes

Setting the arena

"Every night, it resets like this."

The lights coming up, the escape room reset, the first squad walking in.

Why: shows the scale of the place in one clip.

Rooms

Inside the escape rooms

"Zombies or mafia. Pick your nightmare."

One themed room per video: the set, the first clue, the clock.

Why: each room is a short video, and a reason to come back for the next one.

Game

How the target lane works

"Steady hands. Sharp aim. Here is how it scores."

A thirty second explainer of one game, so a nervous first-timer knows what to expect.

Why: answers the question a group has before it books.

Guests

Words at the exit

"Who won, and would you come back?"

Quick answers from squads on the way out, honest and fast.

Why: your own players, on camera, one at a time.

Place

A day at Sports Society

"You came for one thing. Stay for the arena."

A short film placing you inside Sports Society, Mirdif, for the people already there.

Why: catches the footfall that is already in the building.

07 · The plan

A plan built around one shoot.

  1. Step 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the twelve videos. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and plan the booking enquiry the videos point to.

  2. Step 2
    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list across a day in the arena: paintball, laser tag, the target lane and an escape room, players included.

  3. Step 3
    Edit and deliver

    We edit, caption and post the videos to Instagram and TikTok. Each one ends with how to book on WhatsApp.

  4. Step 4
    Handover

    We report what the videos reached and what they brought in, then hand over every video for you to keep and reuse.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short, captioned videos filmed in your arena by a licensed UAE crew to our shot list, edited and posted to launch them. You keep every video.
