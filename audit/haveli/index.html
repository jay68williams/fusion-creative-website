What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The food and the reputation are the real thing

A Michelin-recognised, Bib Gourmand Indian restaurant is a rare thing, and Haveli has held that recognition for years. That is a credential most restaurants would give anything for, and right now it is barely being shown off where hungry people actually scroll.

✓ The proposition is genuinely premium

Contemporary Indian cooking that draws on the major food regions of India, locally sourced ingredients, freshly ground spices, proper technique. That is a clear, confident position that sets you apart from the standard high-street curry house. It gives you something distinctive to build content around.

✓ The room is made for special occasions

An elegant, refined setting built for celebrations, date nights and the meals people remember. Fine dining is an experience, not just a plate, and that experience is exactly the kind of thing people love to watch, save and share before they book.

✓ The posting habit is already there

You are active on Instagram and Facebook with a steady stream of posts. Showing up consistently is the hardest part for most businesses, and you already do it. The issue is not effort, it is format, and format is the easy thing to fix.

✓ The dishes are beautiful

The plating and presentation are already photographed with real care. That raw material is gold for short-form video. It just needs shaping into moving content with a hook, rather than still images that scroll straight past.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the food, the room and the recognition. The job is to turn that into reach and covers: get Haveli in front of the diners across Newcastle and Northumberland who would happily drive out to Darras Hall for a special meal, and make you the name they already trust before they book. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Turn the Food Into Content That Travels

Food is the single most-watched category on short-form video, and your dishes are made for it. The tandoor flame, the sizzle, the sauce pour, the slow reveal of a plate coming together. Filmed properly with a strong hook on frame one, a single dish becomes a piece of content that reaches thousands of local people, not just the followers you already have.

You already invest in beautiful plating. This simply captures it in motion, where it does the most work.

2

Put the Michelin Story to Work

Plenty of people across the North East still do not know there is a Michelin Bib Gourmand Indian restaurant on their doorstep. That is a headline, and headlines travel. Content that leans into the recognition, the craft behind it and what makes Haveli different lifts you above every other Indian restaurant in the region in one line.

It is the kind of proof that makes someone stop scrolling and think, we need to book that.

3

Sell the Occasion, Not Just the Dish

Haveli is where people come for anniversaries, birthdays and the meals that matter. Show that. The candlelit room, the welcome, the little moments of a proper night out. Occasion-led content reaches the people planning something special and plants Haveli as the place to do it.

This is also how you fill the quieter mid-week covers, by giving people a reason to make a Wednesday feel like an event.

4

Show the Craft and the People

People connect with faces and stories, not logos. The chef grinding spices, the story behind a regional dish, the family and team behind the restaurant. Personality and craft-led content builds the familiarity that turns a casual viewer into a booking, and it is content only Haveli can make.

It is also what separates a fine-dining restaurant with a story from a menu that looks like everyone else's.

5

Reels and TikTok for Reach, Plus Targeted Local Ads

A steady posting habit with limited reach tells you the effort is right but the format is not. Short-form video built to travel, with proper hooks rather than static plating posts, is what earns reach. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at diners within driving distance of Ponteland and Newcastle, and you put your best food and occasion content in front of people actually deciding where to eat this weekend.

For a restaurant, a handful of extra covers a week pays for a lot of content and ads, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in a Single Service

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do every night: the dishes, the kitchen, the room and the people. Each one could be filmed on a phone around a normal service.

Idea 01
"The dish everyone orders at Haveli"
SignatureFoodReveal

A fast, cinematic close-up of your signature dish coming together: the tandoor, the sizzle, the sauce, the final plate. Strong hook on frame one ("this is the dish people drive to Ponteland for"). Trending audio. Under 25 seconds. Pure food that stops the scroll.

Why it works: Food is the most-watched category on short-form, and a single mouth-watering dish reel is the best advert a restaurant can post.
Idea 02
"There's a Michelin Indian restaurant hiding in Darras Hall"
ProofStoryLocal

A quick, confident piece that leans into the Michelin Bib Gourmand recognition and the craft behind it. The room, a few standout dishes, the story of what makes Haveli different. Lead with the surprise ("you have a Michelin-recognised Indian restaurant on your doorstep and you didn't know").

Why it works: It answers the only question a diner has, is this worth the trip, with real credibility rather than a claim. Recognition travels.
Idea 03
"Where to take someone you want to impress"
OccasionAmbienceDate night

An occasion-led walkthrough: the candlelit room, the welcome, the plates arriving, the feel of a proper night out. Warm, aspirational, built to be saved and shared by anyone planning a birthday, an anniversary or a special dinner. Tag the location.

Why it works: It reaches the people planning something special and makes Haveli the obvious place to do it, which is exactly how you fill the quieter nights.
Idea 04
"How we make it, from the kitchen"
ChefCraftBehind the pass

The chef to camera, or behind the pass, showing the craft: freshly ground spices, a regional dish explained, the technique most people never see. Honest, human and genuinely interesting. Strong hook ("this is why our curry doesn't taste like anywhere else").

Why it works: People connect with faces and stories. Craft-led content builds trust fast and gives your food a story no competitor can copy.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into booked tables.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone decides where to eat, they search and they read reviews before they ever book. Your Google profile and reviews are doing quiet, heavy lifting, and they deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised for "Indian restaurant Ponteland" and the surrounding areas
  • Funnel every happy diner into a fresh Google review at the end of the meal
  • Add new dishes, seasonal menus and occasion photos regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Fill the Quieter Nights on Purpose

Fine dining lives and dies by covers, and midweek is where most restaurants leave money on the table. The same content engine can be pointed at filling specific nights, giving people a reason to book a Wednesday or Thursday.

  • Occasion and midweek content aimed at date nights and small celebrations
  • Seasonal menus and events turned into a reason to book now
  • A steady rhythm that keeps Haveli top of mind, not just remembered on a Saturday

Targeted Local Ads to Nearby Diners

Because a table is high value, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around Ponteland and Newcastle puts your best food and occasion content in front of people deciding where to eat this week.

  • Target diners within driving distance of Darras Hall
  • Use your strongest dish and occasion reels as the ads
  • Drive a steady flow of bookings to your website
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Haveli: A handful of extra covers a week covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a restaurant can do.
