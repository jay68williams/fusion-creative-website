01 · The short version

A big audience and a photogenic product, aimed at a narrow set of clips.

Crystal Clear Watersports runs clear kayaks, clear boats, waterbikes and stand up paddle from Dubai, with night paddles billed on your own feed as "Dubai's first EVER night kayaking experiences". You have built 66,000 followers over 941 posts, and a Tripadvisor rating of 4.9 from a page of reviews. The twelve most recent posts we read, from early October, almost all show the same thing: a sunset or night clear kayak against the Dubai Creek Harbour skyline. The rest of the range, and the people in the boats, barely feature, and there is no single booking route of your own that a post can send someone to.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A product built for video: clear kayaks and clear boats you can see straight through, waterbikes, and paddles under the Burj Khalifa and Dubai Creek Harbour skyline.
  • Night kayaking you already call "Dubai's first", with LED lit hulls glowing on the water. Few operators have a clip like it.
  • An audience most operators would envy: 66,000 followers, built over 941 posts.
  • A 4.9 rating on Tripadvisor to point back to, and listings on Headout, Expedia and ClassPass that already send you bookings.
  • A setting that sells itself: Dubai Creek Harbour at golden hour and after dark, with the skyline behind every paddle.

You do not need more followers or a new fleet. You need video of the whole range, the guests and the night paddles, posted on a plan, and a booking route of your own that a post can point to, so 66,000 followers turn into direct bookings instead of scrolling past the same sunset.

03 · Your website

A photogenic brand, and no route of your own to send a booking to.

Bookings instead run through Instagram messages and third party listings. These are the changes we would make so a post turns into a booking you keep.

What we sawWhy it mattersFix
The homepage does not load its contentWhen we read the homepage it returned no text or images, and the research notes broken image placeholders with no prices or contact details. A visitor sent there from a post can land on a blank or broken page.Rebuild it light and mobile first around your own video, so it loads fast on a phone and shows the kayaks, the slots and a way to book.

The only way to reach you is an Instagram message, which is easy to miss at 66,000 followers.Add a simple enquiry and a pre-filled WhatsApp button on every page, so interest becomes a booking in one step instead of a message in a crowded inbox.

Crystal Clear posts most days, which is the hard part done. The pattern in the twelve most recent posts we read:

  • Most of the recent posts are the same idea. A sunset or night clear kayak at Dubai Creek Harbour, with the skyline behind it. It is a lovely frame, but seven of the twelve repeat it, so a follower sees the same thing each time.
  • The rest of the range barely features. The clear boats, the stand up paddle and the daytime trips are part of what you sell, but the recent feed leans on the one night-kayak look. Each of the others is a video a new viewer has not seen.
  • The people are missing. The posts show the kayak and the view, rarely the guest in it. The first-timer's face, the couple on a date, the child's first paddle are the moments that reach people who do not follow you yet.

What this means. The audience is already here. Short video of the whole range, with real guests in it, posted on a plan and pointed at a booking route of your own, is how that audience turns into direct bookings instead of another scroll past the sunset.

Your strongest material is the night kayaking, the clear hulls and the skyline. The strongest version of that is a person, a moment and a booking route at the end.

You can see straight through the kayak to the water and the skyline. Film it from inside, from below and from the water. Few operators have the shot.

3

A direct booking at the end

The sunset slot, the night paddle, the waterbike at dawn. Every video ends with a clear way to book direct, not a scatter of listings.

07 · The plan

A plan built around one shoot day on the water.

  1. Step 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the first videos and the range to feature. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and plan the booking route your posts will point to.

  2. Step 2
    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would spend a day at Dubai Creek Harbour and film to our shot list: the clear kayaks, a waterbike, the guests, and the night paddle lighting up.

  3. Step 3
    Edit, caption and deliver

    We edit, caption and grade every clip from our studio in Newcastle, each one ending with a clear way to book direct.

  4. Step 4
    Handover

    We hand over every edited video for you to keep and reuse, with a simple plan for what goes where and when.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos of the kayaks, the night paddles and your guests, filmed by a licensed UAE crew to our shot list, edited and captioned, each ending with a way to book. You keep every video.
