07 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your domes, your desert and your guests.

Every concept uses something you already have: the domes, the sand, the safari route and the people who stay. Six are built to bring bookings in this month. Six are built to keep the feed growing.

Videos that bring bookings in

6 concepts
Stay

Your first morning in a dome

"Here is what you see when you wake up in the desert."

A guest wakes in a dome, opens the curtains and walks out onto the sand. Filmed in one morning.

Why: shows the stay from the guest's side, which is the view that sells it.

Challenge

Can you spot the camp?

"Ten seconds of desert. Where do you think we are?"

Quick guesses from the crew, then the reveal: the camp, the domes, the sunset.

Why: a question keeps people watching to the end.

Guest

Tourist or local?

"Where are you visiting from, and where are you sleeping tonight?"

Guests answer on the way to their dome, from the UAE, Europe and further afield.

Why: people plan trips from other visitors' stories.

Safari

First dune drive, first person

"What to expect on your first dune drive."

A short first-person run through the safari, from pick-up to the sunset stop.

Why: it answers the question people ask before they book.

Skit

Who is on the marshmallows?

"The campfire is ready. Who is on the marshmallows?"

Two friends arrive at the camp and argue over the fire, ending at the table.

Why: makes a camp stay feel like a shared night out.

Family

A family night in the desert

"The kids have never seen stars like this."

A family at the tent in the evening, with the food and the night in the camp.

Why: families book on what the children will do.

Organic that builds the audience

6 concepts
Series

Dome of the week

"Every week, a different dome. Here is this one."

One dome a week, filmed at dusk and at dawn, with a room tour.

Why: a series people come back for.

Ideas

Packing for one night

"What to bring for one night under the stars."

Practical clips on what to wear and bring, with the camp in the background.

Why: useful clips get saved and sent on.

Season

Winter in the desert

"Winter in the desert feels different. Here is why."

The season told through the camp: cooler evenings, the campfire, the stars.

Why: gives people a reason to book the season, not just a stay.

Team

Meet the hosts

"Who runs the camp, and how they look after you."

The team at the gate, in the kitchen and at the campfire, in their own words.

Why: people book the hosts they trust.

Staycation

A staycation in the Dunes

"One night away from the city, with the desert right outside."

A staycation film: check-in, dome, dinner, sunrise.

Why: the short break is what your posts already promote.

Direct

Why book direct?

"Why book with us, and not through a booking site?"

A simple, honest explainer on booking direct, with the camp and the team speaking to camera.

Why: turns your booking route into content.
