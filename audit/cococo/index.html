What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The brand has a clear, warm identity

"Coco & Co" is short, friendly and easy to remember, and it reads like an independent, not a chain. That kind of name gives you a head start on social, because people root for a local spot with a bit of character.

✓ You are building in public

Sharing the fit-out and the build-up before you even open is exactly the right instinct. People love watching a place come together, and every one of those posts is a reason for a future regular to follow now rather than after opening day.

✓ You are already on the right platforms

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook are exactly where a new coffee shop should be. The channels are live and the habit of posting is there, which is the hard part for most businesses. The opportunity now is format and consistency, and both are the easy things to fix.

✓ The timing could not be better

Launching is the single best moment to build an audience. There is a natural story to tell, a countdown to opening, and everyone loves being early to a new local favourite. Grow the following now and you open to a room that already knows your name.

✓ Newcastle loves an independent coffee spot

The city has a genuinely strong, loyal coffee community, and short-form video is where that community discovers its next go-to. A distinctive independent, filmed properly, is exactly the kind of place that spreads by word of mouth and by algorithm.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You have the brand and the timing. The job is to turn that into reach and footfall: get Coco & Co in front of Newcastle coffee lovers so they follow you before you open and walk in once you do. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Launch a Content Moment

You only get to open once, so film all of it. The countdown, the first bag of beans, the sign going up, the machine getting dialled in, the very first coffee you serve, the queue on opening morning. Each of these is a ready-made piece of content that builds anticipation and pulls people in on day one.

A launch is the best story a new business ever has. Told as short video, it turns quiet build-up into a following that is waiting for the doors to open.

2

Own "Coffee in Newcastle" on TikTok and Reels

When someone in Newcastle wants a coffee, more and more they open TikTok or Instagram, not Google. "Best flat white in Newcastle", "new coffee spot in town", a quick POV of walking in and ordering. Discovery content like this is what puts a new cafe on the map fast.

This is content only a local spot can make, and it is exactly where you can get in front of people who are actively deciding where to get their next coffee.

3

Show the Craft, Not Just the Cup

The process is the content. The pour, the latte art, the steam, the beans, the syrup going in, a drink coming together in close-up. These satisfying, sensory clips are some of the most watched and saved videos in the whole coffee category, and they make people want a cup right now.

You already do this every day behind the counter. Filmed well, it becomes the kind of clip people tag a mate in.

4

Turn Regulars Into Reach

Once you are open, your customers become your best marketing. A spot worth filming, a signature drink worth showing off and a gentle nudge to tag you turns every visit into content that reaches their friends. User-generated clips carry more trust than anything a brand posts about itself.

Make the place, and the drinks, genuinely worth filming, and the community does a chunk of the posting for you.

5

Build a Signature Drink People Come For

The cafes that travel on social almost always have a hero: a signature drink, a seasonal special, a menu item people screenshot and come in specifically to try. Give people one clear thing to talk about and film, and give them a reason to make the trip rather than settle for the nearest chain.

One standout drink, launched properly with its own video, can do more for opening week than a month of general posts.

6

Consistency Plus a Targeted Local Ad Push

Posting is happening, but reach comes from rhythm and format: proper hooks, short cuts and a steady flow rather than the odd upload. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at people in and around Newcastle, and you put your best build-up and launch clips in front of exactly the people who might pop in.

For a new cafe, a little paid reach in the launch window goes a long way, because you are catching people right when curiosity about a new local spot is highest.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film Around Your Launch

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already have: the opening, the craft, the drinks and the place. Each one could be filmed on a phone in a single morning behind the counter.

Idea 01
"Come with me to open a coffee shop in Newcastle"
LaunchBuild-upSeries

A running series of the build-up: empty unit to finished cafe, the machine arriving, the first test pour, the sign going up, counting down to opening day. Strong hook on frame one ("We are opening a coffee shop in Newcastle, here is day one"). Trending audio. Short and honest.

Why it works: People get invested in a journey and want to be there on opening day. It turns quiet build-up into a following that shows up the moment you open.
Idea 02
"The very first coffee we ever served"
Opening dayFirst pourMoment

Opening morning captured start to finish: unlocking the doors, the first customer, the first coffee going across the counter, the reaction. Lead with the moment ("our first ever customer"). A few seconds of nerves and buzz, ending on a happy first cup.

Why it works: A genuine "first ever" moment is emotional and shareable, and it invites the whole local community to come and be part of a new place.
Idea 03
Signature drink reveal, in close-up
CraftASMRMenu

One hero drink built in satisfying close-up: the pour, the steam, the syrup, the finish, the first sip. No talking needed, just great sound and great visuals. End on the name of the drink and where to get it. Made to be watched twice and saved.

Why it works: Sensory craft clips are some of the most watched content in the coffee category, and a signature drink gives people one clear thing to come in and try.
Idea 04
"New coffee spot in Newcastle you need to try"
LocalDiscoveryPOV

A POV walk-in: the door, the counter, the vibe, ordering, the drink landing on the table. Tag the location. Frame it as a local recommendation ("if you live in Newcastle, save this"). Helpful, watchable and built to be shared with a mate.

Why it works: This is how people actually find cafes now, by searching and scrolling. Discovery content puts you in front of Newcastle coffee lovers deciding where to go next.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your online reach into people through the door.

Get the Google Business Profile Pulling Foot Traffic

When someone nearby searches "coffee near me" or "coffee shop Newcastle", your Google profile is what decides whether they find you. For a new cafe, this is quiet, heavy lifting that should be set up properly from opening day.

  • Claim and fully build out the profile with photos, hours and location
  • Optimise for "coffee shop Newcastle" and your local area terms
  • Nudge happy first customers into fresh Google reviews from week one
  • Keep it fed with photos of drinks, the space and specials

Turn Customers Into Content

Your regulars will be your biggest marketing channel. A little encouragement to tag you, plus a space and drinks worth filming, and their posts do the reaching for you across their own friends and followers.

  • A spot in the cafe that people naturally want to photograph
  • A clear handle and prompt to tag, on the menu and at the counter
  • Reshare the best customer clips to build community and trust

A Small Local Ad Push Around Opening

Because a coffee is a low-commitment, repeat purchase, a little paid reach in the launch window pays back fast in footfall and first visits. A tightly targeted budget puts your best clips in front of people right around Newcastle.

  • Target people in and around your part of Newcastle
  • Use your strongest build-up and launch reels as the ads
  • Point people to your location, opening hours and socials
  • Keep it accountable so you can see what is driving visits

Why it fits Coco & Co: opening week is when curiosity about a new local spot is highest, so a small push at exactly that moment gets you off to the strongest possible start.
