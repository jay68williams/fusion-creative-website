What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The product is naturally scroll-stopping

Glossy chocolate, fresh strawberries, the drizzle and the finished box. This is exactly the kind of visual that makes people stop, watch and tag a friend. Most businesses have to work hard to make their product look good on camera. Yours already does. That is a massive head start.

✓ You are showing up on Instagram

You have a live, product-led Instagram under @dipped_delights_ncl. The habit of posting and putting the product out there is already in place, which is the part most small businesses struggle with most. The foundation is there to build reach on top of.

✓ You are in the right place at the right time

Chocolate-dipped strawberries, and the Dubai chocolate version in particular, are one of the most talked-about dessert trends right now, including here in Newcastle. Demand is already there and people are actively searching for it. You do not have to create the appetite, you just have to be the local name that shows up when they look.

✓ It sells itself as a gift

Birthdays, anniversaries, date nights, thank-yous, a treat for yourself. A dipped strawberry box is an easy, repeatable purchase people come back to for every occasion. That built-in gifting demand is a content goldmine, because every occasion is a fresh reason to post.

✓ It is local and personal

A handmade, made-to-order Newcastle business has a story the big brands cannot copy: the person behind it, the care in each box, the local delivery. People love buying from someone real in their own city, and that is exactly the kind of story that builds a loyal following.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the product, the local demand and the trend timing. The job is to turn that into reach and orders: get in front of far more people in Newcastle and beyond, and make it effortless for them to go from watching a video to buying a box. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Let the Product Be the Content

The single biggest opportunity is short video that leans into how satisfying your product is to watch. The strawberry going into the chocolate, the pour, the drizzle, the setting, the pull-apart, the finished box being closed. Filmed close up with the right sound, this is the content that racks up views, because people cannot help but watch it to the end.

Photos show the product. Video makes people crave it, and craving is what turns a scroll into an order.

2

Ride the Trending Dessert Waves

Dubai chocolate strawberries, pistachio, freeze-dried toppings, seasonal flavours. There is a constant stream of dessert trends and trending audio moving through TikTok and Reels right now. When you jump on one early with your own version, the algorithm does a lot of the reaching for you.

A big part of our job is spotting the wave before it peaks and getting your product on it while it is climbing, so you catch the reach rather than the tail end.

3

Scale Onto TikTok

Instagram is where you are now, but TikTok is where dessert content genuinely explodes, and where a single video can put you in front of hundreds of thousands of people who have never heard of you. A brand active on Instagram almost always translates straight onto TikTok, so this is about scaling what you already do onto the platform built for exactly this kind of content.

The same films can run across TikTok, Reels and Facebook, so one shoot feeds every channel and multiplies your reach.

4

Turn Occasions Into a Content Calendar

Valentine's, Mother's Day, birthdays, exam results, "just because" treats. Every occasion is a reason for someone to order, and a reason for you to post. Planning content around the calendar means you always have a timely hook, and you are in people's feeds right when they are thinking about a gift.

Occasion-led content sells, because it reaches people at the exact moment they have a reason to buy.

5

Give People a Simple Way to Order

Right now the content can build appetite, but there is no website to catch it, so orders rely on people finding their way to your messages. A simple ordering page, with flavours, occasions, delivery areas and a clear "order now" button, turns all that attention into actual sales, and takes the back-and-forth out of taking orders.

Content creates the demand. A simple website makes sure you capture it instead of losing it in the DMs.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already make. Each one could be filmed on a phone while you are putting an order together.

Idea 01
"The most satisfying dip you'll watch today"
ASMRClose-upSatisfying

Shot tight and slow: the strawberry into the chocolate, the lift, the drizzle, the set, the sprinkle of topping. Real sound turned up, trending audio underneath, under 20 seconds. Hook on frame one ("wait for the drizzle").

Why it works: Satisfying, close-up food is one of the most rewatched formats there is, and rewatches are what the algorithm pushes.
Idea 02
"Making a Dubai chocolate strawberry box"
TrendBuildFlavour

Jump on the trend everyone is searching for: the pistachio, the kataifi crunch, the chocolate, built into a box start to finish. Lead with the hook ("Newcastle, your Dubai chocolate strawberries are here"). Tag the location.

Why it works: Riding a live trend early means the algorithm is already pushing that content to a hungry audience, and you are the local name on it.
Idea 03
"POV: your Dipped Delights box just arrived"
UnboxingGiftReaction

The box arriving, the ribbon, the lid coming off, the reveal of the strawberries inside. A real customer reaction or a clean unboxing works brilliantly. End on the finished box and a simple "order yours for [occasion]".

Why it works: Unboxings sell the gifting experience, not just the product, and they make the viewer picture receiving one themselves.
Idea 04
"Making [name]'s birthday box"
OccasionPersonalBehind the scenes

A quick behind-the-scenes of a real order coming together for an occasion: personalising it, the finishing touches, packing it up ready to go. Warm, personal, and a reminder that every box is made to order for someone.

Why it works: It ties your product to occasions and shows the person behind the brand, which is exactly what builds a loyal, local following.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into orders.

A Simple Website and Ordering Page

When someone sees your video and wants a box, they need somewhere to go. A clean, mobile-first site with your flavours, occasions, delivery areas and a clear order button turns attention into orders and saves you managing everything through DMs.

  • Flavours and boxes laid out clearly, with photos that sell
  • Occasions and delivery areas front and centre
  • A simple order or enquiry form, straight to you
  • The link in every bio so every video has somewhere to send people

Get Found When Newcastle Searches

People actively search "chocolate strawberries Newcastle" and "Dubai chocolate Newcastle". A Google Business profile and a findable website mean you show up when they do, not just when they happen to scroll past a post.

  • A Google Business profile with photos, area and how to order
  • Reviews from happy customers feeding it constantly
  • Your best videos and boxes kept fresh and visible

A Small, Targeted Local Ad Budget

Once we have videos that perform, a modest budget aimed at Newcastle and the surrounding area puts your best content in front of local people who love a treat or need a gift, right when occasions come around.

  • Target Newcastle and nearby postcodes
  • Use your strongest dip and unboxing videos as the ads
  • Push harder around Valentine's, Mother's Day and the festive run
  • Track cost per order so the spend always pays back

Why it fits Dipped Delights: A treat is an easy, low-cost yes, so putting a mouth-watering video in front of the right local person turns into orders fast.
