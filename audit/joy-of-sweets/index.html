Credit Where It Is Due

✓ Your niches are ad audiences

Halal, vegan, gluten free, US imports, retro childhood favourites. Every one of those is a passionate, targetable audience on Meta, and almost nobody serves them all in one store. For an ads engine this is a goldmine: five distinct hooks, one checkout.

Where The Opportunity Is
The Plan: Store First, Then Ads, Then Email

The order here matters more than the ingredients. Ads are what you asked about, and ads are the engine, but pouring paid traffic into a store with falling conversion is how ad budgets die. So we sequence it: fix the store, then fuel it, then keep the customers it wins. Everything below is done by us, filmed by us, managed by us, completely out of your hands.

1

Fix the Store Before Spending a Pound

Website sales down 49% is the number that decides the order of everything else. Before any ad money moves, the store gets a conversion overhaul: fast mobile pages, product pages that sell rather than list, trust signals where doubt creeps in, a checkout with no friction, and dedicated landing pages for each ad audience so the click always lands on exactly what it was promised.

Every point of conversion recovered makes every future ad pound work harder. This step is what makes the rest of the plan safe to spend on.

2

Run the Candle Company Ads Playbook

This is the bit Ravi told you about, and it starts the moment the store is ready. We come in and film the ad creative ourselves, launch a spread of ads, watch the numbers daily, scale the winners and rotate out the laggards, week in, week out. For the candle company that meant 708 new customers in three weeks at 2.20 each, with the best single ad returning 18.3 times its spend. With Q4 gifting season ahead, the timing to build this is now.

Every ad points at your store, every order is tracked back to the ad that caused it, and you see it all in a live dashboard. No guesswork, no vanity metrics, just cost per order.

3

One Store, Five Audiences

Halal sweets, vegan sweets, gluten free, American imports, retro favourites. Each niche gets its own creative angle and its own tightly targeted campaign, because a halal household, a vegan teenager and a nostalgic forty-something respond to completely different hooks, and you genuinely serve all of them.

Niche campaigns are usually the cheapest customers on Meta, because the message matches the person perfectly and almost nobody else is talking to them.

4

Content and Socials, Fully Off Your Plate

Alongside the ads we run the content engine: packing reels, wall tours, custom pouch challenges, the formats that feed both the organic channels and the ad account with fresh creative. We film it, edit it, post it, and manage the comments, across TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. You run the sweet business, we run the marketing.

The best organic performers become the next ads, so the two engines feed each other instead of competing for your time.

5

Email and Remarketing: the Full DTC Structure

Once the ads are winning customers, email keeps them. Welcome flows for new buyers, abandoned basket recovery, new-drop announcements, and repeat-purchase nudges timed to when the last pouch runs out. Paired with remarketing campaigns to past buyers and browsers, this is the remarketing layer a proper DTC business runs on, and it is the cheapest revenue in the whole system.

Ads win the customer once. Email and remarketing are what turn one order into a customer worth many.

6

De-Risk the TikTok Dependence

None of this means stepping back from TikTok, it is winning and we would keep feeding it. But a business earning everything on one platform is one algorithm change away from a very bad month. The plan above deliberately builds revenue on channels you own: your store, your ad account, your email list. TikTok stays the spearhead. It just stops being the whole army.

When the website converts, the ads print, and the email list buys again, a quiet week on TikTok stops being a quiet week for the business.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in One Afternoon at the Unit

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what already happens at the unit every day: the wall, the scoop, the pack and the people. All four could be captured in a single afternoon, and the best performers become the first ads.

Idea 01
"Pack this order with me"
Packing ASMRReal ordersUnder 25s

A real customer order packed on camera: the ticket, the wall, the scoops, the pour, the seal, the name on the box. Natural sound up, cut tight. The single most proven sweet shop format there is, and it is your existing workflow with a phone pointed at it.

Why it works: Satisfying, personal and endlessly repeatable. Customers start ordering hoping to see their own pack, which turns fulfilment into marketing.
Idea 02
"500 sweets. One wall."
The rangeWall tourThe claim

A fast tour of the biggest custom Pick n Mix range in the UK: whip pans down the wall, close-ups of the categories, the rarest US imports, the retro corner. Hook on frame one ("the UK's largest Pick n Mix wall is in Newcastle").

Why it works: Scale is inherently watchable, and the claim plants your moat in every viewer's head. This is also the perfect first ad creative.
Idea 03
"Sweets you thought disappeared in 2005"
NostalgiaRetroTag a mate

The retro range as a nostalgia hit: "if you remember these, your knees hurt", childhood favourites held up one by one, the playground ranking debate. Comments write themselves, and the same format flips for the US imports ("sweets you cannot buy in UK supermarkets").

Why it works: Nostalgia is the highest-share emotion in food content, and every comment thread is an argument that feeds the algorithm.
Idea 04
"We built the UK's biggest Pick n Mix from Newcastle"
FounderFamily storyTrust

David to camera: the family behind it, starting in 2021, how the range got to 500, what gets taken seriously (allergens, halal, vegan) and what does not (everything else). Cut with the unit at full tilt. Warm, honest, no polish needed.

Why it works: A family business people can root for beats a faceless webshop, and trust is what converts a first-time ad click into an order.
