01 · The short version

The audience is already there. Your own posts are not reaching it.

Sixteen thousand people follow Art House Cafe, and visitors who film the room do well: one visitor reel drew 1,602 likes on 3 October. Your own nine posts since 11 September drew between 2 and 11 likes each, a median of five, and none is a reel. The audience is there; your own content is not converting it. You have plenty to point a camera at: a cafe established in 2014 in Al Bateen, upcycled furniture and interior design, a courtyard wall of painted plates, Paint a Plate, Paint a Brick, Paint a Face and Lock Your Love while the food is cooked. What is missing is a steady flow of short video with people in it, so people in Abu Dhabi have a reason to come this week.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A concept built into the room. Your own site says it: the cafe was built "to show sustainability by creating art from waste products", with upcycled furniture and interior design.
  • Things guests do with their hands. Paint a Plate, Paint a Brick, Paint a Face and Lock Your Love, "while waiting for delicious food". Each one is a video.
  • A wall of painted plates that visitors already film. Three visitor reels and posts from the last four weeks drew 1,602, 165 and 55 likes.
  • A cafe in Al Bateen, with a courtyard, a statue and a sign that visitors film. The walk in is a story on its own.
  • A menu with breakfast and pancakes, plus catering on site.

You do not need a new concept. You need short video of the room, the painting, the team and your guests, posted on a plan, so the people who already follow you, and the people who have not found the cafe yet, know why to come in this week.

03 · Your website

A brochure site, with no online way to book a table or a painting session.

The site has the story, a menu, a gallery, an activities page, an events page and a contact page. These are the changes we would make so it turns the videos into covers.

What we sawWhy it mattersFix
No online booking or orderingWe could not find an online booking or ordering step on the site. The route in is the phone number, 02 666 0175, and the contact page.Add a one-tap WhatsApp button and a simple booking step for tables and painting sessions, so every video can end with "book here".
The room is on the site, few people areThe gallery and the activities page show the plate wall, the dining room and the dishes. There are few short videos of people taking part.Put the first videos on the homepage, so the site shows what a visit feels like, not just what it looks like.
No dated listing of eventsThe events page lists special events, business lunches and catering, with no dates. We found no list of what is on this week.A short "this week" strip on the homepage, updated with each video.
No Meta pixelWe found no Meta pixel on the site, so nobody who visits it can be reached again on Instagram or Facebook.Install the pixel now. When ads make sense later, the audience will already be waiting.

Your captions are clear and well written. The pattern in your recent posts:

  • Eight posts on one day, then one more. Eight of your nine recent posts went up on 11 September, and the ninth on 9 October. A batch is not a rhythm.
  • Still images, no reels. Your nine posts are photographs of food and drink, some with text on top. None is a reel. The weakest, 2 likes, is your matcha post.
  • Visitors who film the room do far better. A visitor filmed your sign and courtyard: 1,602 likes. Another visitor filmed the plate wall and the statue for a "Cafe Check-in Series": 165 likes and 24 comments. A third visitor's post says almost everything inside has had a past life: 55 likes.

What this means. The visitor reels we found, which show the room, drew far more than your own posts. Short video of the room, filmed in a day and posted on a plan, is how 16,000 followers turn back into covers.

07 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your plate wall, your paintbrushes and your guests.

Every concept uses something you already have. Six are built to bring people in this week. Six are built to keep your Instagram growing.

Videos that bring people in

6 concepts
Skit

The wait

"When you ask how long the food will be and they hand you a paintbrush."

A guest checks their watch. A server puts down a plate, a brush and a palette. Cut to the finished plate going on the wall.

Why: turns your own line, painting while you wait, into a joke.

Challenge

Beat the clock

"Can you finish your plate before the food arrives?"

Two friends race to paint a plate. The kitchen sets the clock.

Why: a race gives people a reason to watch to the end.

Skit

Every piece has a past

"This was something else before it came here."

A server introduces the furniture in the room, built from recycled material, and what each piece was before.

Why: makes the sustainability story funny, not worthy.

Challenge

Find your plate

"You painted this here. Can you find it?"

A returning guest hunts the wall for the plate they painted. Real reactions when they find it.

Why: past guests have a reason to come back and look.

Local

Hidden in Al Bateen

"Hidden next to the Etihad Modern Art Gallery, and almost everything inside has had a past life."

First person: the street, the sign, the courtyard, the first table. Thirty seconds, no cuts needed.

Why: the walk-in story behind the visitor reel that drew 1,602 likes, made properly.

Activity

Paint a Plate, start to finish

"Enjoy painting while waiting for delicious food."

A guest paints a plate from blank to finished, then it goes on the wall. Cut from the first brush stroke to the plate on the wall.

Why: the activity is already on your site, and the wall is the finish line.

Organic that builds the audience

6 concepts
Series

What this was before

"Before it was in this room, it was something else."

One upcycled piece a week, its past life and its place in the room.

Why: a weekly series people can follow, built from your own room.

Dish

Shakshouka to the table

"Comes to the table hot, rich and satisfying."

The pan, the eggs, the walk to the table, the first scoop. Your own caption, as a film.

Why: the shakshouka post drew 5 likes as a still. This is the same dish with movement.

Guests

What did you paint?

"What did you paint, and who is it for?"

Quick answers from guests holding their finished plates, before they go on the wall.

Why: guests tend to share videos they appear in.

Family

The kids table

"Pancakes, a paint palette and a table for the kids."

A family table on a weekend morning, the kids painting while the food comes.

Why: families look for places their children can enjoy.

Creators

Invite a creator in

"We asked a cafe creator to show you round."

One invited creator, filmed by the crew as well, posted on both accounts.

Why: a visitor's reel drew 1,602 likes, the most we found for the cafe.

Catering

Lunch, in a room like this

"Your office lunch, inside a room full of art."

A business lunch being set in the dining room, and the catering service on site.

Why: your site already offers business lunches and on-site catering.

08 · The plan

A plan built around the room you already have.

    Plan

    A call to pick the videos and agree the shot list, built for your room. We brief a licensed UAE crew and add a one-tap WhatsApp booking button to the site.

    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list and brief in your cafe: a service, a painting session and the walk in from the street, in one day. We direct remotely from Newcastle.

    Edit and deliver

    Handover
