A burger the BBC has listed, and a front door that belongs to Talabat.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A menu that fits on one line: SNGL, DOBL, TRPL. Your own posts describe it plainly: black Angus beef, American cheese, homemade gherkins.
  • BBC Good Food Middle East listed you in its guide to smash burgers in Dubai (7 May 2026) as "a no-frills smash burger spot known for customisable stacks". That is a line you can use.

Dr Smash posted in a burst, then stopped. The pattern in those posts is clear:

  • The burger beats the ingredient. Your 253-like and 234-like posts lead the account. The menu graphics drew 111 to 177. Close-ups of cheese, onion and gherkins drew 26 to 45.
  • None of the twelve is a reel. A smash burger is sound and motion: the press, the sizzle, the cheese going on, the stack. None of that appears in the last twelve posts.
  • 172 days of silence. 7,729 people chose to follow you, and nothing has been posted to them since 21 April.

What this means. People respond to the product when they see it. Short video of the smash, the stack and real customers, posted on a plan, is how it reaches the people who already follow you and the people near each branch who have not heard of you yet.

07 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your stack, your sauce and your customers.

Every concept uses something you already have. Six are built to bring orders in, and six to grow your Instagram and send people to your site.

Videos that bring orders in

6 concepts
Skit

SNGL, DOBL, TRPL

"When your mate orders a single at Dr Smash."

The whole table stops when the TRPL arrives. Built on the three names from your own menu.

Why: turns your menu into a joke people tag a friend in.

Challenge

How many can you finish?

"Order as many patties as you can finish."

Customers go for four, five, six. Honest faces, a clean plate or not, filmed on set by the crew.

Why: a challenge gives people a reason to come in and try.

Skit

Just make it spicy

"He said make it spicy. He did not say how spicy."

Built on your "Just make it.. spicy" post, with the kitchen taking the brief literally.

Why: makes your spiciest build a dare.

Challenge

The sauce blind test

"Can you tell the sauces apart?"

Three sauces, one blindfold, regulars guessing at the counter.

Why: puts your sauce at the centre of a game.

Local

Nadd Al Hamar lunch

"Attention Nadd Al Hamar. Lunch is sorted."

A short film of the shop, the counter and a collection order being handed over, aimed at the streets around you.

Why: speaks to one neighbourhood, and the same format works for Abu Dhabi.

Reaction

First smash

"First time at Dr Smash. Go."

A first-timer at the counter, the first bite and the verdict, filmed by the crew.

Why: shows a newcomer ordering and reacting, which is what people nearby need to see.

08 · Your orders, direct

Every order today pays a platform. Some of them do not need to.

The online route we can see is Talabat, and the one ordering link on your Linktree goes there too. We would not tell you to leave Talabat. It brings new customers and riders. The point is the people who already know you.

Talabat put its average commission from restaurants at 17% to 18% on its Q1 2025 results call. That is a group-wide average across its markets, reported in a FAB Securities note on 13 May 2025. It is the platform's figure, not ours. Whatever your own terms are, the commission applies to every order, including the ones from regulars who would have ordered from you anyway.

Those regulars are the opportunity: 7,729 followers, the offices and homes around each branch, and anyone who read the BBC guide and then searched your name. Today the only ordering page they can find is Talabat. With your own site they could order collection direct, message you on WhatsApp for a group order or catering, and find both branches with hours and a map.

What the site would be: your own address, the menu with the single, double and triple builds, both locations, collection ordering, a WhatsApp button on every page with a pre-filled message, and tracking so you can see which visits and messages come from your posts. Built around your photos and the videos from the first filming day, and looked after every month.

09 · The plan, month by month

A plan that builds the site and the videos together.

  1. Week 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the first twelve videos and agree the site: menu, both locations, collection ordering, WhatsApp and tracking. We write the shot list for the crew.

  2. Week 2
    First filming day

    A licensed UAE crew films in your shop to our shot list and brief: the press, the stack, the pops, your team and your customers, in one service.

  3. Weeks 2 to 4
    Site live, posting on a plan

    We edit, caption and post on set days on Instagram and TikTok. The site goes live with the first videos on it, and every post ends with the direct way to order.

  4. Month 2
    More of what works

    We look at what brought orders in and film more of it. Each new video links to the site and the WhatsApp route.

  5. Month 3
    Review together

    We report what the videos reached, what came through the site and WhatsApp, and what to do next.

Every month you get: a licensed UAE crew filming in your shop to our shot list, edited short videos, posting on a plan, the site looked after, and a simple monthly report. You keep every video.
