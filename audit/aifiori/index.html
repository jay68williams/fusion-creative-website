01 · The short version

You are doing the work. The reach is not following.

Ai Fiori posts constantly: Sunday lunch, Student Tuesdays, wood-fired pizza, Christmas bookings. You are even posting reels, not just graphics. The problem is that almost none of it travels. Your recent posts sit between one and nineteen likes against 1,264 followers, and the videos are built like adverts, with text laid over the top, rather than something a person actually wants to watch. The ingredients for strong video are already in the building. They are just not being filmed the way that gets seen.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A wood-fired oven and authentic Neapolitan pizza, the single most filmable thing a pizzeria owns.
  • Fresh pasta and a full Italian menu, with a Sunday lunch you already push hard.
  • A genuinely handsome room: warm lighting, the bar, the Ai Fiori sign, a red Jesmond shopfront that lights up at dusk.
  • A ready-made weekly hook in Student Tuesdays, in the middle of one of the most student-heavy parts of Newcastle.
  • Christmas bookings already live, the busiest booking window of the year to put video behind.

You do not need a new menu, a new room or a new website. You need short video of the oven, the pasta, the team and your guests, with a hook at the front and a reason to book at the end, posted on a plan. That is the gap between a feed that gets nineteen likes and one that fills tables.

03 · Your website

A smart-looking site, with a few things quietly holding it back.

The site looks the part and has your menus, booking and contact details. These are the changes we would make so it works harder for the video and for the people searching for you.

What we sawWhy it mattersFix
No tracking at allWe could not find a Meta pixel, Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager anywhere on the site. Right now you cannot see who visits, and you cannot show your videos again to people who looked at your menu.We add a Meta pixel and analytics, so every person who watches a video or views the menu can be shown your next one.
Half the site says "coming soon"Delivery and collection, the breakfast menu and the panuozzi and stuffed croissant menu are all still marked coming soon. A visitor ready to order hits a dead end.Finish or hide them. A page that promises and does not deliver costs you the booking.
Some photos are stock imagesA few of the pictures on the site are stock photos, going by their Unsplash file names, including people who have nothing to do with the restaurant.Replace them with stills of your own oven, pasta, room and team from our filming days. Nobody else can use those.
No TikTokWe found Instagram and Facebook only.Post the same short videos to TikTok, where a new Jesmond restaurant video can reach thousands of local students who are not on Instagram.

Ai Fiori is clearly trying. You post often and you do make video. The pattern in your recent posts tells us why it is not landing:

  • The videos are built like adverts. Big text laid over the clip, a menu graphic, a "book now". People scroll past an advert. They stop for a person, a joke or a reveal.
  • No hook in the first second. Your reels open on a logo or a line of text. The videos that get seen open on the pizza coming out of the fire, or on someone about to do something.
  • Your best raw material is barely used. A wood-fired oven, fresh pasta being pulled, Student Tuesdays in the student heart of Newcastle. That is a month of content, filmed properly, not a single graphic.

What this means. The followers are already there. They are just not being given anything worth watching or sharing. Short video with a hook at the front, a real person in it and a reason to come, made every month and posted on a plan, is how you turn 1,264 quiet followers into booked tables.

Pull the pizza

Table for eight

"When your group of eight tries to book on a Friday."
