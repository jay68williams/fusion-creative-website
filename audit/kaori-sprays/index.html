01 The website

Fail

Multipacks advertised to £89.99 are charging £2.00

Three hang up products show pack prices on the page. All three add to the basket at £2.00. Two are live and buyable right now, so anyone who has found these pages could have bought a hundred hang ups for the price of one.

Advertised on the page
£89.99
Charged at the basket
£2.00
Dark Cherry Hang Ups · buyable now£2.00
Black Hang Ups · buyable now£2.00
Kaori Vanilla Hang ups · sold out£2.00
Cause: the pack sizes were entered as Shopify option names and the prices as option values, so only one variant was ever created, at 200 pence. The radio buttons render but there is nothing behind them to change the price. Each product returns a variant count of 1, titled “£17.99 / £69.99 / £89.99”. Check it yourself in fifteen seconds: open Dark Cherry Hang Ups, add it to your basket and read the total. It stays £2.00 whichever pack you press. To see the cause rather than the symptom, open the same product's data and look at variants: there is only one, priced 200.
Fail

The bundle page promises delivery checkout does not offer

Build Your Bundle, where your homepage button sends people, says free next day delivery is applied automatically at checkout. We built a real bundle and asked your store for its actual rates.

Actually offeredStandard £0.00, or Fast £5.00

People who chose you for that promise find out at the final step. That is the most expensive place to lose someone, and it produces refund requests rather than just a lost order.

Advisory

A third of the range is unavailable

Five of fifteen products are sold out: SAV Hang Ups, Kaori Vanilla Hang ups, Kaori Black, Kaori Kreed and Kaori Guilt. With a range this focused that is a large hole, and sold out lines are still sitting high on the page rather than being moved down.

It matters more than usual now, because the shows finish in November and the website has to carry the winter.

Advisory

Your live theme is still named “DEV Working”

The published theme is T updates master-lite-2-1 (DEV Working). It works, so this is not urgent. But it means there is no safe copy to work on, so every change is made live in front of customers. That is how the pricing fault above survived unnoticed.

02 The ads

Fail

There is no website analytics running at all

You cannot see how many people visit, what they look at, where they leave, or what a visit is worth. You are the one paying for traffic with no way to judge it. This has to exist before any money goes into advertising, or you will be guessing about your own results.

Pass

Your Meta pixel is clean, and that is rarer than you would think

One pixel, correctly installed, no duplicates. Plenty of shops we look at are running two or three and counting every sale more than once. Yours is right, so when the analytics gap above is closed you will have a solid base to advertise from.

Advisory

Your ads land on a page with sold out products near the top

You have partnership creative most brands your size could not get near. Sending that traffic to a homepage where several of the first products are unavailable wastes the hardest part of the job, which is earning the click.

03 The email

Pass

Klaviyo is properly installed

Account live, reviews module loaded. The foundation is there and working.

Advisory

The shows are not feeding the list, and that is the winter answer

You said shows bring £5,000 to £10,000 a month from March to November. If those customers are not captured onto a list at the stall, then every winter you begin again from nothing with people who have already bought from you, liked it, and would buy again.

Capturing at the stall costs nothing and turns a seasonal business into a year round one. After the pricing fault, this is the highest value item on the report.

04 The content

Advisory

111 Google reviews, and none of them on your product pages

Your site mentions over a hundred five star reviews, but someone deciding on a product page cannot see them at the moment they are deciding. Proof belongs where the doubt is, not on the About page.

You already pay for the tool that shows them. This is a switch, not a project.

Summary  ·  3 fail  ·  5 advisory  ·  2 pass

We have done this before, for a British candle company.

The Northumberland Candle Company came to us at £6,650 a month. Shopify and Klaviyo, same as you, and similar to where you are online.

We rebuilt the store to convert first, then ran the ads and the email as one system. Sixty days later they were at £112,230 a month. August was 3,650 orders against 248, at a blended 7.8x return on ad spend. Their real Shopify figures.

We mention it because the order of work is the whole point. Fixing a £2.00 price on an £89.99 product is worth more to you today than any advert we could write.

What we would do first: the pricing fault today, because it is live. Then analytics, so you can see what is happening. Then the delivery promise and the sold out lines. None of that needs an advertising budget.

After that the real conversation is winter, and whether the show customers you already have can carry December through February.
