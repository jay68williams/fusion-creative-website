01 · The short version

A large following, and a website that is not yet working for it.

Extreme Equestrian Events runs from Qidfa, Fujairah. You offer horse riding, hiking with horses, swimming with horses, farm and heritage tours, private events and team building, and the farm itself is a film set. The audience is already there: over 25,000 followers on Instagram. What is missing is what those people see next: a website whose cookie banner and footer are in Swedish, and whose pages show no amounts.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Over 25,000 followers who have already chosen to follow you.
  • A working farm in Qidfa with horses, oryx, gazelles and fruit trees. Your website says the area is often 10 to 15 degrees cooler than Dubai.
  • Services with clear names, including horse riding, hiking with horses, swimming with horses, team building and private events.
  • A founder with a story to tell. Your website says the business was founded by Angie Antonick.

You do not need a new name or a new farm. You need a steady flow of short video of the horses, the trails and the people on them, posted on a plan, and a website that shows the offer, the amounts and one step to enquire or book.

08 · Your bookings, direct

Your bookings, taken on your own site.

On your website, a guest reaches you by WhatsApp, phone, the contact form or email. The pages we checked show no amount before that step.

4

0

20% to 30%
typical commission on GetYourGuide and Viator (CaptainBook, 9 April 2026)

Booking platforms such as GetYourGuide and Viator take a commission on each booking they send. CaptainBook (9 April 2026) puts both at typically 20% to 30%, and Arival reported in June 2025 that some GetYourGuide operators had been moved from 20% to 30%. One of your tours is listed on GetYourGuide under Extreme Equestrian Events FZE, so some bookings may already run through that platform.

A booking taken on your own site or WhatsApp stays with you. A site that shows the amount for each service, the dates you have, and a booking or enquiry step that ends in WhatsApp is what would change the way guests reach you. We would build it around the way your guests already talk to you.

09 · The plan

A plan built around the season and the farm.

  1. Week 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the twelve videos, add English to the website and set up the Meta pixel.

  2. The shoot
    One shoot day at the farm

    A licensed UAE crew would film the horses, the trails and a full day at the farm, to our shot list.

  3. After the shoot
    Edit, caption and deliver

    We edit and caption the batch of short videos and post them on a plan to Instagram, each one ending with a reason to visit or book. You keep every video.

  4. Handover
    Results review

    We report what the videos reached and what came in, hand over the files, and set out what we would do next.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos from one shoot day at the farm, filmed by a licensed UAE crew to our shot list, edited and captioned, and posted on a plan, with a results review at the end. You keep every video.
