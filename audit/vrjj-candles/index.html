For Valentina

Six years of product, and a shop that is quietly getting in its way.

We read vrjjcandle.co.uk the way a customer does, then measured it the way an engineer does. Almost nothing below is opinion. You can re-check every figure yourself in about two minutes.

Every gift bundle you sell
£0

Not the price. The number of delivery options your £100 bundles return at checkout. All twenty four of them cannot be bought.

We tested it against a control on your own store. A £17.95 candle returns two delivery options, Standard at £5.95 and Express at £9.99. A £100 bundle returns none, so the order cannot complete. Every bundle is set to require shipping, so Shopify is looking for a rate and finding nothing. It reads like a shipping profile that was never finished.

I

The website

Twenty four products  ·  no photograph

Your gifting range is invisible as well as unbuyable

Birthday, Wedding, New Baby, Bridesmaid, Teacher, Housewarming, Valentine's. Every one is something people search for by occasion. Every one is priced at £100. Every one has an empty image.

Across the whole store, 55 products have no image at all, from 316 live.

Mobile

The phone homepage opens on almost nothing

Your hero image is 300 pixels wide and is being stretched across the whole screen. On a phone that is a soft grey blur: no product, no words, nothing to tap. The larger versions of that exact file already sit on Shopify's servers. The theme simply never asks for them.

Serving IMG_8397_300x.jpg, a natural 300 by 263, into a space measured at 659 by 799.
The last screen before payment

Your cart page still carries the theme's demo products

Four blocks reading “Example product $29”, in US dollars, on a British store. They sit on the page a customer sees immediately before paying, which is the worst possible place to look unfinished.

Average order value

Nothing in the basket is working to raise the order

No delivery threshold anywhere. No nudge to add one more thing. No related products, no reassurance at the moment of payment.

Paying is easy, to be fair to you. Klarna, PayPal, Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay are all in place. Nothing is persuading anyone to add a second candle first.

The category

Nobody is selling the gift

We went through what the rest of your category advertises. The angles repeat: mix and match pricing, stock up and save, scent throw claims, review counts, subscription boxes.

Not one is selling a candle as a gift for a particular person or occasion. You already have twenty four occasion bundles built. They are simply unphotographed, unshippable and unadvertised.

III

The email

Already paid for

Klaviyo is installed and barely switched on

A ten percent welcome offer is the only thing capturing anyone, and it appears on every page including the basket, where it interrupts people who had already decided to buy.

There is no back in stock, which matters when a meaningful part of the range is sold out at any time. For something people buy again like candles and refills, email should be a large share of the month rather than an afterthought.

IV

The content

Production

The output stopped, and the shop cannot use what exists

TikTok has not been posted to since March. More to the point, 55 products have no photograph, so even the work you have already done is not reaching the place where people decide.

Hand poured in Manchester is a real story, and almost none of the store tells it.

We have done this once already, for a British candle company.

The Northumberland Candle Company came to us at £6,650 a month. Shopify and Klaviyo, same as you. Same category, roughly the same size.

We rebuilt the store to convert first, then ran the ads and the email as one system. Sixty days later they were at £112,230 a month. August was 3,650 orders against 248, at a blended 7.8x return on ad spend. Their real Shopify figures.

We mention it because the order matters more than the tactics. Sending paid traffic at a store where the £100 bundles cannot check out would only be an expensive way to prove the point.

So we would not start with a retainer. We would start with the shipping profile, the basket, the hero images and the tracking, none of which need an advertising budget. Then photograph the twenty four bundles before Christmas, because gifting is the entire reason they exist.
