What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The cause is genuinely worth shouting about

Making sure local children are fed, safe and busy through the school holidays is the kind of thing a whole community will get behind once they know it exists. You are not trying to manufacture a reason to care. It is already there, which is the hard part most brands never have.

✓ The founder story is your best content

Cold Tea Stories came from a simple belief that every child deserves to feel safe, valued, heard and nourished. That personal "why", in your own words and on camera, is more powerful than any polished advert. People back people and stories, and you have a real one.

✓ The emergency services days are gold

Themed learning days with the police, fire and ambulance teams are exactly the kind of thing that stops a parent mid-scroll. Bright, high-energy, full of kids having a brilliant time. That is content people watch, share and tag their friends in.

✓ You are already showing up online

An active Instagram and Facebook mean the habit of posting is already there, which trips up a lot of small organisations. The issue is not effort or willingness. It is format, and format is the easy thing to fix.

✓ Every session is a ready-made story

The meals coming out, the crafts, the games, the smiles at pick-up, a parent thank-you. You are surrounded by moving, emotive moments every single session. Right now most of them happen and disappear. Captured properly, they are weeks of content.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the cause, the story and the sessions. The job is to turn that into local awareness: get in front of the families who need you, the funders and partners who could support you, and the volunteers who would help, and make Cold Tea Stories a name people in the area recognise. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Put Dylan and the Story on Camera

The single most powerful thing you could post is you, to camera, explaining why you started Cold Tea Stories and who it is for. No script needed, just honest and from the heart. People do not rally behind a logo, they rally behind a person with a mission they believe in.

This one piece of content does more for trust, sign-ups and support than a month of listing what you offer.

2

Turn Each Session Into Short Video, Not Just Photos

A photo update tells the people who already follow you what happened. A short, well-cut video of the same session can reach hundreds of local people who have never heard of you. The activities, the meals, the emergency services days, all filmed and edited to travel, with a strong first few seconds so people stop scrolling.

Same day, same effort on your side. The difference is the format, and the format is what earns the reach.

3

Make Being "Known Locally" the Whole Strategy

You told us success looks like being known in your community, so everything should point there. Tag your town and local area, use local hashtags, get shared into local parent and community groups, and lean into content only a genuinely local project can make. That is how the right families find you before the next holidays.

Local reach also opens the doors that keep a CIC running: schools, councillors, local press and businesses who want to be seen supporting something good on their doorstep.

4

Use Content to Attract Funding, Partners and Volunteers

For a CIC, visibility is not vanity, it is survival. A funder deciding where to put a grant, a business choosing a cause to sponsor, a parent thinking about volunteering, they all look you up first. Warm, credible video that shows the impact you have makes that decision easy, and makes you look established even though you are young.

The same content that fills your sessions also builds the case for the support that keeps them going.

5

Take the Time Pressure Off You

Your honest blocker is time, and you are right, running the club and creating content is two full-time jobs. The answer is not for you to do more. It is to capture footage while you focus on the children, and have the filming, editing, captions and posting handled for you.

Done properly, a single session can produce a couple of weeks of content, so the presence keeps building even in the weeks you are heads-down.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film Around Your Next Session

These are formats working right now on Reels and TikTok, built around what you already do: the sessions, the story, the partners and the impact. Each one could be filmed on a phone around a single holiday club day.

Idea 01
"Why I started Cold Tea Stories"
FounderStoryTrust

Dylan to camera, honest and unscripted, telling the story of why this exists and who it is for. Strong opening line ("Every child deserves to feel safe, valued and fed. That is why I started this."). Warm background from a real session. Under 40 seconds.

Why it works: People support people and missions, not logos. A genuine founder story is the fastest way to earn trust, sign-ups and local support.
Idea 02
"A day at the holiday club"
SessionBehind the scenesFeelgood

A fast, warm montage of one session: breakfast going out, the crafts, the games, the laughing, the tidy-up, the happy pick-up. Trending audio, a simple caption ("This is what a day at Cold Tea Stories looks like"). Built to be shared into local parent groups.

Why it works: It shows a parent exactly what their child would experience, and it is the kind of feelgood content people tag their friends in.
Idea 03
"The day the fire brigade came in"
PartnersHigh energyWow moment

Your emergency services days, captured properly: the fire engine, the kids' faces, the police and ambulance crews, the excitement. A punchy hook ("We surprised the kids with a real fire engine today"). This is your most shareable, scroll-stopping content.

Why it works: Big, joyful, unexpected moments travel furthest, and they show off the real partnerships that set you apart from an ordinary holiday club.
Idea 04
"What your support actually pays for"
ImpactFundersCommunity

A short, sincere piece showing the tangible difference: a proper meal, a safe place, a child who would otherwise be at a loose end. Aimed at the funders, businesses and volunteers who could get behind you. End with a clear, gentle way to help.

Why it works: It turns your impact into something a supporter can see and feel, which is what unlocks grants, sponsorship and volunteers for a young CIC.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into sign-ups, support and partnerships.

Get Found in Local Search

When a parent searches for holiday clubs or childcare in your area, or a funder looks you up, you want to be there, clear and credible. A simple Google Business Profile and a tidy, findable website do quiet, heavy lifting.

  • Set up and optimise a Google Business Profile for your town and area
  • Make session dates, sign-up and contact details easy to find
  • Gather short parent testimonials and reviews as social proof
  • Turn your best moments and reviews into shareable content

Content That Also Wins Funding and Partners

The same content engine that fills sessions also builds the case for the support that keeps a CIC alive. Warm, credible video makes you look established and makes a "yes" easier for the people who could back you.

  • Impact-led clips you can send straight to funders and sponsors
  • Partner shout-outs that make local businesses proud to be involved
  • A clear volunteer story so the right people can put their hand up

Simple, Low-Cost Local Reach

Because your goal is local, you do not need a big budget. A small, tightly targeted boost around your town can put your best session and story videos in front of local parents right before the holidays, when they are actually looking.

  • Target families in your immediate area and postcodes
  • Use your strongest founder and session videos as the content
  • Time it around school holidays when demand peaks
  • Keep it lean and accountable, only what a young CIC can afford

Why it fits Cold Tea Stories: a little local visibility at the right moment fills more places and puts you in front of the people who could fund and support the work.
