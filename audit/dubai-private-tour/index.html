You post almost every day, which is the hard part. The pattern in your recent posts:

07 · Your bookings, direct

Turn a post into a booking, not a browse.

You already take bookings the right way, direct by enquiry form and WhatsApp. The issue is the path from a post to that booking, and how dated the form feels once someone arrives.

you have taken bookings direct, so the funnel just needs to match the video

When a traveller books a Dubai tour through an online marketplace instead of with you, the operator typically pays 20% to 30% of the price in commission, according to 2026 industry rate guides. Your own bookings come in by enquiry form and WhatsApp, which keeps them yours. The gap is the path to them: a post sends someone to a site that lists a price, then asks them to fill a form with a nationality dropdown and a confirm-email field before anyone replies. In the UAE, 85% of residents say they would rather deal with a business over WhatsApp (Zbooni with YouGov, 2024). Putting a clean enquiry and a WhatsApp button next to every tour, so anyone sent there from a reel can book in one message, keeps every booking direct and loses none to a slow form.
