What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The concept is genuinely distinctive

Turkish, Indian and Mediterranean under one roof is not the usual thing, and it gives you a menu with real range: smoky grills, aromatic curries, fresh mezze. That variety is a gift for content, because there is always something new and colourful to film. Most restaurants sound the same. Tava does not.

✓ The location sells itself

A restaurant on Front Street in Tynemouth, minutes from the beach and the priory, is exactly the kind of place people love to be seen at and love to tag. The seafront setting is free marketing every single day, and right now it is barely being shown off where diners actually decide where to go.

✓ The food is made for the camera

Grills coming off the flame, mezze spreads, a tandoor, dishes that arrive sizzling. This is the most scroll-stopping content there is, and you produce it every service without even trying. The ingredients for viral food video are already sitting in your kitchen.

✓ The credibility is real

A 5 star food hygiene rating, online booking, private room hire and a proper Sunday offering. These are the trust signals that reassure a first-time diner, and they mean the experience backs up whatever the content promises. You are not selling smoke, the substance is there.

✓ The posting habit exists

You are already on Instagram and Facebook and putting content out. The discipline of showing up is the hardest part for most restaurants, and you have it. The issue is not effort, it is format, and format is the easy thing to fix.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the food, the setting and the credibility. The job is to turn that into reach and bookings: get in front of people across Tynemouth, Whitley Bay and Newcastle deciding where to eat, and make Tava the place they already want to try before they open the door. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Film the Food in Motion, Not Just on a Plate

A photo of a finished dish is nice. A close-up of a grill hitting the flame, a curry being poured, a mezze board coming together, cheese pulling apart, that is what stops the scroll. Short-form food video that shows the cooking and the sizzle is the single biggest lever you have, because it makes people hungry in a way a static photo never will.

Every service is producing this content already. The only thing missing is someone filming it properly and cutting it to travel.

2

Own TikTok and Reels for Reach

Food is one of the most viral categories there is, and it lives on TikTok and Instagram Reels. Right now the presence leans on photos, which fill the feed but rarely reach anyone new. Proper short-form video, with strong hooks in the first second, is what gets pushed to people who have never heard of you. That is where new diners come from.

A restaurant active on Instagram almost always has an easy win waiting on TikTok. It is the same content, cut for a platform built to hand your food to strangers nearby.

3

Sell the Tynemouth Experience, Not Just the Menu

People do not only choose a restaurant, they choose a night out. The seafront setting, a table by the window, the walk along Front Street, the priory and the beach on the doorstep. Lean into where you are, and Tava stops being "a restaurant" and becomes "the place to eat when you are on the coast".

This is content only a genuinely local, seafront restaurant can make, and it is exactly how you pull in the Newcastle and Whitley Bay crowd looking for somewhere to go.

4

Turn Reviews and Regulars Into Proof

A happy table reacting to a mezze spread, a quick vox pop from a regular, a five-star review on screen over a shot of the dish they loved. Social proof is the most persuasive content a restaurant can post, because it answers the only question a new diner has: is this actually as good as it looks. Show real people enjoying it and the answer is obvious.

Proof travels, and it speaks straight to the person deciding where to book this weekend.

5

Fill the Quiet Nights and the Bookable Extras

You have levers most places would love: a Sunday roast, private room hire, cafe and bar, gift vouchers, festive feasts. Each one is a content moment and a booking driver. Short video around "Sunday roast at Tava", "book the room for your party", "gift a meal on the seafront", pointed at the right local audience, turns your quieter slots and your extras into filled tables.

Pair that content with a small, tightly targeted local ad budget and you put your best food in front of people in the area who are actually deciding where to eat.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in a Single Service

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the food, the flame, the setting and the people. Each one could be filmed on a phone in one evening at Tava.

Idea 01
"This mixed grill lands in 30 seconds"
FoodFlameASMR

A fast, close-up build of a signature grill or mezze board: the flame, the sizzle, the plating, the final table drop. Strong hook on frame one ("the best grill in Tynemouth?"). Real cooking sounds, trending audio underneath. Under 25 seconds of pure hunger.

Why it works: Food in motion is the most shared content there is, and it makes someone book a table before the video even ends.
Idea 02
"Where to eat on Tynemouth seafront"
LocalExperienceDiscovery

A quick tour of the night out: the walk down Front Street, the room, a table by the window, a few standout dishes arriving. Positioned as a local recommendation, not an advert. Tag the location so it reaches people searching Tynemouth and Whitley Bay.

Why it works: It reaches people deciding where to go on the coast and makes Tava the obvious choice, content only a seafront restaurant can make.
Idea 03
"Three cuisines, one table"
ConceptMenuVariety

Show off the thing that makes Tava different: a Turkish grill, an Indian curry and a Mediterranean mezze all hitting one table. Fast cuts between the three, big colour, a hook like "the only place in Tynemouth doing all three". Built to make people curious enough to try it.

Why it works: Your concept is your edge. Showing the range in one video makes Tava memorable and impossible to lump in with everywhere else.
Idea 04
"Sunday roast, but on the coast"
SundayBooking driverSeasonal

A warm, appetising build of the Sunday roast, or the festive feast in season, ending on a full happy table and a clear "book your table" line. A simple, repeatable format you can refilm for roasts, party bookings, private hire and Christmas.

Why it works: It points content straight at your most bookable slots, turning quieter days and your extras into filled tables.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into booked tables.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone decides to eat out, they search and they read reviews before they ever book. Your Google profile and reviews are doing quiet, heavy lifting, and they deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised for "restaurants Tynemouth" and "Turkish restaurant Tynemouth"
  • Funnel every happy table into a fresh Google review before they leave
  • Add fresh food photos and menu updates regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Menu, Bookings and Ordering Working Together

The content drives attention, but the site has to turn it into a table. Make booking effortless from the moment someone lands, and surface the things that sell: the grills, the Sunday roast, private hire and vouchers.

  • Booking front and centre on every page and in every bio link
  • Menus that look as good as the food does
  • Clear paths to private hire, gift vouchers and festive bookings

Targeted Local Ads to Nearby Diners

Because a full table is high value, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around Tynemouth, Whitley Bay and Newcastle puts your best food video in front of people deciding where to eat this weekend.

  • Target diners in your core postcodes and nearby towns
  • Use your strongest grill and seafront reels as the ads
  • Drive a steady flow of bookings straight to your website
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Tava: A handful of extra covers a night covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a restaurant can do.
