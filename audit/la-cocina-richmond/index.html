What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The concept is genuinely distinctive

Yorkshire and Mediterranean dining under one roof, tapas, a proper cocktail bar and a bottomless brunch, all in the middle of Richmond. That is a clear, confident idea that sets you apart from the usual pub and high-street options. Most places blur into one. You do not, and a distinctive concept is gold for content.

✓ The food is the marketing

Tapas, small plates, cocktails and a Mediterranean spread are some of the most watchable things you can film. Colour, steam, the pour, the share. You already make it every service. Right now it is being plated and eaten, when a fraction of it filmed properly could be doing your marketing for you.

✓ You already post consistently

An active Instagram and Facebook shows the habit and the discipline of showing up are there, which is the hardest part for most independents. The issue is not effort, it is format. Still photos hold, video travels, and format is the easy thing to fix.

✓ The offers are built to be shared

A bottomless brunch, two-for-one cocktails and a tapas night are exactly the sort of thing groups screenshot, tag their mates in and plan nights around. These offers are ready-made content hooks, they just need to be shown off as video where the sharing actually happens.

✓ Richmond is a gift for content

A gorgeous market town with a steady flow of visitors and a proud local crowd. Leaning into the place, date night in Richmond, a weekend spot, a slice of the Mediterranean in North Yorkshire, pulls in both the locals and the people looking for where to eat when they visit.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the concept, the food and the room. The job is to turn that into reach and covers: get in front of people in Richmond and the surrounding towns who have not been in yet, and become the name they think of for a nice meal, a brunch or a night out. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Film the Food Properly as Short-Form Video

Your best asset is the food, and it is currently sitting still in photos. The tapas landing on the table, the cocktail being poured, the cheese pull, the sizzle out of the kitchen, filmed close and cut fast to trending audio, is the single most reliable way for a restaurant to get reach right now. One good service can produce a fortnight of content.

Hungry, watchable, made-in-the-room video is what makes someone stop scrolling and think, I need to book that.

2

Launch TikTok and Scale It Alongside Reels

You have Instagram and Facebook, but there is no TikTok, and for food that is the biggest missed channel there is. TikTok is where a single plate of patatas bravas can reach hundreds of thousands of the right local people overnight. The same videos we make for Reels get posted there too, so it is reach for very little extra work.

A restaurant your size, active on Instagram, almost always has a ready audience waiting on TikTok. It just needs to be there and posting.

3

Turn the Brunch and Cocktail Nights Into a Content Engine

The bottomless brunch and the two-for-one cocktail nights are your most shareable moments, the group laughing, the endless pours, the tapas arriving. Filmed properly and posted with a clear call to book, they stop being a one-off and start filling those sittings week after week.

These offers are exactly what people plan nights around, so they should be the content working hardest for you.

4

Sell Richmond and the Experience, Not Just the Menu

"Date night in Richmond." "A slice of the Mediterranean in North Yorkshire." Content about the town, the room, the atmosphere and the occasion pulls in both the locals and the visitors looking for where to eat. It positions La Cocina as the spot for the night out, not just another place that serves food.

This is content only a genuinely local, distinctive venue can make, and it is exactly where you out-market the chains.

5

Put the People and the Story on Camera

People book places run by people they warm to. The chef plating a signature dish, the team behind the bar, the story of why Yorkshire and the Mediterranean sit together on one menu. Faces and personality build the familiarity that turns a scroll into a booking, and it is the content that makes an independent feel like somewhere you already know.

The human side of the room is a story in itself, and right now it is barely being told.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in a Single Service

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the food, the offers, the room and the people. Each one could be filmed on a phone across one busy service.

Idea 01
"POV: your table at La Cocina"
FoodTapasASMR

A fast, close-up run through a tapas spread landing on the table: the sizzle, the pour, the dips, the steam, the share. Strong hook on frame one ("this is dinner in Richmond tonight"). Trending audio. Under 20 seconds. Pure, hungry food content.

Why it works: Close-up, sound-on food is the most reliable reach format there is, and it makes people book before the video even ends.
Idea 02
"Bottomless brunch, filmed"
OfferGroupBook now

The brunch in full swing: the pours, the tapas arriving, the group laughing, the buzz of the room. Lead with the hook ("your Saturday sorted"). End on a clear line to book. Built to be sent to the group chat.

Why it works: It sells the exact experience people plan a day around, and a clear call to book turns the views into sittings.
Idea 03
"Where to eat in Richmond"
LocalDate nightDiscovery

A short, warm walk through the room and the town: the atmosphere, the cocktails, a couple of standout plates, why it feels like a slice of the Mediterranean in North Yorkshire. Tag the location. Built to be saved by anyone searching for where to eat.

Why it works: It reaches locals and visitors deciding where to go and positions La Cocina as the answer, content only a genuinely local venue can make.
Idea 04
"Why Yorkshire meets the Mediterranean"
StoryChefBehind the scenes

The chef or the team to camera, plating a signature dish, telling the story of the concept in a sentence or two. Honest, warm, a little behind the scenes of the kitchen and the bar. Strong hook ("we put Yorkshire and Spain on the same plate, here is why").

Why it works: People book places run by people they warm to. A face and a story build trust fast and make the concept impossible to forget.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into booked tables.

Link Your Socials and Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone decides where to eat, they search, they read reviews and they check the socials before they book. Right now the website does not link out to Instagram or Facebook, and your Google profile is doing quiet, heavy lifting. Both deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Add clear Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links to the website
  • Keep the Google profile optimised for "restaurant Richmond" and "bottomless brunch Richmond"
  • Funnel every happy table into a fresh Google review
  • Add fresh food photos and offers to the profile regularly

Fill the Quieter Sittings With Offers-Led Content

Every restaurant has quieter nights and quieter tables. Content built around the brunch, the cocktail nights and midweek tapas, posted with a clear call to book, is the cheapest way to smooth those out and get bums on seats when you want them.

  • Short, punchy reels tied to a specific night or offer
  • A clear, repeatable "book now" call to action on every one
  • Stories and posts that make the quiet nights feel like the place to be

Targeted Local Ads to Fill Tables

Because a booked table is high value, a small, tightly targeted ad budget around Richmond and the surrounding towns pays back fast. Put your best food and brunch content in front of exactly the people who eat out nearby.

  • Target diners in Richmond and the surrounding North Yorkshire towns
  • Use your strongest food and brunch reels as the ads
  • Drive bookings and enquiries straight to your website
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits La Cocina: A handful of extra covers a night covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things an independent restaurant can do.
