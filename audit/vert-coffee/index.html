01 · The short version

A strong brand on a small account, with nowhere to sell.

Vert has more going on than most cafes its size: a clear green brand, signature drinks from the Dirty Espresso to the Iced Foam Rutab, a frozen Greek yogurt launch, a Mr Froggie loyalty reward, and your own MagSafe wallets for sale. You post regularly, you are clearly trading, and four of your twelve recent posts are reels. What is missing is more short video of the coffee, and a place to actually sell the rest, because on 22 posts you have no website and no online store, and ordering runs through Talabat and the Drivu drive-thru.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A clear, confident brand. The green, the wordmark and the kiosk are instantly recognisable. Most cafes never get this far.
  • Signature drinks worth filming: the Dirty Espresso, the Iced Foam Rutab date coffee and a frozen Greek yogurt line with 20g of protein, all in your own words.
  • A loyalty idea already running. Your Mr Froggie reward and stamp card give people a reason to come back.
  • Your own retail products. MagSafe wallets, posted as "good things travel with you", slim, stylish and on sale.
  • You are trying to sell, which is the right instinct. The only thing missing is somewhere for that selling to land.

You do not need a new brand or a bigger menu. The coffee, the wallets and the loyalty idea are already here. More short video of the drinks being made, posted on a plan, and a simple site that sells the rest, is how a 22-post account in Ajman gets found and turns interest into sales.

07 · The plan

A plan built around your drinks and your retail.

  1. Week 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the twelve videos. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and plan a simple way for every post to end in a visit or a direct sale.

  2. Week 2
    The shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list: the Rutab pour, the frozen yogurt swirl, the wallets and the kiosk, baristas and guests included.

  3. After the shoot
    Edit, caption and post

    We edit, caption and post the videos to Instagram and TikTok. Each one ends with the drink and how to visit or buy direct.

  4. Handover
    Review together

    We report what the videos reached and what they brought in, then hand over every video for you to keep and reuse.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos filmed at your kiosk by a licensed UAE crew to our shot list, edited and captioned, posted to launch them. You keep every video.
