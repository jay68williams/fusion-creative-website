I

What we are going to do

Four things. Measured in enquiries, showrounds booked and dates filled, not likes.

One

Feed the ads you already run

Your ads are live, which puts you ahead of most. The risk is stale creative. A capture day a month keeps fresh film in the ads instead of the same photos for a year.

Two

Own the channel you are missing

You have no TikTok, and it is where couples under 35 now search for venues. We start it from zero and post the Grange where they look.

Three

Turn one wedding into a month

You already have a wedding videos page, so you value film. We make it a system: a third of posts as reels, not one hero video a year.

Four

Give couples a content creator

We film weddings ourselves. Offered as a Horton Grange perk, every wedding we film for a couple becomes your marketing too.

II

How we are going to do it

Week one

Count everything

You already have a pixel and ads, so week one is sharpening the funnel, not building it: every showround tracked to a source, and one clear ask kept across the site and socials.

Month one

First capture day

The house, the gardens and the gazebo, and one full wedding when a couple says yes. Cut into reels within the week and dropped into the ads you already run.

Every month

Capture, post, report

A third of posts as reels, filmed not stock. A one-page report: enquiries, showrounds booked, dates confirmed.

Month two on

The ad

The wedding film, aimed at engaged couples within an hour. Small spend, judged only on showrounds booked.
