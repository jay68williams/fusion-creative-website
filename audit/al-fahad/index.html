What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The concept is genuinely different

Fusion dining that brings Italian, Indian and Chinese under one roof, with signatures like Lamb Mandi, the Al Fahad Special Parmo and the Special Pizza, is a real hook. Most restaurants blend into the crowd. You do not, and a menu that surprises people is gold for content.

✓ The reviews back it up

Al Fahad is a genuine family favourite with warm, consistent feedback on the food, the freshness and the service. That is proof most restaurants would love to have, and right now it is barely being shown off where new customers actually look, on Instagram and TikTok.

✓ You are already making video

You are posting reels on Instagram, not just static photos. That is the hardest habit to start, and you have started it. The raw instinct is right, it just needs shaping into content built to travel and pushing out on the platforms where reach is easiest.

✓ The room and the plates do the selling for you

A freshly refurbished space, a private function room and dishes that look incredible (the Mandi, the cheese pull on a Parmo, a warm brownie) are exactly what food video is built on. You already have the two things money cannot fake, great-looking food and a room that films well.

✓ Family and group appeal

Alcohol-free, family-friendly, great for big tables and celebrations, with a function space for up to 32. That is a clear audience to speak to, and social is the perfect place to reach the families and friend groups looking for exactly that in Newcastle.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the food, the concept and the reviews. The job is to turn that into reach and covers: get Al Fahad in front of hungry people across Newcastle and make it the place they screenshot, save and book. These are the six areas we would focus on to move you toward a room that is packed every night.

1

Turn the Signature Dishes Into Scroll-Stoppers

Your menu is a gift for short-form. The Lamb Mandi being revealed at the table, the cheese pull on the Special Parmo, a pizza sliced on camera, the warm brownie with ice cream melting. Filmed close, well lit, with a strong first frame and the right sound, these are the clips that stop the scroll and make people hungry.

Food that looks this good should be doing the marketing. Right now the dishes are the best asset you have and they are barely on camera.

2

Scale What You Already Post Onto TikTok

You are already making reels on Instagram, which is the hard part. The quickest win is running that same content properly on TikTok, where a brand new restaurant account can still reach thousands of local people fast. We could not confirm an active TikTok for you, so this is close to an open goal.

One well-made food clip can live on Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook at once. Same filming day, three times the reach.

3

Sell the Fusion Story, Not Just the Food

"One kitchen, three cuisines." A Parmo meets a curry, a pizza meets the spice box. Your whole idea is flavours from around the world in one place, and that concept is far more interesting than a plain plate-of-food photo. Lean into the surprise and people share it.

This is content only Al Fahad can make, and it is exactly the angle that gets a video saved and sent to a friend with "we have to go here".

4

Show the Room and the Experience

A freshly refurbished space, a table set for a celebration, the buzz of a busy Saturday, the function room dressed for a party. People do not just choose food, they choose a night out. Showing the atmosphere sells the whole experience, not just a dish.

It also does quiet work for your bigger bookings, group meals, birthdays and private hire, which are some of your most valuable covers.

5

Win Local Newcastle Discovery

"Best fusion restaurant in Newcastle." "Where to eat in Fenham." "Newcastle Parmo, but make it fusion." Local, searchable, tagged-to-the-area content is how hungry people nearby actually find somewhere to eat now. This is how you get discovered by the people who live minutes away and have never walked in.

Getting found locally is the difference between a good dish nobody sees and a full room on a Tuesday.

6

Consistent Hooks, Plus Targeted Local Ads

Posting when there is time is not a strategy. A steady rhythm of hooky, well-made video is what earns reach. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at food lovers within a few miles of Fenham, and you put your best dish clips in front of people deciding where to eat this weekend.

For a restaurant, a handful of extra tables a night pays for a lot of content and ads, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make toward packed every night.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in One Service

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the dishes, the fusion twist, the room and the regulars. Each one could be filmed on a phone in a single evening.

Idea 01
"The Mandi reveal"
SignatureRevealASMR

A tight, cinematic build up to the Lamb Mandi being brought to the table and revealed: the steam, the rice, the tender lamb pulled apart. Strong hook on frame one ("Newcastle, you need to see this"). Trending audio. Under 20 seconds. Pure appetite.

Why it works: A showpiece dish revealed on camera is one of the most reliably shared formats in food right now, and yours is genuinely a showpiece.
Idea 02
"Parmo, but fusion"
LocalCheese pullHook

The Al Fahad Special Parmo cut open for the cheese pull, played against the classic Newcastle Parmo people already know and love. Lead with the twist ("we did something to the Parmo"). Close on the first bite.

Why it works: It ties a viral local favourite to your unique version, which is exactly the kind of "wait, what" hook that travels across the North East.
Idea 03
"One kitchen, three cuisines"
ConceptMenuFusion

A fast montage that captures the whole idea: a pizza, a curry and a Chinese dish plated one after another, all from one menu. Snappy captions, punchy sound. The clearest possible advert for what makes Al Fahad different.

Why it works: It explains your concept in five seconds and makes people curious enough to book. Only Al Fahad can make this one.
Idea 04
"A table for the whole family"

Why it works: It sells the experience and quietly markets your group bookings and private hire, some of your most valuable covers.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into booked tables.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone in Newcastle decides where to eat, they search and they read reviews before they book. Your Google profile and reviews are doing quiet, heavy lifting, and they deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised for "restaurants in Fenham" and "fusion restaurant Newcastle"
  • Funnel every happy table into a fresh Google review at the end of the meal
  • Add fresh dish photos and reels regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Make Bookings and Function Hire Effortless

Once the content is pulling people in, the last thing you want is a fiddly booking journey. Every reel and post should make it obvious how to reserve a table or enquire about the function room.

  • A clear, quick booking link in every bio and post
  • Private hire and big-table enquiries made simple to send
  • Menus and opening times easy to find in one tap

Targeted Local Ads to Hungry Diners

Because a full table is high value, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around Newcastle puts your best dish reels in front of people deciding where to eat this week.

  • Target food lovers within a few miles of Fenham
  • Use your strongest dish and "fusion twist" reels as the ads
  • Push weekend covers, celebrations and function bookings
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Al Fahad: A handful of extra tables a night covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a restaurant can do.
