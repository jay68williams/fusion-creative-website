What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The concept is genuinely distinctive

"Escape to South America, without leaving Gosforth" is a brilliant hook, and the venue lives up to it. Vibrant South American flavours, tequila, mezcal and pisco cocktails, and one of the most striking dining spaces in Newcastle. Most restaurants sound the same. Pique does not, and that is gold for content.

✓ The place is made to be filmed

A theatrical cocktail programme, small plates that arrive looking the part, ceviche, tacos and steaks, and a room with real atmosphere. This is content that half writes itself. The ingredients for scroll-stopping short-form video are already on the pass and behind the bar.

✓ You already have an engaged following

Your Instagram has a real, active local audience who already know and like the brand. That is the hardest part to build from scratch. It means the reach is not a cold start, it is an existing base ready to be grown once the content is built to travel.

✓ The posting habit is there

You are showing up consistently across Instagram and Facebook, which most independents struggle with. The discipline is already in place. The issue is not effort, it is format, and format is the easy thing to fix.

✓ Two audiences under one roof

Brunch and date night drinks Tuesday to Saturday, and a proper late night bar on Friday and Saturday. That is two distinct crowds you can speak to with two distinct styles of content, which doubles the reasons for someone to book.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the brand, the room and the menu. The job is to turn that into reach: get Pique in front of the Newcastle diners scrolling for their next brunch, date night or Friday out, and make it the place they screenshot and share. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Lead With Short-Form Video, Not Photos

Photos hold the feed but they rarely travel. Reels and TikTok are where food and drink venues win reach right now, because the platforms push good video out to people who do not follow you yet. For a place as visual as Pique, moving the centre of gravity from static posts to short, punchy video is the single biggest change, and it is exactly what you asked for.

The same room and the same plates, filmed and cut properly, reach many times the people they do as a photo.

2

Turn the Theatre of the Room Into Content

The cocktail being built and poured, the sizzle of a steak arriving, the plates landing on the table, the buzz of a full Friday night. Pique has genuine theatre most restaurants can only wish for, and right now very little of it is being captured. Every service is a set of ready-made clips waiting to be filmed.

Sensory, close-up, sound-on video is what makes people stop scrolling and think, I need to go there.

3

Make the Food and Drinks the Hero

Ceviche, tacos, steaks and signature cocktails, shot tight, in good light, with the right audio, are some of the most shareable content there is. A single standout dish or a theatrical drink can carry a whole reel. This is content that gets saved and sent to a friend with "we're going here", which is exactly how a table gets booked.

Hero the best few plates and drinks properly and they will do more marketing than any advert.

4

Speak to Both Crowds Separately

Brunch and date night is a different audience from the late night Friday and Saturday bar. Content that works hardest Tuesday to Saturday for the food and the atmosphere, and a separate, higher-energy strand for the late night crowd, means you are pulling in two different bookings rather than blurring into one. It also fills the nights you most want filled.

Two clear stories, two clear reasons to come, aimed at the people most likely to book each one.

5

Put Faces and Personality on Camera

People follow people. The chef plating up, the team behind the bar, the story of why it is South American, the "escape to South America without leaving Gosforth" line brought to life. Personality-led content builds the familiarity that turns a first-time scroller into a regular, and it is what makes a venue feel like somewhere you already know before you have even walked in.

The brand voice is already there. It just needs faces and short video to carry it.

Ready-Made Content
4 Reel Ideas You Could Film in a Single Service

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do every night: the room, the drinks, the plates and the people. Each one could be filmed on a phone in one service.

Idea 01
"Escape to South America, without leaving Gosforth"
VenueRevealAtmosphere

A fast, cinematic tour of the room as it fills up: the interior, the bar, a drink being built, plates landing, the buzz. Strong hook on frame one ("this is hidden in Gosforth"). Trending audio. Under 25 seconds. Your tagline brought to life in video.

Why it works: It sells the feeling of the place in a few seconds, which is exactly what makes someone book a table they did not know they wanted.
Idea 02
"Watch this cocktail come together"
DrinksTheatreSound-on

A signature cocktail built start to finish, tight and close up, with the pour, the garnish and the final serve. Mezcal, pisco, tequila, the theatrical stuff. Satisfying sound, no talking needed. Lead with the finished drink, then rewind to how it is made.

Why it works: Cocktail builds are some of the most saved and shared content on the platforms, and they show off exactly what sets your bar apart.
Idea 03
"The plates that made us"
FoodClose-upMenu

A rapid run through the signature dishes, ceviche, tacos, the steak, shot tight in good light with the sizzle and the sauce. A few seconds each, strong hook ("the three things to order at Pique"). Built to be saved and sent to a friend.

Why it works: Food this good, shot properly, is the most direct route from a scroll to a booking. It answers the only question a diner has: is it worth it.
Idea 04
"Date night vs Friday night at Pique"
Two crowdsPOVSplit

One reel, two moods: the calm early evening date night side, then cut to the late night bar energy on a Friday. Same venue, two very different nights out. Shows the range and gives two audiences a reason to book.

Why it works: It markets both sides of the business in one video and lands the idea that Pique works for more than one occasion, which fills more of the week.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your visibility into booked tables.

Finish the Website SEO Basics

A quick, honest one: the website still shows a placeholder page title and an empty meta description, which is the text Google reads first. It is a small fix with a real payoff, since "South American restaurant Gosforth" and "Newcastle brunch" are exactly the searches you want to be found for.

  • Set a proper page title and description built around Gosforth and Newcastle
  • Make sure the menus, hours and booking link are easy to find and up to date
  • Tidy the basics so the brand looks as sharp on Google as it does in the room

Get the Review Profiles Pulling Their Weight

Before people book somewhere new, they check Google, Tripadvisor and OpenTable. Those profiles are doing quiet, heavy lifting, and they deserve to be fed and front and centre.

  • Keep the Google profile optimised for "restaurant Gosforth" and "brunch Newcastle"
  • Nudge happy guests toward a fresh review while they are still at the table
  • Turn your best reviews and dishes into shareable content

Targeted Local Ads to Newcastle Diners

Once the content is landing, a small, tightly targeted budget puts your best reels in front of exactly the people most likely to book: diners in and around Gosforth and Newcastle.

  • Target Newcastle and the surrounding areas by interest and location
  • Use your strongest food, drink and venue reels as the ads
  • Push the quieter nights and drive bookings straight to the website
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Pique: a filled table on a quiet Tuesday covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a restaurant can do.
