Our honest take

You already have the formula. It just is not on camera yet.

The food, the drinks, the environment and the music are exactly what performs on social right now, and most venues have to manufacture one of those. The plan is simple: film what already happens at Pique, package it the way the platforms reward, and put it in front of the North East every week. That, done consistently, is the whole game.

The biggest gap: Pique is not on TikTok.

TikTok is where Newcastle decides where to eat and drink next, and right now Pique does not exist there. That is not a weakness, it is the single fastest win available. We started Takdir’s TikTok from nothing in May and it is already past 2,200 followers, with the same videos we were filming anyway.

Ready-made content

Four reels we would film first.

Idea 01
“The cocktail that stops the scroll”

One signature serve, tightly shot: the pour, the smoke or flame, the garnish landing against that gold wall. Hook on frame one, under 25 seconds.

Idea 02
“Escape to South America without leaving Gosforth”

A cinematic walk from the street door through the room, landing on a table of small plates and a cocktail. Your whole brand in 20 seconds.

Idea 03
“Small plates you need to try in Newcastle”

A punchy run through three or four standout plates with one-line captions. Built to be saved and sent to a mate.

Idea 04
“Where to go on a Friday night in Newcastle”

The venue after dark: bar busy, cocktails flowing, open until midnight. A different audience to the dinner crowd, and TikTok’s favourite.
