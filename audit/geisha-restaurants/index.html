What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The food and rooms are made for video

Sushi and dim sum coming together, the Japanese-garden interior, cocktails, the Fusion plates. This is exactly the kind of visual theatre that stops a thumb mid-scroll. Most restaurants have to work hard to look filmable. You already are, before a single edit.

✓ The reputation backs it up

Geisha Asia sits at 4.7 on Tripadvisor and ranks among the top restaurants in Newcastle, with reviews raving about the food, the cocktails and the vibe. That is proof the experience delivers. Right now that reputation lives on review sites rather than being shown off where new customers scroll.

✓ You are already on TikTok

You are posting on TikTok, not just Instagram, and local food creators are already tagging Geisha in their own videos. That tells you the appetite is there and the content travels when it goes out. The opportunity is to own that momentum with your own camera, not leave it to other people's.

✓ Two distinct concepts under one roof

Geisha Asia and Geisha Fusion give you two clear stories to tell and twice the content angles, from delicate sushi to a proper Sunday roast. That is a genuine advantage. It just needs each brand given its own clear identity so neither gets lost.

✓ A destination worth travelling for

Benton is not a passing-trade high street, so people choose to come to Front Street. That means every strong video does real work, pulling in someone who would not have found you otherwise. Get the reach right and it lands straight on your tables.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the food, the rooms and the reputation. The job is to turn that into consistent reach and bookings: get Geisha in front of people across Newcastle and the North East, and make Front Street the place they decide is worth the trip. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Give Each Concept Its Own Clear Identity

Right now Geisha Asia and Geisha Fusion share one feed, so a diner scrolling never gets a clean picture of either. Sushi sits next to a burger and the story blurs. We would give each concept its own voice and look, whether through dedicated content pillars on one account or a clearer split, so someone in the mood for pan-Asian and someone after a Sunday roast both instantly get it.

Two concepts is a strength, but only when each one is unmistakable.

2

Turn the Food Theatre Into Reels

The sushi being plated, the dim sum steam, a cocktail being built, the aromatic duck, a dish landing on the table in the garden room. These moments are the single best content you have and they cost nothing extra, they already happen every service. Filmed properly with strong hooks and the right audio, they are exactly the clips that travel.

Appetite is the most powerful hook on social. You have it on every plate.

3

Make Posting Consistent and Systematic

The difference between the odd good video and real growth is rhythm. A steady, planned flow of content, filmed in batches and posted consistently across Instagram and TikTok, is what teaches the algorithm to push you and keeps Geisha in front of people week after week rather than in bursts.

Consistency beats the occasional viral fluke. It is the boring bit that quietly wins.

4

Own What Creators Already Do For You

Local food creators are already filming Geisha and tagging you, which is free reach landing in your lap. We would build on that on purpose: invite the right creators, make it easy and worth their while, and repost the best of it. A steady stream of trusted local voices talking about you is some of the most persuasive marketing there is.

You are already being recommended. The move is to turn that into a repeatable channel.

5

Sell the Room and the Occasion

Bottomless brunch, cocktails, the garden-room setting, private hire, date nights and celebrations. People do not only pick a restaurant for the food, they pick it for the occasion and the feeling. Content that shows the atmosphere, the group tables, the buzz of a Saturday night, sells the experience of coming to Geisha, not just the menu.

Show people the night out, and you sell the booking, not just the dish.

6

Reels and TikTok for Reach, Plus Targeted Local Ads

Short-form video built to travel is what earns organic reach. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at diners within travelling distance of Benton, and you put your best food and atmosphere content in front of the exact people deciding where to eat this weekend.

For a destination restaurant, a handful of extra covers a night pays for a lot of content and ads, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your online visibility into booked tables.

Get the Google and Booking Profiles Pulling Their Weight

When someone decides where to eat, they search, they look at photos and they read reviews before they book. Your Google profile, your Tripadvisor and your booking pages are doing quiet, heavy lifting and deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profiles optimised for "restaurants Benton" and "sushi Newcastle" and the surrounding areas
  • Funnel every happy diner into a fresh Google review at the point of paying
  • Add fresh food and interior photography regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

A Local Creator and Influencer Engine

Food creators are already featuring Geisha off their own backs. We would make that a proper channel: a steady rota of the right local creators through both concepts, briefed and looked after, with the best content reposted to your own feeds.

  • Invite trusted North East food accounts through Asia and Fusion
  • Repost and amplify their content on your channels
  • Build a reliable stream of trusted voices recommending you

Targeted Local Ads to Nearby Diners

Because a table is high value, especially a group booking, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around Newcastle puts your best food and atmosphere content in front of people deciding where to eat this weekend.

  • Target diners within travelling distance of Benton and the wider North East
  • Use your strongest food theatre and occasion reels as the ads
  • Drive bookings and brunch reservations straight to your booking system
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Geisha: A handful of extra covers a night covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a destination restaurant can do.
