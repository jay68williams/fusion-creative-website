Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the product, the platforms and the posting habit. The job is to turn that into reach: get Kekko's in front of the local people who have not walked through the door yet, and make you the spot they already follow before they visit. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Food the Hero

The most reliable food content is the food itself, shot close, shot slow, shot to make people hungry. The pour, the pull, the first cut, the steam. Filmed properly with a strong hook in the first second, one dish can carry a whole reel.

You already make this every day. The only change is capturing it as content built to travel, not a quick phone snap that disappears.

2

Scale the TikTok You Already Have

You are already on TikTok, which is where local food goes viral fastest right now. The opportunity is not starting it, it is scaling it: proper hooks, trending audio, posting rhythm and formats built for the way the platform actually pushes reach.

TikTok will put a great food video in front of thousands of local people for free. That is the cheapest reach in hospitality, and you already have a foot in the door.

3

Show the People and the Process

Behind the counter, the early prep, the regulars, the story of why Kekko's exists. Personality-led and behind-the-scenes content builds the kind of loyalty a plain menu photo never will. People do not just follow food, they follow the people making it.

This is content only you can make, and it is what turns a one-time visitor into a regular who tells their friends.

4

Win Your Local Area on Reach

For a food business, the people who matter live nearby. Content tagged and tuned to your town, "best in the area" style videos and local hooks, gets you in front of the exact people who can actually walk in this week.

One video that reaches your local area properly can fill tables and busy the counter in a way no flyer ever could.

5

Short-Form for Reach, Plus a Small Local Ad Push

Consistent short-form video built to travel is what earns organic reach. Pair your best food reels with a small, tightly targeted local ad budget and you put your most mouth-watering content directly in front of hungry people within a few miles of the door.

For a food business, one busy weekend pays for a lot of content, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in a Single Service

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do every day: the food, the process, the people and the place. Each one could be filmed on a phone in a single shift.

Idea 01
"The one dish everyone orders"
Hero dishClose-upTrending audio

Your signature item, filmed up close from build to first bite. The pour, the pull, the steam, the crunch. Strong hook on frame one ("this is why people keep coming back to Kekko's"). Trending audio, under 20 seconds, made to make people hungry.

Why it works: A single mouth-watering hero shot is the most reliable format in food, and it puts your best seller in front of thousands of local people.
Idea 02
"How it's actually made"
ProcessBehind the scenesSatisfying

The prep, the making, the plating, sped up and satisfying. People love seeing the work that goes into their food. Show the care, the fresh ingredients, the hands doing it. End on the finished dish looking unreal.

Why it works: Process content builds trust and appetite at the same time, and it is exactly the kind of video people save and send to a friend.
Idea 03
"POV: your first time at Kekko's"
ExperienceLocalPOV

A first-person walkthrough of the whole experience: walking in, the order, the wait, the food landing, the reaction. Tag the location. Warm, real, and built to make someone nearby think "I need to go this week".

Why it works: POV experience videos reach local people deciding where to eat, and they answer the only question that matters, is it worth the trip.
Idea 04
"Why we started Kekko's"
FounderStoryLoyalty

You to camera, straight and honest, on why Kekko's exists and what you care about getting right. A little bit of personality goes a long way. Strong hook ("everyone told us not to open a food place, here is why we did it anyway").

Why it works: People back people. A founder story turns followers into regulars, and it makes the brand impossible to forget.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into covers, orders and regulars.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone nearby is deciding where to eat, they search and read reviews before they ever walk in. Your Google profile is doing quiet, heavy lifting, and it deserves to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised so Kekko's shows up for local food searches
  • Funnel happy customers into fresh Google reviews at the point of paying
  • Keep fresh food photos and opening hours up to date
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Turn Followers Into Repeat Visits

Reach brings people in once. The content engine is also what keeps them coming back: new items, specials, and the sense of a place with a story worth following.

  • Announce new dishes and specials as short video, not just a static post
  • Show the regulars and the community around the place
  • Build a reason to keep watching, so following you feels worth it

Targeted Local Ads to Nearby Foodies

Because a food business lives on local footfall, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget puts your most mouth-watering reels in front of people within a few miles who are deciding where to eat.

  • Target people in your town and surrounding area
  • Use your strongest food and experience reels as the ads
  • Drive orders, bookings and walk-ins
  • Track cost per result so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Kekko's: One busy weekend covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a food business can do.
