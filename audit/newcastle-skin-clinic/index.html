Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the expertise, the results and the booking setup. The job is to get in front of the right people in Newcastle and Gateshead, earn their trust before they ever walk in, and turn that into consultations and treatment plans.

1

Put Nurse Luisa on Camera, Consistently

The most effective aesthetics content right now is practitioner-led: a trusted expert explaining what a treatment actually does, who it is for and who it is not for. Your "natural results" philosophy is a story in itself in an industry full of overdone work.

Every time someone watches you speak honestly about their concern, they get closer to booking a consultation with you rather than whoever is cheapest.

2

Turn the Before and Afters Into Short-Form Stories

A static before and after on a website is proof. The same result told as a 20-second story, the concern, the treatment, the gradual change over weeks, is proof that travels. Sculptra and Sofwave in particular build over time, which is perfect for "week one to week twelve" content.

This is the content that turns a viewer into an enquiry.

3

Educate on the Advanced Treatments

Polynucleotides, exosomes, Sofwave, HArmonyCa: most people have heard the names and do not know which one is right for them. Clear, myth-busting explainers from a nurse prescriber position you as the expert in the North East and answer questions before the consultation even starts.

Educational content gets saved and shared, and the people saving it are the ones already thinking about booking.

4

Scale the TikTok and Reels for Local Reach

You are already on Instagram and TikTok, so the foundation is there. The step up is a steady rhythm of short-form video with proper hooks on frame one, built for the For You page and Reels rather than the existing following, so new people across the region discover the clinic every week.

Reach on its own is not the goal. Reach from people within driving distance of Team Valley is.

5

Retarget Viewers Into Consultations

With the pixel already on your site, the best-performing videos can be put in front of local people who have watched your content or visited the website, with a clear route to book a consultation. In aesthetics the ads need to be handled carefully: prescription-only treatments cannot be promoted, so the ads lead with you, skin health, consultations and treatments like Sofwave and Hydrafacial, and stay compliant.

Because a new client can become a 12-month plan, one extra consultation that converts covers a lot of content.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in the Clinic

These are formats working right now for aesthetics clinics on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do. Each one could be filmed in a single session at the clinic.

Idea 01
"What a Top 40 Sculptra injector actually does differently"
ExpertHookSculptra

You to camera, straight talking, explaining how Sculptra works, why results build gradually and what natural looks like. Lead with the credential on frame one. Under 30 seconds.

Why it works: It turns your strongest credential into a reason to book with you, and it filters in people looking for natural, long-lasting results.
Idea 02
"Week one to week twelve"
ProofResultsJourney

A client journey (with consent) told over the weeks: the consultation, the treatment, the check-ins and the final result. Quick cuts, the date on screen, a natural-light reveal at the end.

Why it works: It shows the gradual, natural change that sets your work apart, and it answers the question everyone has: will it look like me.
Idea 03
"Polynucleotides vs Profhilo: which one is for you?"
EducationMyth-bustingSaveable

A simple, honest comparison of two treatments people often confuse: what each does, who it suits, how long it takes. End on "book a consultation and we will tell you which is right for your skin".

Why it works: Comparison content gets saved and shared, and it brings in people who are already researching treatments, the closest audience to booking.
Idea 04
"What happens in your first consultation"
Behind the scenesTrustClinic

A walk through the clinic and a first appointment from the client's point of view: the welcome, the skin assessment, the honest conversation about what they need (and what they do not).

Why it works: Nerves are the biggest barrier to a first booking. Showing exactly what happens removes the fear and makes booking feel easy.

Beyond the Main Feed

Get More Reviews Working for You

People read reviews before they book an aesthetics treatment, and your clients already speak warmly about you by name. The more of that is visible, the easier the decision.

Content That Sells the Subscription Plans

The 12-month plans are a strong offer that is easy to miss. A short series explaining how the plans work and what a year of skin health looks like turns one-off bookings into long-term clients.

  • "A year of skin with Newcastle Skin Clinic" explainer content
  • Client stories from people on a plan (with consent)
  • A clear route from video to plan enquiry

Compliant Local Ads to Future Clients

A tightly targeted local budget puts your best content in front of people within easy reach of Team Valley, and retargets anyone who has watched or visited the site.

  • Targeted to Newcastle, Gateshead and the surrounding area

Why it fits Newcastle Skin Clinic: with the pixel already installed and a plan-based model, the ads have a proper way to measure and a high value per new client.
