What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The work itself is strong

Your curly cuts, lived-in blonde and grey blending are the kind of transformations that stop people scrolling. The raw material for brilliant content is already happening in your chair every single day. That is the hardest part, and you have it.

✓ You have a genuine niche

Curly hair is a speciality, not just a service, and clients notice when someone actually gets curls rather than treating them like straight hair. Owning a niche is exactly what makes a stylist findable and memorable online, and you already have one.

✓ You are showing up consistently

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the skill, the niche and the habit. The job is to turn that into reach: get your transformations in front of people across Newcastle who want their curls, colour or greys done properly, and make Irina's Salon Suite the name they save and book. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Turn Every Transformation Into a Before and After Reel

Curly transformations are made for short-form video. A quick clip of the before, a few seconds of the process, then the reveal, is one of the most watchable, most shareable formats there is. You are already creating the transformation, we just film it properly and cut it to travel instead of leaving it as a still photo.

This single change, from finished-look photos to before and after video, is the biggest lever you have for reach.

2

Own "Curly Hair Newcastle" as Your Niche

People with curls actively search for a stylist who understands them, because so many have been let down before. If your content clearly says curly specialist, in Newcastle, again and again, you become the obvious answer. Curl tips, dos and don'ts, product advice, the difference a proper curly cut makes.

Niche content reaches fewer people but the right people, and those are the ones who book, come back, and tell their friends.

3

Get Onto TikTok, Where Hair Content Travels Furthest

Right now your work lives on Instagram and Facebook, but TikTok is where hair transformations spread the fastest and reach brand new local audiences who have never heard of you. The same reels you film for the salon can run there too, so it is extra reach for almost no extra work.

This is the clearest quick win in the whole audit: a whole platform of potential clients you are not on yet.

4

Put Yourself on Camera

People do not book a logo, they book a person they already feel they know. A little of you talking to camera, explaining a curl myth, showing how you approach a colour, or just being yourself in the suite, builds trust before anyone walks through the door. It also makes your content unmistakably yours.

Personality is what turns a casual viewer into a loyal client, and it is the one thing no other stylist can replicate.

5

A Sustainable Rhythm, Plus Small Targeted Local Ads

Running a salon and posting every day is a lot for one person, so the plan has to be realistic. A simple, repeatable filming routine around your normal appointments keeps content flowing without eating your evenings. Then a small, tightly targeted ad budget puts your best transformation reels in front of people in Newcastle who are looking for exactly what you do.

A steady rhythm beats occasional bursts every time, and a little paid reach behind your strongest video goes a very long way locally.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do every day: the transformation, the niche, the colour and you. Each one could be filmed on a phone around a single appointment.

Idea 01
"Watch these curls come to life"
Before & AfterTransformationReveal

A fast before, process and reveal of a curly cut. Strong hook on frame one ("she had not had a proper curly cut in years"). A few seconds of the work, then the big bouncy reveal. Trending audio, under 25 seconds. Simple, and it is the format that travels most.

Why it works: Before and after transformations are the most watched, most shared content in the whole hair world, and you produce them every day.
Idea 02
"Curl mistakes you are probably making"
EducationNicheSave-worthy

You to camera with three quick, genuinely useful curl tips: how to dry them, what not to brush, the product step people skip. Fast, friendly, straight to the point. The kind of thing people save and send to a friend with curly hair.

Why it works: Helpful niche content marks you out as the curl expert and reaches exactly the people who will book a curly specialist.
Idea 03
"From grown-out to glowing blonde"
ColourLived-In BlondeGlow-up

A colour transformation reel: the grown-out before, glimpses of the process, the soft lived-in blonde or seamless grey blend at the end. Satisfying, cinematic, set to calm trending audio. Show the finish from a couple of angles so the result really lands.

Why it works: Colour glow-ups are hugely satisfying to watch and show off the full range of what you do beyond curly cuts.
Idea 04
"Come spend the day in my salon suite"
FounderBehind the scenesTrust

A gentle day-in-the-life: the suite, the coffee, a couple of clients, you doing what you love. Warm, personal and calming. It shows the experience of booking with you, not just the result, and lets people meet the person behind the work.

Why it works: People book stylists they feel they know. Showing yourself and your space builds that trust faster than any finished-look photo can.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into booked appointments.

Get Found When People Search Locally

When someone decides they want their curls sorted, they search and they read reviews before they book. A well set up Google profile, full of your work and fresh reviews, does quiet, heavy lifting all day.

  • Set up and optimise a Google profile for "curly hair Newcastle" and nearby areas
  • Ask every happy client for a quick Google review while they are still glowing
  • Add fresh transformation photos regularly so it looks alive
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Let Happy Clients Do Your Marketing

Your clients leave loving their hair, which is the perfect moment to turn them into reach. A little nudge to tag you and share turns one appointment into content that finds their whole network.

  • A simple, natural prompt to film or photograph the finished look and tag you
  • Reshare client posts and stories so your happy clients feed your reach
  • A repeatable way to collect and show off testimonials

Small, Targeted Local Ads

Because a new client is worth a lot over time, even a small ad budget pays back fast. A tightly targeted spend around Newcastle puts your best transformation reels in front of people looking for exactly what you do.

  • Target people in Newcastle and the surrounding areas
  • Use your strongest before and after reels as the ads
  • Drive people straight to your booking page
  • Track it so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits your salon: A single new regular client covers a lot of content and ad spend over the year, which makes local ads one of the highest-return moves a stylist can make.
