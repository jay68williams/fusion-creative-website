01 · The short version

A snack bar people already film, with a feed and a website that have gone quiet.

Your own Instagram has not posted since 17 February, when you announced a Ramadan pause. Since then food guides and partner accounts have kept posting about Barbassi: a creator's reel of your Omani grill collab, a 'teach me something' reel at your counter, and M39's own post, which calls you the neighbourhood snack bar within Madar_39. The interest is landing on their feeds, not yours. Your website has a newsletter form that reports a storage set-up problem, and the pages we read show no menu, hours or address. Nothing on your side is capturing it yet.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A place people describe in their own words. M39's post calls Barbassi 'the neighbourhood snack bar within Madar_39'.
  • Food built in front of people. A 'teach me something' reel shows your team teaching a guest to make the gabagool mabooj.
  • Collabs people already share: an Omani grill collab with @alsaafbym, a breakfast meet with @971fuel, and a collab post with a dragon slicing gabagool.
  • A room people film. Your team in striped aprons is already on other accounts' reels, in the booths at Madar 39.
  • A loyal crowd. "See you in the Mina" is your own line, and your two most recent posts drew 182 and 353 likes.

You do not need a new room or a bigger menu. You need short video made in the shop, posted regularly from your own account, and a simple way for people to see where you are and order.

07 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from the counter, the collabs and the team in Madar 39.

Each idea uses something already in your posts or on your counter. Six are built to bring people in, and six keep your Instagram growing.

Videos that bring people in

6 concepts
Skit

Teach me something, round two

"Teach me something: how a plain sandwich gets built at Barbassi."

A guest is taught to build the gabagool mabooj at the counter, from the first slice to the last tray.

Why: it reuses the format that already drew 307 likes, with a guest who is not in the video yet.

Challenge

Name every filling

"Name every tray on the counter before you order."

Guests at the counter try to name each filling under the glass. Honest faces, honest misses.

Why: a challenge gives people a reason to comment and to come in and check.

Collab

The Omani grill collab, round two

"Omani grill, stuffed focaccia, one more night together."

The next collab filmed at the counter, with the two teams building the sandwich together.

Why: your collab reel drew 1,784 likes, more than any post from your own account.

Local

Who eats in Madar 39?

"What do you do here, and what did you order?"

Quick answers from people at the tables, with the sandwich in shot.

Why: shows who already comes in, and tells people nearby where you are.

Event

The breakfast meet

"Breakfast, cars and a sandwich in the car park."

The next car breakfast meet, filmed from the counter to the car window.

Why: your December breakfast meet with @971fuel is already a story; this is the next one.

Craft

The dragon on the slicer

"The dragon on the slicer, and the gabagool it cuts."

The slicer, the gabagool going through it and the artwork from the collab, in one take.

Why: turns the collab artwork into a short craft video about the sandwich.

Organic that builds the audience

6 concepts
Series

The first tray of the day

"The first tray of the day, before the first order."

The slabs coming out, split and stacked under the glass.

Why: a weekly series people can look for.

Founder

Why Barbassi?

"Why did you name the shop Barbassi?"

Thirty seconds with the founder in the shop, in his own words.

Why: a creator post about the founder drew 1,291 likes. Your version belongs on your own feed.

Return

Back from the Ramadan pause

"We paused for Ramadan. Here is what is back."

A short note from the team at the counter: what changed, what is coming, where to find you.

Why: your last own post was the pause notice, so the return needs its own post.

Team

Meet the aprons

"One of us, one sandwich, one reason."

One team member a week, their pick from the menu and their reason for it.

Why: people come back to places where they know the staff.

Ritual

See you in the Mina

"See you in the Mina, every week."

The end of a service at the counter, the team and a last sandwich in the box.

Why: turns your own sign-off line into a series.

Sit-in

Coffee, and a seat for longer

"A sandwich to go, or a long coffee."

The booths in the afternoon: a coffee, a plate, the tiled floor and the lights.

Why: M39's own description of you is a quick bite and a long coffee; the video shows both.

08 · Your orders, direct

Today a visitor cannot order or check hours on your site. It could.

The pages we read on barbassiboom.com offer no menu, no hours, no address and no order option. For a snack bar that trades through the working day, the delivery apps are the other route, and they take a share of each order.

09 · The plan

A plan built around the stories you already have.

  1. Step 1
    Plan

    A call to pick the videos and agree the shot list. We brief a licensed UAE crew, and we map the simplest way for people to see where you are and order.

  2. Step 2
    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film in Madar 39 to our shot list and brief: the first tray of the day, the counter at the busiest hour, the founder and the team. We direct remotely from Newcastle.

  3. Step 3
    Edit and deliver

    We edit and caption every video in our studio and deliver the finished set, ready to post to Instagram and TikTok, each one ending with a reason to come in and where to find you.

  4. Step 4
    Handover

    You get every video to keep, with a simple plan for the order to post them in. When you want more, we plan the next shoot day.

What the shoot gives you: a set of short videos filmed in Madar 39 by a licensed UAE crew, edited and captioned, ready to post, plus a simple plan for the order to post them in. You keep every video.
