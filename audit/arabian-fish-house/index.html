What is already strong, and worth building on

  • The setting. Your own website says it: "a seafront location overlooking the Arabian Gulf". Your terrace has white wicker chairs, in view of the sea.
  • Two branches: Al Hira Beach in Sharjah, and Al Shindagha in Dubai.
  • Open every day, 7am to midnight, according to your website. That gives you a breakfast crowd, a lunch crowd and a late crowd to film.
  • A menu with stories in it: grilled shrimps, kibbeh laban, fried bazri and manakish from the brick oven. Your website describes "unique Arabic twists" on locally sourced seafood.
  • Proof people already rate it: a 4.9 rating from 5,665 reviews shown on your website, and your "Letters From You" post says your reviews mean everything.

You do not need a new menu or a new website. The gain is reach per post: the terrace, the oven, your team and your guests in front of people in Sharjah and Dubai, posted on a plan, so there is a reason to book a table at the beach this week.

06 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your terrace, your oven and your guests.

Every concept uses something you already have. Six are built to bring people through the door this week. Six are built to keep your Instagram growing.

Videos that bring people in

6 concepts
Skit

The wrong table

"When the guest asks for a table without a sea view."

A guest asks for a table away from the sea, and the waiter points at the view. He shrugs.

Why: turns your sea view into a joke people share and tag a friend in.

Challenge

Pick your dish

"Fried bazri, grilled shrimps or kibbeh laban. Which one are you?"

Guests choose their dish, and we follow one from the kitchen to the table.

Why: a choice format asks people to answer in the comments, and that keeps a video in the feed.

Group

Bring the group

"Coming as a big group? Here is what to order."

A group of friends at the table, filmed from the head of the table, with the mezze arriving in waves.

Why: shows groups what a table at the beach looks like.

Challenge

The 7am club

"Breakfast on the beach before work. Can you make it?"

The terrace at sunrise, the first manakish out of the oven, a guest with a coffee at 7:05.

Why: fills the early hours you already open for.

Local

Sharjah or Dubai?

"Same fish, two views. Which branch?"

Al Hira Beach cut against Al Shindagha in Dubai. Guests at each say why they come.

Why: reminds your followers there are two places to go, and speaks to each city.

Reaction

First time at the beach

"First time at Arabian Fish House. Go."

A first-timer led to the terrace, the first look at the view, the first bite, the verdict.

Why: gives a new guest a voice on camera.

07 · The plan

A plan built around the terrace, the oven and one shoot day.

  1. Step 1
    Plan

    A call to pick the twelve videos and agree the booking page fix. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and set up video audiences on your Meta pixel.

  2. Step 2
    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film at Al Hira Beach to our shot list and brief: the oven in the morning, a full lunch service on the terrace, your team and your guests, in one day. We direct remotely from Newcastle.

  3. Step 3
    Edit and deliver

    We edit and caption the batch in our studio. Short videos ready for Instagram and TikTok, each one ending with a reason to come this week and a WhatsApp line to book.

  4. Step 4
    Handover

    You get every edited video to keep and post, with a running order for what to post when. When you want a second day, we plan one at Al Shindagha in Dubai.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos, edited and captioned, ready for Instagram and TikTok, and every one of them yours to keep and post.
