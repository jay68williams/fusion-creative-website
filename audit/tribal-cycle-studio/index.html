What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The product is built for video

A dark room, mood lighting, a proper sound system and themed rides is genuinely rare, and it is exactly what performs on Reels and TikTok. Most fitness businesses have to manufacture something watchable. You just have to point a camera at what already happens every class.

✓ You are genuinely different

The North East's only purpose-built spin studio is a real, defensible position. It gives you a story no gym or chain can copy, and a clear reason for someone to choose Tribal over a generic class down the road. That is gold for content.

✓ The community and reviews are strong

Loyal regulars, glowing Google reviews and instructors people come back for. That loyalty is the hardest thing to build and you already have it. Right now it lives inside the studio, when it could be your most persuasive content.

✓ You are already on Instagram and TikTok

The accounts are live and posting, which means the habit is there. The issue is not effort, it is format and consistency. Getting that right is the easy part to fix, and it is where the reach is hiding.

✓ Themed rides are ready-made hooks

A Taylor Swift ride or a Sam Fender night is a content hook on a plate. Each themed session is its own event, easy to hype before, capture during and clip after. That gives you a natural, repeatable reason to post something people actually want to watch.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the studio, the atmosphere and the community. The job is to turn that into reach and full classes: get in front of the Newcastle audiences most likely to become regulars, and make Tribal the name people already know before they ever book their free class. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Sell the Atmosphere, Not Just the Workout

Plenty of places offer a hard workout. What almost nobody offers is your room: the lights down, the sound up, the whole class moving together. That feeling is what makes someone want to try it, and it is the one thing a photo can never capture. Short video built around the atmosphere is your single biggest opportunity.

Lead with the experience and you are not selling exercise, you are selling a night out that happens to be a workout. That is a much easier thing to say yes to.

2

Make Every Themed Ride a Content Moment

Your themed rides are events, so treat them like it. A hype clip in the run-up ("Taylor Swift ride, this Thursday, one bike left"), a few seconds of the room during, and a highlight reel after. Each theme becomes a reason to post that people look forward to, and a reason to book before it sells out.

This turns your calendar into a content plan on its own, and gives every ride a proper build-up rather than a quiet Instagram story that disappears in a day.

3

Put the Instructors and Riders on Camera

People do not book a bike, they book Isla, or Katie, or the energy of a full room. Instructor-led content, faces, personalities and the community, is the most effective fitness content there is right now. A regular talking about how Tribal changed their week is worth more than any advert you could run.

Familiarity is what turns a scroll into a booking. When someone feels like they already know your instructors, walking in for a first class stops being scary.

4

Scale the Short-Form Video You Already Make

You are already posting to Instagram and TikTok, but posting and reaching are two different things. Content built to travel, with a strong hook in the first second, trending audio and a proper rhythm, is what earns reach rather than just filling the feed. The gap is not effort, it is the format and the consistency.

Get that right and the same studio, the same rides and the same instructors start reaching thousands of the right people in Newcastle instead of the same handful who already follow you.

5

Targeted Local Ads to Fill Classes

You told us you want to reach specific audiences across central Newcastle, and paid ads are how you do that precisely. A small, tightly targeted budget puts your best atmosphere and themed-ride clips in front of exactly the people most likely to book: students, young professionals and the Jesmond and city-centre crowd, with the free first class as the offer.

Because a rider who sticks around comes back week after week, the cost of winning one is paid back quickly. That makes local ads one of the highest-return moves a studio like yours can make.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film in a Single Session

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the room, the themed rides, the instructors and the community. Each one could be filmed on a phone around a single class.

Idea 01
"POV: your first Tribal Cycle class"
ImmersiveAtmospherePOV

A first-person walk in: doors open, lights down, sound building, the bikes filling up, the drop as the class kicks off. Strong hook on frame one ("this is what a spin class in Newcastle actually looks like"). Trending audio, under 20 seconds. It sells the feeling a photo never can.

Why it works: It answers the only real question a first-timer has, what is it actually like, and shows the exact thing that makes Tribal worth trying.
Idea 02
"The [artist] ride was unreal"
Themed rideEventHype

A highlight reel of a themed night, the Taylor Swift or Sam Fender ride, cut to the track, lights and energy at full tilt. Post a teaser before to sell the next one out, then the highlight after. Lead with the theme and the date.

Why it works: Each themed ride is its own event, which gives people a specific reason to book now rather than someday, and a reason to share it.
Idea 03
"Come with me to a 7am spin class in Jesmond"
VlogRelatableTikTok

A get-ready-with-me style clip following one rider: the early alarm, the walk in, the class, the buzz after. Real, relatable, first-person. This format is huge on TikTok right now and quietly does the selling for you without feeling like an advert.

Why it works: It lets a potential rider picture themselves doing it, which is exactly what turns a viewer into a booking.
Idea 04
"Why we built the North East's only spin studio"
FounderStoryTrust

Gary to camera, straight talking, the story behind Tribal and what makes it different. Honest and personal, with a strong hook ("everyone told us Newcastle didn't need a spin studio"). Cut against clips of a packed, lit-up room.

Why it works: People back people. A founder story builds trust fast and makes the brand impossible to forget, and it gives the community something to rally behind.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into booked classes and regulars.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone in Newcastle searches "spin class near me", your Google profile and reviews are doing quiet, heavy lifting. They deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed, because a strong profile turns a search into a first booking.

  • Keep the profile optimised for "spin class Newcastle", "spin studio Jesmond" and similar
  • Funnel happy riders into a fresh Google review at the point they are buzzing after class
  • Add fresh studio photos and themed-ride shots regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Turn First-Timers Into Regulars

The free first class is a brilliant hook. The value is in what happens next. A simple, well-timed follow-up keeps a first-timer coming back until Tribal becomes part of their week.

  • Content and messaging aimed at converting a free class into a session pack
  • Highlight the community and the regulars so newcomers feel part of it fast
  • Make the next booking the easiest possible thing to do
