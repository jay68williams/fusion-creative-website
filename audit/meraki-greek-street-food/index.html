What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The product is genuinely brilliant

Chicken and pork gyros, proper pita wraps, halloumi, skewers and Greek desserts, all cooked fresh. Great food is the hard part, and you already have it. Everything from here is about getting more people to see it.

✓ The reviews back you up

94% recommend on Facebook, with customers repeatedly praising the generous portions and the authentic flavour. That is exactly the kind of word of mouth most takeaways would kill for, and right now it is barely being shown off where new customers actually look.

✓ The family story is a real asset

Family-owned, proud to bring the flavours of Greece to the streets of Stoke. People buy from people, and an authentic family story behind the counter is one of the most powerful things you can put on camera. It is a huge advantage over a faceless chain.

✓ Ordering is already sorted

You are on Just Eat, Uber Eats and Deliveroo, plus your own ordering site and app. The path from someone seeing your food to actually buying it is short and frictionless, which means every extra bit of reach can turn straight into orders.

✓ You are already posting

You are active on Instagram and Facebook, so the habit of showing up is there. The issue is not effort, it is format. Photos and menu posts do not travel the way short video does, and format is the easy thing to fix.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the food, the reviews and the family story. The job is to turn that into reach: get your gyros in front of hungry people all over Stoke and make Meraki the Greek spot everyone already knows about. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Food the Star

Food is the most scroll-stopping content there is, and yours is made for it. The meat coming off the spit, the wrap being built, the cheese pull, the sauce going on, the first bite. Shot close up and cut fast, that is the content that stops a thumb dead.

Right now the food is mostly sitting in photos and menu posts. Filmed properly as short video, the same dishes become the thing people send to a mate with "we need to go here".

2

Scale Short-Form Video Across TikTok and Reels

A small following with a brilliant product tells you the effort is right but the format is not. Short-form video built to travel, with a strong hook in the first second, is what earns reach, and food is one of the categories that blows up fastest on TikTok and Instagram Reels.

This is the single biggest lever. It is exactly what took New Greek from no audience to over 70 million views, and it is the untapped channel that could put Meraki in front of the whole of Stoke.

3

Win Local Food Discovery

"Best gyros in Stoke", "where to eat in Hanley", "cheap eats that are actually good". People search and scroll for exactly this, and content built around it puts you in front of the hungry locals deciding what to order tonight.

Tagging the location, leaning into Stoke and Hanley, and making content only a genuinely local spot can make is how you out-market the chains and own your patch.

4

Turn Reviews and Regulars Into Proof

A 94% recommend rate and customers raving about the portions is gold, and right now it is hidden in review tabs rather than shown as content. Quick clips of packed counters, happy regulars and real reactions to the food are some of the most persuasive things a takeaway can post.

Proof travels, and it answers the only question a new customer has: is this actually as good as it looks.

5

Put the Family on Camera

People do not order from a logo, they order from people they like. The most effective food content right now is personality-led: the faces behind the counter, the story of bringing Greek street food to Stoke, the pride in how it is made. Your family story is a ready-made advantage.

Personality-led content builds familiarity, and familiarity is what makes someone choose you over the other takeaway they have never seen.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do every day: the food, the flavours, the story and the regulars. Each one could be filmed on a phone during a normal shift.

Idea 01
"The perfect gyros, start to finish"
FoodASMRSatisfying

A fast, close-up build of a gyros wrap: the meat coming off the spit, the pita on the grill, the salad, the sauce, the wrap and cut. Strong hook on frame one ("this is how a proper gyros is made"). Trending audio, under 25 seconds, every frame mouth-watering.

Why it works: Satisfying food build videos are one of the most shared formats there is, and yours would make people hungry on sight.
Idea 02
"Best Greek food in Stoke? Watch this."
LocalDiscoveryFood-porn

A punchy montage of your best dishes, gyros, box meals, halloumi, desserts, cut to the beat with a bold on-screen claim ("if you live in Stoke and haven't tried this, you're missing out"). Tag Hanley and Stoke so it lands with locals.

Why it works: It reaches the hungry people nearby deciding where to order tonight and positions you as the Greek spot to try.
Idea 03
"Bringing Greece to the streets of Stoke"
FamilyStoryPOV

A short, warm piece to camera from the family: why you opened, what authentic Greek street food means to you, the pride in every wrap. Honest, human, a little bit heart on sleeve. The story behind the counter that no chain can copy.

Why it works: People buy from people. A genuine family story builds trust fast and makes customers root for you.
Idea 04
"POV: your Friday night just got sorted"
OrderBox mealHook

A box meal reveal: the lid comes off, the camera pans over a loaded feast, steam rising, everything generous. End on a simple call to action ("order on Just Eat, Uber Eats or straight from us"). Made to trigger a hungry, order-now impulse.

Why it works: It turns a scroll into an order by hitting people at exactly the moment they are deciding what to eat.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into orders.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone in Stoke fancies Greek food, they search and read reviews before they order. Your Google profile deserves to be front and centre and constantly fed with fresh photos and reviews.

  • Keep the profile optimised for "Greek takeaway Stoke" and "gyros Hanley"
  • Funnel every happy customer into a fresh Google review
  • Add mouth-watering food photos regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Make the Delivery Apps Work Harder

You are already on Just Eat, Uber Eats and Deliveroo, plus your own site and app. On those platforms, strong photography and well-presented hero items directly lift orders, so the content we shoot does double duty.

  • Proper photography of your best-selling and highest-margin dishes
  • Hero items and offers pushed to the top of each listing
  • Your own ordering site and app promoted in every video for direct orders

Targeted Local Ads to Hungry Customers

A small, tightly targeted ad budget around Stoke and Hanley puts your best food videos in front of local people at peak hunger times, driving orders on the platforms you already use.

  • Target the postcodes around Hanley and greater Stoke-on-Trent
  • Use your strongest food and box-meal reels as the ads
  • Time them around lunch, evenings and weekends
  • Track cost per order so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Meraki: With ordering already frictionless across several platforms, every bit of extra reach can turn straight into orders.
