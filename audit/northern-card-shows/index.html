What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The concept is spot on and the timing is perfect

Trading cards are having a huge moment, and you are running proper, curated, in-person shows across the north. "Helping you grow your collection, one event at a time" is a clear, likeable promise, and the mix of Pokemon, sports, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh and One Piece pulls in a wide, passionate crowd. This is a niche built for content.

✓ The demand is real and provable

Sixteen shows booked through to January, capacities up to 3,000, and vendor tables selling out. That is not hype, that is a business people already want to be part of. Selling out tables is the strongest proof point you have, and right now hardly anyone outside the room gets to see it.

✓ The grading partnership adds real trust

Working with Black Label Grading for PSA, Beckett and CGC gives collectors a reason to turn up beyond just buying and selling. It is a genuine value-add and a content story in itself, most people have no idea how grading works, and that curiosity travels.

✓ You already have an audience that wants more

Nearly 2,700 Instagram followers and around 1,900 on Facebook, off the back of only a handful of posts, tells you the appetite is there. People are following before you have even shown them much. Feed that audience properly and it grows fast.

✓ Every show is a content goldmine already happening

The pulls, the reactions, the queues at the door, the vendor hauls, the community. All of it is unfolding in the room at every event, for free. You are already creating the best short-form content in the hobby, it is just not being filmed and posted yet.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

The goal here is the one you care about most: fill the rooms with organic reach so you spend less on ads. You already have the shows, the demand and a growing audience. The job is to turn what happens at every event into content that travels, warms up each city before tickets go on sale, and cuts your reliance on paid campaigns and discount codes. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Launch TikTok, It Is Where the Hobby Lives

This is the single biggest gap and the single biggest opportunity. Card content, pack openings, big pulls, hauls and show walkthroughs, is one of the most viral things on TikTok right now, and you are not on it. Every one of your competitors and vendors is fighting for attention there, and the room you own in person is the exact content that platform rewards.

Getting on TikTok properly, with content filmed at your shows, is the fastest route to reach that costs you nothing per view.

2

Turn Every Show Into a Content Engine

Each event is a full day of ready-made short-form: the doors opening, the queue, the biggest pull of the day, a collector landing a grail card, vendor hauls, kids opening packs, the buzz of a packed hall. Filmed and cut properly, that footage is the best advert there is for the next show, because it shows exactly what people are missing if they do not come.

One event can produce weeks of content. Right now that gold is walking out the door unfilmed.

3

Post Far More, and Consistently

Around 15 Instagram posts against nearly 2,700 followers means a keen audience is being underfed. The algorithm and your followers both reward regular posting, and a hobby audience will happily watch card content every single day. The raw material from your shows is more than enough to post several times a week across every channel.

Consistency is what turns a following into a habit, and a habit into ticket sales.

4

Build Hype Before Each Show, Not Just Ads

Right now a city gets warmed up mainly through paid ads and a discount code. Organic content can do that job first: countdown reels, vendor reveals, "what to hunt for at the Sunderland show", teasers of what is coming. Warm the room with content and the ads become a smaller top-up rather than the thing carrying every ticket sale.

This is where you start spending less on marketing while still selling out.

5

Put Gary and James on Camera

People follow people, not logos. The story of two collectors building the biggest card shows in the north is exactly the kind of content the hobby loves: why you started it, what goes into setting up a 2,000-capacity room, the wins and the near-disasters. Founder-led content builds a loyal community that turns up again and again.

It also makes Northern Card Shows feel like it is run by people who genuinely love the hobby, because you do.

6

Tap Your Vendors and Attending Creators

Your vendors and the collectors coming through the door already have their own followings. Encouraging them to post from your shows, and cross-posting the best of it, multiplies your reach for free. A tagged pull video from a vendor or creator puts you in front of an audience you never had to pay to reach.

In this hobby, community reach is the cheapest and most powerful marketing there is.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film at Your Next Show

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what already happens at every event. Each one could be filmed on a phone at a single show like Sunderland on 27 September.

Idea 01
"Biggest pull of the day"
ReactionPullViral

Capture the moment someone rips a huge card at the show, the shaking hands, the face, the crowd reaction. Cut fast, lead with the reaction on frame one ("he pulled WHAT at the Sunderland show?"). Trending audio, under 20 seconds. The single most shareable format in the whole hobby.

Why it works: Genuine reactions travel further than anything scripted, and every pull is free content that makes people wish they had been in the room.
Idea 02
"POV: walking into the Sunderland Card Show"
WalkthroughAtmosphereHype

An immersive, first-person walk through the doors: the queue, the tables, the sheer amount of cards, the buzz of a packed hall. Strong hook, fast pace, big sound. Shows the scale and energy that a graphic or an ad never can.

Why it works: It sells the feeling of being there, which is exactly what turns a scroller into a ticket buyer for the next city.
Idea 03
"What to hunt for at the next show"
Pre-eventVendorsCountdown

A pre-show hype reel teasing which vendors are coming, the deals to look out for, the grading on site, and a countdown to doors. Posted in the days before each event to warm up the city organically before a single pound of ad spend.

Why it works: It builds anticipation and sells tickets through content rather than discount codes, which is exactly how you spend less on ads.
Idea 04
"How we build a 2,000-person card show"
FounderBehind the scenesStory

Gary and James to camera on the setup: the empty hall at 7am, the tables going in, the doors about to open, and the story of why you started Northern Card Shows. Honest, high-energy, a real look behind the curtain.

Why it works: People follow the people behind the brand. A founder story builds a loyal community that comes back to every show and brings friends.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more organic reach into ticket sales and repeat visitors.

A Growing Content Library You Own

Every show should leave you with a bank of clips, not just a few posts. Built up over a season, that library becomes an asset: highlight reels, best-of compilations, and proof for vendors and venues that a Northern Card Shows event is the place to be.

  • Consistent capture at every show so nothing is missed
  • Reusable clips for future promos, recaps and ads
  • A visible track record that helps sell tables and sponsorships

Email and Community Retargeting

You already collect ticket buyers through Eventbrite and your site. That list is gold. A simple flow that tells past attendees when a show is coming to their city, backed by your best content, fills seats without touching ad spend.

  • Announce each new city to people who have attended before
  • Lead with highlight reels from the last show, not just a poster
  • Turn one-time visitors into regulars who follow the whole calendar

Smarter, Smaller Paid Ads

The aim is not to kill ads, it is to make them work harder off the back of organic reach. Once your best show footage is proven organically, that same content becomes far cheaper and more effective as a targeted ad, so you spend less to sell the same tickets.

  • Use your top-performing organic reels as the ad creative
  • Target the specific city and the card communities in it
  • Track cost per ticket so every campaign earns its place

Why it fits Northern Card Shows: proven organic content lowers what you pay per ticket, which is the whole point, more sold-out rooms for less marketing spend.
