What is working

360 Sky Lounge is Newcastle's first contemporary shisha lounge. The Versace-inspired interior is stunning. Heated outdoor terraces with city views. Exclusive shisha, mocktails, desserts, milkshakes. Open 7 days a week, midday to late. The venue itself is the content.

The opportunity

4,095 Instagram followers for a venue this visually striking is a fraction of what it should be. Rooftop venues, shisha lounges, and aesthetically-driven bars are some of the highest-performing categories on TikTok and Instagram. People actively search for "best shisha in Newcastle" and "rooftop bars Newcastle." You should be the first result.

City skyline views, smoke art, mocktail pours, neon-lit interiors, dessert reveals. Every element of 360 is designed to be filmed. It just needs to be.
