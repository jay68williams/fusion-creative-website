What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Bell tents in the dunes: a king bed, a sitting area and a walk-in shower, with the orange sand of the Empty Quarter outside the window. Each tent is a set.
  • Experiences that move: camel rides and bicycles for guests, sunset dune hikes, dune bashing and jeep tours, and a seasonal date-farm tour. Each one is a short film.
  • Emirati home-style meals, breakfast and dinner included, that even locals call "a taste from home". The dining room is a scene.
  • The Dune Deck, your new open-air wellness space in the Al Arta garden among the dunes, launched this September and barely filmed.
  • Arabian Salukis on the property, forts a short drive away and a sky full of stars. Reasons to pick up a camera most days.

You do not need a new site or a new offer. The tents, the dunes, the camels and the dinners under the stars are already here, and you already take bookings direct through Reserve. Short video of the place, posted on a plan, is how the people who already follow you, and the people deciding whether a three hour drive is worth it, get a reason to book this season.

Liwa Nights posts with care, and the writing is lovely. The pattern in the numbers:

  • Movement is missing. One of your twelve most recent posts is a reel. Reels are how a page reaches people who do not follow it yet, and a desert in motion is exactly what travels.
  • The place itself is what performs. Your highest post, 509 likes, is a tent interior: "Comfort and dune-side views." The recent feed leans on landscapes, forts and heritage stills, which get fewer.
  • The moments from your own reviews are not on film. A first night under the stars, the dune bashing one guest called "what a ride", the dinner locals call a taste from home. None of those is a reel in your recent feed.

What this means. People respond when a real moment happens in a place they can picture themselves. Short video of that, made on a plan and posted across Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, is how it reaches the people weighing up the drive and the people who have never heard of you.
