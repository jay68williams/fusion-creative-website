01 · The short version

The interest is already there. It just never lands on your own channel.

GreenLand Farm has everything a short film wants: goats and ducks, moringa and date palms, fish ponds, a hydroponics nursery, a cottage and dormitories for up to twenty guests, tent camping and an outdoor party area, all set against the limestone mountains of Ras Al Khaimah. Property Finder featured you in July 2026. Travel creators who visited and tagged you drew thousands of likes each. The gap is that none of that reach is captured on your own feed or turned into a direct booking, because every enquiry becomes a manual WhatsApp or phone conversation with no booking or availability flow on your site.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A farm that is built for video: goats, fish ponds, a moringa farm, a hydroponics nursery and date palms, with the mountains behind.
  • Real places to stay and gather: a two-bedroom cottage and two dormitories for up to twenty guests, tent camping, a kids zone and an outdoor party area.
  • Proof the content travels. Three travel creators filmed a visit and pulled 826, 7,554 and 8,008 likes, and one even shared your own booking number.
  • A feature from Property Finder in July 2026 you can point back to.
  • Activities that each make a clip: hiking, cycling, fishing the ponds, the pool and a barbecue under the stars.

You do not need a bigger farm or a new website. The place already draws creators and guests. What is missing is your own steady flow of short video, and a simple way to go from a post to a direct booking, so the interest those reels create lands on your channel and turns into repeat guests.

The three posts that reach by far the most people are all made by visiting creators, not by you. The pattern:

  • Creators prove the demand. One reel drew 8,008 likes, another 7,554, a third 826 with your own booking number in the caption. People clearly want this farm stay.
  • Your own feed lands far lower. Where a like count shows on your recent posts, it reads zero. The content is pleasant, but it is photos and captions, not the moving video that reaches people who do not follow you yet.
  • Nobody owns the funnel. The reach lives on other people's accounts and stops there. None of it is captured on your channel or turned into a direct booking.

What this means. The audience exists and the place sells itself on camera. Your own steady flow of short video, posted on a plan and pointed at a page built to book, is how that interest becomes direct bookings and repeat guests instead of likes on someone else's reel.
