M'z Origin posted twelve times between 14 July and 8 August, four of them reels. Nothing has been posted since. The pattern in those posts:

  • Anticipation got replies. "THE NEXT CHAPTER" and "Watch this space transform" drew 27 and 30 likes. People responded to something coming.
  • Then nothing followed. The last post, "Sip. Read. Discover." on 8 August, drew 21 likes and 4 comments. In the posts we pulled, there is no reveal and no update since.
  • Lower counts on the product posts. "From origin to cup" drew 10 likes and 1 comment, and "Creamy, homemade, irresistible" drew 12 likes and no comments.

What this means. 1,876 people follow a cafe in Umm Al Quwain, and they responded when you announced something. Short video with your barista in frame, posted every week with a reason to come in, is how you reach them and the people nearby who have not found you yet.

07 · Your orders, direct

Let the people who already know you order direct.

Right now a customer who wants to order for delivery goes through Talabat, and an advance order goes through an Instagram message. We would not tell you to leave Talabat. It brings delivery customers and the rider. The point is the people who already know you.

Talabat has told investors its average commission from restaurants is 17% to 18%, as reported in a FAB Securities note on its Q1 2025 results, 13 May 2025. That is a group-wide average across its markets, and UAE restaurant operators have been quoted higher: a restaurant group CEO told Khaleej Times in April 2020 that apps took 25% to 30%, and Gulf News reported in February 2021 that some take 30% or 35%. You pay that share on every delivery order, including orders from regulars who might order direct.

Those regulars are the opportunity: 1,876 followers, and anyone who wants to order without the app. The delivery route we found is Talabat. With your own page they could order collection direct, message you for an advance order, and see the hours, the menu and the latest video before they come over.

What the page would be: your own address, the menu board as it is on your wall, the coffee by origin, collection ordering, a message button on every page with a pre-filled message, and tracking on the videos so you can see what each one brings back. It would be built around the photos and videos from the shoot day, and handed over to you. Your Instagram bio would point to it.

08 · The plan

A plan built around the bar, the reveal and one filming day.

  1. Week 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the first twelve videos and agree what the page needs. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and set up tracking for the new pages.

  2. Week 2
    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film at Green Belt to our shot list and brief: the first brew, a morning service, your barista, your regulars and the finished space, in one day. We direct remotely from Newcastle.

  3. Weeks 2 to 3
    Edit and deliver

    We edit and caption the videos and hand them over, ready to post on Instagram and TikTok, starting with the reveal. Your own page goes live with the menu, hours, collection orders and a message button.

  4. Handover
    Yours to keep

    You keep every video and the page. We show your team how to post the batch on a plan, so the feed stays steady after the shoot.

What the shoot gives you: a licensed UAE crew filming in your cafe to our shot list, a batch of edited short videos ready to post, and your own page with the menu, hours, collection orders and a message button. You keep every video.
