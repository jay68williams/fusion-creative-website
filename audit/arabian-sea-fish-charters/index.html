01 · The short version

Great catch content, pointed at a website nobody can read.

Arabian Sea Fish Charters runs sport fishing trips out of Fujairah, posts a new catch almost every day, and tags the captains who land them. The Instagram is alive. The website is the opposite: arabianseafishing.com is built so the whole page is drawn by a script after it loads, which means a search engine or a shared link sees only the page title, and the title still reads "Dubai and Abu Dhabi" while you run out of Fujairah. The booking machinery is actually in there, a checkout and a WhatsApp link, but nobody arriving from a post ever gets to use it.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A daily feed of real catches: marlin and sailfish caught and safely released, mahi mahi and yellowfin tuna held up on the deck. Most of the recent posts are reels.
  • Named captains your guests already follow and tag, so the trips come with faces, not just fish.
  • A catch and release billfishing story that sells itself. A guest's own reel of a first billfish drew more than any of your own posts in the same window.
  • Trips people travel for: families, first-timers and serious anglers, with the marina and the open water as the set.
  • The site already has a checkout and a WhatsApp link built in, so the hard part is done. It just needs to be found and finished.

You do not need more catches or a new boat. You post the content already. What is missing is a website that a search engine and a shared link can actually read, with your own catches as the preview and a clear way to book, so the people who find your posts land somewhere that turns them into a booking.

03 · Your website

The content is ready. The website is working against it.

arabianseafishing.com has a modern booking flow with a checkout and a WhatsApp link inside it. The trouble is everything on the page is drawn by a script after the page loads, so search engines, link previews and shared messages get almost nothing. These are the changes we would make so the site turns your posts into bookings.

06 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your boats, your captains and your catches.

Every concept uses something you already run. Six are built to bring bookings in this season. Six are built to keep your Instagram growing and feed a website people can actually find.

Videos that bring bookings

6 concepts
Reaction

The first strike

"He has never held a rod. Watch the moment it goes tight."

A first-timer in the chair, the rod loading up, the captain talking them through it, the fish coming to the boat.

Why: a first-timer is the guest most likely to ask what a trip is actually like.

What to expect

First light to the dock

"Leaving the marina at dawn. Back with a cool box full."

The marina before sunrise, the boat heading out, the first strike, the catch at the dock. One minute, in order.

Why: answers the questions a group asks before they book.

Catch and release

Send it back

"A marlin to the boat, a photo, and straight back in."

A billfish brought boat-side, unhooked and released, the way you already run it.

Why: shows the sport and the care in one clip, and it is exactly what you post.

First person

Through the angler's eyes

"The captain said cast there. So I did."

A guest's own view from the chair as the line goes out, the strike, and the fish coming up.

Why: the kind of clip people send to the friend they want to bring.

Family

The youngest captain

"Two kids. One wheel. First catch."

A family trip, the children aboard, a first fish and the smiles, filmed the way your own kids-at-the-wheel posts already look.

Why: families book ahead and book again.

This week

What is running now

"Yellowfin in town this week. Slots still open."

This week's catch at the marina, the captain naming each fish, and how to book the next weekend.

Why: gives the people who follow you a reason to book this month, not someday.

Organic that builds the audience

6 concepts
Series

Ask the captain

"What do you do when the line goes slack?"

One question from a guest, one answer from a captain, every week, filmed on the boat.

Why: a weekly series people look for, and your captains already have the answers.

Catch log

Fish of the week

"Marlin, sailfish, mahi, tuna. Here is what we landed."

A short weekly round-up of the catches, named and dated, the way your feed already does it but built to be shared.

Why: turns your daily posting habit into a series people follow.

Captain

Meet the crew

"The captain your last catch was tagged to."

One captain per film: how they read the water, where they fish, the catch they are proudest of.

Why: your guests already follow and tag the captains, so put them on camera.

Technique

How we find the fish

"Trolling, casting, jigging. Here is the difference."

Each technique in 15 seconds, the captain showing the lure, the water, the result.

Why: gives a serious angler a reason to book the longer trip.

Place

Why Fujairah

"Different coast, different water, different fish."

A short film on the Fujairah grounds and why the billfishing is here, with the marina and the mountains behind.

Why: puts the right emirate in front of the people searching for it.

Guests

Words at the dock

"First time? What did you think it would be like?"

Quick answers from guests as they step off, honest faces only.

Why: your guests' reactions, on camera, one trip at a time.

07 · The plan

A plan built around one shoot day on the water.

  1. Step 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the videos and the trip. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and line up your own catches as the share image and page text on the website.

  2. Step 2
    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list: a first-light departure from the marina and a full trip, captain and guests included, in one day.

  3. Step 3
    Edit, caption and deliver

    We edit, caption and post the videos to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Each one ends with a clear way to book, pointed at a site people can actually find.

  4. Step 4
    Handover

    We report what the videos reached and hand over every edited video for you to keep and reuse whenever you like.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos filmed on your boats by a licensed UAE crew to our shot list, edited and captioned, posted to launch them. You keep every video.
