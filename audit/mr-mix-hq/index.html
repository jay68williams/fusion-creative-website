✓ A decade of local reputation

✓ A genuinely filmable product

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the personality, the reputation and a genuinely exciting venue on the way. The job is to turn that into reach and bookings: get in front of the North East before the doors open, and make Mr Mix HQ the place people have already decided they are coming to. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Run the Venue Page Behind Your Face

Here is the crux of your reach problem. The engagement lives on you, and it drops the moment the same content moves to a faceless business page. So stop treating them as two separate things. The Mr Mix HQ page should feel like an extension of you, led by your personality, not a polished logo posting event flyers.

When the venue account carries the same energy people already follow you for, it inherits your reach instead of starting from scratch. That single shift fixes more than any posting schedule ever will.

2

Build the Launch, Do Not Just Announce It

A countdown to opening night is content gold, and most venues waste it on a single "we are open" post. We would film the whole build: the empty room, the fit-out, the first bottles going on the shelf, the sign going up, the nerves, the "this is really happening" moments. Bring people along for the journey.

By the time the doors open, the people who watched the venue come to life are the ones queuing on opening night. Anticipation sells tables, and you have months of it to bank.

3

Cocktails Are Built for Short-Form

The shake, the pour, the garnish, the reveal, the first sip, the reaction. Cocktail content performs brilliantly on Reels and TikTok because it is satisfying to watch and easy to share. You already do the hard part, you make the drinks and you have the charisma. It just needs shooting and cutting to travel.

TikTok in particular is an open goal for you and it is barely being touched. The same energy that works on your personal profile scales straight onto it, and cocktail content is exactly what that platform rewards.

4

Turn Every Experience Into Bookable Content

Bottomless brunch, quiz nights, private hire, themed events. Each one is a repeatable content format that doubles as an advert for the thing itself. A brunch hype reel sells the next brunch. A private party clip sells the next booking. The night becomes the marketing for the next night.

This is content with a job to do. It does not just build reach, it fills the calendar, which for a venue is exactly where social should pay you back.

5

Reels and TikTok for Reach, Plus Targeted Local Ads

Organic short-form video built to travel is what earns you reach with proper hooks, not event graphics that only your existing followers see. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at your local catchment, and you put your best launch and brunch content in front of exactly the people who can walk through the door.

For a venue, the maths is simple. A handful of extra covers or one private hire booking pays for a lot of content and ads, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make around a launch.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film Around the Launch

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the drinks, the build-up, the vibe and the personality. Each one could be filmed on a phone in an afternoon.

Idea 01
"The pour"
CocktailASMRReveal

A close, cinematic build of one signature cocktail: the shake, the strain, the pour, the garnish, the final look. Crisp sound, satisfying to watch, strong hook on frame one ("the drink Consett is about to be obsessed with"). Trending audio, under 20 seconds.

Why it works: Cocktail content is made to be watched and shared, and it puts a face on the venue before it opens. Endlessly repeatable with a new drink each time.
Idea 02
"Turning an empty room into Mr Mix HQ"
LaunchBuild-in-publicCountdown

A running series on the fit-out: the bare space, the transformation, the bar going in, the sign going up. Quick, honest, "come with me" energy. Each clip ends on the same hook, a countdown to opening night, so people keep coming back for the next one.

Why it works: A launch is a story with a built-in ending. The people who watch the room come to life are the ones who turn up on the first night.
Idea 03
"POV: bottomless brunch at Mr Mix HQ"
VibeBrunchBooking driver

A fast, high-energy hype reel of the experience: drinks flowing, the music, the crowd, the atmosphere. Sell the feeling of the day out, not the price list. End on a clear "book your table" prompt so the reach turns into covers.

Why it works: People book experiences they can already picture themselves in. One brunch reel sells the next month of brunches.
Idea 04
"10 years of Mr Mix, now we've got a home"
FounderStoryTrust

You to camera, straight talking, telling the story: a decade of shaking cocktails across the North East, and now a place of your own. Honest, personal, a little bit proud. Strong hook ("ten years ago I started this out of a car boot. Now look.").

Why it works: People back people. Your story is the reason the whole area will feel invested in the launch, and it is content only you can make.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into bookings and covers.

Get the Google Profile Ready From Day One

When someone hears about the new venue, the first thing they do is search it. A fully set up Google Business Profile, with photos, opening hours and a booking link, catches that interest before it drifts elsewhere, and a steady flow of reviews from night one builds trust fast.

  • Optimise for "cocktail bar Consett" and the surrounding towns
  • Funnel happy guests into a fresh Google review after every visit
  • Keep it fed with launch photos, event nights and brunch shots
  • Make sure booking and enquiry are one tap away

Content That Fills the Calendar

The point of the reach is bookings. Quiz nights, brunches, themed events and private hire all need bums on seats, and the right content aimed at the right night keeps the diary full rather than just the feed busy.

  • Event-specific reels that sell the next date, not last week's
  • Story-led booking prompts and simple "save your table" links
  • A private hire angle for parties, work dos and celebrations

Targeted Local Ads to Fill Opening Night

Because a table or a private booking is worth real money, a small local ad budget pays back quickly. Tightly targeted around your catchment, it puts your best launch and brunch content in front of the people who can actually come.

  • Target your local towns and postcodes across the North East
  • Use your strongest cocktail and hype reels as the ads
  • Drive opening-night interest and brunch bookings straight to you
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Mr Mix HQ: A full opening night and a few private bookings cover a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a new venue can do.
