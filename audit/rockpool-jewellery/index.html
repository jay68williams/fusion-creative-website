02 · Your Meta ads

Two good instincts, switched off within a week.

Your ads run from your page "Rockpool Jewellery" (1.6K followers). Meta's Ad Library shows two in total, and none running now.

What we found across your ads

1

Six days is not enough for Meta to learn

Meta needs a steady run to find the people who buy. Your two ads ran for one day and five days, so neither had the time to learn who your customers are.

Fix: one always-on campaign, with new videos added each week rather than ads started and stopped.

2

No piece, price or next step up front

Both open with a reflection on craft and stories. In the opening lines we could read, neither names a piece, a price or your WELCOME10 code for a first order.

Fix: keep the story, then end every ad on one piece, its price and a link straight to it.

3

Nothing running into Christmas

Your made-to-order rings take six to eight weeks to craft. Anyone who wants one under the tree needs to see it in October, and right now nobody is being shown it.

Fix: start this month, with a clear "order by" date in the ads.

    Audit and tidy

    Two filming days

    You at the bench carving and casting, on the shore at Whitley Bay and Tynemouth, and your pieces being worn.

  3. Week 3
    Always-on campaigns live

    One campaign for new buyers across the UK who love the coast and handmade jewellery, one for people who have visited your website, both running continuously.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New videos added every week. Gift ads through November, order-by reminders as the date gets close, and the budget moved behind what is selling.

  5. Month 3
    Review on orders

    We report orders, sales and return on ad spend, and set the plan for the new year on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on orders and sales. You keep every video.
