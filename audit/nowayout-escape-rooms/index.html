01 · The short version

A big audience, and a feed it barely reacts to.

NoWayOut runs three escape rooms across Dubai, at JLT, City Walk and Sheikh Zayed Road, with eight rooms from Psycho and Houdini to The Castle and House of Bella. Games run 60 minutes for 2 to 8 players, in English, with online booking and a 10% discount. Your own site calls you the number one escape rooms in Dubai and points to a Tripadvisor "Top 10 Things To Do in Dubai for Fun & Games" badge. You post almost every day to 16,000 followers. The gap is not effort. It is that the posts land in single to low double digit likes, and the moments that travel, real players' faces as the timer runs down, are barely on your feed.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Three Dubai locations, JLT, City Walk and Sheikh Zayed Road, and eight rooms from Psycho and Houdini to The Castle and House of Bella. Each room is a set.
  • Rooms across every genre: horror, mystery, adventure and live actor, 60 minutes for 2 to 8 players. Each genre is a different film.
  • A Tripadvisor "Top 10 Things To Do in Dubai for Fun & Games" badge, by your own post. People already rate you.
  • A corporate and events side: by your own site, more than 1,300 corporate events and over 900 companies, plus birthdays, hen and stag nights and proposals. Each is a reason to film.
  • Online booking with card, Apple Pay and PayPal and a 10% online discount. The route from a video to a booking is already built.
  • Real player reactions already travel: a creator's reel inside your rooms outperformed your own posts. That is the format to own.

You do not need new rooms or a new website. The scares, the timers and the faces are already in every game. Short video of real players reacting, posted on a plan, gives the 16,000 who follow you and the people searching for things to do in Dubai a reason to book this week.

07 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your rooms, your game masters and your players.

Every concept uses something you already run. Six are built to bring bookings in this season. Six are built to keep your Instagram growing.

Videos that bring bookings

6 concepts
Reaction

The last ten seconds

"Nine seconds. One clue left. Watch their faces."

A team against the clock, the buzzer, the reaction. Filmed inside House of Bella.

Why: the final buzzer is the moment every group wants to feel.

What to expect

Your first escape room, start to finish

"The briefing, the door locking, the win. One minute."

A first group from arrival to finish: the rules, the lock, the panic, the celebration.

Why: answers the question a first-timer or group organiser has before they book.

First person

You are holding the torch

"It is pitch black, and the clue is somewhere in here."

A player's own view through a dark room, the torch, the find, the jump.

Why: the clip people send to the friend they want to drag along.

Group

Who cracks under pressure?

"Six friends. One obvious panicker."

A team of friends under the clock, one of them losing it, honest faces only.

Why: people tag the friend who would panic.

This week

October room of the month

"House of Bella at City Walk. She has been waiting for you."

This month's featured room, built from your own post, and the slots open this weekend.

Why: gives followers a reason to book this month, not someday.

Corporate

Your work team, locked in

"Sixty minutes to find out who actually leads."

A company group through a room, the teamwork and the finish, built on your corporate side.

Why: your 1,300 plus corporate events, made into the film that books the next one.

Organic that builds the audience

6 concepts
Series

Game master reacts

"They went the wrong way. Again."

A game master watching a group on the monitors, every week, built on your best recent post.

Why: a weekly series people look for, and it is your strongest format already.

Rooms

Eight rooms in sixty seconds

"Psycho. Houdini. The Castle. Bella. Pick your nightmare."

One quick scare from each room, across three locations, in a single clip.

Why: shows the full range most followers do not know you have.

Reactions

Scared or just dramatic?

"Same jump, eight different faces."

Quick cuts of players at the first scare, honest reactions only.

Why: reaction supercuts are among the formats that travel furthest.

Behind the scenes

Resetting the room

"Ninety seconds to make it terrifying again."

The reset between groups, the props, the hidden mechanisms.

Why: people book rooms they have seen the craft behind.

Guests

Did you make it out?

"Winners on the left. The rest of you, over here."

Players at the door after the game, the winners and the ones who ran out of time.

Why: real guests, on camera, one group at a time.

Place

Three locations, one dare

"JLT. City Walk. Sheikh Zayed Road. Where do you start?"

One room from each location, a short film per site.

Why: sends players to the nearest of your three Dubai venues.

08 · The plan

A plan built around your rooms.

  1. Step 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the twelve videos. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and set up the Meta pixel on nowayout.ae.

  2. Step 2
    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list: real players through a room, a game master at the monitors and the resets, at one location, all with permission.

  3. Step 3
    Edit, caption and deliver

    We edit, caption and post the videos to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube. Each one ends with the room and how to book.

  4. Step 4
    Handover

    We report what the videos reached and what they brought in, then hand over every video for you to keep and reuse.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos filmed in your rooms by a licensed UAE crew to our shot list, edited and captioned, posted to launch them. You keep every video.
