Firas Sweets posts almost daily, and the food looks the part. The pattern in your recent posts:

  • Most posts are a still photo, not a reel. The stills are beautiful, but a still rarely reaches past the people who already follow you. Your one recent reel, a kunafa clip, still beat your photos.
  • The hook is buried. Most captions open with a line of description and then hashtags. A hook in the first line, in Arabic and English, is what makes a stranger stop scrolling.
  • Your best story is underused. Nabulsi kunafa made since 1993, the cheese pull, the live station, the occasion trays. That is a weekly series, not a single photo.

What this means. People respond when a real person has a real moment with the food. Short video of the pour and the pull, posted on a plan, is how it reaches the people who follow you and the people searching for kunafa in Dubai.

06 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your kunafa, your makers and your occasions.

Every concept uses something you already make. Six are built to bring orders and footfall in this week. Six are built to keep your Instagram growing.

Videos that bring orders

6 concepts
Satisfying

The cheese pull

"Wait for the pull."

The nabulsi kunafa cut and lifted, the cheese stretching, the syrup still glistening. One clean shot, slowed right down.

Why: the pull is the single most shared moment a kunafa shop has.

Occasion

Names on the tray

"Tell us the names. We will pipe them."

An engagement tray being piped in pistachio, start to the finished reveal, then boxed.

Why: every couple and family planning an occasion is a direct order.

New item

This week’s new item

"New this week at Firas."

The newest sweet made, plated and tasted at the counter, ending on how to order it.

Why: turns a quiet post into a reason to come in now.

Delivery

Kunafa to your door

"Frozen today, hot at yours tonight."

A frozen kunafa tray from the online store, packed, delivered, then heated and pulled at home.

Why: sells the online store to people who cannot reach the branch.

Live station

The live station

"We will make it in front of your guests."

The kunafa live station at a party, the tray turning, guests watching, first plates served.

Why: books the live station package for weddings and work events.

Taste test

Rough or soft?

"Kheshna or naame. Pick a side."

Two guests try the rough and the soft nabulsi kunafa and call it, honest faces only.

Why: people tag the friend who would disagree, and both are on your menu.

07 · The plan

A plan built around your kunafa and your occasions.

  1. Week 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the twelve videos. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and set up a Meta pixel on firassweets.net.

  2. Week 2
    The shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list: the kunafa made at the counter, an occasion tray piped, the live station and the online-store favourites, in one day.

    Edit, caption and post

    We edit, caption and post the videos to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Each one ends with how to order online or which branch to visit.

  4. Handover
    Review together

    We report what the videos reached and what they brought in, then hand over every video for you to keep and reuse.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos filmed at your counter and kitchen by a licensed UAE crew to our shot list, edited and captioned, posted to launch them. You keep every video.
