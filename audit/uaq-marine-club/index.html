01 · The short version

A club full of things to film, and a feed that has gone quiet.

UAQ Marine Club calls itself a secluded spot of paradise, a family getaway far from the hustle of the city, with watersports, kayaking and canoeing, fishing, houseboat tours around the mangrove islands, a high ropes course, camping, Barasti chalets, a pool and a restaurant by the beach. Your guests love it enough to post their own reels tagging you. The gap is that your own account does not. Your newest post is from 29 April 2026, the last reel you posted yourself is from March 2025, and your recent posts sit on single digit likes, so the activities that would drive bookings are being filmed by visitors, not by you.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Watersports and kayaking on a calm, sheltered lagoon. Your own page already teaches the basics: paddling, turning and self-rescue. Each one is a short video.
  • Houseboat tours around the mangrove islands, described on your site as a private party on the water. That is a film people share.
  • A high ropes course, zip line, archery, raft building and a maze. A family day with a dozen moments to film.

07 · Your bookings, direct

Give people one place to book.

Right now a booking means finding an email address or a phone number. These are the facts around it and what we would change.

Your site asks visitors to book by emailing reservations@, oliver@ or schooladventures@, or to call. There is no online booking and no single enquiry button, and the search widget on the home page leads to the wrong continents. For a weekend plan, that is one hurdle too many.

In the UAE, 85% of residents say they would rather deal with a business on WhatsApp, and 88% say it is the easiest way to get a quick answer (Zbooni with YouGov, 1,000 residents, 12 February 2024). A simple WhatsApp enquiry button next to each activity, on a site that loads over https, turns the video we make into a message you can answer in seconds, and keeps the whole booking with you.
