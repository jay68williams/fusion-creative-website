03 · Your website

A working site, with a few things pulling against it.

Your site has the menus, the branch list and ordering. These are the changes we would make so it works harder for the video.

What we sawWhy it mattersFix
Orders leave your siteOnline ordering runs on a Radyes page, and delivery sits on a Magicpin listing, so the customer leaves your brand at the point of order.Keep a clear order button on your own pages, with collection and dine-in listed, and link out only for delivery.
The branches are a locatorThe branches page is a location search with a radius filter. Its text does not list the branch addresses, so a visitor has to search to find theirs.List each branch in text with its address, opening hours and phone number, and link each one to its video.
No video on the homepageThe homepage text leads with the menu and ordering. Its page carries no video, so the team and the counter are not shown up front.Put the latest short video above the fold, and refresh it each week from the shoot day.

07 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your counter, your team and your neighbours.

Every idea uses something you already have. Six are built to bring people in this week. Six are built to keep your Instagram growing.

Videos that bring people in

6 concepts
Skit

Extra toum, extra drama

"She asked for extra toum. Here is what happened next."

A customer asks for extra toum and the team keep count. Built on the line from your October reel.

Why: your most-liked reel already runs on this moment.

Challenge

Guess the order

"Shawarma, falafel or both? Guess what they ordered."

Guests read the menu board, guess the order, then the answer arrives at the table.

Why: a question with an answer at the end keeps the viewer watching to the end.

Local

Open since 1979

"Open since 1979. Come and see why it has lasted."

Regulars and new customers at the counter, asked why they come back. Branches in two cities, one answer.

Why: turns a long run into a reason to try it this week.

Dish

The cheese manakish

"Cheese manakish, straight from the oven."

The dough, the cheese and the pull, filmed close, with the sound up.

Why: your May 2025 manakish reel drew 112 likes. A tighter cut with a hook could do more.

Branches

Which branch is nearest?

"Dubai and Sharjah. Find your nearest Al Mallah."

Quick cuts of each branch, with the address and phone number on screen.

Why: people look for a branch when they are already hungry.

Ordering

Order it, collect it

"Order on the app, collect it at the counter."

A screen recording of the app, then the wrap handed over the counter. Shows the loyalty points too.

Why: the app is already built, so the video only has to show it working.

Organic that builds the audience

6 concepts
Series

Behind the counter

"Every wrap starts at this counter. Here is the first cut."

Short weekly episodes from the team at work, one station each.

Why: a series people come back for.

People

Meet the team

"Meet the people who make your wrap."

One team member a week, in their own words, filmed in the room.

Why: people order from places they feel they know.

Ramadan

Iftar rush, from the counter

"Iftar at the counter, filmed from start to finish."

An iftar service through the evening, cut into one short film.

Why: your nine reels from February and March drew 18 to 95 likes each. One longer film is the next step.

Branches

Dubai and Sharjah

"What guests notice first, in each branch."

A walk through two branches, with the team and the wrap.

Why: shows the group is one place across two cities.

Reactions

First bite

"What did you order, and what is the first bite?"

Guests at the table, asked one question each, with the wrap in hand.

Why: a person on camera is the format of your 38K reel.

Loyalty

Points on every wrap

"Earn points on the app, then spend them here."

The loyalty points explained in 20 seconds, with a regular at the counter.

Why: gives regulars a reason to open the app.
