Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the brand, the space and proof your content can travel. The job before September is to convert that into local bookings: get in front of the right people in Newcastle and make Body Image Fitness the studio they want to try. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Aim the Reach at Newcastle, Not the World

A viral video that reaches everywhere is fun, but it does not fill a class in Heaton. The fix is content and targeting built around this city: local hooks, Newcastle references, location tags, and a small ad budget pointed at your postcodes. You have shown the content can fly, so let's make sure it lands where people can actually book.

Reach only pays the bills when it reaches people who can walk through your door, and that is entirely fixable.

2

Make the September Timetable a Launch Moment

Your biggest ever timetable, the most instructors, more classes than ever. That is a story, not just a schedule. A run of short videos counting it down, introducing the new classes and instructors, and showing what a session actually feels like turns a quiet update into an event people book onto.

A launch given the content it deserves fills classes from day one instead of hoping word of mouth catches up.

3

Sell the Feeling, Not the Fitness

People do not choose you for the equipment, they choose you because of how it feels to walk in: welcoming, colourful, no judgement, no comparison. That feeling is your whole edge, and it is the thing most gym content completely misses. Film the atmosphere, the laughs, the community, the moment a nervous first timer relaxes.

The person who is put off by a big intimidating gym is your ideal member, and this is the content that reaches them.

4

Put the Instructors on Camera

People book a class because of who is teaching it. Your instructors are the reason members come back, so let new people meet them before they ever book. Quick, warm, personality-led clips of each instructor, what their class is like and who it is for, build trust and take the fear out of trying somewhere new.

Familiar faces turn a scroll into a booking, especially for someone deciding whether to walk through the door for the first time.

5

Turn Members Into Proof and Reach

Short member stories, first class nerves to feeling at home, are the most persuasive content a studio can post, because they answer the real question: will I fit in here. Pair that with encouraging members to film and tag you in your very taggable space, and your community becomes an engine that reaches their friends across Newcastle.

Nothing sells a class like a real person who was nervous, came anyway, and now would not miss it.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film Around Your September Launch

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already have: the space, the classes, the instructors and the community. Each one could be filmed on a phone in a single session at the studio.

Idea 01
"The most colourful studio in Newcastle"
SpaceRevealLocal

A fast, punchy walkthrough of the studio in all its colour, set to trending audio, with a strong local hook on frame one ("POV: you found the least intimidating gym in Newcastle"). Show the space, the vibe, a class in full swing. Under 20 seconds. Location tagged Heaton.

Why it works: Your space is the hook other gyms do not have, and a local frame turns scrollers into people who realise it is round the corner from them.
Idea 02
"Our biggest ever timetable drops in September"
LaunchCountdownClasses

A launch reel building anticipation for September: new classes, new instructors, a quick taste of each session. Lead with the excitement ("we have never done this many classes"). End on a clear call to book your spot while there is room, because there are only ten.

Why it works: It turns a timetable update into an event, and scarcity (max 10 per class) gives people a real reason to book now rather than later.
Idea 03
"Nervous about your first class? Watch this."
CommunityTrustFeeling

Walk a first timer through exactly what happens: where to go, what to wear, how welcoming it is, no comparison and no judgement. Warm, honest, reassuring. Show real members, real smiles, the moment the nerves disappear.

Why it works: It speaks straight to the person your brand is built for, the one put off by big intimidating gyms, and removes the only thing stopping them booking.
Idea 04
"Meet the instructor" series
InstructorsPersonalitySeries

A short, warm clip for each instructor: who they are, what their class is like, who it is perfect for. Personality first, a few seconds of them teaching, a friendly invite to come along. A repeatable series that introduces the whole September team.

Why it works: People book classes because of who is teaching them. Meeting the instructor first builds trust fast and makes booking feel like seeing a familiar face.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into booked classes.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone decides to try a studio, they search "fitness classes near me" and read reviews before they book. Your Google profile is doing quiet, heavy lifting and deserves to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Optimise the profile for "fitness classes Heaton" and "gym Newcastle" and the surrounding areas
  • Funnel happy members into a fresh Google review after a great class
  • Add photos of the studio, classes and the new timetable regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Make Booking Effortless From Every Video

Every piece of content should have a clear, single next step to book a class. The easier and more obvious the path from watching to booking, the more of your new reach turns into people actually in the room.

  • A clear "book a class" link in bio and on every relevant post
  • A simple first class or taster offer to lower the barrier for newcomers
  • Consistent calls to action so no one who is interested drifts away

Targeted Local Ads to Fill Classes

Because a member who joins comes back week after week, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around Newcastle puts your best studio and class content in front of the people most likely to book.

  • Target people in Heaton and the surrounding Newcastle postcodes
  • Use your strongest studio and community videos as the ads
  • Drive a steady flow of class bookings and taster sign ups
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Body Image Fitness: One new regular covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a studio can do.
