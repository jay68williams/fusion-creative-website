What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The location does half the work for you

Right on Longsands, with the tide, the surfers and those sunsets, you have a setting most hospitality businesses would pay a fortune to fake. Every coffee, every plate and every event already has a backdrop built for scrolling. That is a genuine, rare advantage.

✓ Twenty years of loyalty and a real story

A family-run business that has looked after locals and visitors on the same beach for over two decades has something no new opening can buy: trust and a story. People do not just come for the food, they come for Crusoe's. That heritage is gold for content.

✓ You are already on all the right platforms

Instagram, TikTok and a large Facebook following are already in place, so you are not starting from scratch. The habit of posting is there. The gap is not presence, it is format and consistency, and that is the easy part to fix.

✓ The offer is broad and endlessly filmable

Coffee and famous muffins by day, street food skewers, seafood and koftas on the grill, cocktails on the covered terrace, then live music and comedy at night. That is a full calendar of visual, shareable moments most venues would love to have.

✓ The events already give you a reason to post

Supper clubs, comedy nights and live music are ready-made content and a clear reason for people to book. Right now they sell mostly to the people who already follow you. Filmed properly, each one can reach a whole new audience across Tyneside.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the location, the food and the loyalty. The job is to turn that into reach: get Crusoe's in front of people across Tyneside who have not been down yet, and turn that reach into covers, event tickets and function bookings. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Lead With the View, Every Single Time

Your setting is the hook other venues wish they had, so use it on frame one. Open on the beach, the tide, the sunset, the terrace filling up, then bring in the coffee or the food. That opening shot is what stops the scroll and makes someone think, I need to be there this weekend.

The view is your unfair advantage. Put it front and centre and it does the hard work of earning attention for you.

2

Make the Street Food Travel

Food is some of the highest-performing content on Reels and TikTok, and yours is made for it: skewers on the grill, koftas, seafood, the sizzle, the close-up, the first bite. Shot properly, with a strong hook and the right sound, a single dish can reach far more people than a menu photo ever will.

Turn the plates you already serve into short video built to travel, and the kitchen becomes your best advert for filling tables.

3

Sell the Nights Out, Not Just the Days

Live music, comedy and supper clubs are exactly the kind of thing people share and tag their mates in. Filming the energy of a night, the crowd, the act, the terrace at golden hour, gives you content that sells the next event before tickets even go on sale.

Each event becomes a piece of content, and that content fills the next one. It is the cheapest, most effective way to keep the calendar busy.

4

Put the Family and the 20 Years on Camera

People connect with people, and your story is a good one. The family behind the counter, the regulars, twenty years of running the best spot on the beach, the little rituals only Crusoe's has. That is the personality-led content that builds a following, not just a feed.

Faces and story turn a cafe into a place people feel part of, and that loyalty is what keeps them coming back and bringing friends.

5

Scale Short-Form for Reach, Then Add Targeted Local Ads

You are on the platforms, but photo posts mostly reach the people who already follow you. Short-form video built with proper hooks is what earns new reach. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget around Tyneside, aimed at your busiest days, your events and your function packages, and you put your best content in front of people actively deciding where to go.

A full terrace, a sold-out supper club or one booked function pays for a lot of content and ad spend, which makes this one of the highest-return moves you can make.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the view, the food, the nights out and the people. Each one could be filmed on a phone in a single shift.

Idea 01
"The best table on Longsands"
LocationPOVSunset

A slow, cinematic POV: the walk down to the beach, the terrace, a coffee or a cocktail set down with the tide behind it. Strong hook on frame one ("this might be the best seat on the North East coast"). Trending calm audio. Under 20 seconds. Pure setting envy.

Why it works: Your view is the scroll-stopper. Lead with it and people save it, send it to a mate and plan a visit.
Idea 02
"Skewers on the grill" sizzle reel
Street foodASMRClose-up

Tight, satisfying macro shots of the street food: skewers hitting the grill, the flip, the char, seafood, koftas, the plate landing on the terrace. Sound up on the sizzle. Quick cuts, one hero dish, end on someone tucking in with the sea behind them.

Why it works: Food and satisfying sound are two of the most shared things on short-form. This is the content that makes people hungry for a table.
Idea 03
"A night at Crusoe's"
EventsEnergySocial proof

A fast montage of an event night: the terrace at golden hour, drinks going out, the live act or comedy, the crowd laughing, the buzz. Hook it with "this is a Friday night at Crusoe's" and end on the next event date so people can book.

Why it works: Nothing sells the next event like the last one looking unmissable. Fear of missing out fills seats.
Idea 04
"20 years on this beach"
StoryFamilyHeritage

A short, warm piece to camera from the family, or a voiceover over old and new clips: what it means to run Crusoe's, the regulars, the famous muffins, why you are still here. Honest, local, a little bit proud. The story only you can tell.

Why it works: People back businesses they feel something for. Twenty years of heritage builds a following, not just a view count.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into covers, tickets and bookings.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone searches "cafe Tynemouth", "food on Longsands" or "where to eat by the beach", your Google profile and reviews decide whether they walk in. That listing is doing quiet, heavy lifting and deserves to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised for Tynemouth, Longsands and the coast searches
  • Funnel happy customers into fresh Google reviews while they are still on the terrace
  • Keep photos of the food, the view and the events up to date
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Make Events and Functions Easy to Book

Every reel about a supper club, comedy night or private function should lead somewhere simple to book. The less friction between someone seeing the content and holding a table, the more of that new reach turns into real revenue.

  • A clear events and functions page the content can point to
  • Simple enquiry and booking capture for private hire and parties
  • Event dates pinned and linked from social so nothing is missed

Targeted Local Ads Around Tyneside

Because a full terrace, a sold-out event or a booked function is high value, a small, tightly targeted budget pays back fast. We can put your best view, food and event content in front of the right people at the right moment.

  • Target Tyneside, day trippers and the coastal postcodes
  • Use your strongest view, food and event reels as the ads
  • Push harder around events, sunny weekends and function season
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Crusoe's: One extra function or a full event covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a venue can do.
