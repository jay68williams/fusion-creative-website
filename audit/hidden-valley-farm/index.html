01 · The short version

A large audience, a beautiful setting, and a feed that never moves.

Hidden Valley sits among the limestone mountains off Masafi Road, a 25 minute drive from Fujairah city. The place has everything a short film wants: a four-bedroom farm home with an outdoor pool and gardens, two glamping units, tent camping, an outdoor party area, and even an outdoor cinema with the mountains behind. You have built an audience of 18,000. The gap is what that audience is being shown: the twelve most recent posts are all static branded graphics, none is a reel, and they draw three to seven likes each. And every booking is still a WhatsApp conversation worked out by hand, with no booking or availability flow on the site.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A setting built for video: the mountains, the gardens, the pool, the glamping tents and the party area lit up at night.
  • Real places to stay and gather: a four-bedroom farm home, two glamping units, tent camping, and an outdoor party area for events.
  • An audience most farm stays would envy: 18,000 Instagram followers already following along.
  • A full offer to film: stays, camping, birthdays, weddings and proposals, corporate days, wellness and dining, all on your own pages.
  • Guests who already love it. Your site carries a review that ends "will recommend to my colleagues".

You do not need a bigger farm or a new audience. The place and the following are already here. What is missing is short video of the setting, the stays and the evenings, posted on a plan, and a simple way to go from a post to a direct booking, so 18,000 followers become repeat guests instead of three likes on a still.

03 · Your website

A warm site, with no way to go from interested to booked.

hiddenvalley.ae shows the farm home, the glamping, the camping and the events, and the photos are your own. What it does not have is a way to actually book, or any moving video. Every enquiry becomes a WhatsApp conversation by hand. These are the changes we would make so the site turns interest into direct bookings.

What we sawWhy it mattersFix
Every booking is WhatsApp, by handOn every page the only call to action is "message us on WhatsApp for availability". There is no way to see dates or request a stay, so each booking is worked out one message at a time.Add a simple enquiry and availability flow with a pre-filled WhatsApp button, so interest becomes a booking in one step.
No prices anywhereThe site describes the farm home, the glamping, the camping and the events, but a visitor cannot see what any of it costs, so many leave before they ask.Show clear starting points for each stay and package, so people can picture it and come to you ready to book.
Template text that is not your farmSome pages still carry generic hotel template wording, including room descriptions that read "view beach" and "breakfast included". Your farm is in the mountains, so this reads as unfinished.Replace it with your own rooms, your mountains and your own words, so the site matches the place guests actually arrive to.
No Meta pixel on the siteGoogle Analytics and Google Ads are in place, but we found no Meta pixel. The people who watch your videos cannot be grouped and shown the next one.Add the pixel, so anyone who watches a stay or opens a booking page can be reached again with the next video.

Hidden Valley posts regularly, which is the hard part done. The pattern in the twelve most recent posts we read:

  • Every recent post is a static graphic. A photo with your logo and a line of text laid over it. Not one of the twelve is a reel, and reels are how a page reaches people who do not follow it yet.
  • The numbers show it. On an account of 18,000 followers, these posts draw three to seven likes. The audience is there, but a still with text on it gives them little reason to stop, watch or share.
  • The setting is doing none of the work. The mountains, the pool, the tents at night and the party lights are exactly what moves on video. On the feed they sit still behind a caption.

What this means. The audience exists and the place sells itself on camera. Short video of the setting, the stays and the evenings, posted on a plan and pointed at a page built to book, is how 18,000 followers become direct bookings and repeat guests instead of three likes on a still.

07 · The plan

A plan built around one shoot day at the farm.

    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the videos and the stays to feature. We write the shot list and brief for the crew, and plan the booking page your posts will point to.

  2. Step 2
    One shoot day

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list: a stay from check in to morning, the glamping, the pool, the setting and the party area, in one day.

    Edit, caption and deliver

    We edit, caption and grade every clip from our studio in Newcastle, each one ending with a clear way to book direct.

  4. Step 4
    Handover

    We hand over every edited video for you to keep and reuse, with a simple plan for what goes where and when.
