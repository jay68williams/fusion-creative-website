A strong reputation, and no ads behind it.

What we found

Your best proof stays on your website

The day and night footage slider and the old and new alarm photos are exactly what a homeowner wants to see. Without ads, they only reach people who already found you.

3

The dark nights have started for others

L&B Security started two new CCTV ads on 2 and 3 October, and CVS Security launched its winter CCTV offers on 21 September. Each week without ads is a week Meta learns nothing about who books with you.

Fix: one always-on campaign, live within three weeks, with new videos added every week.

  • A real installer beats a product shot. L&B's newest CCTV ad opens on an installer in a branded hoodie holding a camera. People are letting someone onto their property, so they want to see who.
  • Footage is the proof. CVS ran an ad of night footage from four cameras for almost a year. Your own day and night footage is sharper and more local.
