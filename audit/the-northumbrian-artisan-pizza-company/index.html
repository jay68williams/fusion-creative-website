What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The product is genuinely special

Four-day sourdough BIGA bases, wood-fired, made to order. That is real craft, and it is a world away from the standard takeaway pizza most people expect. A product this good is the easiest thing in the world to make crave-worthy on camera, and right now that craft is barely being shown off where new customers actually look.

✓ The setting sells itself

Budle Bay, Waren Mill, right on the England Coast Path near Bamburgh, with walkers, holidaymakers and coastal footfall passing by. Most food businesses fight to look this good. You just have to point a camera at it. The location is not a backdrop, it is a huge part of the pull.

✓ The local supplier story is gold

Doddington Dairy ice cream, Northumbrian Cheese Company, Hammond Charcuterie, Northumbrian Pantry, Marlish Waters. Sourcing this local is exactly the kind of story people love to watch and share, and it is proof of quality before anyone has even tasted a slice.

✓ You are already showing up

An active Instagram and a live Facebook page mean the habit of posting is already there, which is the hardest part for most small food businesses. The issue is not effort, it is format, and format is the easy thing to fix.

✓ The reviews and word of mouth are warm

People who find you clearly love it, and that goodwill is doing quiet work in the background. Turn that into content people can see before they visit, and word of mouth stops being a happy accident and starts becoming a reliable stream of new footfall.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the product, the setting and the supplier story. The job is to turn that into reach: get in front of the tourists heading to the coast and the locals within driving distance, and make the Pizza Company the place people plan their trip around. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Turn the Pizza Itself Into Short-Form Video

The dough stretch, the wood fire, the leopard-spotted crust coming out of the oven, the first slice pull, the toppings going on. Food this good was made for close-up, slow-motion, sound-on video. A single great pizza reel does more for cravings and footfall than a month of flat photos.

This is the single biggest opportunity: your product is already brilliant, it just needs filming in the format that actually travels.

2

Sell the Day Out, Not Just the Pizza

You are not only selling a pizza, you are selling a wood-fired pizza with a coast-path view, a walk on the beach, an ice cream in the sun. That is a whole day out, and it is exactly what people search for when planning a trip to Bamburgh and the Northumberland coast.

Content that shows the setting, the drive out, the sunset slice, turns you from a takeaway into a destination people build a day around.

3

Get on TikTok, Where Food Travels Furthest

There is no TikTok in your name yet, which means an open goal. Food and small independents built on genuine craft do enormous numbers there, and the same reels you make for Instagram can run on TikTok to reach a whole new audience of people looking for exactly this.

You are already active on Instagram, so this is not starting from scratch, it is scaling what you have onto the platform where it spreads fastest.

4

Get a Website of Your Own

Right now you rely on a SumUp booking link, which does a job but it is not a home. A simple site of your own gives people one place to find your opening days, your menu, your story and how to order, and it lets you show up in Google when someone searches "wood-fired pizza near Bamburgh" or "pizza Northumberland coast".

A destination business needs a destination people can find, follow and trust before they set off.

5

Beat the Seasonality With a Following

A seasonal, four-days-a-week trade lives or dies on being front of mind at the right moment. A real following, fed with content, means you can fill the quieter days, remind people you are open this weekend, and lean into scarcity ("open four days, get it while it is here") rather than hoping people just happen to walk past.

Reach built now compounds. By next season you are the coast pizza spot people already follow, not the hidden gem they wish they had found sooner.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Season

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the pizza, the place, the suppliers and the buzz of a busy hatch. Each one could be filmed on a phone across a single trading day.

Idea 01
"Four days to make. Four minutes to sell out."
ProductCraftSatisfying

The journey of one pizza: the four-day sourdough, the stretch, the toppings, into the wood fire, the leopard crust, the first slice pull. Close-up, sound on, satisfying. Strong hook on frame one ("this pizza takes four days to make"). Trending audio. Under 25 seconds.

Why it works: Craft plus a great cheese pull is one of the most reliably shareable things on social, and it shows exactly why you are worth the drive.
Idea 02
"A wood-fired pizza with this view"
SettingDay outDestination

The full experience: the drive out along the coast, Budle Bay, the hatch, the pizza in hand, the beach, a Doddington Dairy ice cream to finish. Sell the day, not just the slice. Tag the location so it reaches people planning a trip to Bamburgh.

Why it works: It turns you from a takeaway into a destination people build a day out around, which is exactly how tourists decide where to go.
Idea 03
"Everything on this pizza is from Northumberland"
LocalSuppliersStory

A quick tour of the local suppliers behind the menu: the cheese, the charcuterie, the dairy, the water, all made within a few miles. Show the ingredients, name the makers, land it on the finished pizza. Proud, local, genuine.

Why it works: Provenance is proof of quality before anyone tastes it, and people love to share and support a genuinely local supply chain.
Idea 04
"Open four days. Get it before it's gone."
ScarcityThis weekendFootfall

A punchy weekly "we are open this weekend" reel: the fire lit, the first pizzas going out, the queue building, the sell-out board. Lead with the days and the hook ("we are only open four days, here is when to catch us"). A simple, repeatable format that drives footfall on the day.

Why it works: Scarcity and a clear "come now" call to action is what turns followers into people actually turning up at the hatch that weekend.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into footfall.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When a family is deciding where to eat on a day out to the coast, they search and they read reviews before they set off. Your Google profile is doing quiet, heavy lifting, and it deserves to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised for searches like "pizza near Bamburgh" and "food Northumberland coast"
  • Nudge every happy customer into a fresh Google review
  • Keep opening days, photos and menu bang up to date, especially around the season
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

A Website of Your Own

A simple, beautiful site gives people one home for your story, menu, opening days and how to order, instead of relying on a booking link. It also lets you own your presence in Google rather than renting it on someone else's platform.

  • Menu, opening days, location and directions all in one place
  • Ordering and bookings joined up, not buried in a third-party link
  • Built to show up when people search for pizza on the Northumberland coast

Targeted Local and Tourist Ads

A small, tightly targeted budget puts your best pizza and setting reels in front of two perfect audiences: locals within driving distance and holidaymakers heading to the Bamburgh area.

  • Target the towns and postcodes within a comfortable drive
  • Reach visitors planning a trip to the Northumberland coast
  • Use your strongest pizza and "open this weekend" reels as the ads
  • Track it so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits the Pizza Company: a busy trading day fills fast, so putting the right content in front of the right people at the right moment pays back quickly.
