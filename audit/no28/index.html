What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The hidden gem is a ready-made hook

A secret staircase tucked into the Grainger Market entrance is the kind of thing people love to discover and share. Most venues have to invent a reason to be talked about. You already have one built into the building.

✓ Bottomless brunch is already an event

Your bottomless brunches are billed as award-winning and run every day. Brunch is a group booking by nature, birthdays, hen dos, catch-ups, so every table that sits down is a potential piece of content and a reason for the next group to book.

✓ You cover the whole day

Pies and Sunday roasts by day, small plates and cocktails after work, DJs until 3am at the weekend. That range means you can talk to very different crowds, and fill very different slots, from one venue.

✓ Real credibility to lean on

Your DesignMyNight listing names recommendations from The Guardian, The Telegraph, the Evening Standard and Visit Britain. That kind of third-party proof is rare for an independent and it deserves to be in front of people, not buried in a listing.

✓ The booking path is already there

You have a Book now page, a bookings email and your Instagram feed pulled straight into the homepage. The plumbing to turn attention into tables exists. It just needs more of the right people arriving at it.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the venue, the story and the offer. The job is to turn that into bookings: more brunch tables filled, more Sunday covers, more private hire enquiries and a fuller room at the weekend. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Hidden Staircase the Hook

"POV: you found Newcastle's hidden bar." The walk past the market, the doorway most people miss, the climb, the reveal at the top. That discovery moment is exactly the kind of short video people send to the group chat with "we're going here".

It turns your biggest quirk into your best advert, and every share is someone planning a visit.

2

Turn Brunch Into the Weekend's Most Booked Table

Bottomless brunch sells on atmosphere: the pour, the clink, the food landing, the table in full swing. Filmed properly, it makes people book for their next birthday or catch-up there and then. Each brunch reel should end with one clear action, book your table.

Brunch is a group booking, so every view that converts brings a whole table, not a single cover.

3

Give Every Daypart Its Own Content

The Sunday roast crowd, the after-work cocktail crowd and the 1am crowd are different people. Homemade pies and roast close-ups for daytime, cocktails and small plates for midweek evenings, DJs and the dancefloor for Friday and Saturday. Separate strands mean you can push the slots that need filling, not just post whatever happened last night.

That is how content starts filling quieter midweek covers, not just the nights that are already busy.

4

Treat Private Hire as a Pipeline

Birthdays, engagements, baby showers and business meetings are high-value bookings, and people choose a venue by seeing someone else's event there. Short clips of a room set up and a celebration in full flow, with a simple "planning something? enquire here" at the end, do more than any hire page.

One extra private hire a month makes a real difference, and it is the easiest win to measure.

5

Scale TikTok Where Newcastle Plans Its Nights Out

Your TikTok is live but still early. That is where students, groups and people new to the city look for where to go next, and local nightlife creators already talk about your street. A steady rhythm of hooky, native videos is how the account grows from a placeholder into a real booking channel.

Your student deals and late licence are exactly what that audience is searching for.

6

Join Everything Up to Bookings

Right now the website links out to Instagram only. Adding TikTok and Facebook, putting the Book now link in every bio, and backing your best brunch and private hire videos with a small, targeted local ad budget closes the loop between watching and booking.

We would measure it the way you would: tables booked, brunch slots filled, private hire enquiries, not likes.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Weekend

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the hidden entrance, the brunch, the food and the late night. Each one could be filmed in a single visit.

Idea 01
"POV: you found Newcastle's hidden bar"
DiscoveryPOVReveal

First-person walk past the Grainger Market, into the doorway, up the stairs and into the room in full swing. Hook on frame one ("most people walk straight past this door"). Trending audio. Under 20 seconds. Tag the location.

Why it works: Secret spots are some of the most shared content in city nightlife, and it gives people a reason to bring their friends to see it.
Idea 02
"Bottomless brunch, Newcastle style"
BrunchGroupsBooking

A fast cut of a real brunch table: the pour, the food arriving, the cheers, the table two hours later. End on a clear line, "tag who you're bringing" and book your table.

Why it works: Tagging pulls in whole groups at once, and brunch is the booking most likely to be made straight from a video.
Idea 03
"Homemade pie and a proper Sunday roast"
FoodClose-upDaytime

Slow, satisfying close-ups from the kitchen: the pie cut open, the gravy pour, the roast plated. Minimal talking, great sound. Perfect for filling daytime and Sunday covers.

Why it works: Food close-ups are the most reliable format on both platforms, and it shows a side of No28 that the late-night crowd may not know exists.
Idea 04
"12pm to 3am at No28 in 30 seconds"
Day to nightTimelapseAtmosphere

One fixed angle or a quick sequence across a full Saturday: lunch, brunch, after-work drinks, the DJ arriving, the dancefloor. A clock on screen. Shows the whole venue in one watch.

Why it works: It sells every daypart at once and answers "what is it like?" for anyone who has never been up the stairs.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your visibility into bookings.

Get Reviews Pulling Their Weight

When people pick a bar or a brunch spot, they check Google and Tripadvisor before they book. Your reviews are doing quiet work and deserve to be fed and shown off.

  • Keep the Google profile tuned for "bottomless brunch Newcastle" and "late bar Newcastle"
  • Ask brunch and private hire groups for a review while the night is fresh
  • Add fresh photos and short clips to your profiles regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

A Private Hire Enquiry Flow

Private hire deserves its own short, clear path: a page with real event footage, what is possible, and a simple enquiry form, linked from every private hire video.

  • Real event clips instead of stock wording
  • A quick enquiry form that captures date, group size and occasion
  • Every enquiry tracked so you can see which videos bring them in

Targeted Local Ads

A small, tightly targeted budget around Newcastle puts your best brunch, discovery and private hire videos in front of people planning a night out or a celebration.

  • Target Newcastle and nearby areas, plus students in term time
  • Use your strongest brunch and hidden-bar reels as the ads
  • Drive people straight to your Book now page
  • Track cost per booking so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits No28: brunch and private hire are group bookings, so a single conversion brings a whole table, which makes local ads one of the highest-return things a venue can do.
