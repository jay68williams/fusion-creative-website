06 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your camp, your guides and your guests.

Every concept uses something you already run: Camp 1770, the Hike and Abseil, the wadis, your guides and your guests. Six are built to bring bookings in this season. Six are built to keep your Instagram growing between shoot days.

Videos that bring bookings

6 concepts
Reaction

The first lean back

"Lean back. Your first abseil."

A first-timer on the Hike and Abseil, the moment they lean back over the first drop, and their face at the bottom.

Why: for activities, a real person doing it for the first time is the format we would test first.

What to expect

Camp 1770, start to finish

"Sunset walk and dinner at 1,770 metres. Here is the evening."

The walk up, the sunset, the dinner. One minute, in order.

Why: answers the questions a group organiser asks before they fill in your form.

First person

Swimming with race camels

"The camel went in before I did."

A guest's own view of the race camels training in the water on the beach.

Why: it is the kind of thing people send to a friend.

Guide

Which hike is yours?

"Which hike is yours? Here is how to pick."

A guide explains each hike in 30 seconds, one per line: the time, the distance and the fitness it needs.

Why: turns the difficulty notes on your site into something people watch, and puts the right person on the right hike.

Groups

Ten of you, one mountain

"Camp 1770 is for groups of ten or more. Here is the evening."

A group night at the camp: the arrival, the table, the dinner, the stars.

Why: your camp is group bookings only, so the video speaks to the person who organises the group.

Season

Cooler up the mountain

"The cooler months are back. Here is the mountain."

A thermometer, a view, a guest in a jacket, filmed on the first cool evening of the season.

Why: built on the cooler months your site describes, and gives the 10,000 people who follow you a reason to book this season.

Organic that builds the audience

6 concepts
Series

Ask the guide

"How much water for Wadi of the Giants?"

One question from a guest, one answer from a guide, every week.

Why: a weekly series people look for, and the answers come from what your guides already know.

Place

The wadi after the rain

"After the rain, this canyon is a different hike."

The Wonder Wall canyon after rainfall, the boulders and the climb, with a guide crossing them.

Why: your Wonder Wall page already describes recent rains turning the wadi into an adventure park.

Moment

Camp 1770 at dusk

"Lights on at 1,770 metres. Who is coming up?"

A timelapse from Camp 1770 with a guest in the frame at both ends of the evening.

Why: your full moon reel shows the view on its own. This is the version with a person in it.

Diary

Three days to the top

"Day one of three. Here is the first camp."

Your 3 Day Trekking Expedition in short episodes, told by one guest.

Why: people follow a story with a next part.

Guests

Words on the way down

"What did you think it would be like?"

Quick answers from guests at the end of Sheha Peak, honest faces only.

Why: puts a real guest voice on the trail, one question at a time.

Values

What we carry out

"Carry out what you carry in."

A guide briefing on carrying out what you bring in, filmed at the trailhead.

Why: a values story that suits a mountain operator, filmed on the trail.

07 · The plan

A plan built around your season.

  1. Week 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the twelve videos. We write the shot list and brief for a licensed UAE crew, update the homepage dates and add a WhatsApp button to the site.

  2. The shoot
    One shoot day on the mountain

    A licensed UAE crew would film to our shot list: Camp 1770 at sunset and one Saturday hike, guides and guests included. We direct remotely from Newcastle.

  3. After the shoot
    Edit, caption and deliver

    We edit and caption the batch of short videos and post them on a plan to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, each one ending with the next date and how to book. You keep every video.

  4. Handover
    Results review

    We report what the videos reached and what bookings and enquiries they brought in, hand over the files, and set out what we would do next.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos from one shoot day on the mountain, filmed by a licensed UAE crew to our brief, edited and captioned, and posted on a plan, with a results review at the end. You keep every video.
