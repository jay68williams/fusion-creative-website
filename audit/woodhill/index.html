What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The venue itself is the content

A Mediterranean-inspired courtyard, the Orangery, triple tepees, a secret bar and 100 acres of private Northumberland grounds. Most venues would dream of spaces this photogenic. You do not have to manufacture anything to make, the raw material for scroll-stopping video is already standing there.

✓ The proposition is genuinely different

An entirely exclusive, two-day wedding experience on a private estate is a clear, confident position. Most venues sound the same and hire by the hour. Woodhill does not, and a distinctive promise is exactly what gives content something to say.

✓ The reputation and awards back it up

A multi-award-winning venue with the recognition to match is proof most places would love to have. Couples plan a wedding on trust, and that credibility is gold, it just is not being shown off where couples actually look, which is short-form video.

✓ You already invest in beautiful imagery

Your website and Instagram are full of lovely photography and you post consistently. That puts you ahead of most venues before we even start. The habit and the eye are there, it just needs shaping into short video built to reach, not only still images.

✓ The demand is already visible

Search Woodhill on TikTok and there are couples, planners, florists and hair and make-up artists posting from your weddings right now. That is proof people already want to share this venue. The opportunity is to own that channel and turn borrowed reach into your reach.

Where The Opportunity Is
Five Things We Would Focus On

You already have the venue, the proposition and the reputation. The job is to turn that into reach and enquiries: get in front of newly engaged couples in the North East and beyond, and make Woodhill the venue they have already saved before they ever book a viewing. These are the five areas we would focus on.

1

Turn Every Real Wedding Into Content

Every wedding at Woodhill is a ready-made story: the first look at the tepees lit up, the ceremony in the courtyard, the Orangery set for the wedding breakfast, the golden hour across the estate. Filmed properly and cut for short-form, one wedding can produce a week of content that couples save and send to each other.

A real wedding is the single best advert there is, because it shows the next couple exactly what their day could feel like here.

2

Own and Scale Your TikTok

The demand is already there, guests and suppliers post from your weddings, but that reach lands on their accounts, not yours. An owned, active TikTok, posting the same kind of dreamy venue and wedding content, captures those searches and pulls them straight back to Woodhill.

TikTok is where a huge amount of wedding planning now starts. Being the venue that actually shows up there, with a similar handle to your Instagram, is one of the highest-return moves you can make.

3

Show the Spaces as Venue-Tour Reels

"Come see every space at Woodhill." A cinematic walk through the courtyard, the Orangery, the tepees, the secret bar and the bedrooms is exactly the content newly engaged couples binge, save and share with their partner. It answers the first question every couple has: what does it actually look like, all of it.

Venue-tour and "get ready with me at Woodhill" style reels are some of the most saved content in the wedding space, and only you can film them here.

4

Answer the Questions Every Couple Asks

"How many guests can stay over?" "What is included?" "Can we have the whole place to ourselves?" The questions couples message you are the perfect scripts for short, helpful videos. Each one reaches couples at the exact moment they are comparing venues, and it saves your team answering the same thing over and over.

Helpful content builds trust fast and quietly does the selling for you, so the enquiries that come in are already warm.

5

Reels and TikTok for Reach, Plus Targeted Local Ads

Photo posts hold your feed together, but short-form video built to travel, with proper hooks rather than static galleries, is what earns new reach. Pair that with a small, tightly targeted ad budget aimed at recently engaged couples in the North East and the surrounding regions, and you put your best wedding and venue content in front of people actively looking for somewhere to marry.

One extra booking is worth a great deal, which makes reaching the right couples one of the highest-return things a venue can do.

Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your visibility into viewings and bookings.

Get the Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When a couple is choosing between venues, they search and read reviews before they ever get in touch. Your Google profile and reviews do quiet, heavy lifting, and they deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep the profile optimised for "wedding venues Northumberland" and the surrounding areas
  • Funnel every happy couple into a fresh Google review after their wedding
  • Add recent wedding photos and updates regularly
  • Turn your best reviews into shareable content

Feed Video Into the Wedding Platforms

You already appear on the big wedding directories, and couples browse those with intent. Most venue listings there are still photo only, so short video makes yours stand out instantly and gives couples a reason to enquire from the listing.

  • Add your best venue-tour and real-wedding video to your directory listings
  • Keep imagery fresh so the profiles reflect the venue at its best
  • Point couples from those platforms back to your own channels

Targeted Local Ads to Newly Engaged Couples

Because a wedding booking is high value, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget puts your best venue and real-wedding content in front of couples who are recently engaged and actively looking.

  • Target recently engaged couples across the North East and beyond
  • Use your strongest venue-tour and wedding-day reels as the ads
  • Drive a steady flow of viewing and enquiry requests to your website
  • Track cost per enquiry so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Woodhill: One extra wedding covers a lot of content and ad spend, which makes targeted ads one of the highest-return things a venue can do.
