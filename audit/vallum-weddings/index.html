I

What we are going to do

Four things. Measured in show-arounds booked, not likes.

One

Film the 2 December open day

Jonny cooking the Signature Menu, the barn dressed, the tipi at dusk, couples reacting. One day, a month of content, and it is already happening.

Two

Run the late-availability ad properly

Your Whirlwind Wedding idea was right. Run it again with a pixel behind it, aimed at engaged couples within 40 miles, so every show-around becomes an audience you can reach again.

Three

A capture day at Vallum every month

Walkthroughs, setup transformations, the kitchen, guest moments, Christmas party footage. Reels for Instagram, a TikTok started from zero, creative for the ads.

Four

Give couples a content creator

We film weddings ourselves. Offered as a Vallum perk, every wedding we film for a couple becomes Vallum marketing too.

II

How we are going to do it

Week one

Count everything

Meta pixel beside the analytics you already have. Show-around bookings become an audience. One button, "book a viewing", stays the single ask.

2 December

First capture day

The open day, filmed. Tasting, barn, tipi, fire pits, Jonny plating. Cut into reels within the week.

Every month

Capture, post, report

A capture day, a third of posts as reels filmed at Vallum, and a one-page report on enquiries and show-arounds.

January on

The ad

Late-availability Saturdays and the 12 January open day, small always-on spend, judged only on viewings booked.
