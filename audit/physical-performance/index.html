01 · The short version

The offer works. The campaign keeps stopping.

Most gyms we look at have no offer, no tracking and no proof. You have all three: a clear 30 day kickstart trial, a Meta pixel and Google Analytics on your website, and members on video talking about what changed for them. What is missing is a campaign that stays on. Your ads run in bursts of two to six weeks, then stop, and the gyms near you are already advertising for the autumn and the new year.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A clear audience, said plainly on your homepage: a private fitness gym helping over 30s in Newcastle.
  • A 30 day kickstart trial with a 121 consultation, movement screening, 12 small group sessions, nutrition support, body composition analysis and private WhatsApp support.
  • Real member stories on video: Diane is 3 stone down and off diabetes medication, and Emily is in the best shape of her life.
  • A Meta pixel and Google Analytics already on your website, so every ad can be measured from day one.
  • Past ads that already speak to one group at a time, filmed with a coach to camera, which is the right instinct.

You do not need a new offer or a new website. You need your members and coaches on camera, one always-on campaign, and new videos every week so the ads never go stale.

02 · Your Meta ads

Good ads that keep switching off.

Your ads run from your page "Physical Performance Ltd" (884 followers). Meta's Ad Library shows about 110 in total and none live today. These are three of the most recent.

What we found across your ads

1

Stop, start, stop

The ads we looked at each ran for between two and six weeks. Every restart asks Meta to learn your audience again, and the gaps are when other gyms pick up the people you were reaching.

Fix: one always-on campaign, with new videos added each week instead of new campaigns started and stopped.

2

Your members are missing

The ads we saw lead with a coach talking. Your homepage has six members on video, including Diane, 3 stone down and off diabetes medication. That kind of proof persuades in a way no coach can on their own.

Fix: one member story in every batch of ads, with permission, filmed in your gym.

3

Quiet since July

The most recent ad we found ended on 11 July. The run up to January is when many people decide to start again, and Newcastle Fitness Club launched an autumn ad on 6 October.

Fix: campaigns live within three weeks, so you are running well before January.

08 · The plan, month by month

Live in October, well before the January rush.

  1. Week 1
    Tracking and a landing page

    Check the pixel events and add Conversions API, track trial applications and calls booked, build one landing page for ad traffic and take down the old template page.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    Your coaches to camera, member stories with permission, a full trial filmed start to finish, and the gym floor at 6am.

  3. Week 3
    Always-on campaigns live

    One campaign for over 30s across Kenton, Gosforth and the rest of Newcastle, one for people who have visited your website, both running continuously.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New videos added every week to the same campaigns. We switch off what is not bringing applications and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on members

    We report applications, trials started and members who stay on, and set the January budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on applications, trials and new members. You keep every video.
