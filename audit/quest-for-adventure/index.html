01 · The short version

A protected reserve, resident flamingos, and a feed that is mostly photos.

Quest for Adventure runs its tours from the Al Zorah Nature Reserve: a RAMSAR listed reserve with resident flamingos on the channel, one and two hour kayak tours, electric boat tours for people who do not want to paddle, school programmes from Year 1 to Year 13, dragon boat team days and a mangrove tree nursery. Of the nine posts we read from 24 September to 9 October 2026, three were reels and six were photo posts. You already take bookings through your own online portal. What is missing is short video with people in it, posted on a plan, so families across the UAE have a reason to book a weekend tour.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A protected venue to film in. Your site describes the Al Zorah Nature Reserve as RAMSAR listed, over 2 square kilometres, with resident flamingos.
  • Tours with a clear arc: a one or two hour kayak tour, and the two hour tour through the narrow and canopy channels and the flamingo channel, tide permitting. Children from four years old can join.
  • Three audiences in one place. Families on the kayak and electric boat tours, schools on Mangrove Moments and Mission Mangrove, companies on dragon boat planting days and beach clean ups.
  • A mission people can share. Your Climate Action page says over 12 million mangrove trees are needed each year in the UAE, and you sponsor and grow them.
  • Direct bookings. Book Now on your homepage goes to your own online booking portal.

You do not need a new venue or a new product. You need short video of the flamingo channel, your guides and your guests, posted on a plan, so people across the northern emirates know Quest for Adventure is there and why it is worth a booking.

07 · Twelve videos we would make

Built from your channels, your guides and your guests.

Every concept uses something you already have. Six are built to bring bookings and enquiries. Six are built to keep your Instagram growing.

Videos that bring bookings in

6 concepts
Reaction

The flamingo channel

"Our flamingos are wild birds, so no promises. Can you spot one?"

A family paddles into the flamingo channel on a two hour tour, tide permitting. The reaction is filmed from the guide's kayak behind them.

Why: a first time reaction is the video people send to the friend they would bring.

First person

The two hour tour, start to finish

"POV: you booked the two hour tour and the tide is in."

From the paddling demo to the narrow channels to the flamingo channel, cut to 40 seconds with the time on screen.

Why: answers "what do you actually get" before anyone has to ask.

What to expect

Can my six year old do this?

"Minimum age is four. This is what that looks like."

A parent and child in a double kayak, the guide alongside, and the child's reaction to the first thing they spot.

Why: the question a parent asks before they book.

Local

The drive out

"Is a day on the water worth the drive out?"

Filmed to our shot list: the road out, the launch, the channel, and the way home.

Why: a drive-out film for families planning a day, with your booking link at the end.

Team day

Boardrooms to boats

"Swap boardrooms for boats and emails for eco-action."

A company's dragon boat planting day from first paddle to the last sapling in the mud. Ends on the contact page for team bookings.

Why: your own caption, made into a film an HR manager can forward.

Schools

Mission Mangrove in 30 seconds

"What a Year 9 field trip looks like when the classroom is a lagoon."

A school group on dragon boats, a guide explaining the roots, and a teacher's one line verdict at the end.

Why: heads of year can see what the trip looks like before they book it.

Organic that builds the audience

6 concepts
Series

Tide report

"Check the tide before you book. This is the flamingo channel today."

One short clip a week from the launch, the same framing every time, with the day's conditions.

Why: a weekly series people can follow through the cooler season.

Guides

Meet your guide

"Meet the guide who will paddle beside you."

One guide at a time, 20 seconds each, on the water, with their favourite spot in the reserve.

Why: people book people, and your guides are the face of every tour.

Challenge

Name three birds

"Name three birds you could see on the way back to the launch."

Guests guess on the way back to the launch, and the answers are shown on screen.

Why: challenges get comments, and each comment puts the reserve in front of someone new.

Climate

Three inches tall

"These young mangroves may only be 3 to 4 inches tall. Here is where they go."

From the nursery tray to the planting site at low tide, in one film. Built on your own saplings post from 29 September 2026.

Why: connects the tree planting and the CSR day for the same viewer.

Electric boat

The quiet way in

"No paddling. Electric power. Up to six of you, and the birds."

The electric boat tour for families who will not kayak, filmed from the bow.

Why: gives families who will not paddle a tour to book, and a film to show them what it looks like.

Season

The cooler season

"The cooler months are here. Here is a weekend on the water."

The first weekend of the cooler season at the launch, ending on your booking link.

Why: gives everyone who has been meaning to come a date to do it.

08 · The plan

A plan built around the tide, the season and the stories you already have.

  1. Week 1
    Plan and set up

    A call to pick the twelve videos. We write the shot list and brief for a licensed UAE crew, put the Meta pixel on questforadventure.net and add a WhatsApp button beside Book Now.

  2. The shoot
    One shoot day at Al Zorah

    A licensed UAE crew would film at Al Zorah to our shot list, timed to the tide: a two hour tour, a family in a double kayak, the flamingo channel and your guides. We direct remotely from Newcastle.

  3. After the shoot
    Edit, caption and deliver

    We edit and caption the batch of short videos and post them on a plan to Instagram and TikTok, each one ending with a reason to book and your own booking link. You keep every video.

  4. Handover
    Results review

    We report what the videos reached and what bookings and enquiries they brought in, hand over the files, and set out what we would do next.

What the shoot gives you: a batch of short videos from one shoot day at Al Zorah, filmed by a licensed UAE crew to our brief, edited and captioned, and posted on a plan, with a results review at the end. You keep every video.
