What Is Already Working Well

✓ An established brand with real trust

Trading since 2007, your own FitCookie product line alongside the big names, eight physical stores and a loyal customer base. That is a foundation most ecommerce brands would love to start from. The trust is already built, it just is not being amplified on social the way it could be.

✓ The product range is made for video

Protein cookies, powders, pancakes, snacks, pre-workout. This is some of the most filmable product going: taste tests, mixing and prep, before-and-after, gym-context content. A lot of brands have to invent things to film. You have shelves of it.

✓ You already post consistently

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and store-level accounts, all active. The habit of showing up is there, which is the hardest part for most businesses. The issue is not effort, it is format, and format is the easy thing to fix.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the brand, the range and the customers. The job is to turn that into short-form video that actually sells, then put fuel behind it with paid ads and email. Content that drives orders, not just views. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Product Content That Sells, Not Just Sits There

Supplements and protein snacks are perfect short-form material. Taste tests, the satisfying mix and pour, recipe hacks, "which protein cookie actually tastes best", macro breakdowns. Content where the product is the star and the viewer wants it by the end.

This is the difference between a post that gets a few likes and a video that sends people straight to the product page. The range is the content.

2

Go All In on TikTok and Reels

TikTok is where supplement and fitness-food brands are being discovered right now, and it is your single biggest untapped channel. Hooky, fast, native short-form video (not repurposed adverts) is what earns reach there and pulls in buyers who have never heard of you.

You have the products and the stores to film in. The gap is a consistent stream of content built for the platform, not the shop window.

3

Turn the Range Into a Reason to Come Back

A huge catalogue is a content goldmine: new-in drops, "what to stack with what", goal-based edits (lean, bulk, recovery, vegan), best-sellers and staff picks. Series people follow for, so your feed becomes a reason to keep checking in rather than a one-off scroll.

Recurring formats build a following that buys again and again, which is where the real money is in supplements.

4

Paid Ads to Scale What Works

Once a piece of product content proves it converts, you do not wait and hope it goes viral. You put spend behind it. Short-form video ads on Meta and TikTok, pointed at your best-sellers and your free-shipping offer, are how supplement brands buy predictable, trackable sales.

Content and ads working together means you are not reliant on the algorithm being kind. You control the reach, and every pound is measured against orders.

5

Email That Does the Quiet Selling

You already have traffic and a customer list, and for an ecommerce store that is money left on the table if the email is not working hard. Welcome flows, abandoned-cart and browse reminders, back-in-stock, and a regular campaign around new products and offers bring people back to buy again.

Email is consistently one of the highest-return channels in ecommerce, because it sells to people who already know and trust you.

6

Use the Stores as a Content Engine

Eight physical stores is a real advantage most online-only brands do not have. In-store filming, staff recommendations, local community content and store-opening moments give you authenticity and a steady supply of real, on-the-ground video, plus footfall to your local shops.

The stores make the brand feel real and trusted, and that trust is exactly what turns a viewer into a customer online and in person.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already have: the products, the range and the stores. Each one could be filmed on a phone in a single session.

Idea 01
"Ranking every FitCookie protein snack"
Taste testProductSeries

An honest taste test ranking your protein cookies, bars and treats, best to worst, with the macros on screen. Strong hook on frame one ("I tried every FitCookie snack so you don't have to"). Trending audio, fast cuts, a clear winner at the end.

Why it works: Taste tests are hugely watchable, they show the product doing its job, and they send people straight to the exact product to buy.
Idea 02
"What to actually take for your goal"
EducationalStackSave-worthy

A quick, no-nonsense guide: "Building muscle? Here's your stack." "Cutting? Here's what actually helps." Point to the specific products, keep it honest and simple. Built to be saved and shared, not a hard sell.

Why it works: It uses your huge range as a strength, positions you as the trusted expert, and answers the exact question new buyers are already searching for.
Idea 03
"The satisfying protein shake ASMR"
ASMRPrepProduct hero

Slow, satisfying close-ups: the scoop, the pour, the mix, the first sip. Clean product-hero shots of a best-seller with the flavour front and centre. Minimal talking, maximum craving. Under 20 seconds.

Why it works: Satisfying prep content performs brilliantly on short-form, makes the product the star, and works perfectly as a paid ad too.
Idea 04
"A day in a FitCookie store"
Behind the scenesStoreTrust

Behind the counter in one of your stores: staff picks, the most-asked question, a customer's real order, the same-day dispatch happening. Friendly, human, local. Shows there are real people and real shops behind the website.

Why it works: Your stores are an advantage online-only rivals cannot copy. Real faces and real shops build the trust that turns a viewer into a customer.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your traffic and reach into orders.

Make Email a Revenue Channel

An established store has traffic and a list, and email is one of the highest-return channels in ecommerce because it sells to people who already trust you. Right now it is almost certainly leaving money on the table.

  • Automated flows: welcome, abandoned cart, browse reminders, back-in-stock, post-purchase
  • Regular campaigns around new products, bundles and offers
  • Segmented by goal and buying history so the right products reach the right people
  • Every send tracked against revenue, not just opens

Turn Reviews Into Content and Trust

Supplement buyers read reviews and want proof before they order. Your happy customers and results are your most persuasive marketing, and they are barely being shown where new buyers look.

  • Funnel happy customers into fresh reviews at the point of purchase
  • Turn your best reviews and results into shareable short-form content
  • Feed the store-level Google and social profiles so local search works for you

Paid Ads That Scale What Works

Because you have a clear product and a strong offer, paid ads pay back fast once the content proves it converts. A tightly managed budget on Meta and TikTok puts your best product videos in front of new buyers.

  • Promote your best-selling products and the free-shipping offer
  • Use your strongest taste-test and product reels as the ads
  • Retarget website visitors and abandoned carts to recover lost sales
  • Track cost per order and return on ad spend so every pound is accountable

Why it fits FitCookie: With a proven store and a repeat-purchase product, content plus ads plus email is the classic ecommerce engine, and it is exactly what we build.
