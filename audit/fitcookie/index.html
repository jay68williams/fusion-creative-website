What we would do

Conversion first. Then traffic. Run as one system.

There is no point paying to send people to a site that will not convert them. So the site comes first, then ads, email and content go in as one machine. This is exactly the order we ran for the case study further down.

01 / THE SITE

Fix the leaks before buying traffic

On mobile the first thing a visitor sees is a cookie wall, then a generic "UK's best" headline with no offer. On product pages Add to Basket sits below the fold and there is no video. Small things, huge impact when you multiply by every paid click.

  • Offer-led homepage hero, not a category statement
  • Sticky Add to Basket and product video on every PDP
  • Reviews pulled onto product pages, you have 4,813 of them
  • Bundles and "for your goal" landing pages built for ads
02 / PAID ADS

Your creative, your offers, tested weekly

Retire the supplier templates. We make the ads and run them: FitCookie-voiced creative, real offers, new angles every week, winners scaled and losers killed fast. Our current product client is at 7.8x blended return on exactly this approach.

03 / EMAIL

The cheapest revenue you are leaving on the table

You already have the list and the traffic. Launches, restocks, "short BB date" clearance, goal-based flows for new customers. Email turned our candle client's one-time buyers into a channel worth a large share of the month.

04 / CONTENT

Filmed in your stores, with real advice

Supplements are one of the most filmable categories there is, and you have eight stores full of them. Taste tests, mix-and-pour, "what to actually take for your goal", staff picks, filmed with the staff who are comfortable on camera. Content where the product is the star and the viewer wants it by the end.

I know this world from the inside

I run Fusion, I have trained for years and I live in this stuff. That matters, because supplement content only works when the advice behind it is real. I would not be the one on camera: your staff would, the people who already know the products and are comfortable in front of a phone. My job is everything around them. Knowing which questions gym goers actually ask, which products deserve the spotlight, and how to turn straight, honest advice into content that builds trust and gets shared in group chats. Not an agency guessing at your customer. Someone who is your customer, directing people who serve them every day.

Ready-made content

Four videos we would film in month one.

IDEA 01

"What to actually take for your goal"

A member of your team in store, three shelves, three goals (muscle, fat loss, endurance). Honest, no upsell, the video people save and send. Ends on a bundle built for each goal.

IDEA 02

"Ranking every FitCookie protein snack"

Your own-brand line, taste tested on camera with brutal honesty. Own-brand margin, maximum shareability, and a reason for people to argue in the comments.

IDEA 03

"The mix and pour"

Satisfying, silent, ASMR-style shake and pancake content. The lowest effort, highest reach format in the category, and it sells the product on sight.

IDEA 04

"A day in a FitCookie store"

The eight stores, Newbury included, are a story nobody online knows. Staff picks, the regulars, the clearance shelf, the same-day dispatch bench. Local trust turned into national reach.
