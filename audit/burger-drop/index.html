What Is Already Working Well
Credit Where It Is Due

✓ The product is the whole game, and yours is strong

Freshly ground patties, a signature brioche bun and a brand built on obsession. In food content the product is the hook, and a proper smash burger with a cheese pull is some of the most watchable content there is. You are starting with the raw material most brands wish they had.

✓ A real, loyal halal following

Halal smash burgers speak to a large, passionate and highly engaged audience that actively seeks places out and shares them. That community travels well on social, and it is a big head start most burger brands simply do not have.

✓ You are already on Instagram and TikTok

Both accounts are live and on-brand, which is exactly where this audience lives. The habit is there. The job now is not starting, it is scaling: more volume, sharper hooks and content built to travel rather than just to fill the grid.

✓ Multiple sites means proven demand

Growing across the North East and into Manchester and Edinburgh tells you the concept works and people keep coming back. Each location is also a fresh audience and a fresh reason to post, which most single-site brands can only dream of.

✓ Every opening is a ready-made event

New sites, launch nights and drops are natural content moments with real buzz baked in. That is gold for short-form video, and it is happening on a schedule you already control.

Where The Opportunity Is
Six Things We Would Focus On

You already have the product, the brand and the following. The job is to turn that into reach: get Burger Drop in front of every hungry person in your cities, and make it the smash burger they already know before they choose where to eat. These are the six areas we would focus on.

1

Make the Food the Hero

The single biggest lever is close-up, mouth-watering food content: the smash on the griddle, the sear, the cheese melt, the stacked build, the first bite and the pull. Shot properly, with the right lighting and sound, a burger is one of the most stopping pieces of content you can put on a feed.

You have the product to do this every single day. The gap is turning it into a steady stream of short video built to make people stop scrolling and get hungry.

2

Pour Fuel on TikTok

Food travels further on TikTok than almost anywhere else, and it is where new customers discover where to eat. You are already on it, which is the hard part. The opportunity is volume and consistency: more posts, stronger hooks in the first second, and formats designed to be shared and saved rather than just admired.

This is the fastest route to reaching people who have never heard of Burger Drop yet, in every city you are in.

3

Turn Every Launch and Drop Into a Moment

You are opening new sites and running drops, and each one is a built-in event. Film the queue, the countdown, the first burgers out of the pass, the reactions. Give people a reason to turn up and a reason to share that they were there.

A launch filmed well does two jobs at once: it packs out the new site on day one, and it becomes the content that pulls people to the next opening.

4

Work With Local Food Creators

The North East has a strong food-review and foodie creator scene, and halal food content in particular has huge, engaged audiences. Getting the right creators through the door, and creating content worth them filming, puts Burger Drop in front of exactly the people most likely to visit.

Handled properly, creator content compounds: it borrows their audience, builds trust fast, and gives you a library of clips to reuse.

5

Put the People and the Obsession on Camera

"A brand built on obsession" is a story, not just a tagline. The team, the standards, the reason the patty is ground fresh, the little details most places skip. People follow brands with a face and a point of view, and yours has one worth showing.

Behind-the-counter and founder-led content builds the kind of loyalty that turns a one-time visit into a regular, and a regular into someone who brings their mates.

6

Location-Targeted Ads to Fill Every Site

Your best food and launch content, put behind a small, tightly targeted ad budget around the site, reaches hungry people within delivery and walking distance at exactly the right moment. For a food brand this is one of the highest-return moves there is.

Content earns the reach, ads make sure it lands in the postcodes that actually turn into covers and orders.

Ready-Made Content
4 Video Ideas You Could Film This Week

These are formats working right now on TikTok and Instagram Reels, built around what you already do: the food, the launches, the people and the following. Each one could be filmed on a phone in one of your kitchens.

Idea 01
"The smash, start to finish"
FoodASMRBuild

A tight, satisfying build reel: the ball of fresh mince hitting the griddle, the smash, the sear, the cheese melt, the stack and the pull. Real sound, no music over the sizzle. Strong hook on frame one ("this is how a smash burger should be made"). Under 20 seconds.

Why it works: Food this good is its own hook. Close-up build reels are some of the most watched, saved and shared content on the platform.
Idea 02
"We opened and this happened"
LaunchHypeEvent

A fast, punchy launch reel of a new site: the countdown, the queue down the street, the first burgers out, the reactions on the first bite. Lead with the buzz ("we gave away the first 50 burgers and this is what happened"). Built to make the next opening even bigger.

Why it works: It turns an opening you are already doing into content that fills the room and pulls a crowd to the next one.
Idea 03
"North East's best smash burger? You decide."
LocalPOVReaction

A local-pride, POV-style reel: a customer or creator trying the signature build for the first time, honest reaction, the pull, the verdict. Tag the location. Invite the comments war that food fans love ("go on then, prove me wrong").

Why it works: It taps local pride and the halal foodie community, and reaction plus a bold claim is built to be argued about, shared and reached.
Idea 04
"Why we grind the meat fresh every day"
BrandBehind the scenesObsession

A short behind-the-counter piece on the obsession: the standard, the fresh grind, the detail most places skip and why you refuse to. Straight talking, a bit of personality, the story behind the tagline. Ends on a finished burger.

Why it works: People follow brands with a point of view. Showing the obsession turns a good burger into a brand people root for and come back to.
Digital Enhancements
Beyond the Main Feed

A few extra wins that would compound the content work and turn more of your local visibility into covers, orders and repeat visits.

Get Every Location's Google Profile Pulling Its Weight

When someone is deciding where to eat, they search and they read reviews first. For a multi-site brand, each location's Google profile and reviews are doing quiet, heavy lifting, and they deserve to be front and centre and constantly fed.

  • Keep every site optimised for "halal burgers" and "smash burger" plus its city
  • Funnel happy customers into fresh Google reviews at each location
  • Keep photos current with your best food and launch shots
  • Turn your strongest reviews into shareable content

Content That Also Powers New Openings

You are a brand that expands. The same content engine that fills your current sites can pre-build hype for the next one, so a new location opens to an audience that already knows the name.

  • "Coming soon" teasers that build a waitlist of hungry locals
  • Opening-day reels that become the template for the next launch
  • A repeatable playbook so every new site gets the same head start

Location-Targeted Ads Around Every Site

Because a burger is an easy, frequent purchase, paid ads pay back fast. A small, tightly targeted budget around each location puts your best food and launch content in front of hungry people nearby at the right moment.

  • Target the postcodes around the site, within walking and delivery distance
  • Use your strongest build and launch reels as the ads
  • Drive orders, covers and follows off the back of the content
  • Track cost per result so the spend is always accountable

Why it fits Burger Drop: A small local budget around the site compounds fast, which makes targeted ads one of the highest-return things a growing food brand can do.
