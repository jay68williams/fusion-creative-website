Heart notes

Where the money actually is
Repeat purchase

Refills are the business hiding inside the business

Every one of your eight core scents has a reed diffuser refill, and refills already have Subscribe & Save on a monthly or two monthly cycle. That is the most valuable thing on the site. A person who buys a diffuser today is a refill customer in a few months, without you paying to find them again.

Your email runs through Mailchimp (we can see it connected to the store). What we cannot see from outside is whether a diffuser buyer gets a well timed “running low?” email with a one click refill. If they do not, that is the first thing to build, and it costs nothing but an afternoon.

Basket size

Free delivery starts below the price of your own hero products

Free delivery kicks in at £20. Your glass candles and reed diffusers are £25. So the one product most people come for already qualifies on its own, and the threshold never asks anyone to add a second thing.

You already sell the perfect second thing: a £12 tin candle or a £13 refill in the same scent. A threshold that sits just above one hero product, with a “add the matching tin” nudge in the basket, is the cheapest basket size lever you have. Worth testing for a month and comparing.

Base note

What lasts into winter
Christmas

Christmas is already on your shelves. The content to sell it is not made yet.

You have personalised hampers, trio gift sets, perfume and candle gift sets, and a limited edition Winter Icing candle. Your Trustpilot score is 4.9, and gifting comes up again and again in the reviews. The range is ready for the busiest eight weeks of the year.

Your photography is genuinely good. Clean, well lit studio work that makes the packaging look as premium as it is. That is exactly right for product pages. It is not what stops a thumb on Instagram or TikTok. Paid social in December runs on short video: hands pulling the printed ribbon, the watercolour box opening, a match to a black glass candle. Both your accounts are set up, so the channels are there. The footage is what is missing.

Advertising costs climb from mid November. Anything filmed in October can be tested cheaply first, so by the time it matters you already know which two or three videos to put money behind.
