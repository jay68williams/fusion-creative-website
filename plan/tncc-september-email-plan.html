Before we press send

Stock and setup notes

Turn on inventory tracking for the plushes
The four new singing plushes are inventory-untracked, so depth cannot be confirmed before we email 20,000 people about them. Tracking on, counts confirmed, before 23 September.
Feature deep stock, name-check low stock
Emails hero the items that can absorb 20k sends (pumpkin tins 547, candles, diffusers 1,493). Items under 20 units appear only as "almost gone" lines, never as the main push.
Publish before promoting
The £45 Halloween Mega Bundle, the mini pumpkin pots (553 units) and the Christmas drafts are unpublished. Each goes live, is test-purchased, and gets its collection placement before its email.
Sunday resends the reliable way
Non-opener resends use Klaviyo's built-in resend feature, not custom segments. The 31 August segment-based attempt silently matched nobody; any segment used for targeting gets its profile count verified before send.
