Content plan · Shoot one

Tomorrow's shoot, and the ideas we are bringing with us.

This is the plan for Saturday and the list of videos we intend to film at Geisha Asia. You do not need to prepare anything beyond what is set out below. Have a read through and tell us if there is anything you would rather we did not film.

Shoot date
Saturday 12 September, from 2pm
Location
Geisha Asia, Front Street, Benton
Output
24 videos a month, across Instagram, TikTok and Facebook
01

How the day works

One filming day covers a full month of content. We start straight after the lunch rush, as Simone suggested.

  • Arrival. We arrive at 2pm and film through the quiet window from 2.30 to 4.30, while the room is clear and the lanterns and the sakura tree are at their best. If service picks up we work around you rather than stopping you.
  • Access. We will need the sushi counter, the kitchen pass, the main room and the bar at various points. Simone is briefing both head chefs on what to plate and when.
  • Food. A run of dishes made fresh through the afternoon. The list at the bottom of this page is the idea, and we will finalise it with you on the day.
  • Duration. Expect us for most of the afternoon. Everything is edited afterwards and delivered as finished videos.
02

Nobody has to perform

This is built into the plan.

We ask every person before they appear on camera, exactly as you asked. The kitchen team can stay to hands and process, which is often the best footage anyway: the knife, the rice, the roll, the plate landing.

Where a video needs a presenter or someone to act, that is Jay or Anya from our team. If Simone fancies a line on camera we will make room for it, and if not, nothing in this plan depends on it.

03

The food and the story

Built around the dishes you told us matter most, and the one thing you want people to understand about Geisha.

  1. First time at Geisha? Here is what we recommend

    Simone's picks for a first visit, either said to camera or as words on screen over the plates.

  2. If you love Asian food and have not tried Geisha, you are missing out

    Salt and chilli chicken, the massaman, Geisha KFC and the sizzling teppan, filmed close and satisfying.

  3. Fancy sushi, but your partner isn't a fan?

    Two restaurants on one street: sushi for you, a burger or a parmo next door for them. The answer to every "where shall we go" argument.

  4. Two very different restaurants, one name

    Pan-Asian upstairs, dishes from all over the world downstairs. This is the video that makes the point that Fusion is not just an Italian.

  5. Our crispy aromatic duck, and what is coming next Fusion

    The duck, then a first look at the new Fusion menu: the sharing seafood paella, build a burger, the new curry. Filmed on the Fusion visit.

  6. How many countries are on the menu? Fusion

    Counting them off, one dish per country. Filmed on the Fusion visit.

04

Trend formats and edits

Short videos built around the audio and editing styles working this week. These mostly need the room, the plates and the cocktails. Two carry a reference so you can see the style.

  1. Menu flash-through

    Every dish, one frame each, cut fast to the beat.

    Reference

  2. Sushi date

    Two people, one sharing platter, the whole evening in fifteen seconds.

  3. By the table

    A rotating point of view that lands first on a cocktail, then chopsticks, then a plate of sushi, then the full table.

    Reference

  4. Steak, sushi, or the whole table?

    Three options, one choice. Viewers pick in the comments.

  5. Around the world in one room Fusion

    Dishes from different countries, one after the other. Tomorrow we film the Asian side; the Fusion dishes get picked up on the next visit.

  6. Outside the front

    The arrival edit: Front Street, the sign, through the door and into the room.

  7. From above

    Drone shots of the frontage and the street, weather permitting.

  8. POV: the chef makes your sushi

    The counter from the chef's eye line, start to finish.

  9. Sushi, no talking

    The ASMR version: rice, knife, nori, the roll, the slice. Sound does the work.

05

Games and challenges

Filmed with willing guests or our own team. One carries a reference so you can see the format.

  1. Sushi maths

    Eat a piece, answer a sum, keep going. Gets harder as the plate empties.

  2. Noughts and crosses, with sushi

    Third date energy. You already know each other's favourite colours, so the plate becomes the board.

    Reference

  3. How much can I get for it?

    What the 2 for 14 cocktails and the daytime mix and match actually put on one table. A straight, satisfying value video.

06

Scene based videos

Short, light hearted scenarios filmed in the restaurant. No dialogue needed, the wording appears on screen.

  1. Plans for the week?

    A week of "plans" that all turn out to be a table at Geisha.

  2. I lost my glasses

    Someone hunting for their glasses while the sushi in front of them steadily disappears.

  3. He already knows the order

    First date, and he orders for her before she has opened the menu. He gets it exactly right.

  4. Everyone is watching the guest

    The whole team quietly spying on one table until the first bite lands.

  5. Tell me I'm handsome and it's free

    A cheeky offer at the counter, and someone from our team appearing from behind to accept it.
