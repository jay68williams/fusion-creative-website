01 · The short version

Your customers love you. Your marketing has not caught up.

A perfect 5.0 from 65 Google reviews and 47 Facebook reviews at 100% recommend is rare in any trade. So is a customer who has brought you seven vans. But your last ad ran for one week in January, your Instagram posts reach a handful of people each, and your website gallery is empty.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Reviews most converters would pay for: 5.0 on Google from 65 reviews, 47 on Facebook, 100% recommend.
  • A customer who has brought you seven vans. That is the best testimonial in the business.
  • Your own CNC furniture: precision-cut, around 30% lighter, designed in-house.
  • Fast turnaround: most builds done in one to two weeks, which means more enquiries can actually become work.
  • Beautiful professional photos of your VW Crafter build on the clifftop, and Craig, a named person buyers can talk to.

You are already posting regularly. The problem is not effort, it is format. Photos and long captions do not travel. Short videos with a strong first two seconds do, and they make ads cheaper.

02 · Your Meta ads

Two short bursts in a year, then nothing.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK over the past year. For your page (4.9K followers) it shows two, each live for one week. Neither is running now.

What we would change

1

A week is not long enough to learn anything

Meta needs time to find the people who enquire. Switching off after seven days means paying for the learning and stopping before the results arrive.

Fix: an always-on campaign at a steady daily budget.

2

Still photos, not video

Both ads were single images. The national converters spending most right now (Denby, Spartan) lead with video, including their workshops.

Fix: short videos of your builds, starting with the before and after.

3

Your reviews never appear

A 5.0 from 65 Google reviews is the strongest reason to choose you, and neither ad mentions it.

Fix: put the reviews, and the seven-van customer, on screen.

You posted ten times in September, which is more than most converters manage. Each post earned between 1 and 4 likes. Three things are holding it back:

  • Photos with long captions. "FORD TRANSIT HI-TOP, FULL INTERIOR TRANSFORMATION" (24 September) is a great before and after. As a photo post, 4 likes. As a 20 second reveal, it is the kind of thing that travels.
  • The same post, two or three times. The hire post went out three times on 25 September, the VW Crafter twice on 14 September, the Citroën Relay twice on 5 September. Instagram shows repeated posts to fewer people.
  • The great one-offs get lost. Your bespoke ambulance conversion in August is a genuinely unusual build. It earned 3 likes.

Proof it can work in the North East. Northern Conversions in Northumberland has 43.9K Instagram followers and runs no Meta ads at all. Their whole audience is built on short videos of their builds.

08 · The plan, month by month

Tidy up, film the builds, then run ads that stay on.

  1. Week 1
    Set up

    Fill the gallery, delete the "Hello world!" post, install the Meta pixel, and agree what a typical full and part build costs.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    One in the workshop with a build in progress and the CNC running, one with a finished van and a handover. We film around your work.

  3. Week 3
    Always-on campaign

    Three angles tested: the before and after, the reviews, and the two week turnaround. A steady daily budget, not one-week bursts.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on builds booked

    We report enquiries, quotes and builds booked, ad by ad, and decide the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, organic posting, and a one-page report on enquiries, quotes and builds booked. You keep every video.
