The store · Weeks 1 to 2 · Included

The new store

A full rebuild of joyofsweets.com and a reshoot of the products that carry it, so the click always lands somewhere that sells. Included when you sign for three months.

  • The whole website redone, top to bottom. New design on your existing Shopify, built for phones first: fast pages, a home page that sells the range, collection pages that make choosing easy, product pages that convert rather than list.
  • Checkout and trust fixed. Free delivery threshold and dispatch times where people look for them, reviews, allergen and dietary badges (halal, vegan, gluten free), a basket that upsells the bundle.
  • Landing pages for each ad audience, so a halal household, a retro nostalgia buyer and a gift buyer each land on their own page.
  • Product imagery redone. A clean, consistent studio look across the ranges that drive the store: the pouches, mixes, jars, tubs, bundles and gifting, plus lifestyle and packing shots for the site, the ads and Amazon.
  • Email flows switched on. Welcome, abandoned basket, post-purchase and win-back, built and live before the ads start so nothing leaks.
  • Tracking wired properly. Meta pixel and conversions API, Google Analytics. Every order tracked back to what caused it.
  • Web support after launch. New products, new landing pages, changes, fixes, anything website related, covered for as long as we work together.
Why first: website sales are down 49%. Every point of conversion we win back here makes every ad pound work harder.
The engine · Every month · From day one

The engine

The same fully managed setup we run for Ravi, built around your range and pointed at the new store. Starts the week you sign.

  • 20 ad creatives a month to start, filmed at the unit: packing, the wall, the niches, the family. Every one is also posted to TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, so the ads feed the socials. Twenty is the right number to learn what sells; as the winners show themselves and the ads scale, we make more.
  • Two launches a month. A product, a bundle, a seasonal drop or one of your offers, each with its own creative, landing page, email and ad push.
  • Meta ads fully managed. Campaigns for each audience, tested weekly, winners scaled, laggards rotated out, retargeting and past-customer campaigns on all the time.
  • Amazon ads managed across your 160-plus gift jar listings: Sponsored Products on the best sellers, budgets and bids tuned weekly.
  • Two emails a week to the list, designed in your brand: launches, offers, the gifting calendar, and the flows from phase one running underneath.
  • Socials posted. Every creative goes out on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, scheduled and captioned by us.
  • Monthly report: spend, orders, cost per order, return, what we are rotating out and why.
Your part: run the sweet business, keep stock moving, and tell us which offers you want to push. We do the rest.
