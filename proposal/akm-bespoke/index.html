02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, while bathroom firms advertise to your customers.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. We found no advertiser page for AKM Bespoke and no ads mentioning you. When we loaded akmbespoke.co.uk, neither a Meta pixel nor Google Analytics loaded.

What that means

1

Homeowners are being sold renovations elsewhere

Waterstone Bathrooms is advertising "Renovate Your Bathroom, All In for £7,000". Priory Bathrooms is advertising November and December installation slots. Your customers see them, not you.

Fix: a campaign built from your own projects, shown to homeowners in the areas you work.

2

No way to measure anything

With no analytics or pixel, you cannot tell how many people visit your service pages, or follow up with them.

Fix: Meta pixel, Conversions API and Google Analytics in week one.

3

Premium work, priced by someone else

When the only renovation ads people see lead with £7,000, a bespoke quote looks expensive. Showing the craft first changes that.

Fix: lead with the work, then the process, then the conversation about budget.

08 · The plan, month by month

Measure the site, film Low Fell, then advertise steadily.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Meta pixel, Conversions API and Google Analytics, quote requests counted, real project photos added to the main service pages.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    The Low Fell project in progress, a finished room, and the team on camera.

  3. Week 3
    First campaign live

    The Low Fell series and your best finished projects, shown to homeowners in the areas you work.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week from the live project. We switch off what is not bringing quote requests and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on projects won

    We report quote requests, site visits and projects won, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting, and a one-page report on quote requests, visits and projects. You keep every video.
