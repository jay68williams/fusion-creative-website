02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, no pixel, and no way to count enquiries.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. We opened the library page for your Facebook page, "Eves & Lewis Landscape Design", and it shows no ads. When we loaded evesandlewis.co.uk, no Meta pixel loaded.

What that means

1

Other firms are reaching your customers

Wilkinsons Landscapes, DMR Property Maintenance & Landscaping and C.S landscapes & Driveways are all running ads to Teesside and North East homeowners this week.

Fix: a campaign shown only to homeowners in the areas you work, built from your own gardens.

2

Gardens are designed in winter

A garden built in spring is designed months before. Wilkinsons ran one "book now for summer" ad from January to September. If you are not in a homeowner's feed this winter, someone else will be.

Fix: start in October with a spring design-slot message.

3

No way to follow up

Someone who spends ten minutes looking at your Jesmond canal garden cannot be shown your work again without a pixel.

Fix: Meta pixel, Conversions API and up-to-date Google Analytics in week one.

08 · The plan, month by month

Fix the tracking, film the gardens, then reach the right homeowners.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Google Analytics 4, Meta pixel and Conversions API, consultation requests counted, the template pages removed, and the BALI medal and one clear line on the homepage.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    Finished gardens your clients are happy for us to film, Chris and Karen on camera, and a design in progress.

  3. Week 3
    First campaign live

    Shown only to homeowners in the areas you work, from Teesside to Newcastle, with a spring design-slot message.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing consultation requests and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on commissions

    We report consultation visits, designs and gardens won, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on consultation visits, designs and gardens won. You keep every video.
