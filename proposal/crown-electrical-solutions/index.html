02 · Your Meta ads

Three good starts, each switched off within ten days.

Your ads run from your page "Crown Electrical Solutions Ltd" (496 followers). Meta's Ad Library shows about five since July 2026. Here are three.

What we found across your ads

1

Short bursts do not let Meta learn

Each ad ran for five to ten days. Meta needs a steady run to learn which homeowners enquire. Every new burst starts from scratch.

Fix: one always-on campaign, with new videos added each week instead of new ads started and stopped.

2

No pixel, so no way to measure enquiries

We found no Meta pixel in your website code. Without it, Meta cannot tell which ads brought an enquiry, and cannot show your work again to people who visited your projects.

Fix: install the pixel and track the contact form in week one.

3

Too many services, too little video

The flyer lists seven services and the other two ads are still photos. The electricians winning attention locally use video of real jobs and real people.

Fix: one service per ad, filmed, starting with lighting and full installs.

Source: Meta Ad Library, United Kingdom, page "Crown Electrical Solutions Ltd" (page ID 2158981047463782), all ads, checked 3 October 2026. Pixel check made on your website's page code the same day.

08 · The plan, month by month

Lighting first, before the dark evenings.

  1. Week 1
    Audit and tidy

    Install the Meta pixel and track the contact form, fix the email on the EV article, and bring two lighting projects to the top of the homepage.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    A garden or exterior lighting job at dusk, a finished room, Andy and Steven on camera, and an EV install from board check to charger.

  3. Week 3
    Campaigns live

    One always-on lighting and full-install campaign for homeowners in Ponteland, Darras Hall, Gosforth, Jesmond and Northumberland. A smaller EV campaign beside it.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New videos added every week to the same campaigns. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and move the budget to what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on jobs won

    We report enquiries, quotes and jobs won by service, and decide together whether EV earns more budget or less.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on enquiries, quotes and jobs won. You keep every video.
