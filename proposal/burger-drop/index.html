The best beef in the city. Time everyone scrolling knew it. The plan we talked about, on paper. Prepared for Hassan by Fusion Creative, August 2026.

Good to finally sit down

Thanks for having us over, showing us round and feeding us properly. The burgers speak for themselves, and after seeing the operation up close, so does everything behind them. This is the plan we discussed, written up so you can see exactly how a month would run.

The short version: Burger Drop's biggest advantage is the thing customers currently can't see. Bakers making buns to recipes that exist only for you. Butchers preparing prime beef that goes straight to your own facility, no middleman anywhere in the chain. The people comparing you on price against cheaper local shops are comparing blind. Our job is to put that difference on every phone screen in the area, so the price reads as exactly what it is: very fair for what's in the box.

The quality content works on both types of customer. The ones who think it's pricey see why it costs what it costs. The ones who assume takeaway burgers mean junk see clean sourcing and change their mind. Either way, they order.

The Big Two
Two Flagship Series

Everything hangs off two recurring formats only Burger Drop can make, because nobody else has the supply chain or the kitchen to back them up.

The quality story
The Source

We film the supply chain itself. The baker mixing a recipe that exists for one customer. The butcher breaking down prime beef. The straight run from butcher to your own facility. One supplier visit a month gives us three to four episodes, released weekly.

Why it works: restaurants almost never show this. It justifies the price to the people comparing you to cheaper shops, and it flips the people who assume takeaway means junk. Nobody argues with footage.

The monthly event
Burger of the Month

Turned from a menu line into a proper franchise. The chef who created it fronts it: the development tasting, the launch film on the 1st, the making-of, and a last call on the final weekend before it disappears for good.

Why it works: scarcity gives people a reason to come in this month, and the chef gives the brand a face people follow. Next month's teaser drops as this month ends, so the loop never stops.
