Where you're at

Thanks for having us down, showing us the operation and feeding us properly. Quick recap of how we see it.

We're Fusion Creative, a content marketing agency based in Newcastle. We work with hospitality businesses across the North East, running their short-form content and paid social so the owners can run the business.

Burger Drop is not competing on the same pitch as the cheap burger shops around each site, and that's the point. Bakers producing buns to recipes that exist only for you. Butchers preparing prime beef that goes straight to your own facility with no middleman touching it. Four sites across Newcastle, Whitley Bay and Sunderland, a franchise model on the way, and food that converts anyone who actually tastes it.

The gap is simple: the customers comparing you on price can't see any of that. Right now the quality story lives inside the building. The job of the content is to put it on every phone screen in the area, so the price point reads as what it is: very reasonable for what's actually in the box.

Two flagship series

Everything hangs off two recurring formats that only Burger Drop can make, because nobody else has the supply chain or the kitchen to back them up.

The quality story

The Source

We film the supply chain itself. The baker mixing a recipe that exists for one customer. The butcher breaking down prime beef. The straight run from butcher to your own facility. One supplier visit a month, three to four episodes each. Restaurants almost never show this, and it does two jobs at once: it justifies the price to the people who compare you to cheaper shops, and it flips the people who assume takeaway burgers mean junk. Nobody argues with footage.

The monthly event

Burger of the Month

Turned from a menu line into a franchise. The chef who created it fronts it: the development tasting, the launch video on the 1st, the making-of, and a last call on the final weekend before it disappears. Scarcity gives customers a reason to come in this month, and the chef gives the brand a face people follow. Next month's teaser drops as this month's ends, so the loop never stops.

A month of content

Around 18 posts a month across Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, from two shoot days. Split roughly half and half between entertaining and informative, and all of it shot to entertain first.

On ad spend

Ad spend is separate and paid by you directly to Meta, so you keep full control and full visibility. We'd recommend starting around £300 to £500 a month; the Source footage makes unusually strong ad creative, because it sells the reason to pay your price, not just the picture of a burger.

One more thing this buys you

You're franchising. Every Source episode and every packed launch day is also proof for prospective franchisees that this brand is worth buying into. The same content works both jobs without costing a penny more.
