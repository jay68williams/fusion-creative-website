02 · Your Meta ads

Always on, one message, one product.

Your ads run from your page "Enhance Conservatories, Windows & Doors" (1.9K followers). We read 32 of the roughly 40 ads in the Library: 22 videos for customers, between 16 and 69 seconds long, and 10 image ads, six of them recruiting fitters and bricklayers. Here are three.

What we found across your ads

1

A limited-time sale that has run for a year

"Up to 1/3 Off" has been in your ads since at least 28 September 2025, under Summer Sale, Spring Sale, Biggest Ever Sale and New Showroom Opening Offers headlines. A "Summer Sale" ran until 14 October 2025 with "Spring Sale" in the text. The people who see an offer most often are the ones closest to buying, and they notice when "final days" lasts six months.

Fix: a dated offer for each season that genuinely ends, and ads about the work itself in between.

2

Your award-winning product is missing

Every customer ad sells "your dream all-year-round conservatory". None mentions orangeries or your National Orangery Installer of the Year award, and extensions appear only in an Easter showroom ad, though your own guides put kitchen conservatory extensions at £20,000 to £60,000 and more.

Fix: separate campaigns for orangeries and extensions, conservatories, and roof replacements, each with its own video.

3

The pictures do not always match the words

Three of your conservatory videos use a green front door as their cover frame, and one is still running ten months on. The "New showroom, now open" headline has run since 28 September 2025.

Fix: the first two seconds always show a finished room, and headlines are refreshed every few weeks.

4

Meta cannot see your quote requests

We found no Meta pixel in your website code or your Google Tag Manager container. Your newer ads send people to your site with a "Get quote" button, so Meta has no way to learn which ads produce quote requests and which only produce views.

Fix: the Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, firing on the quote form and the showroom booking form.

08 · The plan, month by month

Fix the tracking, split the message, keep it always on.

  1. Week 1
    Tracking and audit

    Meta pixel and Conversions API on the quote and showroom forms, a review of all 40 ads, and a dated offer calendar for winter and spring.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    A finished orangery, a kitchen extension, a roof replacement, two customers in their new rooms, and the Benton showroom.

  3. Week 3
    Three campaigns live

    Orangeries and extensions, conservatories and roofs, and showroom visits by area. Your testimonial ad keeps running.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New videos added every week to the same campaigns. We switch off what does not bring quote requests and put the budget behind what does.

  5. Month 3
    Review on builds

    We report quote requests, design visits, showroom visits and builds sold, and set the spring budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on quote requests, showroom visits and builds sold. You keep every video.
