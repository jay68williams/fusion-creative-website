01 · The short version

A strong estate with a narrow set of ads.

Many venues we look at have no ads and no prices online. You have both: about 25 Meta ads since October 2025, a Create Your Day planner with budget bands, a 3D tour and a viewing calendar. 02 · Your Meta ads

Good-looking ads, one message, and nothing for your deadlines.

Your ads run from your page "The Hidden Oak" (5.1K followers). Meta's Ad Library shows 4 live today and about 25 since October 2025. Here are three.

What we found across your ads

1

One message for eleven weeks

All four live ads carry the same text and started on 16 July. The same couples have now seen the same words for eleven weeks. A venue is chosen over months, so the message needs to move on with them.

Fix: keep the best of these running, and add a new video every week to the same campaign.

2

Video has gone from your ads

From October 2025 to May 2026 your ads were short videos, with prices like "from £3,850", "2027 dates from £4,325" and "the Sapling Package from £7,865". The live set is still graphics with no price.

Fix: real weddings, the lakes and the Farmhouse on video, with a guide price on screen.

3

Your deadlines have no ads

Your homepage offers two Farmhouse nights for the price of one on five spring 2027 dates, a saving of £2,310, for bookings made this month only. Six Après-Ski nights are on sale for 4 to 19 December. No live ad mentions either. Your Garden Party had its own ad from March to July.

Fix: a short campaign for the spring dates now, and one for Après-Ski from mid October.

4

We could not confirm what Meta can see

Your website pages carry Google Analytics but no Meta pixel we could find. Your brochure and enquiry forms run on a separate system that loads Meta's pixel code, but we did not see it report a visit when we loaded them.

Fix: in week one, check that brochure downloads and viewings booked are reported to Meta, so ads are judged on viewings, not clicks.

08 · The plan, month by month

Fill the deadlines first, then keep the wedding campaign fresh.

  1. Week 1
    Audit and tidy

    Read your ad account, check what the brochure and viewing forms report to Meta, fix the 20 and 21 figures, give the spring offer its own page.

  2. Week 1
    Spring dates live

    A short campaign for the five spring dates, using footage you already have, before the offer closes.

  3. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    A wedding with permission, the lakes, the Barn, the Farmhouse, the team and a viewing walk.

  4. Week 3
    Campaigns live

    Weddings, judged on brochure downloads and viewings booked. Après-Ski, judged on tickets sold.

  5. Months 2 and 3
    Add, do not restart

    New video every week in the same campaigns. We report enquiries, viewings booked and dates filled, and set the spring budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed for weddings and events, Instagram posting support, and a one-page monthly report on enquiries, viewings booked, dates filled and tickets sold. You keep every video.
