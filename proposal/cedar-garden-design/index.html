01 · The short version

Real expertise and lovely gardens, almost invisible online.

A garden designer sells trust. People are inviting you into their home and handing you a large budget, so they need to know who you are before they call. Your website shows the work and the reviews, but nobody finds it unless they already know your name. We found no Meta ads from Cedar Garden Design, no Meta pixel or Google Analytics on your website, and nine Instagram posts in total. Meanwhile landscapers are advertising to North East homeowners this week.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Qualifications that set you apart from a landscaper: garden designer, horticulturist and gardening tutor.
  • Five consecutive golds for Whickham Library Garden in Northumbria in Bloom.
  • A wide range of clients: homes, hospitals, schools, golf clubs and community health services.
  • Clear, published fees: a £50 consultation within 10 miles of Whickham, and a £250 one-off consultation.
  • The Design Journey: one garden photographed from the original, through the build, to three years later.

You do not need to change how you work. You need people to see you, hear you explain a garden, and then see your work again when they are ready to plan. That is what video and a small, steady ad budget do.

02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, and no way to measure any.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. We found no advertiser page for Cedar Garden Design and no ads mentioning you. When we loaded cedargardendesign.co.uk, no Meta pixel, Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager loaded.

What that means

1

Landscapers are reaching your customers

CK Home Improvements, GLM Driveways and SJB Landscaping are all running ads to North East homeowners this week. None of them is a designer, but they are the ones homeowners are seeing.

Fix: a small campaign shown only to homeowners within reach of Whickham, built from your gardens and your voice.

2

Gardens are planned in winter

A garden built in spring is usually designed months before. If you are not in front of homeowners between now and March, they will book whoever they saw.

Fix: start this autumn with a "design it this winter" message.

3

No way to follow up or count

Someone who looks through your portfolio cannot be shown your work again without a pixel, and you cannot tell which visits turn into consultations.

Fix: Meta pixel, Conversions API and consultation tracking in week one.

Garden design is one of the most visual trades there is, and your website links to Instagram and Facebook. The formats that build trust for a designer:

  • The designer on camera. Why the path curves, why that plant, why that stone. It is what turns a beautiful garden into trust in the person who made it.
  • Before and after. You already photographed one garden from the original to three years later. On video, that story sells itself.
  • Useful planting advice. A horticulturist and tutor explaining one plant a week is content people save and share.

What this means for your ads. You do not need a following to win the next commission. Ads show your work to homeowners within reach of Whickham whether they follow you or not. Instagram matters because it is where they check you out before they call.

What the numbers say

  • They sell the build. You sell the thinking. Their ads are about patios, driveways and renovation costs. A scaled drawing, a planting plan and a lighting plan are a different product, and none of these three is advertising it.
  • They name the place. "Newcastle homeowners" in the first line. "Whickham" or "Gateshead" would stop your clients.
  • They compete on price. GLM leads with £10,500. Your published fees and a calm explanation of the process are a better answer than a discount.
  • None of them has an award. Five years of Northumbria in Bloom gold is proof none of these advertisers can claim.
