"One taste and you're hooked."

Part 02 · The Royal Hotel

Sea views, Spanish City, and seven rooms about to reopen.

People choose a seaside hotel from a phone screen. Right now the only place they can see the Royal is a booking site listing, which takes a cut of every room.

What is working for seaside hotels

Show the room, show the view.

Small, independent hotels are some of the biggest winners on short form video, because a phone shows a room and a view far better than a listing photo does.

Owner walk throughs

Fyfield Manor B&B, Oxfordshire

The owner and her daughter walk through the rooms using trending sounds. Around 10 million views from just 43 videos, and guests now say they booked because of them.

40K TikTok followers, 43 videos
Video ads to bookings

The Botanist, Edinburgh

Creator style videos run as paid ads on TikTok: 1.5 million views and over 700 bookings reported by the platform partner. This is what our Direct Bookings package does with your own footage.

700+ bookings reported
The open goal

Whitley Bay and Tynemouth

We could not find a single Whitley Bay seafront hotel posting short form video. The Grand Hotel in Tynemouth has 13 TikTok followers and no videos. The first hotel on the coast to do this properly owns it.

No local competition yet

Why booking direct matters

Booking.com's base commission for UK hotels is around 15% of the room price, and independents often pay more once the visibility programmes are added. Every guest who books direct because they saw your video keeps that money in the hotel.
