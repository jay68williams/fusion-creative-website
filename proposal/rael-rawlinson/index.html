What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A diploma in Garden Design, and specialist qualifications in wildlife-friendly land management.
  • Gardens for notable homes: Godwit House in Warkworth, the most northern certified Passivhaus in England at the time, a second Passivhaus in Tynemouth, and the RIBA award-winning Rocket House.
  • A clear process: an initial meeting, a site survey, three mood boards, a concept plan, a hand-drawn final plan and a planting plan.
  • Warm client words on your homepage: "Working with Raël on our garden design has been a dream."
  • An Instagram audience that responds, with every recent post we read earning 50 or more likes.

You do not need a new brand or more content. You need your best work shown to homeowners who are planning a garden, and one simple step for them to book a free initial meeting.

Homeowners planning a garden are not seeing you

Fix: a campaign shown to homeowners in the areas you work in, from North Northumberland to Newcastle.

2

Your best posts stop at your followers

Your new build garden post earned 115 likes and 11 comments. Without spend behind it, almost nobody outside your 1,399 followers sees it.

Fix: put a small budget behind the kind of posts that already work, shown to people who do not follow you yet.

3

No way to follow up

Someone who reads three of your project pages cannot be shown your work again without a pixel, and we cannot tell which posts bring enquiries.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, with every booking counted.

08 · The plan, month by month

Add a front door, film the gardens, then reach homeowners before spring.

    Fix the basics

    A simple form to book a free initial meeting, Meta pixel and Conversions API, every booking counted, and your Passivhaus and Rocket House work named on the homepage.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    Finished gardens in autumn light, a design in progress, the drawing board, and Raël on camera.

  3. Week 3
    First campaign live

    Shown to homeowners from North Northumberland to Newcastle, with a "design this winter, plant in spring" message and your best-performing posts behind it.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing bookings and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on designs

    We report bookings, initial meetings and designs commissioned, and set the spring budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on bookings, initial meetings and designs commissioned. You keep every video.
