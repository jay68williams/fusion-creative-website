02 · Your Meta ads

Three sale banners. The 4D design appears in none of them.

These are every ad running on your Facebook page (2K followers) on 1 October 2026, from Meta's public Ad Library. All three send people to an instant form on Facebook.

What these ads have in common

1

A sale that has run for eight months is not a sale

"Up to 30% off" has been live since February under three different names. Customers learn to wait for the next one, and the ASA expects "limited-time" offers to be genuinely limited.

Fix: drop the permanent discount and give a dated reason to act, such as a build slot or a design deadline.

2

Your best asset is invisible

The 4D walkthrough, the sun simulation and the "changed the roof lantern twice on screen" customer story are on your website. None of it is in an ad.

Fix: lead with a screen recording of a real 4D design next to the finished room.

3

Still images, no faces, no Meta pixel

All three ads are still images. Your site has Google Analytics but no Meta pixel, so the people who browse your projects cannot be shown an ad again.

Fix: video creative and a pixel in week one.

An audience of nine thousand would normally bring a hundred or more likes a post. Your ten most recent posts, from May to 1 October, earned between 2 and 11. The pattern is clear:

  • Sale posts get least. Four July posts repeating "save up to 30%" earned 2 to 3 likes each.
  • People and places get most. The reel of Oli from your team showing an orangery kitchen (22 likes), the Darras Hall transformation (9), the Boldon Colliery completion (8).
  • A large audience that has stopped watching. If your followers were gained through competitions or ads, many may never have been interested in extensions. New, relevant viewers matter more than the follower number.

What this means for your ads. On Meta and Instagram, the creative does the targeting. Video of a real 4D design and a real finished room will find the people planning an extension. Another sale banner will find people who like sales.

08 · The plan, month by month

Fix the offer, film the design process, then put it in front of the right people.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the live account

    Replace the permanent sale with a dated reason to act, install the Meta pixel and Conversions API, and link the website to your Instagram.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    One with a 4D design session and the finished room it became, one on a live build and a handover. Your team keeps working; we film around them.

  3. Week 3
    New campaign live

    Three angles tested: the 4D design, real customers, and the problem first. Separate ads for kitchen extensions, orangeries and conservatory replacements.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing consultations and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on signed projects

    We report enquiries, design sessions and signed projects, ad by ad, and decide the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, organic posting, and a one-page report on enquiries, design sessions and signed projects. You keep every video.
