02 · Your Meta ads

Plenty of ads, two loose ends and a missing half.

Your ads run from your page "Newton Hall, Bold, Romantic & Fantastical" (32.4K followers). Meta's Ad Library shows about 15 live today and about 120 since July 2025. We read 91 of them. Here are three.

What we found across your ads

1

Nothing sells the rooms

All 91 ads we read are about weddings. The Bridal Suite with its bronze bath, the dog-friendly courtyard rooms and the beach never appear. Stays are an income that runs all year, and Meta is good at filling rooms.

Fix: a separate, always-on stays campaign with its own videos and budget, judged on room nights.

2

Too many short-lived ads

44 of the 91 ran for a few hours before being switched off. Each restart sends Meta back to learning, and spend goes on testing rather than on the ads that work.

Fix: fewer ads, kept running for weeks, with new videos added rather than whole campaigns restarted.

3

Price first, venue second

Since 1 August every new ad we read leads with a price or a bid: £1,000 starting bids, a £3,999 winter package, packages worth up to £20,000. Offers fill dates, but couples choose a venue on how it feels.

Fix: offers for the dates you need to fill, alongside an always-on campaign of real weddings, the chapel and the beach.

4

Mistakes a couple would notice

Another venue's name in one ad, two different package values in another. A couple about to place a bid will spot both.

Fix: every ad checked by a second person before it goes live.

08 · The plan, month by month

Tidy the loose ends, then give the rooms their own campaign.

  1. Week 1
    Audit and tidy

    Read your ad account, check both pixels and the booking engine, fix the draft text and the room count, shorten the brochure form.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    A wedding with permission, the chapel, the beach, the rooms, the team and a venue tour.

  3. Week 3
    Two campaigns live

    Weddings, judged on venue tours booked and dates filled. Stays, judged on room nights. Each with its own budget.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New videos added every week to the same campaigns. We switch off what is not bringing tours or bookings and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on bookings

    We report enquiries, tours, dates filled and room nights, and set the spring budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed for weddings and stays, Instagram posting support, and a one-page monthly report on enquiries, venue tours booked, dates filled and room nights. You keep every video.
