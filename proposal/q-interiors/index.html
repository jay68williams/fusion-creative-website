02 · Your Meta ads

Lovely rooms, switched off too soon.

Your ads run from your page "Q Interiors" (3K followers). Meta's Ad Library shows around 26 from that page, and none running on 3 October 2026. Here are three of the eight we read.

What we found across your ads

1

Short bursts do not let Meta learn

Each of the eight ads we read ran for 8 to 22 days, with weeks between them. Meta needs a steady run to learn which people enquire. Every new burst starts again from zero.

Fix: one always-on campaign, with new video added each week rather than new ads started and stopped.

2

Rooms, but never people

Every ad we read is a finished room. No designer, no seamstress, no client, and only one before. A full-home project is a big decision, and people buy it from people they trust.

Fix: put Becki, Cath, Jill and Ian on camera, and lead with befores.

3

Only homeowners are spoken to

None of the eight ads we read speaks to housebuilders, hotels or Airbnb owners, yet showhomes and hospitality are two of your biggest services.

Fix: a small, separate campaign for each, with its own message and its own proof.

4

A different button every time

Your ads end with "Sign Up", "Send message", "Learn More" or "call us". Each sends people somewhere different, so it is hard to tell which ads bring enquiries.

Fix: one enquiry route, tracked as a lead through the pixel and Conversions API.

08 · The plan, month by month

Switch to always on, and speak to three audiences.

  1. Week 1
    Audit and tidy

    Check the pixel events and add Conversions API, track the contact form as a lead, add project type and budget to the form, and move the Miller Homes quote to the showhome page.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    Your team on camera, a seamstress at work, the consultation room, and a finished room or showhome with its before.

  3. Week 3
    Always-on campaigns live

    One campaign for homeowners in the areas you cover, one for past website visitors, and a small one for housebuilders and hotel owners.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New video added every week to the same campaigns. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on projects

    We report enquiries, consultations and projects won, and set the budget for spring on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on enquiries, consultations and projects won. You keep every video.
