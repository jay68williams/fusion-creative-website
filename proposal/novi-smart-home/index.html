02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, no pixel, and buyers planning now.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. We found no advertiser page for Novi Smart Home in the UK. The only "Novi Smart Home" in the library is an unrelated business in India. When we checked novismarthome.co.uk, it had Google Analytics but no Meta pixel.

What that means

1

Other installers are advertising cinema rooms every day

Finite Solutions in Yorkshire has run cinema and smart home ads almost continuously since March, aimed at people "building or renovating". Couture Digital in London asks "How much would this cinema cost in your home?" North East homeowners see none of yours.

Fix: an always-on campaign shown only to the North East areas you cover, built from rooms you have finished.

2

The best jobs are decided at first fix

Your process starts with the wiring at first and second fix. A self builder or renovator picks their electrician and smart home installer months before they move in. If you are not in their feed while they plan, someone else gets the job.

Fix: a campaign for self builders and renovators, running all year, not just when work is quiet.

3

No way to follow up

Someone who reads your Allandale cinema write-up for five minutes cannot be shown your work again without a pixel. Buyers spending this much often look several times before they enquire.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, with quote requests counted.

Your Instagram has the right material. It just does not appear often enough to build anything. The pattern:

  • Your best post was about the work ahead. On 26 November 2024 you posted about "the level of projects we have in the next 6 to 8 months". It earned 50 likes, more than anything since.
  • The highlights are already there. Award, Cinema Room, Loxone and Allendale. Each one is a series waiting to be filmed properly.
  • Media walls are in your bio, not on your website. Your bio lists "Home Cinema Design | Media Walls | Multi room Audio". The website has no media walls page, so an ad for them would have nowhere to land.
  • The website does not link to it. Anyone who finds you on Google cannot see your recent work on Instagram.

What this means for your ads. Follower numbers do not matter much here. A 30 second video of a cinema room going dark, or a house being wired at first fix, will find homeowners and self builders across the North East through ads, whether or not they follow you.

What the numbers say

  • Finite advertises every day. Cinema and smart home ads almost continuously since March, with copy aimed at "Yorkshire homeowners" and "Building or renovating in North Yorkshire?". That is the same self builder your quote form is written for.
  • Couture answers the price question. Every cinema buyer wonders what it costs. Their ad asks it out loud, then gives an estimate in five questions. Your cinema page already says you can work to any budget; no ad says so.

08 · The plan, month by month

Fix the tracking, film the rooms, then keep it running.

    Fix the basics

    Meta pixel and Conversions API, quote requests counted, a Projects page built from your five write-ups, one address everywhere, and your Instagram linked.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    One at a finished cinema room and a finished home, one on a live install with the team. You keep working; we film around you.

  3. Week 3
    Two campaigns live

    Cinema rooms and media walls for North East homeowners, and full Loxone homes for self builders and renovators, both running every day.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing quote requests and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on jobs

    We report enquiries, consultations and jobs won, campaign by campaign, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on enquiries, consultations and jobs won. You keep every video.
