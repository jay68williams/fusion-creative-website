02 · Your Meta ads

Good ads, switched off too soon.

Your ads run from your page "The Byirs Weddings" (1.1K followers). Meta's Ad Library shows around nine since January 2026, and none running on 3 October. The page your website links to, "The Byirs" (27.5K followers), shows no ads at all. Here are three.

What we found across your ads

1

Short bursts do not let Meta learn

Your ads ran for two to thirteen days each. Meta needs a steady run to learn which couples book a viewing. Each new burst starts from scratch.

Fix: one always-on campaign, with new video added each week rather than ads started and stopped.

2

Open days need weeks, not days

Your March showcase had two days of ads, and couples were asked to register by email. Your 18 October showcase has no ads behind it yet.

Fix: advertise every open day for at least three weeks, with a registration form that takes one tap.

3

No pixel, so no follow up and no proof

No Meta pixel loads on thebyirs.com. A couple who reads your pricing guide or starts the viewing form cannot be shown your venue again, and no ad can be credited with a viewing.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, with viewing requests and brochure clicks tracked.

4

Two Facebook pages

Your website links to "The Byirs" (27.5K followers). Your ads run from "The Byirs Weddings" (1.1K). Couples who click through land on a different page from the one they saw.

Fix: choose one page for ads and the website, so every couple sees the same name and the same following.

What the numbers say

  • Open days are advertised for weeks. Bradbury Glade advertised its spring open day for four weeks, and its autumn open day ad has run since 15 September. Red Row advertised open days for eight weeks. Your March showcase had two days.
  • The brochure is the next step. Bradbury Glade and The Hidden Oak both end their ads with "Download the brochure". Yours is already on your homepage, and no ad points to it.
  • Everyone says exclusive. The Hidden Oak says "one wedding per day". You take only two a week, and that promise is only in your brochure.
  • Nobody else has your view. Woodland, orchards and lakes are all here. A nine-metre wall of glass onto a reservoir is not.

08 · The plan, month by month

Always on, with every open day advertised for weeks.

  1. Week 1
    Tracking and tidy

    Meta pixel and Conversions API, viewing requests and brochure clicks tracked as enquiries, one Facebook page for ads, and the details on your site made to match.

  2. Weeks 1 to 2
    Your next open day

    Ads for your next showcase, using footage you already have, running for at least three weeks before it with a simple registration form.

  3. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    The drive in and the gates, the Island Room at golden hour, the byre, Colin's Bar, the rooms, and the family to camera.

  4. Week 3
    Always-on campaigns live

    One campaign for newly engaged couples, one for people who have visited your site or watched your videos, both running continuously.

  5. Month 3
    Review on viewings and bookings

    We report enquiries, viewings booked and dates filled, and set the budget for January, when newly engaged couples start looking, on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on enquiries, viewings booked and dates filled. You keep every video.
