A strong product range that site owners are not being shown.

You make 15 pod designs in your own workshop, from a 5m Classic Pod to fully fitted Retreats, plus DIY kits for sites a crane cannot reach. Your brochure has every price, and your FAQ answers planning, VAT, foundations and payment terms. But we found no Pods by Future Rooms page in Meta's Ad Library. What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A full range: traditional, modular and DIY pods, built to order in your own workshop.
  • Real prices in your September 2026 brochure, from £25,250 plus VAT for a Classic Pod.
  • A buyer-friendly offer: 5% VAT on fully specified pods, a £1,000 deposit to hold a build slot, 8 to 12 weeks to delivery, and a 10 year structural guarantee.
  • Named case studies in the North: Peaks & Pods at Boustagill Farm, and two Retreat Pods for Wold Escapes near Driffield.
  • Video that already works as an ad: a reel showing a pod build "in less than 3 minutes".

You do not need a new product or a new website. You need a pod campaign aimed at site owners, video from your workshop and your customers' sites, and a way to follow up every landowner who reads your brochure.

What that means for your pods

1

Site owners are being reached by other makers

The British Pod Co. has about five ads live to people planning glamping and leisure sites. UK Leisure PODs has been running "Start Earning with Luxury Glamping Pods" since February.

Fix: an always-on pod campaign aimed at farmers, landowners, campsite and holiday park owners, kept separate from garden rooms.

2

No way to follow up a serious buyer

We found a Google tag on podsbyfuturerooms.com but no Meta pixel. A landowner who reads three model pages and downloads your brochure cannot be shown your pods again.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, plus a Meta lead form that asks about land, planning, number of pods and timing.

3

Winter is when next season is planned

Your pods take 8 to 12 weeks from order. A site owner who wants guests in spring 2027 is choosing a supplier now.

Fix: start in October with a "pods on site for spring" message.

Your social links go to the Future Rooms accounts, which mostly show garden rooms for homes. The pattern in the 12 most recent Instagram posts, 23 August to 2 October 2026:

  • Pods are a small part of the feed. Nine of the twelve posts are garden rooms and workshops for homes and schools. A farmer who taps your Instagram icon sees garden gyms first.
  • Your best pod content is already filmed. "How long it takes to build a premium glamping pod... in less than 3 minutes" (30 September) and two Lodge pods "Fresh off the production line" (2 October). Each earned 1 like.
  • Low likes are a reach problem, not a content problem. The people who follow a garden room account are homeowners. The people who buy pods are site owners, and organic posts will not find them.

What this means for your ads. The build reel and crane-day footage should be put in front of site owners with paid reach. Pods need their own campaigns, their own audience and ideally their own name on the ads, so they are never mixed with garden rooms.

What the numbers say

  • Price is now a selling point online. The British Pod Co. advertises a Pod Builder that shows a price "instantly", with ranges from £37,500 plus VAT. Your Classic Pod starts at £25,250 plus VAT, and your DIY kits start lower, but that is only in your brochure.

08 · The plan, month by month

Pod campaigns live before the spring 2027 orders are placed.

  1. Week 1
    Tracking and a lead form

    Meta pixel and Conversions API, brochure downloads tracked, a Meta lead form that asks about land, planning, number of pods and timing, and a "from" price on each model page.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Filming

    We are in Newcastle, so we film in blocks: two days at your Worcestershire workshop (builds, the team, a crane day), then northern customer sites such as Peaks & Pods and Wold Escapes, with the owners' permission.

  3. Week 3
    Pod campaign live

    Pods only, aimed at farmers, landowners, campsite and holiday park owners. We start in the North of England, where your North page already points, and widen once the cost per enquiry is known.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New videos added every week to the same campaign. Everyone who downloaded the brochure or opened a model page is shown your case studies.

  5. Month 3
    Review on orders

    We report enquiries, site visits, quotes and £1,000 deposits, and set the budget for the spring on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads for your pods written, built and managed, Instagram posting support for your pod content, and a one-page report on enquiries, quotes and deposits. You keep every video.
