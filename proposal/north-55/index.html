01 · The short version

The vans sell themselves. Nothing else is doing any selling.

Your homepage lists eleven converted Transporters, from £27,000 to £42,000, and all eleven are marked SOLD. That is real proof of demand. But your Instagram has been quiet since August 2025, there are no ads, and a buyer who lands on your site today finds nothing to buy and no way to get on a list for the next one.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A track record that sells: eleven vans from £27,000 to £42,000, all sold.
  • A founder with a story: Joric, a Geordie with ten years of conversions and a background as superyacht crew and head of yacht maintenance.
  • Everything made in-house: rock and roll beds (M1 safety tested), cab seats, upholstery and lightweight ply furniture.
  • A clear, priced product: a Standard and a High-Spec Standard⁺ conversion, with a one-year conversion warranty.
  • Reviews that name you: "Joric builds what you ask without issue."

The fix is not complicated. Film each van as it is built, so buyers are queuing before it is finished, and give them a way to join that queue.

02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, and no list of people waiting.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK over the past year. For the Facebook page linked from your website, it shows none, live or past. We also could not find a Meta pixel on your site.

What that means

1

Each van has to find its own buyer

Without ads or an audience, every finished van starts from zero. Denby Campervans runs a separate ad for each van in stock, with the price in the first line, so buyers see it the week it is ready.

Fix: one short video ad per van, live while it is being built.

2

Nobody can get in the queue

When every van is SOLD, a keen buyer has nowhere to go. There is no "notify me", no waiting list, no "next van" page.

Fix: a simple "be first for the next van" sign-up, fed by the ads.

3

Meta knows nothing about your visitors

Without a pixel, nobody who browses your sold vans can be shown the next one.

Fix: install the pixel in week one.

  • A person and a process. "Here at North 55° Campers we can reupholster your cab seats to match..." (13 likes) and "Our latest newly converted VW T6 headed off yesterday with its proud new owner" (14 likes) beat the product shots.

08 · The plan, month by month

Get a queue started, film every van, then put them in front of buyers.

  1. Week 1
    Set up

    Add "In build now" and a "be first for the next van" sign-up to the site, install the Meta pixel, refresh the footer.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    One in the workshop on the van being built, one with a finished van and Joric. We film around your work.

  3. Week 3
    New campaign live

    One ad per van in build, plus conversion enquiries for people who own a Transporter. Aimed at VW and campervan buyers across the North and Scotland.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on vans and builds sold

    We report sign-ups, viewings and sales, ad by ad, and decide the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, organic posting, and a one-page report on sign-ups, viewings and sales. You keep every video.
