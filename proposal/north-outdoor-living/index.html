02 · Your Meta ads

Two ads, 21 days in total.

Your ads run from your page "North Outdoor Living" (12.3K followers). Meta's Ad Library shows two, and we read both. Neither is running today.

What we found

1

Fifteen days in April, then nothing

"Dreaming of your very own relaxation garden oasis. Contact us today." It ran from 1 to 15 April 2026. A soft message and a short run, followed by five months with no ads.

Fix: one campaign that stays on, with new video added each week.

2

Your best idea ran for six days

"A GARDEN ROOM WITH A DIFFERENCE" ran from 17 to 22 September 2026: a video of the garden room and hot tub space you built last year. A real project on film is exactly what works on Meta. It stopped after six days.

Fix: give it a proper run, and film one finished project every month.

3

A pixel with nothing to follow up

Your website has a Meta pixel, so it is counting visitors. Without ads, the people who looked at a hot tub or a garden room are never shown your work again.

Fix: Conversions API and a campaign for recent website visitors and your Facebook followers in week one.

08 · The plan, month by month

Start now, and run through the winter.

  1. Week 1
    Audit and tracking

    Check the pixel events and add Conversions API, track wet test bookings and enquiries, and fix the delivery page.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    One at a private wet test in the showroom, one at finished installs and garden rooms.

  3. Week 3
    Campaigns live

    One campaign for new buyers in the areas you cover, one for website visitors and your Facebook followers, each with a wet test as the goal.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New video every week into the same campaigns. We switch off what is not bringing wet tests and enquiries and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on sales

    We report wet tests, enquiries and sales, and set the spring budget for garden rooms and decking on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, posting on Instagram and Facebook, and a one-page report on wet tests, enquiries and sales. You keep every video.
