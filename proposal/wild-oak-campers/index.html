What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A real origin story: a builder who converted his own van in 2017 to travel Europe, then built for others.
  • Interiors with genuine character: oak, butler sinks, tiled floors, open shelving, off-grid solar.
  • A workshop in Byker, close to most of the region's buyers.
  • Customers who say it plainly: "Home from home! Couldn't be happier!" (Liz and Stu).
  • Good instincts on Instagram before it went quiet: named customer reveals and build-progress reels.

You do not need a bigger workshop. You need your builds filmed, posted every week, and put in front of people in the North East who are planning a van.

02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, and no Instagram since last September.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. We found no advertiser page for Wild Oak Campers and no ads mentioning you. Your website loads Google Analytics but no Meta pixel.

What that means

1

Buyers see national converters instead

Denby Campervans and Spartan Campers are running ads with a specific van and a price in the first line. A buyer in Newcastle is shown them, not a workshop three miles away.

Fix: a small, steady campaign showing your builds to people planning a conversion.

2

A quiet Instagram looks like a closed business

A buyer who finds you on Instagram sees nothing newer than September 2025. Many will assume you have stopped trading.

Fix: post every week, starting with the vans you have built since.

3

Nothing to follow up with

Without a pixel, people who look at your projects page cannot be shown your work again.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one.

What the numbers say

  • One van, one ad. Denby builds a separate ad for each van, with its price. You could do the same for every build you finish.

08 · The plan, month by month

Restart the feed, film the builds, then advertise steadily.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Meta pixel and Conversions API, a simple build enquiry form, footer updated.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    The workshop, a build in progress, and one or two past customers with their vans.

  3. Week 3
    First campaign live

    Your story, a reveal and a workshop walk-round, shown to people in the North East planning a conversion.

  4. Month 2
    Weekly posting and ads

    New video every week, on Instagram and in the ads. We keep what brings enquiries and cut what does not.

  5. Month 3
    Review on enquiries and deposits

    We report enquiries, workshop visits and deposits, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, weekly Instagram posting, and a one-page report on enquiries, visits and deposits. You keep every video.
