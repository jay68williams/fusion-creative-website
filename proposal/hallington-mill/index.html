I

Everything, in the order it happens

The plan you have already read, turned into a calendar. Fusion films and buys the media in the same team, so the shoot, the posts and the ads are one plan rather than three suppliers.

Week one

The pipeline gets a front door

  • Google Analytics and the Meta pixel on hallingtonmill.co.uk, so every enquiry has a source and every visitor can be reached again.
  • Email and phone on the contact page, next to the forms. One button, "book a viewing", on every page and in every bio.
  • Your Bridebook and Airbnb reviews on the homepage, where a couple lands from Instagram.
  • Bridebook, Hitched and Google Business Profile checked and brought up to date, with the 2027 and 2028 brochures linked.
Month one

First capture day, and the wedding

  • A capture day at the mill in autumn: the woodland, the Hay Shed dressed, the stays, the valley, you two on camera if you are willing.
  • The three-day wedding filmed start to finish, Thursday arrival to Sunday round the kitchen table, when a couple says yes.
  • Instagram taken over: 20 plus edited pieces a month, a third of them reels, filmed not stock. A TikTok started from zero. Facebook kept alive.
Every month

Capture, post, promote, report

  • A capture day each month. Weddings when there are weddings, the stays and the seasons when there are not.
  • Captions, hashtags, scheduling and replies to comments and messages handled, so an enquiry in the DMs never sits.
  • One open afternoon a season, mill dressed, fire lit, promoted to couples within an hour and filled.
  • A one page report: enquiries and where they came from, viewings booked, dates confirmed, what the ads cost per viewing.
Month two on

The ads

  • The three-day film and the best capture-day pieces run as ads to engaged couples within an hour of Hallington.
  • Retargeting on the pixel: everyone who visited, watched or enquired sees the open afternoon and the 2027 dates.
  • Budget agreed with you and paid direct to Meta on your own card. Judged on one number: viewings booked.
Always

Contact

  • A WhatsApp line to Jay for anything in between.
