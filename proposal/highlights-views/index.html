08 · Airbnb or booking direct

What a booking is worth to you on Airbnb, and booked direct.

Your listing shows guests one all-in price with no separate service fee. That means Airbnb's host-only fee applies: 15.5% of every booking, taken from your payout. Airbnb says UK service fees carry 20% VAT; its pages do not say whether that sits inside the 15.5% or on top, and some industry sites put the total at about 18.6% for hosts who are not VAT registered. Booking.com is typically 15% and up. If you are paying closer to 30% in total, the rest is likely your management fee, which we have not seen and have left out of every figure below.

What booking direct needs

  • A website with a live calendar synced to Airbnb, so a night can never be sold twice.
  • A booking engine. Smoobu is £26.69 a month billed annually with no booking fee (or £24.57 a month plus 0.9% a booking); Beds24 and Lodgify are alternatives.
  • Card payments: Stripe charges 1.5% + 20p on standard UK cards, with no monthly fee.
  • Damage cover. Airbnb's AirCover only covers bookings made on Airbnb. For direct guests we would use Superhog (about £2 a month plus £2.50 a booking, a third party price to confirm) or a refundable deposit held on the guest's card.
  • An ID check, house rules, a cancellation policy, and refunds handled by you rather than Airbnb.

Our recommendation. Keep Airbnb. It brings the demand and holds your reviews. Add a direct site alongside it for returning guests and for everyone our ads reach. The fee saving alone, about £108 to £117 a booking, will not pay for our plan. What pays is filling empty nights: about 5 extra nights a month booked direct (about 6 if they book through Airbnb) covers the three month plan, £10 a day of ads and the booking software, and 6 or more puts you ahead.

Sources, checked 8 October 2026: airbnb.co.uk help articles 1857 (service fees), 436 (VAT) and 2869 (Host Damage Protection terms); stripe.com/gb/pricing; smoobu.com/en/pricing; beds24.com/pricing; Superhog price via a third party review.
