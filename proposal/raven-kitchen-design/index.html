02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, no pixel, and a quiet feed.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. Your page "Raven Kitchen Design" (469 Facebook followers, 1.8K on Instagram) has never run one. When we loaded ravenkitchendesign.co.uk, Google Analytics loaded but no Meta pixel did.

What that means

1

Other kitchen companies are reaching your customers

Kutchenhaus Newcastle is running around 11 ads for German kitchens right now. Kitchens Plus Bathrooms is offering "20% OFF Your New 2026 Kitchen" to Newcastle homeowners.

Fix: a campaign shown only to homeowners in the areas you work in, built from your own kitchens.

2

Kitchens are planned months ahead

A £20,000 kitchen is often researched for months before a showroom visit, and often planned around an extension. If Raven is not in someone's feed while they plan, the first showroom they visit is someone else's.

Fix: an always-on campaign, so you are in front of planners all year, not only when they search.

3

No way to follow up

Someone who spends ten minutes in your Completed Projects gallery cannot be shown your work again, because there is no Meta pixel on the site.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, with showroom appointment requests counted.

08 · The plan, month by month

Install the pixel, film the kitchens, then reach the right homes.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Meta pixel and Conversions API, showroom appointment requests counted, old pages removed, one experience line used everywhere.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    The showroom, one or two finished kitchens, an install in progress, and the two of you on camera.

  3. Week 3
    First campaign live

    Shown to homeowners in the areas you work in, with a showroom appointment as the next step.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on kitchens

    We report enquiries, showroom visits, designs and kitchens ordered, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting, and a one-page report on enquiries, showroom visits and kitchens ordered. You keep every video.
