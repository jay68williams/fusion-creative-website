02 · Your Meta ads

No ads. Smaller installers are filling the gap.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK over the past year. For your Facebook page (2.7K followers) it shows none, live or past. We also found no Meta pixel on your website.

What that means

1

Installers with a fraction of your audience are advertising

Essex Epoxy Coatings (356 Facebook followers) and ResGen (252) were both running garage floor ads to homeowners on 1 October. Your Facebook audience is bigger than both put together, several times over.

Fix: a steady Meta campaign behind your best garage and metallic floor videos.

2

A DIY brand is teaching homeowners to do it themselves

Epodex has kept a floor video ad running in the UK since December 2024: "Design your floor with EPODEX epoxy resin." That is aimed at the people who would otherwise call you.

Fix: show what a professional pour looks like, and why the preparation matters.

3

No pixel

Without a Meta pixel, people who read your cost guide or look at your gallery cannot be shown your work again.

Fix: install the pixel in week one.

08 · The plan, month by month

Fix the links, sharpen the edit, then put a budget behind it.

  1. Week 1
    Set up

    Fix or create the TikTok account and the website link, install the Meta pixel, and agree the jobs to lead with (garage, metallic, countertops).

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    One full pour from preparation to reveal, one with finished floors in use and Justin on camera. Plus your existing clips, re-edited with stronger openings.

  3. Week 3
    Campaign live

    Three angles tested: the pour, the garage reveal, and the price. Aimed at North East homeowners first, then national for metallic and commercial work.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing quotes and put the budget behind what is. TikTok and Instagram posting four times a week.

  5. Month 3
    Review on quotes and jobs

    We report enquiries, quotes and booked floors, ad by ad, and decide the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram and TikTok posting, and a one-page report on enquiries, quotes and booked floors. You keep every video.
