You are spending on ads. The ads are not spending your best material.

Meta's Ad Library shows around 84 ads from APS Detailing Ltd since March, two of them running today. Almost all repeat three messages about swirl marks and quick car washes, and most are still images. What we found across the 19

1

Three messages, recycled

"Your car might look clean...", "Ditch quick car washes..." and "Stop wasting time on quick car washes..." make up nearly every ad. Three of the 19 are video. People who scroll past once will scroll past every time after.

Fix: new creative every week from real jobs in your workshop.

2

Ads with no pixel behind them

Your site loads Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, but no Meta pixel. Meta cannot tell which ads brought a quote request, and cannot show your ads again to people who priced up PPF and left.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, with quote requests counted.

3

Selling a car wash to people who want protection

The offer is "Book now, get a FREE maintenance wash". Your highest-value work is PPF and wraps from £1,450 to £4,500 plus VAT. The ads speak to the cheapest end of what you do.

Fix: separate campaigns for PPF, ceramic coating and wraps, each with its own price and proof.

08 · The plan, month by month

Measure the ads, film the work, then rebuild every week.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Meta pixel and Conversions API through your Tag Manager, quote requests counted, the homepage typo and placeholder post cleared up.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    Real jobs in the bay: a PPF install, a wrap, a correction, and the team on camera. You keep working; we film around you.

  3. Week 3
    New campaigns live

    Separate campaigns for PPF, ceramic coating and wraps, each with its price and its own proof, replacing the recycled car-wash ads.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week from that week's cars. We switch off what is not bringing quotes and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on booked jobs

    We report quote requests, bookings and booked value, campaign by campaign, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, posting on Instagram, and a one-page report on quotes, bookings and value. You keep every video.
