What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Full conversions on VW and Ford vans, plus the smaller jobs that bring repeat customers: pop-tops, heaters, electrics, awnings.
  • Interiors with real personality: colour-matched leather, VW floor logos, patterned tiles.

02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, and nothing to measure them with.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. Your page, Custom Fit Campers (879 followers), shows none. When we loaded customfitcampers.co.uk, Google Analytics loaded but no Meta pixel.

What that means

1

National converters fill the gap

Denby Campervans and Spartan Campers are running ads with a specific van and its price. A VW owner in Durham sees them, not a workshop down the road.

Fix: a steady campaign showing your builds and upgrades to people near you.

2

Small jobs are your best way in

A pop-top or a diesel heater is a quicker decision than a full build, and a customer who trusts you with one often comes back for more.

Fix: separate ads for upgrades, with a starting price.

3

Nothing to follow up with

Without a pixel, people who browse your work cannot be shown it again.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one.

08 · The plan, month by month

Track it, film it, then advertise steadily.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Meta pixel and Conversions API, "from" prices on the main services, footer updated.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    The workshop on a busy day, a pop-top or heater install, and two finished vans with their owners.

  3. Week 3
    First campaigns live

    One campaign for full conversions, one for upgrades, both shown to van owners near you.

  4. Month 2
    Weekly posting and ads

    New video every week, on Instagram and in the ads. We keep what brings enquiries and cut what does not.

  5. Month 3
    Review on jobs booked

    We report enquiries and jobs booked, campaign by campaign, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, weekly Instagram posting, and a one-page report on enquiries and jobs booked. You keep every video.
