02 · Your Meta ads, ad by ad

Eight live ads. All sale banners, one stock video, no people.

These are every ad running on your Facebook page on 1 October 2026, taken from Meta's public Ad Library. All of them send people to an instant form on Facebook, which is why your website has no Meta pixel (more on that below).

What these ads have in common

1

Three ads are selling deadlines that have passed

"Avoid 2025 price increases", "this spring" and "ready before autumn" are all live in October 2026. A homeowner reads that as a business that is not paying attention, which is the opposite of what you are selling.

Fix this week: pause or rewrite all three.

2

The discounts fight your own promise

Across the live ads you are offering 20%, 30% and 40% off, free installation, and "was £7,995, now £4,995". Your website offers £250 off and says "no hard sell" and "always-honest prices". When the ad shouts "Hurry, slots are filling fast!" and the website says the opposite, people trust neither. Stacked percentages also train customers to wait for the next sale.

Fix: one offer, the same on the ads and the site, and let proof do the selling.

3

A price shown without VAT

The £4,995 window bundle says "excluding VAT". The UK advertising code (CAP Code, rule 3.18) expects prices shown to consumers to include VAT. "Was" prices also need to be prices you genuinely charged for a meaningful period. Worth fixing before a competitor or customer raises it.

Fix: show the VAT-inclusive price, £5,994.

4

Nobody from Paramount appears in any ad

Every image is a designed banner or a stock-style photo. Your strongest claim, your own team doing every job, is never shown. In this market, faces and real homes are what earn trust, as the competitor section shows.

Fix: the core of our proposal. Film your team at real jobs.

5

Three versions of the brand, and two stray pages

Your website logo reads "Paramount windows, doors, extensions". Most of your ads use the triangle "Paramount Home Improvements" logo. One runs as "Paramount Windows & Doors". Searching Meta also brings up two extra Facebook pages using your name ("Paramount Home Improvements", 46 followers, and "Paramount Home Improvements NE", 0 followers). Customers checking you out can land on the wrong one.

Fix: one name and one logo everywhere; claim or close the stray pages.

Your last twelve Instagram posts (4 June to 29 September) earned between 0 and 3 likes each. That is not because the work is uninteresting. Look at what is in the captions:

  • 25 Sep: "our customer had windows and doors fitted badly by a big national company, she had drafts, damp..." That is a ready-made hook. As a photo post it reached almost no one.
  • 23 Jul: "Huge structural steels being installed for one of our customers in Backworth, Whitley Bay." Steel going in is exactly the kind of moment people stop scrolling for. It needed the first two seconds to show it.
  • 16 Aug: "We are looking to add another team to our building division... one of the fastest growing companies in the North East." You are hiring, which tells us you have the capacity to take on more work.

Why this matters for your ads, not just your followers. On Meta and Instagram, the ad creative does the targeting. Real, specific video of your team and your jobs is what the platforms show to the right homeowners at a lower cost. Sale banners are what they show to everyone, at a higher one.

08 · The plan, month by month

Fix what is live, then film, then scale what works.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the live account

    Pause or rewrite the three out-of-date ads, remove the duplicate, show VAT-inclusive prices, settle on one brand name and logo, and agree one offer for ads and site. Install the Meta pixel and Conversions API inside your existing cookie banner. Correct the banner and opening hours.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    One day at live jobs (a roof, an extension in progress, a windows install), one at the showroom and a handover. We bring the camera, the plan and the questions. Your team just does their job.

  3. Week 3
    New campaign live

    Three angles tested against each other: your team as proof, the problem first, and honest pricing. Separate ads for conservatory roofs, windows and doors, and extensions, so we can see the cost of an enquiry for each.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is. Organic posting four to five times a week alongside.

  5. Month 3
    Review on sales, not likes

    Using the enquiry source your website already records, we report enquiries, surveys booked and jobs won, for each ad. That decides the budget for the next quarter.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, organic posting, and a one-page report on enquiries, surveys and sales. You keep every video, to use anywhere.
