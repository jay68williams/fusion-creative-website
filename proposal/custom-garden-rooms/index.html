02 · Your Meta ads

Your ads run from your page "Custom Garden Rooms & Living Spaces - North East" (3.8K people follow it). Meta's Ad Library shows about three ads from it, all now finished. Here they are.

What we found across your ads

1

Nothing is running as winter planning starts

A garden room or extension is planned months ahead. Homeowners who start looking this winter will book spring and summer dates with whoever is in their feed.

Fix: one always-on campaign from October, so it has learned by the new year.

2

Your price ad was switched off after three days

"From £18,250" tells a homeowner straight away whether you are right for them. That filters out time-wasters before your site visit. Three days is not long enough for Meta to learn who enquires.

Fix: keep the price ad running, and add new videos to it every week.

3

Graphics, not the builds

Two of the three ads are designed graphics with lists of services. Your Instagram shows the builds themselves, inside and out, and the people who use them.

Fix: video walk-throughs of real builds and your showroom, filmed by us.

08 · The plan, month by month

Switch on before homeowners plan for spring.

  1. Week 1
    Audit and tidy

    Check the pixel events and add Conversions API, track quote requests and showroom bookings, and put the price and the showroom on the homepage.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    The showroom, a finished office and gym, a build in progress, and a customer or school if they agree.

  3. Week 3
    Always-on campaigns live

    One campaign to homeowners within your 25 to 30 miles, one for people who have visited your site, both running continuously into spring.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New videos added every week to the same campaigns. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on builds booked

    We report site surveys, showroom visits, quotes and builds booked, and set the spring budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on site surveys, showroom visits, quotes and builds booked. You keep every video.
