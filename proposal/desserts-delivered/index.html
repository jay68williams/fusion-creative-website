Who actually buys

Your buyers are senders, not eaters, and they are all over the UK. London is the biggest single town in your reviews, followed by Glasgow, Birmingham, Manchester and Liverpool. The North East is under 3% of reviewers. Birthdays dominate, especially milestone ages, which is why age balloons and gift notes are two of your four best sellers.

Birthdays, milestone ages
The far-away sender

A parent, grandparent or partner who cannot be in the room. The trigger is realising the birthday is a few days away and they will not be there. They need to see it arrive in one piece, on the day, with their message.

What is working for them
  • Always on. A settled set of ads that runs all year, with seasonal bursts added on top.
  • One promise, repeated. "Order by 4pm for next day" is in the headline again and again. It answers the buyer's real fear.
  • Cheap, native phone video. Unboxings, piping behind the scenes, candles being lit. Vertical, about 16 seconds. None of it looks like an ad.
  • The gift, not the cake. Branded box, ribbon, tin and a free personalised message are the star.
  • A big seasonal hero. A £120 Cupcake Advent Calendar on pre-order now, pushed with 29 ads last Christmas and a 478K-follower brand giveaway this week.
  • Trust off the ad. 4.7 on Trustpilot, a corporate page with big-brand logos, and a phone number at the top of the site.
Where they leave room for you
  • Almost entirely cupcakes. No whole celebration cakes, no cheesecakes, no brownie boxes to buy year round. The "birthday cake delivered" buyer is yours.
  • Their creative has gone stale. Nothing new since 10 August, and most of the live set is 8 to 10 months old. Fresh monthly creative will out-learn it.
  • No faces. Hands only in the ads we sampled, no founder, no real customers on camera. You have a bakery team in Newcastle and we are there to film them.
  • Weak offers. No free delivery and no bundle offers in their ads, one 10% code last Christmas.
  • Generic landing pages. Most ads drop people onto a 135-product cupcake collection.
  • Value. A box of 6 cupcakes is £24 to £28.50 plus £6.95 delivery. Your 7" cake serves about 14 for £44.99.

What wins across the market
  • A deadline and "tomorrow" in the headline. Bear's, bakerdays and MailBakes all run it for months at a time.
  • Build-your-own or mix-and-match boxes. Your variety and Choose Your Own boxes fit straight in.
  • Sending love from a distance. The far-away sender is the category's strongest emotion.
  • A real review as the creative. One quote, big, on the image.
  • Humour. GET BAKED and Cakehead keep funny ads running for months.
  • Winners get relaunched, not retired. The same idea runs for a year or more under new ads.
Gaps almost nobody is filling
  • Pay in 4. No ad we found names Clearpay or Klarna, and only one gifting brand mentions paying later, in small link text. You take Clearpay: £11.25 today for a cake that serves 14.

What the overhaul covers
  • A homepage built for a stranger from an ad: what you are, star rating, "order by 2pm, next day UK-wide", your real cakes, occasion tiles.
  • The popup fixed: delayed, a real reason to sign up, and switched off for people arriving from ads.
  • A proper basket: a slide-out basket with the top add-ons inside it, no terms wall, no forced tickbox.
  • One delivery story everywhere: one cut-off, the delivery price on the product page, frozen explained as a positive, one shelf life.
  • Product pages that sell: price, rating, delivery date and the button on the first screen, gift note on the page, real slice and packing photos, allergen line fixed.
  • Collections that are easy to browse on a phone: two per row, star ratings, "serves 14", best sellers first.
  • Occasion landing pages for the ads: birthdays, Christmas thank-yous, Mother's Day.
  • Reviews where people decide: ratings on the homepage and collections, and automatic review invites after every order.
  • Tracking cleaned up: one Meta pixel path with the Conversions API confirmed, duplicate Google tags removed, a proper cookie banner.
  • Speed: heavy images compressed, unused apps and duplicate scripts removed, a static hero in place of the slider.
  • The clean-up: Lorem ipsum, perfumes, the Covid notice, empty collections, typos and conflicting "Our Story" dates.
  • Done on a copy of your theme, so the live shop keeps trading. You see it and sign it off before anything switches.
