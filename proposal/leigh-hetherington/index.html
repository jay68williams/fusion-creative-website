01 · The short version

A real couturier, sold by word of mouth alone.

Most bridal shops sell dresses made by someone else. You design them, your team cuts a paper pattern, makes a toile and fits it in your own studio, and you have done it for over twenty years. Your brides clearly love the result: one bride's own post about her dress earned 347 likes, more than ten times your latest post. But that love is not being used. We found no Meta ads from Leigh Hetherington Bridal Wear, no Meta pixel or Google tracking on your website, and three other bridal shops advertising to North East brides right now.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • A true bespoke service: consultation, sketches and fabric swatches, measurements, a paper pattern, a toile fitting, then the dress.
  • Over twenty years as a couturier, after studying fashion design at Northumbria University.
  • A studio in the centre of Newcastle with large cutting tables, a chandelier and three sash windows: made for video.
  • More than gowns: two collections, occasion wear for mothers of the bride and groom, alterations, handmade headpieces, veils and heirloom brooch bouquets.
  • Proof already in public: an Unveiled magazine photoshoot, a real bride's story on your website, and brides tagging you in posts that hundreds of people like.

You do not need a new brand or a new website. You need video of what happens in your studio, your brides' own posts working as adverts, and a steady campaign in front of newly engaged couples before the new year.

02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, no pixel, and the busiest season ahead.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. Your page "Leigh Hetherington Bridal Wear" (1.1K followers) shows no ads, active or past. When we loaded leighhetherington.co.uk, no Meta pixel and no Google Analytics loaded.

What that means

1

Other bridal shops are reaching your brides

Wedding Wardrobe, Wed4Less and The Bridal Box Boutique are all running ads to North East brides this week, with studio tours, offers and reviews.

Fix: a campaign shown to newly engaged people within reach of Newcastle, built from your studio and your brides.

2

A bespoke dress needs a head start

Your process runs from consultation to sketches, measurements, a toile fitting and the finished dress. A bride who books late may go to a shop with dresses on the rail. Many couples get engaged over Christmas and New Year and start looking soon after.

Fix: start in October, so the campaign has learned by January.

3

No way to follow up

A bride who spends ten minutes looking at your Modern Classics collection cannot be shown your work again, because there is no pixel on your site.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, and appointment requests counted.

06 · What is working in your market

A person, a place, and a reason to book.

These ads are live now from the bridal shops above. Some of the patterns transfer to couture; the tone should stay yours.

1

Put Leigh on camera

Twenty years of couture is best heard in your own voice, at the cutting table, not read on a website.

2

Let brides speak

Run brides' own posts as partnership ads, with their permission. Nothing a shop says about itself is as believable.

3

Show the making

Sketch, pattern, toile, fitting. No shop advertising here can show it, because none of them makes the dress.

08 · The plan, month by month

Track first, film the studio, then reach brides before January.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Meta pixel and Conversions API, an appointment request form, requests counted, the journal and footer brought up to date.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    The studio, a toile fitting, a headpiece being made, and Leigh on camera. We ask past brides for permission to use their posts.

  3. Week 3
    First campaigns live

    One shown to newly engaged people within reach of Newcastle, one for alterations, both running continuously.

  4. Month 2
    Add brides' posts

    Partnership ads from brides' own posts go live. New video every week; we switch off what is not bringing appointments.

  5. Month 3
    Review on orders

    We report appointment requests, consultations and orders, and set the January budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on appointment requests, consultations and orders. You keep every video.
