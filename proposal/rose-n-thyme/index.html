Two ways to work together.

Both start with the same week of fixes to the store. One of them then puts the bouquet in the bag in front of the rest of the UK, and keeps it there through Christmas, Valentine's and Mother's Day.

Option one Recommended

Content and managed ads

The store fixed, a shoot in the shop every month, and the ads run for you as one system. Fusion films and buys the media in the same building.

a month, three months minimum

Week oneThe store, fixed

  • Subscription price table corrected at every size and length, and the subscription put in the menu and on the homepage with proper photography.
  • Delivery date picker and card message field on every bouquet, at the point of choosing.
  • Reviews on every product page, with your Facebook recommendations imported, and a review request sent after every order.
  • Nationwide delivery priced for the whole UK, and the nationwide collection filled with all thirty bouquets rather than four.
  • Occasion pages built and linked: birthday, thank you, sympathy, new baby, just because. Each with the date, the card message and the vase in reach.
  • Top bar typo, "Compliment" wording, product types, the duplicate casket sprays and wreaths, and the two "Decemeber" listings tidied.

Week oneTracking

  • Meta pixel checked and the Conversions API added, so purchases are counted properly on the server as well as the browser.
  • Google Analytics purchase and add-to-basket events.
  • Email capture with a first order offer, so nationwide visitors who do not buy today are not lost.

Every monthContent

  • A shoot day in the shop each month, Bearsden or Hyndland. Hands, ribbon, box, water, door, the window display, the workshops. Florists on camera if they are willing, the product if not.
  • Fifteen to twenty short videos and stills a month, cut for ads and for organic posting.
  • Posted and scheduled across Instagram, Facebook and a new TikTok, with captions and hashtags written for you.
  • Every occasion and peak gets its creative built the month before it lands.

Every monthThe ads

  • Glasgow radius campaign: the shops, same-day delivery, and the wreath workshops. Ten miles, where no other florist is advertising.
  • Nationwide campaign: "arrives in water, in our box" by occasion, each ad landing on the page that answers it.
  • Retargeting on the pixel you already have: visitors, basket abandoners, past customers.
  • Creative refreshed from each shoot; losers switched off, winners given the budget.
  • Budget managed against the numbers. Your ad spend is paid straight to Meta on your own card; we suggest £50 to £100 a day to start and let the first two weeks decide.

November onwardThe peaks

  • The subscription sold as the Christmas gift from November, with the price table fixed and the product in the menu.
  • Christmas bouquets, wreaths and the workshop dates.
  • Valentine's and Mother's Day, each built a month ahead with the numbers from the previous peak setting the budget.

AlwaysReporting and contact

  • One report a month in plain English: spend, sales, return, what moved and what did not.
  • A WhatsApp line to Jay for anything in between.

From youWhat we need

  • Shopify collaborator access and access to your Meta Business Manager (page, ad account, pixel).
  • A shoot date each month and an hour of your time on the day.
  • Ad spend on your own card, set at the level you are comfortable with.
Billed monthly in advance, first month on signing. Three months minimum, which takes you through Christmas, then month to month. Ad spend is separate and stays yours.
