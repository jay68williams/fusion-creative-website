Garden design is one of the most visual trades there is, and the platforms that sell it are visual. The formats that work:

  • Slow reveals. A gimbal walk through a finished garden at golden hour.
  • Before and after. The same view, months apart.
  • Designer to camera. Why the path curves, why that tree, why that stone. It is what turns a beautiful garden into trust in the people who made it.

What this means for your ads. You do not need a following to win the next commission. Ads show your work to homeowners in exactly the postcodes you serve, whether they follow you or not.
