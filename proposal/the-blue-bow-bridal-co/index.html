02 · Your Meta ads

One good ad, for one week.

Your ads run from your page "The Blue Bow Bridal Company" (11.1K followers). Meta's Ad Library shows one ad from that page, for your Festive Super Sale.

What we found

1

A week is not long enough for Meta to learn

The ad ran for seven days and then stopped. Meta needs a steady run to learn which brides book and buy. Nothing has run since, so every new start begins from scratch.

Fix: one always-on campaign for brides across your areas, with new video added each week.

2

We could not confirm a Meta pixel

Your website has Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, but we found no Meta pixel in the page code from outside. Without one, Meta cannot see who sent an enquiry, and cannot find more brides like them.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, counting form enquiries and taps on your phone number.

3

The ad sold a sale, not the boutique

The bridal shops advertising today compete on price and stock. Your difference is the two-hour private appointment, the designers and the team. None of that has been in an ad yet.

Fix: lead with the appointment and real brides. Keep the Festive Super Sale for people who already know you.

  • The outlets compete on price. Bridal Factory Outlet and Wed4Less lead with discounts and walk-ins. You should not try to beat them there.

08 · The plan, month by month

Always on before the engagement season.

  1. Week 1
    Tracking and booking

    Meta pixel and Conversions API through your Tag Manager, counting form enquiries and phone taps. An appointment request at the top of every page.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    Real appointments with permission, the suites and the Luxe Lounge, gowns from each designer, and your team.

  3. Week 3
    Always-on campaign live

    One campaign for newly engaged brides across Durham, Teesside, Sunderland, Gateshead, Washington and Darlington, and one for people who already know you.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New reels added every week to the same campaign. The Festive Super Sale goes to warm audiences. We switch off what is not booking appointments.

  5. Month 3
    Review on gowns sold

    We report appointments booked and gowns sold, and set the January budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on enquiries, appointments booked and gowns sold. You keep every video.
