02 · Your Meta ads

Real stories, switched off after a few days.

Your ads run from your page "Kate Collins Couture" (844 followers). Meta's Ad Library shows about five since December 2025, two of them video, in three bursts: 30 December to 5 January, 6 to 14 March, and 2 to 6 September. Here are two.

What we found across your ads

1

A week at a time does not let Meta learn

Your ads ran in bursts of four to nine days, then stopped for months. Meta needs a steady run to learn which brides enquire. Each new burst starts again from nothing.

Fix: one campaign that stays on, with a new video added each week.

2

No pixel, so no way to know what worked

We found no Meta pixel on kate-collins.com. The brides who clicked your ads and looked at your galleries could not be counted, and cannot be shown your dresses again.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, with the contact form tracked as an enquiry.

3

Your stories are ads no shop can copy

Alice on the tube. The Lartington Hall bride: "A wedding dress doesn't have to be long, or have lace." These are the reasons to choose a designer over a shop, and they were shown for days.

Fix: a bank of bride stories that runs all year, ending with "book your free consultation".

You post often, and you show the work. The pattern in your recent reels:

  • Process beats product. "How do you design a wedding dress?" (11 likes) and "Buttons! 60 of them and 3 hours sewing time" (8) did better than "Which one would you choose?" (2).
  • Short dresses are a theme. Your mini dresses reel earned 12 likes, and two of your three ad bursts were about short dresses.
  • The coast is part of the story. You have posted that you are "so very lucky to live within minutes of this". Brides spend two to four fittings with you, and where they spend them matters.

What this means for your ads. With under a thousand followers, most brides will find you through ads, not through your feed. The posts to put money behind are the ones about making: the sketch, the buttons, the fitting, the bride on the day.

08 · The plan, month by month

Stay on through New Year, when your ads have run before.

  1. Week 1
    Audit and tidy

    Add the Meta pixel and Conversions API, track the contact form as an enquiry, add a consultation button, fix the placeholder page and the footer link.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    A consultation and sketch, a fitting, the details at the table, the studio and the coast.

  3. Week 3
    One campaign live

    Shown to brides planning weddings in the North East, plus a second to people who have visited your site. Both run continuously through New Year.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New reels added every week to the same campaign. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on orders

    We report consultations booked and dresses ordered, and set the budget for spring on those numbers and on how many dresses your studio can take.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on enquiries, consultations and dresses ordered. You keep every video.
