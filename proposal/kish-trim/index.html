01 · The short version

A national-standard workshop, with no paid reach.

Your website already makes the argument for us: "Specialist trim work is rarely just a local purchase." Clients choose the workshop best suited to the job, not the nearest one. But those clients can only choose you if they find you. We found no Meta ads from Kish Trim, no Meta pixel or Google Analytics on kishtrim.co.uk, and only two videos among your last twelve Instagram posts. Meanwhile other trimmers, including one that names Sunderland in its ad, are advertising seat retrims this week.

What is already strong, and worth building on

  • 120 Google reviews averaging 4.9, with reviewers naming Angus and the standard of the finish.
  • A portfolio people stop for: an AC Cobra from a bare shell, a Bentley Corniche hood, Ferrari rugs, a Lamborghini Huracan seat.

You do not need a new website or a new brand. You need the work filmed, and a steady campaign that shows it to car, boat and aircraft owners who have never heard of a trim shop in Sunderland.

02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, no pixel, and a national market.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. We found no Facebook page for Kish Trim in the library and no ads mentioning you. When we loaded kishtrim.co.uk, no Meta pixel and no Google Analytics tag loaded.

What that means

1

Other trimmers are reaching your customers

Furniture Clinic's North East branch is running a car interior ad that names Sunderland. Samco Interiors and Kent Trimline both started new seat retrim ads in the last week of September.

Fix: your own campaign, built from your own jobs, running every week.

2

Your best jobs are not local

A classic convertible owner looking for a new mohair hood will travel, or send the car. You say so yourselves. Ads are how that owner, 300 miles away, sees your work.

Fix: UK-wide audiences of classic, prestige and convertible car owners, plus boat and private aircraft owners.

3

No way to follow up or measure

Someone who looks through your seven workshop stories cannot be shown your work again, and nobody can see which visits become enquiries.

Fix: Meta pixel, Conversions API and enquiry tracking in week one.

You post most weeks, with honest captions about each job. The pattern in your last twelve posts, from July to September 2026:

  • A person in the story wins. "Great to have Chris in the trim shop, refurbishing his 2 front seats..." earned 342 likes, five times your next best post.
  • The finished piece beats the close-up. The Lamborghini Huracan seat (66 likes) opens on the finished, branded headrest. Posts that open on a rear window, a badge or a patch of headlining earned 13 to 26.
  • Your captions are already scripts. "Diagnosed, new motor fitted, now all back together and working perfectly." That is a 20 second video: the fault, the fix, the result.

What this means for your ads. Your followers mostly know you already. Ads put the same work in front of classic, prestige, boat and aircraft owners across the UK who have never heard of you, and a stitching video will travel much further than a photo of a finished seat.

08 · The plan, month by month

Track it, film it, then show it to the whole country.

  1. Week 1
    Tracking and tidy

    Meta pixel and Conversions API, enquiry form and phone clicks counted, and a real job at the top of your homepage.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    A full retrim in progress, close-up stitching, a hood being fitted, and Angus on camera.

  3. Week 3
    Two campaigns live

    One UK-wide for classic, prestige and convertible owners. One for the North East, for repairs and dealer work.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on jobs booked

    We report enquiries, quotes and jobs, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on enquiries, quotes and jobs booked. You keep every video.
