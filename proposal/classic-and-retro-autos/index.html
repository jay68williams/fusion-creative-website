02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, no pixel, and restorations only one customer sees.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. Your page "Classic & Retro Autos Ltd" (4.1K followers, listed as a vehicle restoration service) has never run an ad. When we loaded classicandretroautos.co.uk and read its code, no Meta pixel was present.

What that means

1

Restoration specialists elsewhere are advertising to your customers

Auto RARA has run around 250 ads, with about 10 live today. Farringtons advertises a UK wide collection service, so a Durham owner is within their reach.

Fix: a campaign shown to classic car owners within a drive of Witton Gilbert, built from your own restorations.

2

Two revenue lines, no campaign for either

Restorations and car sales need different ads. A restoration ad finds an owner with a project. A sales ad finds a buyer for your 1956 Bentley S1. Right now neither is advertised.

Fix: two campaigns, one for restoration enquiries and one for the cars you have for sale.

3

No way to follow up

Someone who reads the whole DB6 case study is exactly who you want to see again. Without a pixel, you cannot show them your next restoration.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one.

08 · The plan, month by month

Film what you already do, then put it in front of owners and buyers.

  1. Week 1
    Set up tracking

    Meta pixel and Conversions API on your website, enquiries counted by type (restoration, sale, inspection), and a "sell your classic with us" page.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    Cars mid-restoration, the welding bay, a walkaround of each car for sale, and the team on camera.

  3. Week 3
    Two campaigns live

    One for restoration enquiries from classic owners within a drive of Durham, one for the cars you have for sale, shown to buyers across the UK.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on jobs and sales

    We report enquiries, inspections, restorations booked and cars sold, and set the next budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram and Facebook posting support, and a one-page report on enquiries, restorations booked and cars sold. You keep every video.
