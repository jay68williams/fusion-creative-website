What is already strong, and worth building on

  • An award: Best T6 in Class at Busfest 2026. Most converters never win one.
  • Full 3D CAD design and a 3,000 by 2,000mm CNC machine in your own workshop since 2018. Almost nobody shows how a van is actually made, and you can.

02 · Your Meta ads

No ads in the last year. The pixel is waiting.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK over the past year. For your page, Northshore Custom Conversions (1.9K followers), it shows none, live or past. Your website does have a Meta pixel installed, so Meta has been quietly recording your visitors with nothing to show them.

What that means

1

The people who visit your site are never followed up

Someone reads your Hydro (Slide) page for ten minutes, leaves to think about it, and never sees Northshore again. With the pixel already installed, a simple ad showing that person a Hydro build the following week costs pennies.

Fix: a small always-on retargeting campaign in week one.

2

Your competitors are filling that space

Denby Campervans has 12 live ads and Spartan Campers 11, most showing a specific van with its price. When a VW owner in Durham starts looking, those are the conversions they are shown.

Fix: put your builds in front of them first, starting with the award winner.

3

Nothing tells a buyer what a Northshore conversion costs

Your range pages describe each layout in detail but show no price. Your own resale listings mention conversions with "almost £29,000" and "almost £34,000" spent on them. A "from" price lets the right buyers qualify themselves before they email.

Fix: a "from" price on each range, used in the ads.

08 · The plan, month by month

Fill the gaps, film the workshop, then put it in front of buyers.

  1. Week 1
    Set up

    Add a build enquiry form and "from" prices, and use the Meta pixel already on your site to start a small retargeting campaign for people who have visited.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    One in the workshop (CAD, CNC, a build in progress), one with the award van and a collection. You keep working; we film around you.

  3. Week 3
    New campaign live

    Three angles tested: the award, the workshop, and price. Aimed at VW Transporter owners and people interested in campervans across the North and Scotland.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing enquiries and put the budget behind what is. Organic posting three to four times a week.

  5. Month 3
    Review on builds booked

    We report enquiries, workshop visits and builds booked, ad by ad, and decide the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, organic posting, and a one-page report on enquiries, visits and builds booked. You keep every video.
