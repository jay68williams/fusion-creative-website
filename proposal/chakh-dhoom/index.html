Where you're at

Thanks for taking the time to sit down with us. Quick recap of how we see it.

We're Fusion Creative, a content marketing agency based in Newcastle. We work with hospitality businesses across the North East, running their short-form content, email and paid social so the owners can run the venue.

Chakh means to taste. Dhoom means smoke. That's a brand built for the camera, and you've already proved the food deserves the attention: an award-winning street food restaurant that fills up by 6pm on Osborne Road. And with Dhoom Bar open next door, you've got something most operators would kill for: two venues, one door apart, feeding each other customers. The restaurant sends people next door for drinks, the bar sends people in for food.

But right now only one of those brands exists online. Dhoom Bar is open and pouring, yet nobody scrolling Instagram in Newcastle knows it's there. That's not a problem, it's an open goal: a ready-made venue with zero content debt, waiting to be put on the map.
