02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, no pixel, and rooms made for video.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. Your page "AVNS Ltd" has run none, now or in the past. When we loaded avnscinema.co.uk, no Meta pixel, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager or Google Ads tag loaded.

What that means

1

Other installers are selling cinema rooms with ads

Finite Solutions in Yorkshire and Couture Digital in London both have cinema ads running today. Couture has had the same cinema ads running since 4 February. Businesses rarely keep paying for months for ads that bring nothing.

Fix: one always-on campaign shown only to homeowners in the areas you cover.

2

A big purchase needs repeat visits

Nobody books a cinema room after one look. They come back to the photos several times before they call. Without ads, the only way back to you is remembering your name.

Fix: show your rooms to the same local homeowners over weeks, ending with your no-obligation quote.

3

No way to follow up or measure

Someone who spends five minutes on your gallery cannot be shown your rooms again, and you cannot tell which visits turn into quotes.

Fix: Meta pixel, Conversions API and contact form tracking in week one.

Your audiences are small, which is normal for an installer that has grown on recommendation. The formats that sell cinema rooms:

  • Lights down. The room lit, then the lights dimming, the ceiling coming on and the first scene playing. A photo cannot do that.
  • Before and after. The empty garage or loft, then the finished room, from the same spot.
  • The installer to camera. Why that projector, why the seats sit there, what 17 years has taught you. It turns a nice room into trust in the people who built it.

What this means for your ads. You do not need a following to win the next room. Ads show your work to homeowners in exactly the areas you cover, whether they follow you or not.

08 · The plan, month by month

Track first, film your best rooms, then stay on.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Meta pixel and Conversions API on your website, contact form and phone clicks counted, the "Hello world!" post removed, Instagram linked, and captions added to the gallery.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    In two or three finished rooms, with your customers' permission, and at a live install. You keep working; we film around you.

  3. Week 3
    Always-on campaign live

    Shown to homeowners in the areas you cover, sending people to your website and asking for a no-obligation quote.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing quote requests and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on jobs

    We report enquiries, site visits, quotes and jobs won, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, posting on Facebook and Instagram, and a one-page report on enquiries, quotes and jobs won. You keep every video.
