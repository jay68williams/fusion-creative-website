01 · The short version

The groundwork is done. Nobody is being shown it.

Your website is unusually thorough: 159 pages, with honest price ranges in your cost guides and town pages from Whitley Bay to Whitby. You film your jobs too. What is missing is the part that puts your work in front of homeowners who are not searching yet: short video, a Meta pixel, and ads. What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Over 300 completed projects, and recent five-star reviews shown on your website.
  • Real prices in public: your guides give ranges such as block paving at £70 to £110 per m² and resin-bound at £50 to £90 per m². Homeowners want exactly this before they call.
  • More than 90 town and area pages, from Ponteland and Darras Hall to Yarm and Whitby.
  • A clear promise in your own words: "Do it Once, Do it Right!"
  • Strong photographs of finished work: block-paved driveways, porcelain patios, a circular patio set in a new lawn.
  • You already film: 13 videos on YouTube, and three embedded on your homepage.

You do not need more pages. You need short, vertical video of the jobs you already do, tracking on the site, and a steady campaign shown to homeowners in the towns you want work in.

Your latest YouTube video, "Garden Clearance Costs in Newcastle Before a Landscaping Project", shares its title with one of your blog guides. About a month after it went up, it had 6 views. The knowledge is good; the format does not suit the feed. These are the formats that work for driveways, patios and gardens:

  • Before and after. The same view, from the same spot, at the start and the end of the job.
  • The build, sped up. Dig-out, sub-base, laying, jointing. It shows what "do it once, do it right" actually means.
  • A person to camera. "A driveway on a Newcastle semi costs this, and here is why." Your guides already hold the answers.

What this means for your ads. You do not need followers to win the next driveway. Ads show your work to homeowners in the postcodes you choose, whether they follow you or not.
