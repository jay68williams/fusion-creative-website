02 · Your Meta ads

Good videos, all pointing at saunas.

Your ads run from the page "Better Living Outdoors" (8.5K followers). Meta's Ad Library shows around 41 ads from it, five of them live, and we could read 32. Here are three.

What we found across your ads

1

Hot tubs are the headline, but not in the ads

Your website is titled "Hot Tub Showroom" and the hot tubs we saw on it run from £4,699 to £26,995. All five live ads are for saunas and chill tubs. Of the 32 ads we could read, two mention hot tubs: a Black Friday ad in November 2025 and the one above.

Fix: a hot tub campaign running beside the sauna one, filmed in your showroom and at your installs.

2

The £1,500 saving is only on the website

"Save up to £1,500 on selected spas and saunas" sits at the top of every page. None of the 32 ads we read mention it. Buyers who never visit the site never see it.

Fix: put the saving in the first line and in the picture, with the price before and after.

3

Swim spas and Bullfrog have never been advertised

The Chronos Mini Pool Swim Spa is £29,999 and the Bullfrog range, on display at Shrewsbury, runs from £8,995 to £26,995. Neither appears in any ad we could read. At our assumed margin, one swim spa sale would cover three months of the plan below.

Fix: a small, steady campaign for each, aimed at people who have already visited those pages.

4

The same words, restarted often

"Don't cut corners" appears in 10 of the 32 ads. Several ran for under a day, such as two on 17 June 2026 that ran for three hours. Each restart makes Meta learn again.

Fix: fewer campaigns that stay on, with new video added each week.

08 · The plan, month by month

Hot tubs and swim spas live before the winter evenings.

  1. Week 1
    Account and tracking check

    Review your ad account and pixel events, add Conversions API, and track showroom bookings, WhatsApp video call requests and online orders separately.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    The Durham showroom, a hot tub install, a swim spa walk-through, and the people behind the business on camera.

  3. Week 3
    New campaigns live

    A hot tub campaign with the £1,500 saving, and swim spa and Bullfrog campaigns for site visitors, running beside your sauna ads.

  4. Month 2
    Add, do not restart

    New video added every week to the same campaigns. We switch off what is not bringing bookings and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on sales

    We report showroom visits, video calls and sales by range, and set the budget for the new year on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting support, and a one-page report on bookings, video calls and sales by range. You keep every video.
