  • Two showrooms people can sit in, in Yarm and Harrogate, open Tuesday to Saturday.
  • Cinema rooms designed with Dolby and Artcoustic, plus smart home, WiFi and listening rooms.
  • An online shop of pre-owned and ex-display Bang & Olufsen, from a £187.50 ex-demo Beosound Explore to a £13,000 Beosound Theatre.

02 · Your Meta ads

About 130 ads, in short bursts, mostly pointing away from you.

Your ads run from the page "Bang & Olufsen Harrogate". Meta's Ad Library lists around 130 since October 2025. We loaded 90 of them and checked each one. Here are three.

What we found across all 90

1

Most clicks went to Bang & Olufsen

57 of the 90 ads linked to bang-olufsen.com or bang-olufsen.co.uk, 5 to Instagram and 4 to impactav.uk. Your pixel is on impactav.uk, so most visitors could never be measured or shown an ad again. Some of this may be required by Bang & Olufsen's co-op rules; if so, keep those for brand ads and run your own for cinema rooms and pre-owned.

Fix: send your own campaigns to your own pages, with the pixel and Conversions API counting bookings.

2

Bursts, then silence

Ads started in October, November, January, February, one in March and five in August. Many ran for under a week; several for less than a day. Meta needs a steady run to learn who books. Nothing is running now, going into the busiest gifting months of the year.

Fix: one always-on campaign, with new creative each week.

3

Your two most profitable stories are missing

Cinema rooms appeared in one two-week burst with product images. Pre-owned and ex-display stock, which most dealers do not offer at all, has never been advertised.

Fix: separate campaigns for cinema rooms, new Bang & Olufsen and pre-owned.

You have two Instagram accounts, and neither is reaching people. The pattern is clear:

  • The big account is not a real audience. @impactav_uk has 11K followers but follows almost 5,000 accounts and gets 0 to 6 likes a post. That usually means follow-for-follow growth. It has not posted since 13 December 2025.
  • The active account posts in bursts. @bangandolufsenharrogate posted almost daily in late May and early June, then nothing until 1 October.
  • Long captions, still images. Your writing is good ("You've been meaning to do this for years"), but almost every post is a still photo. Sound is the product, and nobody can hear it in a photo.

What this means for your ads. Follower numbers do not matter much here. A short video of someone hearing a pair of Beolab 28s for the first time, or a cinema room going dark, will find the right buyers in Harrogate and Teesside through ads, whether or not they follow you.

06 · What is working in your market

Real rooms, a reason to visit, a price question answered.

These ads are live now. These are the patterns we would build your campaign on, filmed in your showrooms and in rooms you have built.

1

Show your rooms

Cinemas and listening rooms you have built, filmed properly, with the lights going down. Brand imagery cannot compete with a real room.

2

Faces, reactions, a record going on. Sound is the product; the video has to carry it.

An hour in the listening room, a home demo, a cinema estimate. One clear next step in every ad.

08 · The plan, month by month

Point the ads at your own site, film the rooms, then keep it running.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Conversions API alongside your pixel, booking and enquiry events counted, page titles fixed, and the website linked to your active Instagram.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    One in the Harrogate and Yarm showrooms with real customers listening, one at a finished cinema room and a live install. You keep working; we film around you.

  3. Week 3
    Three campaigns live

    Cinema rooms, new Bang & Olufsen and pre-owned, each sending people to your own website, running every day.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week. We switch off what is not bringing bookings and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on sales

    We report enquiries, showroom bookings and sales, campaign by campaign, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, posting on Instagram, and a one-page report on enquiries, bookings and sales. You keep every video.
