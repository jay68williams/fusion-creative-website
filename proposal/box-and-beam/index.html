What is already strong, and worth building on

  • Your own prices on the website: £250 to £300 a day, £350 a day for container work, £65 to £85 to hang a door.
  • Rated 5.0 from 8 Google reviews, and the eight shown on your website were all left between 24 September and 1 October 2026.
  • Fifteen jobs in your job book, each with a town and a month, from a hand-cut roof in 2021 to 14 oak doors in Cramlington in September 2026.
  • Build work that films well: cut roofs, a warm roof, pergolas, a kitchen in a timber lodge, and container conversions, the work Box & Beam started with.
  • The cover written down: £5m public liability with Markel, CSCS, DBS and SSSTS, and the balance paid only after a walk-round.

You do not need more trust signals. You need homeowners to see one kind of build, again and again, so they know to ring you for it.

02 · Your Meta ads

No ads, and no pixel to measure them.

Meta's Ad Library shows every ad run in the UK. We found no advertiser page for Box & Beam and no ads mentioning you. When we loaded boxandbeam.co.uk, Google Analytics loaded but a Meta pixel did not.

What that means

1

Other trades are in your customers' feeds

Waterstone Bathrooms and Priory Bathrooms are both advertising to North East homeowners this week. Priory is selling "November & December slots". The homeowners you want see them, not you.

Fix: a small campaign, shown only around Morpeth, Newcastle and Northumberland, built from your own jobs.

2

Everything at once does not advertise well

Your website covers ten services, from a £65 door to a container conversion. An ad that tries to say all of it says nothing. And at your prices, a door hang will never pay for the ad that found it.

Fix: lead with one high-value build, container conversions and garden buildings, and let reviews and referrals bring the rest.

3

Analytics, but no way to follow up

Google Analytics tells you how many people visit. Without a Meta pixel, someone who reads your container page cannot be shown your work again, and an ad cannot be judged on quote requests.

Fix: Meta pixel and Conversions API in week one, with the quote form counted.

You have the work to post. What is missing is reach and one clear place to send people:

08 · The plan, month by month

Pick one build, film it, then advertise small and steady.

  1. Week 1
    Fix the basics

    Meta pixel, Conversions API and quote requests counted. One Instagram account, linked from your website. We agree the one build to lead with.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3
    Two filming days

    On a live build, plus you to camera and a finished job or two.

  3. Week 3
    First campaign live

    One build type, shown to homeowners around Morpeth, Newcastle and Northumberland, at £400 a month.

  4. Month 2
    Cut, keep, scale

    New video every week from the live job. We switch off what is not bringing quote requests and put the budget behind what is.

  5. Month 3
    Review on builds won

    We report quote requests, site visits and builds won, and set the next quarter's budget on those numbers.

Every month you get: 20 to 25 edited videos, two filming days, all Meta ads written, built and managed, Instagram posting, and a one-page report on quote requests, site visits and builds won. You keep every video.
