The case study

A small North East candle brand. Nothing viral. Just an engine.

The Northumberland Candle Company came to us doing £6,650 a month. Good product, loyal customers, no system for growth. No agency would have taken them at that size. We did, because the size was never the problem, the missing engine was.

We started on 1 July, and the first thing we did was not run a single ad. We rebuilt their entire website from the ground up, every page, built to convert. Then we took over their paid ads and their email: built the creative, ran the campaigns, engineered the offers, and turned their email list from an afterthought into a revenue channel. Every number on this page is from their Shopify and Meta accounts.

Every two days now makes more than their old best month.

June, their best month before us: £6,650. August daily average with us: £3,458. That is the difference between posting and hoping, and running ads and email as one system built to sell.

How we did it

Conversion first. Then traffic. Run as one system.

There is no point paying to send traffic to a site that will not convert it. Getting people to your website is only half the challenge. The other half is turning them into customers. So the site comes first, and only then do we scale what goes into it.
