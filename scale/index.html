The case study

A small North East candle brand. Nothing viral. Just an engine.

The Northumberland Candle Company came to us as a small store. Good product, loyal customers, no system for growth. No agency would have taken them at that size. We did, because the size was never the problem, the missing engine was.

We started in June, and for a full month we did not run a single ad. We spent June rebuilding their entire website from the ground up, every page, optimised and ready to convert before a penny went into traffic. Then, from the start of July, we switched the ads on and took over their email: built the creative, ran the campaigns, engineered the offers, and turned their email list from an afterthought into a revenue channel. Every number on this page is from their Shopify and Meta accounts.

Every two days now makes more than their old best month.

That is the difference between posting and hoping, and running ads and email as one system built to sell.

How we did it

Conversion first. Then traffic. Run as one system.

There is no point paying to send traffic to a site that will not convert it. Getting people to your website is only half the challenge. The other half is turning them into customers. So the site comes first, and only then do we scale what goes into it.

1. The website

Rebuilt to convert

We spent our entire first month rebuilding their site from the ground up before spending a penny on ads. Every page, every product, built for the moment a paying visitor lands. It is a huge part of why the numbers above happened.

2. Paid ads

Creative and management

We make the ads and we run them. Testing angles, scaling winners, killing losers fast. Meta-attributed return peaked at 8.2x in month one.

3. Email

Campaigns and flows

Launches, offers and flows in your brand's own voice, sent to people who already want to buy. The cheapest revenue you are currently leaving on the table.

4. Offers

Offer engineering

Bundles, launches and moments worth buying now. Ads bring the traffic, the site and offers convert it, email brings it back.
